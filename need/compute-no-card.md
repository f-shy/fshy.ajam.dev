# Free compute with no card

> Usable compute offers whose stated identity requirement is not a card.

Ranked by `vorder`, ascending; the rows with no parsed number follow, listed and not dropped, and the self-hosted rows come last. The same list: `https://fshy.ajam.dev/api/v1/need/compute-no-card`, the MCP tool `need`, or this SQL:

```sql
SELECT rid, vorder FROM record WHERE topic='compute' AND grp='usable' AND needs_card=0 AND identity_basis='stated' AND duplicate_of IS NULL AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no free %' AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no published free %' AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no perpetual free %'
ORDER BY self_hosted, vorder ASC NULLS LAST
```
