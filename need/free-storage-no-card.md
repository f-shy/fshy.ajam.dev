# Free storage with no card

> Usable storage with a stated free allowance, in the storage topic or in egress with an S3 interface, with a stated identity requirement that is not a card, best verdict and strongest evidence first. A paid plan with no free allowance is not listed.

Ranked by `vorder`, ascending; the rows with no parsed number follow, listed and not dropped, and the self-hosted rows come last. The same list: `https://fshy.ajam.dev/api/v1/need/free-storage-no-card`, the MCP tool `need`, or this SQL:

```sql
SELECT rid, vorder FROM record WHERE (topic='storage' OR (topic='egress' AND interfaces LIKE '%s3%')) AND grp='usable' AND needs_card=0 AND identity_basis='stated' AND duplicate_of IS NULL AND rid IN (SELECT rid FROM claim WHERE kind='has-free-tier' AND num=1)
ORDER BY self_hosted, vorder ASC NULLS LAST
```
