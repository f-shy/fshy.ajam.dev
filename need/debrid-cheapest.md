# Debrid and retrieval services by monthly price

> Debrid offers with a parsed monthly USD price, cheapest first, then the offers with no parsed price. An offer priced per TB and not per month is ranked in debrid-by-tb, because a price per TB and a price per month are not one unit.

Ranked by `usd_month`, ascending; the rows with no parsed number follow, listed and not dropped, and the self-hosted rows come last. The same list: `https://fshy.ajam.dev/api/v1/need/debrid-cheapest`, the MCP tool `need`, or this SQL:

```sql
SELECT rid, usd_month FROM record WHERE topic='debrid' AND grp IN ('usable','paid','trial') AND NOT (usd_month IS NULL AND usd_per_tb_month IS NOT NULL) AND duplicate_of IS NULL
ORDER BY self_hosted, usd_month ASC NULLS LAST
```
