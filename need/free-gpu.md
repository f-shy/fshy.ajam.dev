# Free or trial GPU time

> Compute offers that mention GPU in their category or text and are usable or trial. Read the free tier line for hours per week or month.

Ranked by `vorder`, ascending; the rows with no parsed number follow, listed and not dropped, and the self-hosted rows come last. The same list: `https://fshy.ajam.dev/api/v1/need/free-gpu`, the MCP tool `need`, or this SQL:

```sql
SELECT rid, vorder FROM record WHERE topic='compute' AND grp IN ('usable','trial') AND (category LIKE '%GPU%' OR rno IN (SELECT docid FROM text_fts WHERE text_fts MATCH 'offer:gpu')) AND duplicate_of IS NULL AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no free %' AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no published free %' AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no perpetual free %'
ORDER BY self_hosted, vorder ASC NULLS LAST
```
