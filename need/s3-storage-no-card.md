# Free S3-compatible storage, no card

> Usable storage offers with a stated free allowance, in the storage topic or in egress, with an S3 interface whose identity requirement was read and does not include a card. A paid plan with no free allowance is not listed. Sorted by USD per TB-month where a price parsed.

Ranked by `usd_per_tb_month`, ascending; the rows with no parsed number follow, listed and not dropped, and the self-hosted rows come last. The same list: `https://fshy.ajam.dev/api/v1/need/s3-storage-no-card`, the MCP tool `need`, or this SQL:

```sql
SELECT rid, usd_per_tb_month FROM record WHERE (topic='storage' OR (topic='egress' AND interfaces LIKE '%s3%')) AND grp='usable' AND (interfaces LIKE '%s3%') AND needs_card=0 AND identity_basis='stated' AND duplicate_of IS NULL AND rid IN (SELECT rid FROM claim WHERE kind='has-free-tier' AND num=1)
ORDER BY self_hosted, usd_per_tb_month ASC NULLS LAST
```
