> Usable ways to get traffic in or out: tunnels, relays, IPv6 brokers, VPN egress.

```sql
SELECT rid, vorder FROM record WHERE topic='egress' AND grp='usable' AND duplicate_of IS NULL AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no free %' AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no published free %' AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no perpetual free %'
ORDER BY self_hosted, vorder ASC NULLS LAST
```
