# Inference APIs with no card and no phone

> Usable model-inference endpoints, in the inference topic or categorised as inference elsewhere, whose stated identity needs neither a card nor a phone.

Ranked by `vorder`, ascending; the rows with no parsed number follow, listed and not dropped, and the self-hosted rows come last. The same list: `https://fshy.ajam.dev/api/v1/need/inference-no-card-no-phone`, the MCP tool `need`, or this SQL:

```sql
SELECT rid, vorder FROM record WHERE (topic='inference' OR category LIKE '%inference%') AND grp='usable' AND needs_card=0 AND needs_phone=0 AND identity_basis='stated' AND duplicate_of IS NULL AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no free %' AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no published free %' AND coalesce(summary,'') NOT LIKE 'no perpetual free %'
ORDER BY self_hosted, vorder ASC NULLS LAST
```
