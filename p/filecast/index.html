Two contradictory VAT statements sit on the same page: '결제시 부가세 10%가 포함됩니다' beside the price table and '모든 이용권 금액은 부가세 10% 별도 입니다' in the notes. The conservative reading (VAT extra) is the one recorded.
Bindingnone There is no byte meter to hit; a monthly transfer of 1 TiB is limited by what the catalogue holds, not by a quota.
Identitycountry-phone The product is a Korean domestic 정액제: the charge flow is the vendor's own '캐스트 원페이' and the refund clause speaks to a mobile number ('환불로 인한 휴대폰 정보는 ...'), which places a Korean handset in the payment path. No card rail for foreign issuers is documented on the page.
EgressUnlimited downloads inside the 30-day window of the domestic catalogue; the pass excludes partner content ('제휴콘텐츠 제외'). Fair-use clause: '일반적인 이용이 아닌 다른 용도로 사용할 경우 다운로드가 제한될 수 있습니다'.

Retentionnot applicable: a catalogue, not client storage
Interfacerest Web plus a Windows download client (the site advertises speed through its own client) and a mobile app. No API.
ToolingThe vendor's own PC client and mobile app; the catalogue is not exposed to rclone or JDownloader.
Composes withNot a multi-hoster and not chainable into one: it retrieves only Filecast's own catalogue, so it solves Korean-content gaps rather than host gaps. For the stack it is a source of Korean media, never of arbitrary URLs.
ReliabilitySubscriptions are Korean-time based ('월정액 상품의 사용기간은 대한민국 표준시간을 기준으로 합니다'), refunds only within 48 hours and only once, and downloads are throttled for non-ordinary use under the fair-use clause.
NotesKorea's retrieval market is not debrid at all: it is webhard, a domestic catalogue sold by the month. That is why the Korean queries return no premium-link generator. Trigger condition: Filecast publishes an overseas payment path.
Evidence notedocumented Charge page read with prices and clauses. The identity claim rests on the domestic payment flow plus the mobile-number clause; completing a signup from outside Korea would test it, and that test is the thing this record says will fail.
