Free plan ceiling is medium for automatic sizing and 3 custom images kept; the desktop needs Pro. A workspace daily cap can refuse creates, forks and starts with daily_cap_reached, and no-card workspaces with zero credit cannot create after credit runs out.
Bindingvolume-quota The one-time USD 10 credit is what runs out first: at USD 0.0175 an hour it is 571 hours of a small sandbox, before the 2-at-once concurrency limit or the storage charge after 48 hours.
Identityemail (placeholder) unverified: no captured or fresh first-party page names the sign-up or sign-in method; the CLI quickstart says only 'zode login opens your browser and prints a code' and the console renders only its page metadata, so the exact registration fields are unverified: behind a sign-up. A personal workspace starts with USD 10 and no card; adding a card is needed to continue past zero credit. The terms set an 18-year minimum age but name no phone or ID document.
EgressThe docs describe outbound network use and a private mesh with security groups, but publish no egress allowance or per-GB price. The homepage states 'Egress and AI keys: $0'.
Per-object capThe small sandbox has 50 GB of disk, medium 50 GB, large 110 GB and xlarge 250 GB; no larger published disk size exists.
RetentionStorage is free for the first 48 hours of every sandbox, then USD 0.10 per GB-month until the sandbox is deleted; stopping a sandbox ends its compute charge but not its storage charge. A sandbox with no card and zero credit has its running sandboxes stopped within about 15 minutes. (2 days)
Interfacecli, rest, chat A zode CLI and SDK plus a REST API with a GET /v1/usage endpoint; MCP sandbox_create is documented, and an AI chat agent runs in the sandbox. A Linux desktop is available on paid plans and fails with plan_required on Free.
endpoint: https://api.zode.dev/v1/sandboxes
Toolingzode CLI with new, cost and sandbox commands; SDKs; REST API; MCP server.
Composes withSandboxes run on the same machines as Zode CI and can be forked and snapshotted; a workspace can cap daily spend, and AI provider keys stay on the user's own accounts with no markup.
ReliabilityA workspace with no card and zero credit has running sandboxes stopped within about 15 minutes; after the 48-hour storage grace period a deleted or stopped sandbox still incurs storage charges until deletion. The service is in early access and features, limits and prices may change. No page read states the registration identity.
NotesThe rate card is USD 0.00875 per vCPU-hour, so the 2 vCPU / 4 GB small sandbox is USD 0.0175 an hour and the USD 10 credit is 571 hours of that sandbox. Storage is free for 48 hours then USD 0.10 per GB-month of declared disk size, and the homepage states egress and AI keys cost zero. The hosted VM is the offer; the install script in the homepage installs a CLI client, not a self-hosted service.
