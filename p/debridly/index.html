unit mixed; metered on unstated; A free account exists but no free allowance is published anywhere on the pages read. Some hosts are marked Offline in the list (daofile.com 'Offline Daily cap 3 GB' on the read date). The premium page warns that hosts such as Rapidgator and Tezfiles sell a separate premium package for 'exclusive' content that Debridly cannot unlock.
Bindingvolume-quota At 1 TiB/month the per-host daily cap is the constraint that binds: 200 GB/day on 1Fichier is 6 TB/month, but a month spread over the 2-5 GB hosts (FileDot, Emload, DaoFile) hits their caps at tens of GB, so host mix decides whether 100 GiB is reachable at all.
Identityemail A free account needs only an email, and the generator checks 'email verification' before issuing a link. Premium is a one-time Stripe-style purchase; no card is needed for the free account.
EgressDirect CDN links from the host's own servers with 'no throttling added by Debridly'. Counted per host per 24 hours, reset at 00:00 UTC.
free allowance: unpublished free allowance; Premium caps per host, e.g. 200 GB/24h on 1Fichier
Per-object capnot applicable: a URL is unlocked and a direct link returned; nothing is stored by Debridly
RetentionDirect links are valid for 24 hours on Premium ('24h link validity'). (1 days)
Interfacerest Web generator that returns a direct URL; the page lists file name, size, host and expiry. No public API documented on the pages read.
ToolingAny HTTP client on the returned direct URL; the site says 'No browser extensions or software required', which is also a note that no CLI exists.
Composes withThe published per-host caps make it the only provider among debrid providers where a reader can plan a month: 200 GB/day on 1Fichier alone is 6 TB/month, far past the 1 TiB monthly volume, so the annual USD 49.99 is the cheapest documented route over 100 GiB.
ReliabilityPer-host caps are enforced at generation time and nothing is deducted when a link fails with LINK_D0WN; hosts listed Offline return nothing; premium cannot unlock hosts' own 'exclusive' tiers.
NotesThe only provider among debrid providers that publishes a numeric per-host daily cap instead of an 'unlimited' adjective. The annual term is sub-USD-5 per month; the 30-day term is not. Free tier exists but its allowance is unpublished, which is why the record is priced at the premium tier. The hosts page count differs between the page text ('90+ hosts') and the premium page ('70+ supported hosts'), so the true count is between the two. The newest service in the set and the only one with no archive footprint whatsoever, so it is a price with no history rather than a stable price. Its 365-day term at USD 49.99 is the second-cheapest annual in the set after OffCloud's EUR 40.
Evidence notedocumented Prices and the per-host caps are quoted from the vendor's own pages. The free tier's allowance is unstated, so the free half of the record is unverified.
