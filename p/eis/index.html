Composes withThis is the chain evaluated providers was looking for and it fails at the last step: e-Residency gives a non-resident a 100 percent-owned Estonian company without travel, the company clears the incorporation test, and then the substance rule asks for Estonian payroll, tax or R&D presence, which is the same relocation cost Hub71 charges in a different currency. The EUR 75,000 to EUR 350,000 development grant has no published closure and is the larger target once substance exists.

NotesThe 5,000 euro tranche paid only at project end is a cash-flow fact that changes the usable value: the founder must fund the work before the final fifth arrives. e-Residency alone does not make the grants reachable: the e-Residency programme's own page is unusually candid that incorporation alone is not enough. The Start-up Grant is closed: the budget was fulfilled on 11 August 2026 and applications closed 14 August 2026. The Innovation and Development Grant publishes its size with no closure notice, and the substance rule is general to EIS grants. Only its article on amount and intensity was read.
