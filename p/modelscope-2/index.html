Free tierFree API access to selected open-source LLM, multimodal and text-to-image models, described as a non-commercial, non-profit product. Quota and concurrency are adjusted dynamically with platform load. Calls can be redeemed with Magicubes: about 0.5 per call for lightweight models, about 1 for standard and about 2 for flagship. A per-day call count is not stated on the page read
unit requests; metered on request-count; perpetual-free-allowance

  • volume-quota 2000 API-Inference calls per day per registered user, as announced by the operator; the current limits page gives no daily figure and says quotas adjust dynamically with platform load source
  • volume-quota per-model cap reported as 200 calls per day (100 for the largest DeepSeek models), within the daily total; the operator's current limits page gives no per-model figure and says limits adjust dynamically source

Identitynational-id the ModelScope account must first bind to an Alibaba Cloud account that has completed real-name verification before API-Inference works normally. A ModelScope maintainer stated the binding is open only to Alibaba Cloud China-site accounts, not international-site accounts; a China-site account registers with a mainland China mobile number and individual verification needs an identity-verified personal Alipay account. The international site verifies by passport or driver's license, but its accounts are a separate system and are not accepted for the binding. unverified: whether the China-site Alipay verification accepts a non-mainland document
