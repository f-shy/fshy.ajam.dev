Free tierHobby is $0 per month plus usage, with $10 of starter credits for 60 days (about 43 hours of Standard environments), 1 engineer, 3 projects, 4 concurrent vCPUs and 1 self-hosted runner; add a card anytime to pay as you go with no subscription.
unit credits; metered on wall-clock; The $10 credit is one-time and lasts 60 days, not renewing, so it is a trial rather than a recurring free allowance. Standard is 2 vCPUs and 8 GiB at $0.230 per hour on the pricing page; the limits reference lists $0.0504 per vCPU-hour and $0.0162 per GiB-hour past the allowance, which at 2 vCPU/8 GiB is $0.2376 an hour. The Hobby plan allows 1 engineer, 3 projects, 2 model providers and 2 access connections. Starter credits run out after 60 days or when spent, and a card is needed to keep running.
Bindingvolume-quota The $10 starter credit covering about 43 hours in 60 days binds before the 4 concurrent vCPU ceiling, because paid usage stops when the credit runs out and no card is held at signup.
Identityemail The sign-in page at app.kosem.ai/sign-in says 'Sign in or create an account.' and renders 'Continue with Google', a GitHub button, 'Sign in with a passkey' and an email field posting to /v1/auth/email/start; the page offers up to 20 passkeys. No phone or document field is visible, and no card is asked for before the $10 starter credit is used. Card details inside Billing are unverified: behind a sign-up.
EgressNo sandbox egress price or allowance is stated on the pricing or limits pages; the pages price running vCPUs, memory and disk only. Static sites published by agents are included on every plan. Sandbox-level outbound traffic terms are unverified: behind a sign-up.
Per-object capThe largest single environment on Hobby is 2 vCPUs, 8 GiB and 50 GiB disk; agent files are capped at 50 MiB per file and 1 GiB per agent, bounded by the organization's 1 GiB file storage quota.
RetentionAn agent's environment stops 5 minutes after its turn and is deleted after 2 days stopped unless extended up to 7 days on Hobby; inactive agents, transcripts and attached files are retained 90 days. (7 days)
Interfacebrowser, cli, rest Agents are driven from the kosem app and CLI; the platform exposes LLM Gateway API endpoints and a documented REST API for quotas, while the microVM is reached through the agent environment rather than a published SSH endpoint.
endpoint: https://api.kosem.ai/v1
Toolingthe kosem app, the kosem CLI and the documented API; access connections for GitHub, AWS and Google Cloud; no SSH endpoint or rclone backend is published for the environment.
Composes withEach agent gets its own identity and short-lived, revocable credentials for GitHub, AWS and Google Cloud, and connects its own model accounts without a token markup, so a sandbox run can open a pull request or read cloud logs within granted scope.
ReliabilityProvider rate limits pass through to agents and their 429 responses are not smoothed by kosem. Stopped environments are deleted after 2 days unless extended to the plan cap, and environments whose billing account reaches $0 are deleted after a 14-day grace period.
NotesHosted coding-agent microVMs with $10 of starter credits for 60 days, Standard at $0.230 per hour and $0 per month on Hobby. The starter credit is one-off, not renewing, so the free allowance is a trial. The 4-concurrent-vCPU cap binds after the credit. Self-hosted runners run environments on the user's own hosts at $0 kosem compute, listed last; the hosted Hobby offer is the primary one here.
