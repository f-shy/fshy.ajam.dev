Three unlimited terms (monthly, quarterly, annual) plus metered 50/100/200 GB and non-expiring blocks. Blocks are listed twice on the same page at two price columns: 100 GB at $15 or $17, 500 GB at $47 or $49, 1000 GB at $87 or $89. A separate page offers a 100 GB block for $5 and a 50 GB metered plan for $3/mo.
Bindingnone The annual unlimited plan publishes no byte counter. At 1 TiB/month nothing in the vocabulary binds on that tier; the metered tier would bind at 50 GB by construction.
