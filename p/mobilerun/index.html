Free has no overage charges: when the allowance runs out, prepaid packs are required or the user upgrades. Paid plans continue past the allowance at USD 0.03 per phone credit, USD 0.01 per AI credit and USD 10 per GB of residential traffic, up to a monthly overage limit.
Bindingvolume-quota The 60 monthly phone credits run out after 60 Android minutes or 20 iPhone minutes, well before the 1 GB storage cap, and on Free there is no overage billing once they are spent.
Identityemail The service requires a DroidRun account: the terms state that registration and setting up a DroidRun Account is required. The console sign-in page is a JavaScript app whose configuration reports emailSignUpEnabled false, so the exact sign-up fields on the live page are unverified: behind a sign-up. The pricing FAQ states that eligible accounts can claim one free slot to connect their own phone with no credit card required; that is an add-on, not the Free cloud-phone plan.
Egressresidential traffic is pooled across the phones per month: 1 GB included on Free, then a USD 3 top-up of 100 phone credits does not add traffic; paid plans charge USD 10 per GB
free allowance: 1 GB residential traffic a month on Free

RetentionA stopped Android phone keeps its apps and data; free-plan storage is 1 GB.
Interfacerest, cli, browser A Cloud REST API at api.mobilerun.ai/v1, TypeScript and Python SDKs, a hosted MCP server, and a web console; a cloud device is created, driven by a task, then stopped.
Tooling@mobilerun/sdk (npm), mobilerun-sdk (pip), MCP server, cURL against the REST API, and Puppeteer or Playwright over CDP on a cloud Android phone.
Composes withAn agent provisions a hosted Android phone by the minute, drives it with an SDK or MCP, and reads SMS and email from add-on numbers and mailboxes; a dedicated always-on Android phone costs USD 50 a month, or USD 64 a month billed annually with a dedicated residential IP.
ReliabilityFree has no overage charges and stops at its allowance until a top-up or upgrade.
NotesHosted Android and iPhone phones by the minute: 60 free Android minutes or 20 free iPhone minutes a month on the USD 0 plan, with 1 concurrent phone and no overage charges. Paid plans start at USD 20 a month; an always-on hosted Android phone is USD 50 a month. The open-source agent framework is a separate self-hosted option and is not the offer priced here.
