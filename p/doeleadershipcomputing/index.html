Annual call only (mid-April to mid/late June), awards 1-3 years with a peer-reviewed annual renewal request. Post-project archival storage is explicitly at the facility's discretion ('The facilities reserve the right to limit the amount and duration of archival storage once a project has been completed'). Data must be research data.

NotesTwo review phases: an international peer-review panel on scientific merit, then a technical assessment of computational readiness by LCF staff. Requests under 20% of the average award are redirected to Director's Discretionary or ALCC. Early Career Track reserves 10% of allocatable time for PIs within 10 years of a PhD who have not previously been INCITE PI. Polaris is no longer available; Aurora and Frontier are the only INCITE resources for 2027. The 20% utilisation criterion is the real filter at this scale: a solo researcher must show the campaign consumes a fifth of an exascale machine. Node-hour to GPU-hour conversion depends on the system (Frontier nodes carry 4 MI300A APUs), so earlier readings should not convert without a per-system figure.
