EgressRate-limited to the point of unusability from a datacenter IP: 10/10 requests returned 429 on a cold burst, and a single request after a 5 s pause also returned 429. Published posture: 'Rate limiting has begun on ipfs.io and dweb.link for hotlinked and backend traffic, and will be increasing.' Docs: 'no SLA, no support, and shared rate limits that can throttle or block your traffic at any time, without notice.'
Interfacerest rest
Toolingcurl/any HTTP client; path gateway at ipfs.io, subdomain at dweb.link; trustless subset at trustless-gateway.link.

ReliabilityThis is the single most important measurement in the slot: the 'free IPFS CDN' that every tutorial assumes is (a) gone as of 2026-09-21 and (b) already 429-ing datacenter IPs before that date. Any architecture that depends on ipfs.io/dweb.link for serving is dead on arrival.

unit mixed; metered on stored-bytes
Pricenone stated

Bandwidth of the VPS; DHT lookup latency on cold CIDs; no excess-use protection by default ('Unlike ipfs.io, a self-hosted gateway has no abuse protection by default').
Bindingper-ip-shaping one stream is shaped, and aggregate throughput is the shaped rate times the number of streams
Identityemail (placeholder) not published
EgressHost bandwidth is paid directly to the server provider. IPFS documentation recommends fronting gateways with caching CDNs using standard Cache-Control, ETag, and X-Ipfs-Roots headers to minimize origin bandwidth consumption.
Interfacerest, webdav, rclone rest
ToolingKubo (go-ipfs), Rainbow (github.com/ipfs/rainbow), Someguy for delegated routing, Helia in-browser; rclone can talk to the Kubo RPC API via a webdav/MFS shim but there is no first-class rclone backend.
Composes withDoes not multiply free storage. It only re-serves content you already paid to pin. Combined with a free pinning service it can turn 5 GB of free pinning into unmetered-looking reads, but only for 5 GB of content.
ReliabilityThis is the honest answer to 'does self-hosting change the economics?': it changes who meters you, not whether you are metered. It just moves the bill to the VPS or CDN.
