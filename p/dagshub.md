- identity: email
- interface: s3-compatible
- limits note: 20 GB free is the binding constraint; private repos limited to 2 collaborators; >100 tracked experiments in private repos needs a paid tier; Team plan is where 1 TB / 2M files starts.
- interface detail: s3
- tooling: Genuinely good: boto3 / s3fs clients generated by the dagshub client, `dagshub upload --bucket owner/repo`, FUSE mount (`dagshub.storage.mount`) on Linux, plus plain `dvc push`/`dvc pull`. Endpoints supported: ListObjectsV2, GetObject, HeadObject, PutObject, CopyObject, DeleteObject + the full multipart set. So this is a real S3 API, not a shim.
- composes with: 20 GB per account x unlimited free GitHub-linked accounts = linear, trivially repeatable. Also per-REPO buckets: if the 20 GB is enforced per repo rather than per account (not stated on the pricing page), it becomes 20 GB x unlimited repos on ONE account. Worth probing with a throwaway account before committing to it.
- reliability: Small company (not hyperscaler-backed); storage is per-repo and tied to repo lifecycle. Deleting a repo deletes its bucket. No reported data-loss pattern, but no SLA either.
- notes: the same as the free tier
