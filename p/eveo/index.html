EgressZERO, stated as the product's entire pitch: «Zero Egress e API … a saída é gratuita, operações PUT, GET, LIST não pesam na fatura» (egress is free; PUT/GET/LIST operations do not weigh on the invoice). Also «paga apenas pelo GB armazenado … e acessa o que é seu sem taxas de pedágio». No retrieval fee. WORM immutability native.
Interfaces3-compatible, rclone s3
ToolingS3-compatible with SigV4 presigned URLs. So it can sit behind Cloudflare the same way as any S3 store. rclone s3, restic, Veeam. Two-region replication inside Brazil, Tier III datacentres.
Composes withPure per-GB, so 5 TB and 20 TB cost the same per TB. No bucket cap below 5 PB.
ReliabilityEnterprise-oriented Brazilian provider with Tier III DCs and two-region replication; no independent price/performance benchmark located. The zero-egress claim is on the vendor's own page and uncontradicted, but there is no published price list PDF to cross-check the R$0.05 number against, so confidence on the exact rate is medium even though confidence on the egress policy is high.
