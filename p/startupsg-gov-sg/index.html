Free tierCompetitive grant for deep-tech commercialisation with a POC cap of SGD 400,000 and a POV cap of SGD 800,000, both raised on 2 January 2025 from SGD 250,000 and SGD 500,000. The grant carries an equity component: Enterprise Singapore holds the right to subscribe to shares, and paid-up capital of 10 percent of the POC grant or 20 percent of the POV grant must be shown before award.

Composes withThe 30 percent local-ownership rule is what makes this hard to compose: a foreign founder must bring a Singaporean or PR shareholder holding a third of the company before the SGD 800,000 becomes visible, which is a different cost from the capital contribution the grant asks for.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesThree gates were considered. (a) Singapore incorporation: confirmed, but it is the cheapest of the three and a foreigner can incorporate a Pte Ltd without a resident director only by appointing a nominee. (b) Local shareholding of 30 percent: confirmed, and this is the binding one, because it is an ownership test rather than a registration step. (c) A paid-up capital requirement: confirmed at 10 or 20 percent of the grant, which for the POV cap is SGD 160,000 that must exist before award, so the grant is really a matching instrument. The 'artificial intelligence (e.g. deep learning)' line under Information & Communications Technologies is the only sub-area that names AI directly, so a project must be framed inside one of the five listed areas to qualify at all.
