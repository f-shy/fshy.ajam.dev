The page sells host coverage by count (23, 31 or 35 hosts) and never by volume. The only volume figure in the bundle ecosystem is the host's: BRFiles states 2 TB/month on its own premium page.
Bindingvolume-quota At 1 TiB/month the first thing to fail is the per-host transfer allowance of whichever host the files sit on, because the reseller adds no traffic of its own.
Identityemail A store account is needed to order; delivery is by email or panel after payment. The payment methods offered were not read, so whether a card is mandatory is unverified.
EgressNo egress number is published by the reseller. The buyer gets premium access at each named host and is bound by that host's own transfer allowance. The bundles are priced per period, not per byte.
Per-object capnot applicable: the seller publishes no object cap. Each bundled account carries the host's own per-file cap.
Retentionnot applicable: accounts are sold for a fixed number of days; no storage is sold by the reseller.
Interfaceunknown A WooCommerce-style storefront; delivery of the account or voucher is arranged after payment. No API and no endpoint are named on the page.
ToolingThe delivered credentials are used at each host's own site or its download manager integration. The reseller ships nothing executable.
Composes withA single R$ 39.99 bundle covers 23 hosts including 1fichier, Mega, MediaFire and BRFiles for 31 days, which is the cheapest way to hold all four at once without a multi-host; but each account is separate, so a script still has to know which host a link belongs to.
ReliabilityThe buyer receives an account somebody else registered, so the original owner can change the password or the host can bind the session to one IP; the reseller's own warranty is the only recourse and its terms are not on this page.
NotesThe commercial model is a bundle of separately registered host accounts, resold. That is a different object from a multi-host: coverage is high but each host has its own login, its own quota and its own simultaneous-session rule. The seller has run since at least the date its generator page claims ('ha mais de 10 anos'), which is longer than most services among debrid providers.
Evidence notedocumented Every price and host list here was read from the vendor's purchase page. Whether the bundled accounts are exclusive to the buyer or shared between buyers is not stated anywhere on the page, which is why the verdict keeps a caveat rather than a qualification.
