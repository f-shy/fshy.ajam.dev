The failed-unlock rate is the real ceiling: 287 of 369 attempts in the last 7 days were errors. A failed attempt costs the ad pages and the wait, which the site reports as up to 20 minutes on a slow host.
Bindingrequest-rate No byte quota is published, so the constraint that actually stops 1 TiB of monthly transfer is throughput of successful unlocks: at the self-reported 21.1% seven-day success rate, a user needs roughly five attempts and five ad-gate traversals per file, and the queue waits dominate over any bandwidth figure.
Identityanonymous No account, no email and no card on the free path. The site says 'No account, no payment, no software install'.
