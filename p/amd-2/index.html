Free tierAMD runs two sites. On the China site the AMD AI Developer Program is free to join and new members start with 100 points, which is 100 GPU-hours at 1 point per hour; further points come from courses and activities, and an account may redeem at most 20 points a day. Token Factory, an OpenAI-compatible inference endpoint listing 16 public free models, is free, always on, and spends no credits.

Composes withA recurring 20 GPU-hours a day on a ROCm container with SSH, a CLI and a persistent volume is the first free allowance found among evaluated offerings that is not a one-time credit or a notebook tab, and it composes with Token Factory: inference costs nothing, so the 20 hours can be spent entirely on training.

NotesThe China site signs in with a mobile number or email plus a slider captcha, or with a ModelScope, CSDN or GitHub account, so the identity is a phone number and not a national ID; whether the AMD China account itself demands a real-name check after signup is unverified. The Global site at radeon-global.anruicloud.com signs in with email or GitHub, needs no China number, and its credit route is unstated in the documentation. Open question: does the Global site grant the same points, or none? The docs say the Global site does not use the developer program, which suggests it has no free credit at all - that asymmetry is the thing to settle.
