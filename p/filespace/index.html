The storage figure is a stored-byte ceiling, not a transfer allowance; the transfer side is only shaped (100 KB/s), never metered.
Bindingper-ip-shaping 100 KB/s is about 8.6 GB a day and about 260 GB a month on a link that never idles, so 1 TiB of monthly transfer does not fit the free tier at all. On premium the throttle is removed and the 500 GB storage ceiling becomes the next wall.
Identitycard Payments listed as 'Bank Cards | PayPaI | Bitcoin | Ethereum'. The site also shows a WebMoney passport identifier (411266485536), which is a payment-processor identity rather than a signup requirement.
EgressPremium: 'Download speed: Unlimited', 'Simultaneous downloads: Yes', 'Direct downloads: Yes'. Regular: 100 KB/s, one at a time, one hour between downloads. No byte allowance is published on either column.

RetentionRegular: '60 days after last download'. Premium: 'Unlimited'. (60 days)
Interfaceunknown Web upload, remote URL upload and FTP upload on premium. The site is an XFileSharing-style host, which normally exposes an API module, but no endpoint was found or read here.
ToolingFTP upload on premium is the only non-browser path stated. No rclone backend. The host is on both the PrimeLeech and Leechall generator lists, so a leecher can pull from it without a premium account.
Composes withA 500 GB premium host at $15.95 a month, priced per 30 days rather than per byte, with 330 days at $117.95 the cheapest per day. Useful as a redirect target for a release the debrids do not cache.
ReliabilityThe 1-hour wait and the 100 KB/s cap are on the account, not the file, so a batch of small files on the free tier takes a day in queue time alone.
NotesCheapest per day is the 330-day plan at USD 0.357/day against USD 0.532/day for the 30-day. Nothing here prices transfer, so a client storing 500 GB and pulling it monthly is paying for storage only.
