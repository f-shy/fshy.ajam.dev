Free tierLocal VMs are free forever. Cloud VMs bill per second, only while they run, with no plans and no seats. Local sandboxes are always free. In the cloud you pay only while your sandboxes run.
unit none; metered on wall-clock; The only free capacity is local, on the user's own machine, and needs no account. The hosted cloud lane has no published free allowance: a card tops up €20 of credit when the balance is empty, with automatic €20 top-ups; the cloud lane is a private beta requiring a signup request. Rates are €0.0438 per vCPU-hour, €0.0141 per GB-hour of memory and €0.000087 per GB-hour of disk.

A sandbox can have up to 16 vCPU, 32 GB of memory and 200 GB of disk on the calculator; the resources page permits up to 256 vCPU and 1 TiB of memory on a hosted sandbox. Disks only grow. Without a card, or if a top-up fails, new sandboxes and starts are refused at once, and running sandboxes stop after 24 hours. Human developer seats are USD 18 per developer per month; agents are not seats.
Bindingvolume-quota The hosted lane has no free credit, so a balance topped up by card binds first, ahead of the 24-hour stop without a card and the organization's sandbox limit.
Identityemail The signup form asks for Name, Email and Password (at least 12 characters with uppercase, lowercase, a number and a symbol), then an optional invite code and 'What would you like to build?' for review; the cloud lane is a private beta accessed by request. Local VMs need no account. Billing needs a card to top up €20 of credit.
Egressnot published: the pricing page states no egress allowance or price; outbound traffic passes through the credential proxy under per-sandbox network policies
Per-object capnot applicable: the billed unit is vCPU, memory and disk while a sandbox runs, not an object; a sandbox can have up to 16 vCPU, 32 GB memory and 200 GB disk
RetentionStorage is kept when a sandbox stops; running sandboxes stop after 24 hours without a card or a failed top-up, and their storage is kept. Credits do not expire.
Interfacecli, ssh, rest One CLI and SDK (TypeScript, Python, Go) drive local and cloud sandboxes with the same commands; cw info NAME shows a hosted sandbox's policy and enforcement status, preview URLs expose services, and SSH is available from any device.
endpoint: https://codewire.sh
Toolingthe open-source Codewire CLI (curl -fsSL https://get.codewire.sh | sh), SDKs for TypeScript, Python and Go, and integrations for Rex, Orca, Claude, Codex, Cursor, Kimi, Pi and Gemini CLI.
Composes withThe hosted cloud lane is the offer for readers without their own machine: sandboxes run on private bare-metal machines in Amsterdam and Frankfurt, with a credential proxy and an encrypted agent-to-agent mesh. Running Codewire locally or on a Kubernetes cluster is the self-hosted last resort and is not the answer while the hosted lane is available.
ReliabilityHosted cloud is in private beta with an access-request form; without a card or after a failed top-up new sandboxes are refused and running ones stop after 24 hours; no published SLA.
NotesCodewire sells hosted cloud sandboxes billed per second in a private beta, with no hosted free tier; only local VMs on the user's own machine are free. The hosted lane is the offer recorded here; the open-source CLI and Kubernetes control plane are a self-hosted last resort, not the answer while the hosted lane exists.
