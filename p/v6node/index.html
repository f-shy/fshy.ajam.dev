NotesThe architecture is the most useful of the IPv6-only hosts, and the price is the least documented. DNS64/NAT64 is the answer to the caveat that governs every other row here: a guest on this host can reach IPv4-only destinations outbound without buying an IPv4 address, which is exactly what a health monitor polling IPv4 endpoints needs. What could not be established is what EUR 1 actually buys: the marketing site says 'starting at €1 per month', the deploy page starts at EUR 4.49, and the only shape figure is a tracker's Node 2GB at EUR 3.00. The control panel is login-walled, so the entry rung's RAM, disk and traffic are unverified. The entity is a sole trader with a German VAT ID, which is a weaker counterparty than a GmbH but is a real registered taxpayer.
Evidence notereported the EUR 1 entry price, the IPv6-only statement, the DNS64/NAT64 statement, the /64 and the payment rails are quoted from first-party pages today, but the entry rung's shape and traffic allowance are not published on any page reached, and the control panel is login-walled.
