Junior EUR 2.46/mo (40 Mbit, 25 connections), Medior EUR 3.69/mo (80 Mbit, 25 connections), Senior EUR 5.75/mo (unlimited speed, 50 connections). No automatic renewal: 'Your subscription expires when your subscription days have passed.'
Bindingvolume-quota The free path is a 30 GB one-off, so at 1 TiB/month the allowance is what fails, and it fails after 30 GB. On Senior the vendor publishes no volume ceiling.
Identityemail An email address, and the trial has three explicit gates: 'You can't register with a disposable e-mail address', 'You can't use a VPN or proxy server to register' and 'You can't have multiple accounts'. The VPN ban means the signup must come from a real residential address.

Retention4085 days retention on the home page, 4084 days on the try-out page (4084 days)
Interfacenntp NNTP over TLS on port 563. No HTTP or object API exists for the download path, which is driven through SABnzbd, NZBGet or nzbdav.
Composes withA 30 GB Abavia allowance tests whether an Abavia spool completes the requested content before any money is spent; after that the Junior tier keeps it for EUR 2.46/mo.
ReliabilityThe try-out page also carries the line 'Payment will show up on your statement as NectoFarm B.V.', which is odd for a product listed at 'Total cost: FREE' and suggests a payment method may still be captured. That was not tested at checkout, so it is flagged rather than resolved. Abavia reseller operated by NectoFarm BV. The 30 GB try-out is the largest card-free allowance found on any provider among debrid providers, and the VPN ban is the price of it.
NotesThe caveat is the identity gate rather than the product: no disposable email, no VPN or proxy at signup, one account. A client behind a VPN cannot claim this trial at all.
