Composes withFor a client in Iran this is the substitute for a card: the firm takes Toman and settles the foreign charge, so a debrid subscription priced in euro becomes payable without a foreign card and without a reseller's markup on the account itself.
ReliabilityThe subscription depends on the intermediary's own foreign accounts staying open; when an intermediary loses its settlement path, every subscription it paid for lapses at once.
