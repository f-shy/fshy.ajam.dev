NotesRegistry lookups of the published test addresses, one address per location, ARIN RDAP and RIPEstat on 2026-11-03: Seattle 209.200.234.1 is registered to a 'Private Customer' under a parent held by Internet Utilities NA LLC and announced by AS21743 Atlas Networks Corporation; New York City 67.101.108.2 is an RCN assignment announced by AS6079; Charlotte 70.63.196.2 is registered to ATS INC (announcing ASN not looked up). The Ashburn and Los Angeles addresses were not looked up. The vendor's ISP label disagrees with the registrant for the New York City address (label Optimum WIFI, RCN assignment), the Seattle registrant is a private customer and not an ISP, and the Charlotte registrant ATS INC was not tied to Spectrum by any lookup made. The record is a caveat, not a clean pass, because the class of the address a buyer receives is the vendor's choice within the stated ISP list.
