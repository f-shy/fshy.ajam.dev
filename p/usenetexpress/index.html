Monthly $10, 6 months $50, yearly $90 (USD 7.50/mo equivalent), block 500 GB $20. All unlimited plans include free headers, free posting, header compression and a VPN account.
Bindingnone Unlimited volume and speed; the vendor publishes no fair-use figure, so nothing in the vocabulary binds at 1 TiB/month.
Identitycard Email plus a paid plan. No trial exists. Payment methods listed by the vendor: PayPal, UK/EU/USA debit and Bitcoin; accounts are provisioned after payment.
EgressUnlimited, with 50 connections included and an upgrade path to 150
free allowance: unlimited
Per-object capno per-object cap is published; no per-object cap is published; the unit of retrieval is a Usenet article, approximately 750 KB by convention, so 1 TiB of monthly transfer corresponds to roughly 1.4 million article…
RetentionThe vendor homepage states 'Many Articles Over 4000 Days Old' and 'Exceptional Binary and Text Retention'; no binary retention depth in days is stated. The r/usenet map records a 3,000-day backfill; that third-party figure is the recorded number. (3000 days)
Interfacenntp NNTP over TLS on port 563. No HTTP or object API exists for the download path, which is driven through SABnzbd, NZBGet or nzbdav.
Composes withDirect access buys the 150-connection upgrade no reseller sells, and the 500 GB block stacks as a fill under any other primary.
ReliabilityOwn Tier-1 backbone with US and NL farms, AS62296 in the US and AS50673 in Amsterdam. Sells to resellers (NewsDemon, NewsgroupDirect, CubeNet, ThunderNews, UsenetPrime, UsenetNews) and directly.
NotesSecond independent backbone after Omicron by retention depth, and the only one whose resellers publish blocks under $50/TB.
Evidence notereported Prices and connections are from the vendor's plans page read. Retention is not stated by the vendor anywhere read, so the r/usenet backfill figure is used and the record drops to the reported rung because retention decides the retrieval verdict.
