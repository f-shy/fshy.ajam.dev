Free tierTwo free routes rather than a free tier. The first is a 30-day money-back trial: an individual registers, fills the profile, pays for the chosen tariff, and may claim a full refund inside 30 days, once per account, on any tariff except the promo ones; from the second month the refund covers only the unspent remainder. The second is 60 days free, described as two months at no charge, when moving from another hosting company. Legal entities instead send a taxpayer-number scan and a letter on company letterhead to obtain a 30-day test. The cheapest paid shape is the USA or Netherlands SSD 20 plan at RUB 336 a month list, RUB 285.60 a month on the annual term (RUB 4,032 list for 12 months, RUB 3,427.20 discounted), for one core at 2.8 GHz, 2 GB RAM, 20 GB disk and a 100 Mbit/s port. Foreign bank cards are accepted through the Robokassa gateway with a minimum payment of RUB 1,000, and the provider names the countries whose cards it will not take.
