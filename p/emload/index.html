The daily bandwidth is identical across 1-month, 3-month, 6-month and 1-year plans; only the price per day changes. Nothing on the page suggests the allowance scales with price.
Bindingvolume-quota 35 GB/day is the hard wall: 1 TiB of monthly transfer needs 34.1 GB/day sustained, which leaves 0.9 GB/day of headroom and no room for a retry. The 1 TB storage figure is the same number as the monthly transfer, so both walls arrive together.
Identitycard Account creation is free; premium is bought at checkout. The page does not state which card processors are used, and the signup form fields were not read.
EgressEach plan carries the same '35GB/Daily Bandwidth', which is a download allowance rather than a throttle. 35 GB/day is about 1.05 TB per 30 days.

Composes withOne of the Korean-market hosts ('Webhard') that hold releases which never appear on the Western hosts. 35 GB/day covers 1 TiB of monthly transfer only if the month is exactly 30 days of full use, so it is a top-up rather than a bulk path.
ReliabilityA bandwidth counter that resets daily means an unused day is lost, and a plan that runs at 35 GB/day exactly has no margin for a re-download.
NotesKshared publishes the identical plan at the identical price ('1 Month €17.95, 1 TB Storage, 35GB/Daily Bandwidth', 'Choose Your CloudFLA Plan'), which is one operator or one white-label script. The annual plan is €107.95, or €8.99 a month.
