Transfer in: the transfer price list has no entry for .de, .eu or .uk, while the registration and renewal lists carry all three. For the extensions in the transfer list, one year is added to the expiry at no extra charge on completion; the FAQ names two exceptions, a renewal at the previous registrar within 45 days and a registry maximum (typically 10 years) that the extra year would exceed. A .uk transfer needs no authorization code: the order is followed by an IPS tag change to NAMESILO at the previous registrar. What a cart charges for a .de, .eu or .uk transfer, and whether it adds a year, is unverified: behind a sign-up.
Bindingwrite-gated the renewal price decides what a name costs to keep; the first-year promotion and the Account Funds requirement are payment gates, not quotas
Identityemail sign-up asks an email address and a password behind a reCAPTCHA, or a Google or Facebook sign-in; each domain needs a contact profile with a name, an email address and a phone number, and the registrant email is verified by a link or a code within 15 days of the first notice, which is sent when a registration or transfer completes. No phone verification step is stated on the contact, email-verification, checkout, account-funding or API pages; the API lists the phone number as a required contact field with length and format rules. The checkout page lists the billing contact's name and address, an optional tax ID and the payment method. Payment by card, PayPal, Alipay, Venmo, Bitcoin or Account Funds, so no card is required. The crypto guide on NameSilo's blog documents the Bitcoin path as: log in, open the Account Funds Manager, add funds with Bitcoin or through the Nicky processor, wait for confirmation, then register from the balance at checkout; it lists no phone check or identity-document step and calls crypto purchases more private than card payments. The account-funding page lists Bitcoin without the 'verified' qualifier that the card, PayPal and Skrill sources carry, a card source is verified through the billing manager, and API orders are paid from a verified card or Account Funds. NameSilo's privacy page says proof of identification may be requested, its blog says new accounts making large purchases or registering from high-risk locations may meet payment holds or additional verification requirements, and its account-funding page says a balance can be withheld when the funding source is judged not legitimate; whether a Bitcoin or Account Funds order passes without a phone check or an identity-document review is unverified: behind a sign-up.
