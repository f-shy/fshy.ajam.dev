Free tierRTX4090 24G with 14 vCPU and 32G RAM at CNY 1.92 per hour on demand, CNY 42.33 per day, CNY 1154.64 per month, or CNY 1.31 per hour preemptible, quoted from the price list. The same price list gives the A100 80G (1 GPU, 16 vCPU, 64 GiB RAM) at CNY 10.75 per hour on demand, CNY 236.58 per day, CNY 6452.40 per month or CNY 7.52 per hour preemptible; it has no H100, L40S or L4 table. The same provider sells a no-GPU mode at CNY 0.15 per hour for development on a retained instance, and individual real-name certification pays a one-off CNY 5 credit.

Bindingnone Billing is per second and shutdown stops the meter, so nothing throttles a running job. The first thing that fails is the cheap lane: the CNY 1.31 preemptible rate is reclaimable, and the CNY 1154.64 monthly rate beats on-demand only above about 601 hours a month.
