Free tierA withdrawn offer that was the largest free compute package in the Russian market: twelve months of two virtual machines at 2 vCPU and 4 GiB each, one managed Postgres or MariaDB at 2 vCPU and 2 GiB, network disks up to 40 GB, 100 GB of object storage, one dedicated white IPv4 and a load balancer, for a balance top-up of RUB 5,000 by bank card. The rules page now carries a banner that the campaign is finished and that joining was open only until 8 March 2026 inclusive, with announced terms kept for those who joined earlier. The replacement for new clients is a 30-day trial credit.

Bindingnone Withdrawn: the rules page states the campaign ended and that joining closed on 8 March 2026, so nothing binds now except the absence of the offer; for anyone who joined, a RUB 5,000 top-up was the gate and the package ran 12 months from the top-up date.
Identitycountry-phone an email address, a phone number and a bank-card top-up of at least RUB 5,000: rule 3.1 requires 'registration by email plus a top-up of at least RUB 5,000 by bank card', and the operator's launch article says registration needs an email and a phone number, with the phone requirement attributed to the Ministry of Digital Development. The card is charged rather than authorised, so no hold amount arises; whether a debit or prepaid card is accepted is not stated. The offer closed on 8 March 2026.

NotesClassified as dead rather than dropped, because the landing page still advertises the package and the deadline is visible only on the rules page. Two contradictions a reader should know: the launch article says the activation money is not refundable while rule 3.3 provides a refund minus consumption, and the article says the managed database is 2 vCPU / 4 GiB while the rules and the landing page say 2 vCPU / 2 GiB. The phone gate is described by the provider as a Russian ministry requirement, which is a constraint on who may register rather than on what is provided.
