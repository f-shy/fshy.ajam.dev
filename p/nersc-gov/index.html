Free tier"Research projects that are new to NERSC and/or HPC may apply for small Exploratory awards, up to 250 node hours". Exploratory requests are accepted at any time, decided within four weeks of applying, and last 18 months. A Perlmutter CPU node is 2 AMD EPYC 7763 processors totalling 128 cores and 512 GB of memory, so 250 node-hours is 32,000 core-hours. The NERSC Director's Reserve, the other discretionary pool, is explicitly "not generally open to the public" and needs "prior arrangements with NERSC".

NotesThe finding worth carrying is the refutation: NERSC's Director's Reserve is widely described as an open door and the vendor page says the opposite in one sentence, while the Exploratory award is the real small route and needs an agreement rather than a review panel.
