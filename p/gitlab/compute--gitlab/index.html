NotesThe GPU figure rests on one reading the pages do not settle. Usage is job duration / 60 x cost factor, runner type and project type sit in separate tables, and the project table gives public projects in the GitLab for Open Source program a cost factor of 0.5 with no runner qualifier. Read literally, a GPU job also costs 0.5, so 50,000 compute minutes are 100,000 job-minutes, or 1,667 GPU-hours a month. If the programme factor and the GPU runner factor of 7 multiply, 50,000 / 3.5 is 14,285 job-minutes, or 238 GPU-hours. The two readings differ by a factor of seven, and only a job run in an accepted namespace settles it. GPU runners are offered on Premium and Ultimate only, and the grant is Ultimate. The default runner is 2 vCPU and 8 GB, a job on GitLab.com hosted runners times out after 3 hours, and the grant is a monthly pool rather than unlimited time.
