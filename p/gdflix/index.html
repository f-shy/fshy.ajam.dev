No site-side counter is published. The only meaningful throttle is the one Drive applies, documented by the twin service's changelog as '403: User Rate Limit Exceeded'.
Bindingper-ip-shaping At 1 TiB a month the bytes come from Google and Google shapes them; GDFlix adds nothing that could bind first.
Identityanonymous Downloaders need no account. A publisher needs a Google account to grant the app Drive access; the sign-in is the same OAuth flow as DriveSeed.
EgressOne-click download from the site, no wait. No price, allowance or meter printed.
Per-object capNot stated.
RetentionNot stated; links resolve to a Google Drive id whose life decides theirs.
Interfaceunknown Web only. Google OAuth sign-in, TeamDrive and shared-drive browsing, short-link creation. No first-party API was found; third-party bypass scripts exist for the family.
ToolingBypass scripts (for example gdflix.py in Bypass-Scripts, keyed to new.gdflix.ink) are the practical client. No SDK, no rclone backend.
Composes withInterchangeable with DriveSeed at the bottom of the same chain: the index site can post either, and both end at a Google Drive object. Running two of them gives the operator two hosts for one Drive, which is the resilience the chain relies on.
ReliabilityAn instance of a copied template: three hosts were tried on 2026-10-23 and only gdflix.net answered a site (gdflix.pro was refused by network requests, and the live page itself redirects to new7.gdflix.io). Instances vanish and renumber; a link outlives the hostname that served it.
NotesGoogle Drive wrapper service operating under the GDFlix brand, resolving links to hosted files.
