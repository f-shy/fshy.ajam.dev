NotesTargon is the counter-example to the wallet assumption for Bittensor subnets. Its account creation is a plain email form, its docs never ask for a TAO balance, and the wallet appears only on the provider side where a miner hotkey is used. The GPU business is therefore reachable in principle by any client with money, and not at all by this one. A LinkedIn post from a Targon representative offers free credits on request, which is an interpersonal door and not a documented program.
