NotesThe best RAM-per-dollar row in this category: 512 MB plus 100 MB of swap for CNY 1.99 a month, which is USD 0.2983 at the ECB reference rate of 6.6702 CNY per USD read, and it is in stock at 5 units. The 512 MB matters because it is twice what CrockyHost's entry plan gives for a third of the price, and it is the only sub-USD-0.30 row found that is not 256 MB. Three caveats. The setup fee of CNY 1.00 is charged once on top, so the first month is CNY 2.99 and every month after is CNY 1.99. The port mapping is computed rather than chosen: SSH is 10000 plus the last octet of the private IP and the free range is ten consecutive ports, so a well-known port cannot be selected, and the vendor's own header says the shared IPv4 is 'blocked by default'. And the vendor prints '该机器不对任何运营数据做保证', a blanket disclaimer of any operating guarantee, on the plan itself. No legal entity is named anywhere read, which is the same gap as the other two Chinese sellers among documented hosts. What is not established: the payment rails, because the cart needs a login; whether the ten ports carry UDP, which matters because a WireGuard endpoint needs one and nothing read says so; and whether the plan survives renewal at the same price. The vendor runs an Arabic, Persian, Turkish, Romanian and Ukrainian interface alongside Chinese, which suggests a deliberate non-Chinese customer base, but that is an inference from a language menu and not evidence about identity requirements.
