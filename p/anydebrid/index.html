Bindingrequest-rate Eight links a day is the first wall: 1 TiB of monthly transfer spread over 30 days needs roughly 35 GB per link at 8 links a day, and any link that fails or is retried spends one of the eight.

Composes withWith no account it is the only option among debrid providers that leaves no identity trail, so it works as a one-off fetch for a file otherwise unreachable - not as infrastructure.
