Composes withfree compute whose output has to leave immediately: artifacts are 500 MB, GHCR is 500 MB, and the cache evicts at 7 days. R2's 10 GB free tier is the join that makes the chain storable
Reliabilitythe repository, its workflows and its logs are public, which is a technical property of the free tier and not a policy question
NotesUnlimited minutes is true and it is not the number that decides anything. The capacity is the 20-slot concurrency: 20 slots x 24 h is 480 runner-hours a day, which is the flow ceiling, and the 120 runner-hours the slots hold at one instant is a stock and not a rate. Minutes are not scarce; a single job that runs longer than six hours is impossible. A 256-job matrix at the six-hour ceiling is 1,536 runner-hours, or thirteen waves of 20 and 78 hours of wall clock.
