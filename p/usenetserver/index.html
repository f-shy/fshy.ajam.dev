Composes withThe clearest documented split between the two article codes: 423 means the group is there and the article is not, 430 means the article is unavailable while the group remains readable, which is what a script can branch on.
ReliabilityAn indexer can point at articles the spool no longer holds; each such article is a per-file failure and only PAR2 or a second provider recovers it.
NotesPotential failure points: retention, indexer staleness, or a connection ceiling. The vendor gives a number for none of them; the useful part of this page is the 423/430 split, which lets a user distinguish a missing group from a missing article. A vendor KB article says article access is unrestricted and uncapped; that is the only statement about volume on the site.
Evidence notedocumented The 423/430 distinction and the indexer behaviour are the vendor's own. No removal-by-notice cause is named on this page, which differs from Newshosting's page in the same group.
