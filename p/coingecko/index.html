Free tierDemo plan USD 0/month: 10,000 call credits per month at 100 calls per minute, 1 API key, daily and hourly coin history for the past 1 year; a keyless public API also answers at about 5 calls per minute per IP
unit requests; metered on request-count; 1 call = 1 credit on every endpoint, and requests are counted from the first day of the calendar month to the last. Every request counts toward the per-minute limit, 4xx and 5xx answers included. The keyless public API (api.coingecko.com/api/v3 with no key, and api.geckoterminal.com/api/v2 for onchain DEX data) shares an IP-based pool of about 5 calls per minute that varies with server load; a keyless request past 365 days of history answered HTTP 401 with error_code 10012. The cheapest paid plan is Basic: paid plans start at USD 35/month with 100,000 calls a month at 300 calls per minute, and a second pricing table shows Basic at USD 29/mo billed yearly.

  • request-rate (binding) 10,000 calls per calendar month on the Demo plan source
  • request-rate (binding) 100 calls per minute on the Demo plan source
  • per-ip-shaping about 5 calls per minute per IP on the keyless public API, shared by every caller on that IP source
History window: daily and hourly coin data reach back 1 year on Demo and keyless (a 400-day keyless request answered error_code 10012); no limits kind names a history window. Bulk path: no bulk path; the per-coin CSV/XLS export on the website covers 1 year.
Bindingrequest-rate 100 calls a minute would spend the month's 10,000 credits in 100 minutes, so the monthly count binds first.
Identityemail A Demo key needs a CoinGecko account: Google, Apple, or an email with a password of at least 8 characters (one uppercase, one lowercase, one number, one special character), with an email confirmation step; the pricing page says no credit card is required. The keyless public API needs no account and no key.
Egressnot metered in bytes; the 10,000 calls a month bound what a Demo key can pull
Per-object cap1 year of daily and hourly history per coin on Demo and on the keyless API; 2 years on Basic; daily history from 2013 and hourly history from 2018 on Analyst and above
Interfacerest GET https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3/... with the Demo key in x_cg_demo_api_key (header or query); paid keys use pro-api.coingecko.com and x_cg_pro_api_key. The same root answers with no key at the keyless rate. WebSocket is on paid plans only.
endpoint: https://api.coingecko.com/api/v3
Toolingcurl or any HTTP client; the website's historical-data page exports up to 1 year per coin as CSV or XLS
Composes withCrypto prices and market caps for a year back at 10,000 calls a month; it pairs with a stock or economic source such as Tiingo or FRED when a model mixes asset classes.
ReliabilityA call past the per-minute limit answers HTTP 429; a keyless 429 carries a Retry-After of 60 seconds and a JSON body with status.error_code 429 and the message "You've exceeded the Rate Limit."; keyless limits are shared per IP and vary with server load, so a shared egress address meets 429 sooner.
NotesTwo free routes: the keyless public API (no account, about 5 calls a minute per IP) and the Demo key (email, 10,000 calls a month at 100 a minute). Both stop at 1 year of history; full history from 2013 starts at the Analyst plan.
Evidence notedocumented The Demo quota, rate and history depth are documented on the pricing page and the rate-limit page. Two keyless probes measured the public API: a price answered HTTP 200 and a 400-day history answered 401 with the 365-day limit. A keyless burst measured the 429 answer of the public API. The Demo key's own 429 body and Retry-After, and the answer at the monthly credit cut-off, need a Demo key to measure (unverified: behind a sign-up); the knowledge base says handling at the monthly limit depends on billing settings and describes only the overage-enabled case.
