Free tier100 GB total storage on the free Community plan, but with a 3 GB limit per dataset or project. And their own docs are stricter still: 'Dataset ingested to data.world: 1 GB'; 'Size of a file in a dataset: 1 GB'; 'Number of files in a dataset: 250'; 'Project: 1 GB'. The 100 GB aggregate is therefore unreachable in practice because each unit can only be 1 GB.
unit GB; metered on stored-bytes
PriceCommunity free (100 GB aggregate, 1-3 GB per unit). Professional Community plan $12/month for 20 private projects — ~$120/TB/month against a 100 GB pool, though the per-unit 1 GB cap makes the pool theoretical.

EgressFree but rate-shaped: 'Number of burst streams API calls: 5 in the first second … then 1 per second'; streamed records capped at 1 MiB; streamed request 1 GB.

ToolingA real developer API (developer.data.world) plus SQL over datasets, so it is programmatically clean. The API surface is the best part of the product and the quota is the worst.
Composes with20 private projects/datasets × 3 GB, or the 100 GB aggregate if per-unit limits were the only constraint. In practice 1 GB per dataset × 100 units is the ceiling, so sharding to 500 GB means 500 units. Three times the published unit count.
ReliabilityOwnership change in progress (ServiceNow). The docs' 1 GB ingest limit contradicts the marketing 3 GB figure. Believe the docs.
