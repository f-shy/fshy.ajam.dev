unit requests; metered on request-count; The daily limit resets every day and unused sends do not carry over. Each attachment counts as one more email against the daily limit, so a message with one attachment uses 2 of the 100. The Free plan keeps 3 days of logs and allows single address verification only. Email API and Email Marketing are separate accounts. The cheapest paid Email API plan is USD 19 per month for 50,000 emails, and paid plans have no daily limit.

counts of things, not rates, so no vocabulary kind names them: 1,000 contacts on the Free plan. SMTP accepts 20 concurrent requests from a single IP.
Bindingrequest-rate 100 per day rather than 3,000 per month: the monthly figure is the daily cap summed over 30 days.
Identityemail The Email API signup form asks for an email, a password, a first and last name and a field labelled Address, behind a reCAPTCHA, and offers Google sign-in; it has no card field, and the email is confirmed. A profile survey then asks for accurate information about the primary person and organization behind the account, and the account must be verified before it can send; whether that verification asks for a phone number is unverified: behind a sign-up. A domain the sender owns must be verified from the start (SPF mandatory, DKIM encouraged, a tracking CNAME and DMARC recommended); domains of free providers such as gmail.com cannot be verified, and on the Free plan an added domain cannot be removed from the account.
