Bindingvolume-quota At 100 GB in a month ingest stops and the platform locks until the first day of the next month, so the monthly ingest is a hard ceiling rather than a billing threshold.
Identityemail The sign-up form asks for a company e-mail and a name, or sign-in with Google, GitHub, GitLab or Bitbucket, behind reCAPTCHA; no credit card is required. New Relic's documentation lists Google and Google Workplace as separate social sign-up options, and its Learn site lists the free-account steps as create the account, verify the e-mail address, then create a password. A July 2020 post in New Relic's organization on DEV Community lists only a name and an e-mail address as the sign-up input, and an October 2022 walkthrough on Medium reports that the form accepted a Gmail address. Whether the Company Email field accepts an address at a public mail domain now, and whether a later step asks for a phone number, are unverified: behind a sign-up.

RetentionDefault data retention of at least 8 days. Default namespace retention for the original data option: APM and browser 8 days, logs and custom events 30 days, infrastructure integrations 395 days (13 months). (8 days)
Interfacegraphql NerdGraph, a GraphQL API at https://api.newrelic.com/graphql (US), https://api.eu.newrelic.com/graphql (EU) or https://api.jp.newrelic.com/graphql (JP), with a user key in the API-Key header. It answered 401 without a key.
