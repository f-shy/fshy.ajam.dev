Composes withThe cheapest 2 vCPU / 4 GiB offer found anywhere among evaluated offerings at USD 4.76 a month, but it is the default container instance, not a VM. ReliabilityAutomatic top-up is on by default after the first top-up; when a settle leaves the wallet negative the instances are force-slept and requests return 402 until a top-up.
NotesTwo rows live in this provider and the shape decides the verdict. Default (container) 2 vCPU / 4 GB / 4 GB disk: 2 x 0.80 + 4 x 0.70 + 4 x 0.09 = USD 4.76 a month at 730 running hours - the cheapest 2 vCPU / 4 GiB found, but it is a shared-kernel instance. VM 2 vCPU / 4 GB / 4 GB disk: 2.5 x (1.60 + 2.80) + 0.36 = USD 11.36. A VM under USD 5 is impossible because the smallest VM shape is 2 vCPU / 4 GB. Ingress is the other catch: ports are published as HTTPS at {id}-{port}.agent37.app, so no raw TCP listener and no UDP - it cannot be a WireGuard node.
Evidence notedocumented The rate card, the four shapes, the 2.5x VM multiplier, the per-minute metering, the stopped-disk rate and the card requirement are quoted from the vendor's own documentation read.
