'Vì giới hạn tối đa mỗi máy chỉ được 2 kết nối để download' - the cap is per machine, which is why the guide tells users to queue downloads rather than parallelise.
Bindingper-ip-shaping Two connections per machine caps throughput well below what a monthly transfer of 1 TiB needs unless each connection is fast; no byte figure is published to check against.
Identitycard Not documented on the pages read: the guide and homepage publish no payment page. Every other Vietnamese PLG among debrid providers takes either a card (LinksVIP) or domestic rails only (Getlink.vn), so this is unresolved rather than assumed.
EgressPer-host daily bandwidth, deducted on a successful get: 'Đối với những host có giới hạn băng thông trong ngày ... sau get link thành công, băng thông của host đó sẽ bị trừ.' Re-getting a link to resume does not re-deduct.

Composes withA Vietnamese PLG positioned as the cheap alternative to buying each host account. The two-connection cap makes it a poor fit for 1 TiB of monthly transfer unless the per-host daily cap is large, which is not published.
ReliabilityA hard two-connection limit per machine with no published quota means the failure mode is a slow download rather than an error; support is a Facebook/Zalo channel.
NotesIncluded for the constraint, not the price: it is the only Vietnamese service found that states a connection cap instead of a byte cap, and that changes the failure mode. Trigger condition: a published price and host table appear.
