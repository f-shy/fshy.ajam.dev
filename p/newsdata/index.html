Free tierFree plan, USD 0: 200 API credits a day, 1 to 10 articles a credit, the past 48 hours of news with a 12-hour delay.
unit credits; metered on request-count; Most calls cost 1 credit; an Archive call costs 5 credits and a Count call 50. The pricing page says credits are deducted only for requests that are processed and return results, while the vendor's reference says a call that matches nothing still costs its credits. On the free plan the crypto and market endpoints cover the last 7 days with a 12-hour delay. The next plan up is Basic at USD 199.99 a month for 20,000 credits a month.

  • per-object-cap 1 to 10 articles a request on the free plan source
  • per-ip-shaping rapid bursts of requests from a single IP address are limited separately from the 15-minute limit and answer HTTP 429 with the code TooManyRequests; the burst size is not stated source
The rate-limit documentation page and the rate-limit blog post word the free limit as 30 credits every 15 minutes with no further request served until it resets, while the vendor's reference counts requests, not credits, over a moving 15-minute window. Responses carry an X-RateLimit-Remaining header with the requests left in the current 15-minute window. Unverified: behind a sign-up: whether a live request past the 30 allowed answers 429 RateLimitExceeded, whether a request that returns no results counts toward the limit, and whether the window is fixed or moving.
Bindingrequest-rate 200 credits a day is the ceiling, and 30 requests every 15 minutes paces how fast it can be spent.
Identityemail The sign-up form asks for a full name, an email and a password, each marked required, and offers an optional purpose and an optional phone number, or Google sign-in; the form's script checks a phone number only when one is entered and leaves it out of the sign-up request when the field is empty, and the request carries a reCAPTCHA response. Verifying the email address activates the account. No card for the free plan. Unverified: behind a sign-up: whether the sign-up server accepts a request with no phone number.
