Paid individual tiers are Regular at USD 200 per year for 50 GB, 1,000 compute units and 2 concurrent jobs, and Pro at USD 400 per year for 4 TB, 25,000 compute units and 4 concurrent jobs, with GPU access by request on the two paid tiers only.

NotesThe free tier is small enough that recording it as capacity would mislead: 200 core-hours a year is one afternoon of a single core. Its value is the paid tier as a price floor, and the enforcement history as a reliability fact. It is not the cheapest cost per core-hour in the file: the DesignSafe record carries a TACC purchase rate near USD 0.008 to USD 0.01 per core-hour, but that is a quote-based purchase from a national laboratory rather than a published annual plan. CyVerse also removes accounts for inactivity, announced on its own 404 page, which is a fact a reader storing data there needs.

Free tier5 GB default for every account, explicitly for 'all domestic and international CyVerse account holders'. Above that it is paid: $125/TB/year single-copy up to 4 TB, $105/TB/year beyond, and $250/TB/year for 2025 double-copy. Community Data folders can be granted larger allocations on application, and published data in the Data Commons can be larger. But the free self-serve number is 5 GB.
