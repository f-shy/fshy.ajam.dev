EgressFree downloads are throttled hard, with reports of 300 to 400 KB/s for video: 'UC网盘之前宣称不限速，现在下载视频三四百k'. The paid tier advertises 上传下载不限速 as an SVIP benefit
Per-object cap1 GB per file on a free account: 'UC网盘2t,但单文件就1G，相当难用' (1000000000 bytes)
RetentionPromo capacity is permanent once claimed. No retention clock on stored files is published
Interfacerest
ToolingNo public API. A reverse-engineered client mounts it: the OpenList UC driver takes a pasted browser cookie, root folder id 0. A separate UC TV driver pairs by QR code from the mobile app and supports list and download only. Same backend family as Quark. No official S3 or WebDAV
Composes withCodes are batch-minted, a hundred a month per one poster, and shared in threads, so one account can stack several to 2 TB. Each account is a +86 identity, and the 1 GB per-file ceiling makes the capacity useless as a dataset store
ReliabilityPromo capacity is permanent once claimed, but the 1 GB per-file ceiling makes it a media-hoarding pan and not a dataset store. Download tickets are IP-bound on the shared Quark backend, so a harvested cookie dies on the first replay
NotesCovers both SVIP paid tiers and promo-code allocations. While nominal storage capacity reaches 2 TB permanently, individual file uploads are constrained by a strict 1 GB ceiling.
