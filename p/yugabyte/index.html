Free tierServerless Postgres is free to start, and includes 2 vCPU, 16 GiB RAM, and 100 GiB storage. This allowance is shared by every cluster in the account.
unit mixed; metered on stored-bytes; A perpetual free allowance on the shared serverless pool, one isolated PostgreSQL database per application or tenant with zero-copy branching. To use larger sizes an Admin must create a billing profile with a payment method. A separate single-node Sandbox free tier offers 10 GB, pauses after 10 days idle and is deleted after 15 days.
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 2 vCPU, 16 GiB RAM and 100 GiB storage shared by every cluster in the account source
  • retention-clamped Sandbox cluster: up to 2 vCPU / 4 GB memory / 10 GB storage, up to 15 simultaneous connections, paused after 10 days idle and deleted after 15 days source
  • write-gated larger than free sizes require an Admin to create a billing profile with a payment method source
Bindingvolume-quota The Serverless free allowance is a shared 100 GiB / 2 vCPU / 16 GiB pool, so the storage and compute quota binds before any other stated free limit.
Identityemail sign-up creates an account; the free-trial form takes contact details and a free-trial link is sent to the email address. A billing profile with a credit card is needed only to go beyond the free allowance, and the pricing page FAQ states the database-as-a-service is a paid offering
Egressdata transfer is billed separately: same region $0.01/GB, cross region (other regions) $0.02/GB, cross region (APAC) $0.08/GB, Internet $0.10/GB
Per-object capunverified: no single-object size cap stated on the first-party pages read; the free allowance is 100 GiB of storage shared across the account
Retentionno deletion on the Serverless Postgres free allowance is stated on the pages read; the separate Sandbox cluster is paused after 10 days of inactivity and deleted after 15 days
Interfacerest, cli PostgreSQL wire protocol via the YSQL API, plus a Cassandra-style YCQL API; automate with a REST API, CLI or Terraform provider.
Toolingany PostgreSQL driver or ORM against the YSQL endpoint, CQL drivers against YCQL, the YugabyteDB CLI and Terraform provider; rclone does not apply
Composes witha free Serverless Postgres database with zero-copy branches behind a worker or tunnel gives a programme-accessible store; the self-hosted open-source YugabyteDB server is a last-resort option and is not the hosted free tier
Reliabilitythe free allowance runs on a shared pool with resource isolation between tenants; the Sandbox is single-node and is explicitly not for production or performance testing, and it does not support backups.
NotesThe hosted free offer is Serverless Postgres: 2 vCPU, 16 GiB RAM and 100 GiB storage shared across the account, with zero-copy branches. The legacy single-node Sandbox (10 GB, paused after 10 days and deleted after 15) is a different, more restrictive free path and is recorded here as a limit rather than as the headline. The open-source YugabyteDB server is self-hosted software and a last resort, not the hosted free tier. Egress beyond the free allowance is metered in USD per GB.
