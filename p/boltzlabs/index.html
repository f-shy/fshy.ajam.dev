Free tierA free account includes $1 of trial usage for Small sandboxes and code execution, from the website, the CLI and the desktop app, and can run one Small sandbox at a time. A Small sandbox is 1 vCPU and 4 GB at $0.0219 an hour, so the credit is about 46 hours of a Small sandbox.
unit credits; metered on wall-clock; The machine rates are billed by the second out of the same monthly allowance as execution requests, RL machines, eval model use and billed storage; unused allowance does not roll over. The GitHub org profile at https://github.com/boltzlabs names boltzlabs.cloud as the site.

Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) $1 of trial usage, about 46 hours of the Small size at $0.0219 an hour, out of a monthly allowance that does not roll over source
  • write-gated free accounts run one Small sandbox at a time; Medium and Large need an active monthly subscription, with up to 25 concurrent sandboxes source
  • retention-clamped paused sandbox storage is free for 3 days, then $0.05 per GB per month source
Evals and recordings need an active monthly subscription. Available capacity can limit how many sandboxes a free account starts. The $10 starting price is a monthly subscription, not the price of one sandbox.
Bindingvolume-quota The $1 trial credit is consumed after about 46 hours of the Small sandbox, before the one-at-a-time concurrency gate or the three-day paused-storage clock becomes the main obstacle.
Identityemail The sign-up form at https://boltzlabs.cloud/login?mode=signup asks for 'name email password at least 8 characters confirm password' and offers 'continue with github'. No card, phone or identity document appears on the form. The sign-in page reads 'Sign in to create sandboxes'.
Egressunverified: no egress price or allowance is published for sandboxes; a public preview URL is available for a port when the sandbox is created with internet access
Per-object capnot stated: the unit is a machine, not a stored object
RetentionPausing releases compute while retaining files; storage is free for 3 days after pausing, then $0.05 per GB per month. Deleting a sandbox ends the meter and removes its files. (3 days)
Interfacecli, rest A boltz CLI, a Python SDK and a JavaScript/TypeScript SDK create, pause, resume, fork and delete sandboxes and expose a public URL for a port; a Judge0-style execution API runs code without a sandbox to manage.
endpoint: the boltz CLI and the Python/TypeScript SDK APIs
Toolingcurl -fsSL https://boltzlabs.cloud/cli/install.sh | sh installs the boltz binary; boltz auth login, boltz create, boltz exec and boltz connect, or the boltzlabs Python package with BOLTZLABS_API_KEY.
Composes withA Small sandbox at $0.0219 an hour runs Python commands and a public preview port for an agent; pausing keeps its workspace for $0.05 per GB a month after three days, and the execution API handles one-off code without provisioning a machine.
ReliabilityAvailable capacity can limit how many sandboxes start, and spot-like capacity is not described on the pages read. An active monthly subscription is required for evals and recordings; when paused sandboxes pass three days, storage bills from the allowance.
NotesThe $1 trial allowance is smaller than the operator list's 36 hours: at the published $0.0219 an hour for the Small size, $1 buys about 46 hours, and only one Small sandbox may run at a time. Sign-up asks for an email and password rather than a card. GPU sandboxes are announced as 'on the way' and have no published price.
