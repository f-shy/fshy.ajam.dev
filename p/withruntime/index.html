NotesThe arithmetic at 24/7 for 1 GiB sitting idle: 0.05 vCPU x 0.025 + 1 GiB x 0.0075 = USD 0.00875/hour x 730 = USD 6.39. Busy at 1 vCPU: 0.025 + 0.0075 = USD 0.0325/hour x 730 = USD 23.73. Either way over USD 5, and the free tier is 100 hours, not a month. Egress: USD 0.02/GB x 1000 = USD 20 per TB beyond the 100 GiB allowance. Note the vendor's own framing (42% to 88% cheaper) is measured against agents that wait on a model, not against an always-on box, which is why the 730-hour figure is the one that decides.
Evidence notedocumented Both usage rates, the idle floor, the free allowance, the add-on prices and the paused-sandbox rate are quoted from the pricing page read. What would raise it: the exact bill for a machine kept running with no work, which the vendor models but does not publish.
