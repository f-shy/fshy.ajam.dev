Bindingrequest-rate identity binds before any limit: Harness Cloud on Free needs card validation and a business email domain. Among the limits, the 2,000 monthly credits are 2,000 xsmall minutes, about 33 hours (2,000 / 60); the 1-hour stage timeout binds first for any single long job
Identityinstitution Harness Cloud on the Free plan is limited to business email domains, with Gmail, Hotmail, Yahoo and other unverified domains blocked, and Free plans require credit card validation to use it; the Harness Cloud product page says 'No credit card required', which the CI documentation contradicts
Egressnetwork transfer from Harness Cloud to customer-managed storage is limited to 1 GB on Free, 5 GB on Startup and 10 GB on Enterprise
free allowance: 1 GB of network transfer to customer-managed storage
Per-object capon the Free plan a stage times out at 1 hour and a pipeline at 2 hours; each CI stage runs in a new ephemeral VM
Retentionunverified: no log or artifact retention period for the Free plan was found in the pages read; cache above the 2 GB Free limit evicts the oldest caches
Interfacegit a Harness pipeline built in the visual editor or YAML, with a CI stage on Harness Cloud infrastructure and a Git connector to the repository
ToolingHarness-managed VMs: Linux AMD64 and ARM64 from 2 to 96 cores, Windows, and macOS on Apple M4; Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04 images with preinstalled tools; steps run on the VM or in a Docker container. The local runner, which the user installs and runs on a machine of their own, rented or free, has no build limit and needs no card; it comes after the hosted offer
Composes with2,000 Linux xsmall minutes a month behind a card check and a business email domain; the 1 GB transfer limit to outside storage makes it a place to run work rather than to ship large outputs
Reliabilitycredits used beyond the allowance are invoiced in arrears, and Harness may suspend Harness Cloud executions when they are not paid
NotesThe allowance is real on paper, 2,000 credits a month with 20 concurrent Linux stages, but the account-wide limit of 2 concurrent pipeline executions and the 1-hour stage timeout make it small, and the gate in front of it is a business email domain plus card validation. The Developer 360 post (updated 2026-09-30) lists Continuous Integration among the Free plan modules and the CI documentation still states the 2,000 credits, although the pricing page's Free plan names no CI module; whether a new Free account receives the credits, and whether a custom-domain address passes the business-domain check, is unverified: behind a sign-up.
