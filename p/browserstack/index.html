NotesA hosted real-device cloud for mobile app automation: Appium, Espresso, XCUITest and EarlGrey tests run on physical iOS and Android devices. The free trial is 100 one-time minutes of App Automate, not a recurring allowance; the paid App Automate Device Cloud tier is USD 199 a month billed annually for one parallel device test, and the next listed tier is USD 249 a month billed annually. No general-purpose shell VM is offered; the compute is a managed device session. The signup page's only visible identity gate is OAuth; an email-and-password field appears on the sign-in page, but the exact registration identifier is not established from the sign-up capture.
