The quickstart offers free credits on signup and top-up, but the voucher amounts are not published on the read pages and all funds deposited are non-refundable.
unit none; metered on unstated; The rate at published shape is per GPU-hour: B200 SXM from $7.50, H200 SXM from $2.68, H100 SXM from $2.42, RTX-4090 from $0.48 and RTX-5090 from $0.62 per GPU-hour; full-machine rentals are 8 cards only. Limits
  • write-gated (binding) to launch an instance, the account must have enough credits to cover at least one hour of runtime at the selected specification; if the balance runs out, all active instances are stopped and local data disk contents are cleared source
  • volume-quota GPU instances bill per minute with a one-minute minimum: RTX-4090 from $0.48/gpu/hour up to B200 SXM from $7.50/gpu/hour; CPU instances are a fixed $0.24 per CPU instance/hour and remote storage bills per day per GB used source
  • per-object-cap full-machine rentals are supported with 8 cards only; Spot instances are interruptible by Prepaid or On-Demand instances source
Membership plans ($1,000, $5,000 and $10,000 a month) grant monthly credits that apply only to on-demand pricing and expire 30 days after payment. Vouchers are deducted first, then monthly credits, then the balance. Unused balances are non-refundable.
Bindingwrite-gated A new user without credits cannot launch any instance until a payment is made and the balance covers at least one hour at the chosen rate, so the prepaid balance gates before any GPU price or storage charge accrues.
Identitycard Signup asks for details and optionally a Google account and requires email verification. Adding funds supports Stripe with credit or debit cards, Google Pay and Link; a card added through the console becomes the default for automatic payment. No phone number or ID document is requested on the read pages; the exact signup form fields are unverified: behind a sign-up.
EgressNo per-GB egress price or free traffic allowance is published on the pricing, billing or quickstart pages read; the only published traffic-adjacent sentence is that the local storage on instances is covered at no additional cost. Remote storage is charged per day per GB used, not by bytes out.
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is a GPU instance or a remote storage volume, not a stored object; no per-object size cap is published
RetentionData disks are not persistent once an instance is terminated; for On-Demand and Spot instances the data disk is cleared when the instance is stopped. A remote Storage Volume must be attached to keep data across stop or termination, and storage is billed per day on actual GB used. Local storage on instances is covered at no additional cost.
Interfacessh, browser, rest Instances are reached by SSH with an uploaded public key or through a JupyterLab web button; the platform also exposes an OpenAI-compatible model API at https://api.hpc-ai.com/inference/v1 with a published OpenAI-compatible SDK package.
endpoint: https://api.hpc-ai.com/inference/v1
ToolingAn SSH client with an uploaded key, the JupyterLab web interface, preconfigured CUDA, PyTorch and Colossal-AI images, Docker support and the @ai-sdk/openai-compatible package for the model API.
Composes withA prepaid GPU cloud that runs training and inference jobs and attaches a remote Storage Volume for data that must survive an instance stop; with no published egress price, moving large results out needs a separate egress or storage record for a predictable cost.
ReliabilityInstances stop when the balance runs out and local data disk contents are cleared; AutoPay checks run on an independent hourly schedule and a zero balance before the next check stops active instances. Spot instances are interruptible, and data disks are cleared on stop for On-Demand and Spot instances.
NotesA prepaid GPU cloud at $0.48 to $7.50 per GPU-hour with per-minute billing and a one-minute minimum. No standing free allowance is published; the quickstart mentions signup and top-up vouchers whose amounts are not on the read pages. Data disks are cleared on stop, so a remote Storage Volume is needed for durable data. The model API at api.hpc-ai.com/inference/v1 is a separate product from the GPU instances priced here.
