Bindingper-object-cap Free storage is limited to 5 GB. Global email registration routes accounts to international regions where consumer drive sync features have been phased out. Total account quota is the binding constraint.
Identityemail a Xiaomi account. Xiaomi's own FAQ says a non-mainland account 'can only be created through email', which is an open door into the region where the storage product was killed. Only a mainland account still has the 2 TB tier
EgressNo published cap, and the drive is deliberately not share-oriented: the community notes 不能对外分享. Third-party clients download at full speed with concurrency 20
Per-object capNot published; the drive is sync-oriented and does not expose a single-file ceiling
RetentionAccount ownership is not transferable; the global-region Gallery sync and all memberships were terminated on 2022-10-25 outside the mainland, with stored Gallery data deleted by mid-2023
Interfacerest, cli
Toolinga reverse-engineered REST interface at i.mi.com with a Go CLI, wangbjun/go-micloud, doing login, list, upload, download, share and tree at concurrency 5 up and 20 down; an unmerged OpenList driver pull request 1391 implements list, link, put and mkdir but its body says 还没写完
Composes withThe API is real and a wrapper exists, so it would be the cleanest phone-vendor target. 5 GB free means about 410 accounts per 2 TB and the 2 TB tier is CNY 239 to 408 a year, which is not a stacking play
ReliabilityGlobal-region Gallery sync and all memberships were terminated outside mainland China in three regional waves by 2023-02-01, with Gallery data deleted by mid-2023. An email signup works and lands in the region where the product no longer exists, which is a worse outcome than a closed gate because it looks open
NotesEmail registration succeeds internationally but maps accounts to global regions lacking active drive storage sync. Unofficial client tooling includes experimental Go wrapper scripts and community OpenList driver integrations.
