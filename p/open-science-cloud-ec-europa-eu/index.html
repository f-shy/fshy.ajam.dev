EgressNot metered; the node is EC-procured infrastructure. The binding constraint is the monthly 1 TB transfer allowance, which is the only hard number in the policy.
RetentionRetention: credits 'cannot be transferred to another person or carried over between periods' and are 'automatically renewed every three months'; services are ordered for a fixed period and refunded pro rata if released, so nothing here is permanent

ToolingEOSC EU Node User Space web UI plus credit-metered service orders; no documented S3 endpoint. Treat as API-less until probed by an account holder.
Composes withThe chaining hook is wallets, not accounts: 'When you create a group, a separate Group Wallet will automatically be generated. This wallet is independent of your personal wallet and comes pre-allocated with additional credits.' Investigator groups also carry the 500 GB group file storage. Many groups = many 500 GB pools, and the group storage is separate from the 50 GB personal pool.
ReliabilityCredits are not top-up-able ('additional ones will not be provided to you') and do not carry over. A storage-heavy month silently stops you until the next quarter.
