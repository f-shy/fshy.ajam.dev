Free tierAccess-policy table: File Storage (Personal) = 50 GB for Collaborator and Investigator; File Storage (Group) = 500 GB for Investigator only. Investigator also gets 'File Transfer 1TB/month'. Credits: Collaborator 1,000; Investigator '2,000 (individual) / 6,000 (group)', renewed automatically every three months and explicitly not top-up-able.
unit GB; metered on stored-bytes
Pricenone stated
Identityinstitution eduGAIN / EU Login / eIDAS federated identity; access group is assigned automatically from affiliation information shared by the home organisation; only users from EU27 and Horizon Europe Associated Countries can get a role above Observer.
EgressNot metered; the node is EC-procured infrastructure. The binding constraint is the monthly 1 TB transfer allowance, which is the only hard number in the policy.
RetentionCredits are provided free of charge by the European Commission, are automatically renewed every three months, cannot be transferred to another person or between periods, and additional credits are not provided; services are ordered for a fixed period, so the allocation is not permanent.
Interfaces3, rest rest
ToolingEOSC EU Node User Space web UI plus credit-metered service orders; no documented S3 endpoint. Treat as API-less until probed by an account holder.
Composes withThe chaining hook is wallets, not accounts: 'When you create a group, a separate Group Wallet will automatically be generated. This wallet is independent of your personal wallet and comes pre-allocated with additional credits.' Investigator groups also carry the 500 GB group file storage. Many groups = many 500 GB pools, and the group storage is separate from the 50 GB personal pool.
ReliabilityCredits are not top-up-able ('additional ones will not be provided to you') and do not carry over. A storage-heavy month silently stops you until the next quarter.
