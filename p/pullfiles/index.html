Composes withIt solves the case where the local connection cannot reach a host but the relay can: the relay fetches and re-serves over HTTPS. It is a proxy, not storage, so it must be followed immediately by a real download.
ReliabilityA relay is a single point of failure and a single point of visibility: the operator sees every URL and every byte, and the stream dies if the operator's egress is slow. Nothing on the page states uptime or operator identity.
NotesAnswers a narrower question than the sector asked: it will not resolve a Rapidgator page into a direct link, but it will fetch a direct link that is otherwise unreachable. Worth keeping as the last hop.
