Free tierFree and keyless: 10,256 lines in total across the three protocol files http.txt, socks4.txt and socks5.txt.
unit objects; metered on none; A line is an address and a port and nothing else: there is no country, ASN, uptime, latency or anonymity field to filter on before a line is dialled.
Pricenone stated
Bindingnone Nothing in the vocabulary fails first at 45 GB a month across 599,000 requests, because the list imposes no quota, no request rate and no per-address shape to hit. What fails is the supply: 8 of 100 lines carried a payload, and yield has no vocabulary kind.
Identityanonymous No account, no key and no signup: the three text files are fetched over unauthenticated HTTPS. The hosts behind the lines belong to somebody else, and what each one requires of a caller is not published in the list at all.
EgressNo traffic position is published: the list is a text file with no terms, quota or meter attached to it, and every byte sent crosses the exit host of whichever line it dials, not the list.
free allowance: none published
Retentionnot applicable: a live address, not stored bytes
Interfacerest One unauthenticated HTTPS GET per protocol file, http.txt, socks4.txt and socks5.txt, returned as plain text and read line by line. There is no resource model, no query parameter and no API, so rest is the nearest token in the closed set rather than an accurate description; the lines themselves speak HTTP CONNECT, SOCKS4 and SOCKS5, and the schema's interface enum has no token for any proxy protocol, so the three are named here instead of in the array.
Toolingcurl or wget pulls the three text files; no account, key, SDK or wrapper is involved. Dialling a line needs a client that speaks its protocol, which curl does for HTTP CONNECT and SOCKS5 and a small socket driver does for SOCKS4. The work is not access but triage: a health check that discards the 92 percent which do not answer, run often enough to keep the remainder current.
Composes withA failover supply for the Workers AI gateway's egress: a request the Worker cannot make from its own address is retried through a line, with a short timeout and the next line on failure, so the value is the retry loop rather than any single address.
ReliabilityThe working exits sat in hosting-provider ASNs rather than residential ones, so reputation follows a datacenter block; and the working set churns, with handshakes falling from 17 to 15 between two runs 23 minutes apart while payloads held at 8, and a later recheck of the 8 named lines returning 4.
NotesRun 1 dialled 100 lines drawn from the three files (40 HTTP, 40 SOCKS5, 20 SOCKS4): 17 completed a handshake and 8 carried a payload. The 92 lines without a payload split into five error classes: 58 TimeoutError, 19 OSError (7 of them an HTTP CONNECT answered with status 407), 7 ConnectionRefusedError, 5 body-not-json (an unrelated HTML page came back, such as a video recorder, a network camera or a login form) and 3 ConnectionResetError. Run 2 dialled the same 100 lines: 15 handshakes and 8 payloads, 6 of the payload lines common to both runs. The 8 run-1 payload exits map to AS16276 OVH (51.210.123.18 and 198.244.254.77), AS17451 Biznet, AS51004 Sakhalin Cable Telesystems, AS60404 Liteserver, AS51167 Contabo, AS8075 Microsoft and AS26496 GoDaddy. Four of the eight are SOCKS5 lines on 45.74.31.42 and 45.74.31.46 whose exit address differs from the line's address. The same lookup maps other addresses to AS14061 DigitalOcean and AS398101 GoDaddy; none of those addresses carried a payload in either run. A recheck of the 8 run-1 payload lines returned 4 payloads, with exits in ID, FR, GB and SG, and 4 timeouts.
Evidence notemeasured Measured: the per-line results of two runs over the same 100 lines, of a recheck of the 8 run-1 payload lines and of an ASN lookup of the exit addresses are stored with the three list files as read on 2026-10-24, 2,918 HTTP, 2,425 SOCKS4 and 5,087 SOCKS5 lines. Each rate is one sample of 100 lines dialled twice: enough to show a low yield that repeats, not enough to put an interval on it or to separate a diurnal effect from a dead list.
