They range from as little as $500 to as much as $7,500 each, in fully non-dilutive, no-strings-attached capital.' Eligibility is described as people at the edges rather than by credential: 'You shouldn't be starting a company (though it could lead you to one) but instead you should be working on research, building a prototype, writing, open-sourcing something, or tinkering on a strange project that resists easy explanation.' A second stated category is 'thoughtful people who are simply in transition'. NotesIncluded because it is the same shape at a smaller scale and because its terms are unusually explicit about what it is not: non-dilutive, no company, and a stated appetite for people between projects. That last property matters more than the amount, because a person with no affiliation and no current publication fits the 'simply in transition' category the operator names. The failure mode is size: the ceiling is USD 7,500 and the modal award is likely nearer the USD 500 floor, which the page's own range implies without stating a distribution. One structural caution: the granting entity is a venture firm, so the review may weigh commercial potential even where the page says not to; nothing on the page promises otherwise. Reachability is easy: a form on the page and a named email address, grants@compound.vc.
