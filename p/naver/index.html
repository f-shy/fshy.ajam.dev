Bindingper-object-cap Storage is limited to 30 GB total with a 4 GB per-file cap, and stored content is deleted after 730 days of account dormancy. The 4 GB per-file ceiling remains an immutable boundary across multiple accounts.
Identitynational-id a Naver account that passes Korean real-name verification, which is the wall the forum reports agree on

free allowance: 50,000 downloads a day
