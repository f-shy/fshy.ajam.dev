NotesThe weakest of the four usenet blocks on paper (one size, the least precise retention, the smallest balance) and the only one that explicitly allows sharing. Trigger condition: a stated binary-retention window would replace the 'as long as possible' note. 10 GB is a fifth of the 50 GiB monthly bar, so the trial alone does not clear it. What makes the record qualify is that the trial needs nothing but an email. The step survives, and the reason it needed checking is the finding: four plausible sub-paths (/pricing, /plans, /signup, /register) all answer 404, and the text reader drops the offer, so a verifier who used one route would have recorded this trial as gone. The 10 GB is real and the signup form asks for nothing but an email.
