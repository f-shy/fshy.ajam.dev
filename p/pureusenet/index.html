Bindingper-ip-shaping The trial has no byte counter, so what caps it is the package speed over 7 days: 8 Mbps is 1 MB/s, which is 604,800 s x 1 MB/s = 604.8 GB. On XL at 60 Mbps the same week is about 4.5 TB, so the choice of package is the whole of the trial's ceiling.
