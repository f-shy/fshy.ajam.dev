Drafts are uploaded, stored, and private. That is the actual finding: published storage is capped at 20 items, unpublished storage is uncapped.

4 GB max per show file; shows must be >15 min, tracks <=15 min (so the 4 GB ceiling only applies to long files); 10 published shows + 10 published tracks; RSS-fed podcasts also count toward the 10-show cap ('only the 10 most recent shows will display').

Composes withUnlimited drafts x 4 GB per show x N free accounts. Whether drafts are garbage-collected is not stated. The FAQ only says previous uploads are not deleted when you exceed the publish cap, which is a different promise from 'drafts are kept forever'.
ReliabilityMixcloud is a streaming platform: shows are transcoded for playback and there is no original-file download link on Basic. Drafts are inaccessible to anyone but the uploader, which means they are storage but not retrieval. A draft is only recoverable by publishing it, and publishing consumes one of the 10 slots.
