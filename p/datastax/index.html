Free tierOrganizations on the Free plan receive a fixed amount of credits each month. No more than five Astra DB Serverless databases.
unit credits; metered on request-count; The free allowance is a fixed number of credits per month, not a fixed byte count. Unused credits do not roll over and reset on the first of every month. Storage itself has no practical byte limit on a Serverless database.
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • volume-quota No more than five Astra DB Serverless databases on Free source
  • write-gated (binding) databases are suspended when the monthly credit allowance is consumed and cannot be accessed until credits refresh or the plan is upgraded source
  • retention-clamped inactive databases are automatically hibernated and scheduled for deletion on the Free plan source
Bindingwrite-gated the fixed monthly credit allowance suspends databases once spent, which binds before the five-database count for an active workload
Identityemail You can create an Astra account manually or use SSO to automatically provision an account through your IdP, Google, GitHub, or IBMid account. Your email address is the unique identifier for your Astra account. The manual route is a username and password; no card is named at signup.
EgressData transfer out of a database, including internet egress and cross-region replication, is billed in credits per GB; the free plan draws from the monthly credit allowance
free allowance: fixed monthly credits
Per-object capThere is no practical limit to the number of bytes an Astra DB Serverless database can store.
RetentionInactive Astra DB Serverless databases are automatically hibernated and scheduled for deletion
Interfacerest, cli Data API over HTTP for collections and tables, the DevOps API for management, CQL over the Cassandra wire protocol with a Secure Connect Bundle, and the Astra CLI
ToolingAstra Data API clients, Cassandra drivers with a Secure Connect Bundle, cqlsh, and the astra CLI
Composes witha free Serverless database can back an app through the HTTP Data API and be paired with other free hosts; at most five databases and the monthly credit refresh bound the chain
Reliabilitya Free database is suspended when monthly credits run out and its data is inaccessible until credits refresh or the plan is upgraded; inactive databases are hibernated and scheduled for deletion. The amount of free credits is not published on the pages read, so the exact ceiling is unknown.

Astra is a Serverless Cassandra and vector store, not a general object store, so the lack of a byte cap does not make the credit ceiling irrelevant.
