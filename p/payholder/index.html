The vendor serves 'России и Беларуси' and a separate business site for legal entities; the rail is rubles from a Russian bank account or SBP.
Bindingwrite-gated There is no rate or byte limit - the constraint is that a human confirms each payment and the payer needs a Russian rail. For a client outside Russia the service is unusable, which is why the verdict is fails-identity rather than a quota failure.
Identitycard Russian payment methods only: SBP and a Russian bank card on the gift-card page ('Способ оплаты СБП Карта РФ'), and a Russian current account for the business arm. No foreign card path is offered.
Egressnot applicable: the customer pays in rubles and the intermediary pays the foreign service; no traffic is involved
Per-object capnot applicable: a payment, not an object
Interfaceunknown Web order form plus a manager who confirms the payment; a personal cabinet at lk.payholder.ru and a Telegram bot (@Payholders_Bot).
ToolingBrowser and Telegram. The order is processed by a person ('Оставляете заявку на payholder.ru ... специалист уточняет детали') rather than by an API.
Composes withThe bridge that makes a foreign debrid usable from Russia: pay Payholder in RUB, it pays Real-Debrid or the foreign shop, the account is credited. It is the inverse of a CIS service closed to foreigners - here the foreign service is closed to Russians and Payholder is the workaround.
ReliabilityA third-party review site questions whether the service is a scam; the vendor answers with a refund promise ('Гарантируем возврат денежных средств в случае неуспешной оплаты'). Unverified either way - recorded as a claim, not as a finding.
NotesThe clearest evidence that the wall runs in both directions. A Russian cannot pay Real-Debrid directly, so a Russian intermediary exists; a foreigner cannot use that intermediary, so the two markets have separate payment paths and no single card works on both sides. The vc.ru guides date from 2026 and the service list is live; the exact fee schedule is not published, only a floor.
Evidence notedocumented The service list, the 'от 290 р.' floor, the Russia/Belarus scope and the SBP/Карта РФ rails are quoted from live pages today. The Real-Debrid use case is reported by two vc.ru guides, not by Payholder's own service list as read.
