EgressFree and unmetered for public images (staticflickr.com CDN). Non-public content on free accounts is capped at 50 and at risk of deletion, so egress is real only for public photos.
Interfacerest, rclone rest
ToolingFlickr API (OAuth1), flickr-sync; no native rclone backend; ShareX and Lightroom publish plugins.
Composes withUnlimited free accounts x 1,000 items. This is a per-record cap, not a byte cap. But 1,000 items x 200 MB max = ~200 GB/account at absolute best, and item 1,001 is deleted.
ReliabilityEnforcement is real and documented: over-limit accounts are suspended or have content deleted. Pro is required for any bulk use.
