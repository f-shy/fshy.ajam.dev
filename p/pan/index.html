Bindingwrite-gated Offers 1 TB of free storage with class 2 WebDAV endpoint access without phone verification. the provider explicitly prohibits maintaining multiple accounts, enforcing a strict 1 TB account limit.
Identityemail the signup form at pan.moe/signup asks for email, password, a repeat and a human-verification step, with no phone field
EgressNo published egress cap for the free tier; bandwidth is policed only by a fair-use clause allowing throttling for '占用过多带宽'. Both domestic and overseas storage nodes are offered with cross-region transfer
Per-object capNot published
RetentionNo durability guarantee at all: terms section 2.1 states '不对用户的数据进行任何形式的长期或永久性保证'
Interfacewebdav
Toolinga Cloudreve front end with a live WebDAV endpoint at https://pan.moe/dav answering as DAV class 2 with PUT in Allow, plus the standard Nextcloud-family clients. No S3 layer is exposed
Composes withTerms section 3.1 explicitly routes anyone needing more than 5 TB to a bespoke cold-storage plan, so it is a 1 TB building block and not a pool. Its value is the WebDAV endpoint, which mounts into TrueNAS Cloud Sync, rclone, Duplicati and restic without a wrapper
ReliabilityZero durability commitment in the terms plus an explicit rule against the several-accounts pattern that would make it useful at 20 TB. The domestic storage node carries a compliance clause, so the overseas node is the only one that fits
NotesThe service provides no formal uptime or data durability SLA.
