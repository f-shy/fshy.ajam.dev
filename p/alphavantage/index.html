Free tier25 API requests per day on a free key, covering the majority of datasets; unlimited requests for verified open-source or educational projects
unit requests; metered on request-count; On a free key TIME_SERIES_DAILY returns only outputsize=compact, the latest 100 data points; outputsize=full needs a premium key. TIME_SERIES_WEEKLY and TIME_SERIES_MONTHLY are not marked premium and cover 25+ years of history per call. Intraday, daily adjusted, realtime bulk quotes and the index data endpoints are marked premium, and realtime and 15-minute delayed US market data is premium-only. Premium runs from 75 requests/min at USD 49.99/month to 1200 requests/min at USD 249.99/month, with no daily limit.

Limits
  • request-rate (binding) 25 API requests per day per free key source
  • per-object-cap latest 100 data points per daily series on a free key (outputsize=compact) source
Bulk path: no bulk path on a free key. REALTIME_BULK_QUOTES, which accepts up to 100 tickers per request, is premium. LISTING_STATUS returns the active or delisted US stock and ETF list in one call.
Bindingrequest-rate 25 requests a day is reached before any per-call cap matters; the compact cap binds only the daily series.
Identityemail The free key form asks for a role (investor, software developer, educator, student, other), an organization and an email; it has no card and no phone field. The key is described as lifetime access. The documented demo key answered a TIME_SERIES_DAILY call for IBM with no signup.
Egressnot metered in bytes; the 25 requests per day bound what a free key can pull
Per-object capone JSON or CSV response per call; a free key's daily series is the latest 100 data points (outputsize=compact)
Interfacerest GET https://www.alphavantage.co/query with function, symbol and apikey; datatype=json (the default) or datatype=csv, which returns the series as a CSV file.
endpoint: https://www.alphavantage.co/query
Toolingcurl or any HTTP client; datatype=csv returns a CSV file per call
Composes withOne symbol per free call: weekly and monthly series give 25+ years each, the daily series only the latest 100 points. A wide ticker universe or full daily history needs a source with a bulk path, such as Massive flat files on its paid tiers.
ReliabilityRealtime and 15-minute delayed US market data is premium-only.
NotesThe free key reaches the majority of datasets at 25 requests a day; the premium plans sell request rate, from 75 to 1200 per minute, plus the premium-marked endpoints.
Evidence notedocumented The quota and the premium marks are documented on the support, premium and documentation pages. A demo-key probe returned 100 compact daily points; the 25-per-day cut-off needs a free key to measure.
