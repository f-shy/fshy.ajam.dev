NotesThe gate is the earliest and the only one that can stop an account existing at all; the IP lock is the one that breaks a working account mid-download. Host-count claims conflict inside one site: 75+ (landing), 50 (FAQ), 11 rows (public status table). Flat for 37 months at USD 4.99 / 12.99 / 29.99 / 54.99, and the pricing model removes the promo-versus-renewal question entirely: every term is a one-off payment with 'No Recurring Payment'. The billed monthly rate is USD 12.99, at the highest 30-day entry price in the debrid set, against EUR 2.99-4.00 for the French services. The offer narrowed while the price did not: the FAQ answered 'Do you offer any free trials? A proxy with a price, not a store. The terms and the FAQ agree, which makes this one of the cleanest zeroes. No torrent support, which the comparison corpus and the FAQ both imply. A missing client is not a rejection and neither is a missing document: the API exists and answers, it is just not written down.
