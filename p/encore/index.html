Free tierThe Starter plan is free forever with no time limit: 2 development environments on Encore Cloud, 1M tracing events a month, 7-day log and trace retention, 1 concurrent build. Applications hosted on Encore Cloud carry fair-use limits per application on every plan: 100,000 requests a day, 100,000 Pub/Sub messages a day, 1 GB of database storage, 1 GB of object storage, and cron jobs no more often than once an hour.
