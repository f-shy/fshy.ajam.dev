Composes with400 pushes/hour is the most generous publish rate in the entire niche. 9,600 gems/day, i.e. ~2-9 TB/day if each gem were at a hypothetical 250 MB. If the undocumented size limit is anywhere near npm's, RubyGems becomes the highest-throughput package-registry store found. Confirm the size cap before relying on it.
ReliabilityThe unknown size cap is the risk. The one public 413 report (qtbindings) shows a hard limit exists and is enforced at the proxy, and it is not documented anywhere, so you discover it by probing. Rate limits are the best in class and clearly documented.
