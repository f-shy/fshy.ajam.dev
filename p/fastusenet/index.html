Trial then a 6 GB tier is published at $2.95/mo alongside the existing unlimited, 35 GB and 15 GB plans. The trial carries 15 GB whichever plan is chosen.
Bindingvolume-quota The trial is hard-capped at 15 GB and the two cheap plans are hard-capped at 15 GB and 35 GB per month. At 1 TiB/month the counter is the first thing that fails, on every tier.
Identitycard The signup flow lists four steps: 'Choose Your Plan', 'Confirm Account', 'Payment Information', 'Create Login'. The payment step asks for an email address, first name, last name and a credit/debit card or PayPal, so a payment method is captured before the trial starts.
EgressUnlimited after the trial on the top plan; 35 GB and 15 GB on the two cheaper plans
free allowance: 15 GB during the trial on every plan
Per-object capno per-object cap is published; no per-object cap is published; the unit of retrieval is a Usenet article, approximately 750 KB by convention, so 1 TiB of monthly transfer corresponds to roughly 1.4 million article…
Retention6,676+ days of binary retention, stated on the vendor home page. The retention guide says 6,668+ days; the more conservative of the two first-party figures is recorded. (6668 days)
Interfacenntp NNTP over TLS on port 563. No HTTP or object API exists for the download path, which is driven through SABnzbd, NZBGet or nzbdav.
Composes withOnce the trial converts this is a second Omicron feed and adds nothing to a Newshosting or Eweka subscription; its stack value is the 14-day test window.
ReliabilityOmicron reseller on the sslusenet.com hosts, the same pair Newsgroup Ninja uses. The 14-day window is longer than the 3 to 7 days most competitors allow.
