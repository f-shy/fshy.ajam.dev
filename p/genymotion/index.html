Every started minute is billed as soon as the device is running; payments are processed weekly by default or when usage exceeds a default USD 150 threshold. Prepaid and virtual credit cards are not accepted.
Bindingwrite-gated The trial must be requested and manually approved before any device can start, which binds ahead of the 60-minute allowance; a valid non-disposable email is mandatory.
Identityemail Sign-up is by email address with a verification email, or through Google or GitHub single sign-on; disposable email providers are not allowed for registration. The trial request asks whether the applicant is an individual or a company, requires a valid personal email for an individual or a valid professional email and company details for a company, and is manually reviewed. The default subscription payment method is a credit card: Visa, Mastercard or American Express; prepaid cards are not accepted.
