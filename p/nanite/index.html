NotesHosted compute only: a Nanite microVM with root, 2 vCPU, 2 GiB memory and a 20 GiB disk, or 4 vCPU, 8 GiB and 20 GiB for a new sandbox, billed per second from prepaid credits. Component prices are $0.072/vCPU-hour, $0.0432/GB-hour memory, $0.15/GB-month storage and $0.15/GB out. The 30-second idle sleep makes an idle agent cheap but not free.
