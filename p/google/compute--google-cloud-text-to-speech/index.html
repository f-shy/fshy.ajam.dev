Free tierA recurring monthly free allowance per voice class, not a one-time credit: 'WaveNet voices 0 to 4 million characters', 'Standard voices 0 to 4 million characters', 'Chirp 3: HD voices 0 to 1 million characters', 'Studio voices 0 to 1 million characters', 'Neural2 voices 0 to 1 million characters'. Over the allowance the price is 'US$4 per 1 million characters' for WaveNet and Standard, 'US$30 per 1 million' for Chirp 3 HD and Studio at 'US$160 per 1 million'. At the common 1,000 characters a minute, 4 million characters is about 66 hours of speech a month.
