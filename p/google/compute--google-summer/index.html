Identityemail a Google account: the program website signs in with one, an account must be registered for the current program each year, and the Program Rules treat submitting project proposals through multiple Google accounts as a ground for removal. A contributor must be eighteen or older upon registration, must be eligible to work in the country of residence for the whole program, and may not be a resident of a United States embargoed country; Russia, Belarus and the DNR/LNR regions are named as ineligible, and a country outside the payment processor's list is not eligible for a payment. A payment-processor account is required before a stipend is paid, with proof of residency and tax documentation. The application asks for the contributor agreement and a project proposal submitted through the program website, so no card is required; the Google account signup asks for a username, a password, a birthday and gender, and adding and verifying a phone number is optional, so no phone number is required to register.
