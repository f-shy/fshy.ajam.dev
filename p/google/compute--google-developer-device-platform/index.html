Free tierNo free tier is published. During public preview the published meters are USD 0.083 per device minute for Device Run on physical devices, USD 0.15 per device minute for Device Streaming on physical devices and USD 0.017 per device minute for emulators; billed per second with a 1 minute minimum. Standard pay-per-use rates from April 2027 are USD 0.20 per device minute, and subscriptions from April 2027 are USD 250 per device slot per month for physical devices and USD 200 per device slot per month for emulators.
unit minutes; metered on wall-clock; The product page labels the rates 'Pay-per-use pricing during public preview'; a separate column publishes 'Pay-per-use standard pricing April 2027' and 'Subscription pricing (starts April 2027)'. No zero-cost allowance is named on the page read.
