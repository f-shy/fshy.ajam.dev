1 GB/day egress (~30 GB/mo) is the practical ceiling. A 5 GB store is refetchable only 6x/month. Download-op cap 50k/day. Spark also caps Cloud Storage at 5 GB-months and 100 GB/month downloaded for the default bucket variant. No billing = hard stop, not overage.

ToolingFirebase SDKs (web/Android/iOS), Admin SDK, gsutil/gcloud storage against the underlying GCS bucket, firebase storage:upload; rclone can hit the bucket with GCS (not rclone's 'firebase'. That is the Realtime DB). Public download URLs via getDownloadURL / Firebase download tokens.
Composes with5 GB per PROJECT. Projects per Google account on Spark are limited to 'a small number of projects (usually around 5-10)'. So ~25-50 GB/account, not a big multiplier. Multiple Google accounts is the only axis. Deleting a project takes 30 days and still counts against quota, which badly hurts churn-and-burn cycling.
ReliabilityHard daily reset at midnight; exceeding it fails requests until reset. Project deletion is 30 days and blocked from quota reuse, so account cycling is slow.
