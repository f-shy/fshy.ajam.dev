Published tiers: USD 500 for a one-liner, USD 2,000 for modest complexity, USD 7,500 for moderately complex, USD 15,000 for complicated high-impact work, with a '2x multiplier' and a '3x multiplier' for secure-by-design memory-safety work until the end of 2026.
