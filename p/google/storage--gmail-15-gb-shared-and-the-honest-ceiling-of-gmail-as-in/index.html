Protocol-level rate limits govern transfer capacity. Gmail IMAP upload is capped at 500 MB per day and IMAP download at 2,500 MB per day across editions. SMTP advertises 250-SIZE 35882577 (34.2 MiB) per message. Effective account write rate is approximately 0.5 GB per day and read rate is approximately 2.5 GB per day. Daily transfer quotas operate as the binding operational limit.
