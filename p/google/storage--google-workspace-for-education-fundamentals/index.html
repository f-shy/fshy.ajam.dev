Limits
  • per-object-cap (binding) You can upload and synchronize files up to 5 TB in size. source
Per-object-cap 5 TB per single upload, and 750 GB per user per 24 h (upload+copy). This is the real throttle, and it is refreshing not cumulative. request-rate: standard Drive API quota (per-project, raiseable). retention: none while the tenant is active; data survives account suspension but suspended users still consume the pool. Item cap 500 million items per user. Over-quota: at +25% or 14 days, uploads and Docs creation stop but download keeps working.

EgressDrive API egress quota: 1 TiB per day per project, not yet enforced. The pages read state no per-GB egress price.
Per-object capYou can upload and synchronize files up to 5 TB in size. (5000000000000 bytes)
RetentionNo automatic deletion is stated. An organization over its storage limit by 25% or for 14 days can't upload new files to Drive, and can still view and download its files.
Interfacerest, rclone rest
Toolingrclone backend 'drive' (Tier 1, server-side copy, shared-drive support); Google Drive API v3; GAM (github.com/GAM-team/GAM) for admin-side bulk operations; gclone for service-account fan-out.
Composes withA customer approved for a Google Workspace for Education domain is approved for a single tenant, and no user should be part of multiple tenants. The license-count and add-on figures of the earlier text are not on the pages read.
ReliabilityStable for a decade, but Google already abolished the predecessor's unlimited tier (2021 archive: 'Existing customers ... will continue to have unlimited storage until early 2022'). Quota is now hard-capped and admin-auditable, so a tenant can be spotted by whoever administers it.
Notes100 TB of pooled cloud storage per institution, at 'No cost'. Pooled across Drive + Gmail + Photos and all shared drives. Confirmed twice on Google's own pages: 'Schools and universities with Google Workspace for Education subscriptions have a baseline of 100 TB of pooled storage shared across all users' and 'Google Workspace for Education Fundamentals / Standard — 100 TB total for all End Users'.
