Identitycard a Google account, then a Cloud Billing account: the Free Tier states that a Cloud Billing account is needed, and the Free Trial signup states that a credit card or other payment method valid for the trial period must be provided. A Cloud Billing account must keep a valid payment method at all times and prepaid cards are not accepted; in many countries a debit card or a bank account is accepted as the primary method instead. The Google account signup asks for a username, a password, a birthday and gender, and adding and verifying a phone number is optional, so no phone number is required to open the account. Express mode of the same platform states that no billing information is needed, but its free tier covers only the model APIs that support express mode, not the agent runtime the allowance is spent on.
