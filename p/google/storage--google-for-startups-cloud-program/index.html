Composes withCredits are per billing account and explicitly non-transferable ('Credits may only be used by the original applicant and cannot be shared, split, or resold'). The multiplier is re-application in later years. Google allows re-applying if credits went unused ('You may still be eligible for credits if you did not use your credits in a previous enrollment'). $200K at coldline-class rates is petabyte-scale for a year; the binding constraint becomes the 12-month expiry, not bytes.
ReliabilityRequires a funded company, a matching domain email and a billing account owned by that domain; credits land in a specific billing account ID and cannot be moved afterwards. Year-over-year re-enrolment is not guaranteed.
