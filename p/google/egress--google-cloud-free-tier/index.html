Bindingvolume-quota 1 GB a month is 33.3 MB a day, 0.09% of 1 TiB a month, so a run at a 1 TiB/month pace exhausts the allowance in about 39 minutes, which makes the byte allowance the first wall by three orders of magnitude; the second wall is the address: 'Static and ephemeral IP addresses in use on standard VM instances - USD 0.005 / 1 hour', which the Free Tier usage table does not list among the free amounts, so the free instance carries a public IPv4 bill of about USD 3.65 a month

Reliabilityno idle reclamation was found; the Free Tier has no end date, so the ceiling is the 1 GB allowance rather than the offer expiring
NotesThe 1 GB figure is stated on the Free Tier page, not inferred, and it is the Compute Engine line rather than the Cloud Storage or Cloud Run line, which are also 1 GB and 100 GB respectively. No reclamation rule for an idle e2-micro appears on the page: unlike Oracle's Always Free, the instance is not taken back for being idle. The address charge is an inference from two documented facts, the USD 0.005 an hour line and the absence of an address line in the Free Tier table.
