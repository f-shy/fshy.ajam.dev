Free tier includes 5 GB of standard storage in select US regions and 100 GB per month of outbound internet egress.
Bindingper-ip-shaping one stream is shaped, and aggregate throughput is the shaped rate times the number of streams
Identitycard Free Trial signup requires a credit card or other payment method, and in some countries bank account verification; the Free Tier limits are calculated per billing account.
Egress'100 GB of outbound data transfer from North America to all region destinations (excluding China and Australia) per month'. Free, recurring monthly, applies to all GCP customers, not a trial.
Interfaces3-compatible, rclone s3
ToolingGCS JSON/XML API, S3-compatible XML endpoint (rclone 'gcs' and 's3' with GCS provider); gsutil/gcloud storage; signed URLs for public reads.
Composes with5 GB per billing account. Billing accounts are tied to a payment instrument, so account multiplication is not free/trivial. The real GCP lever is not this tier. It is the $300/90-day trial and the startup credits (below).
ReliabilityRock solid, no data loss or throttling surprises; the limit is purely quota size.
