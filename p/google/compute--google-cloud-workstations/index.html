The pricing page's worked example bills 198 workstation hours a month at USD 0.32 an hour plus USD 10 for a 100 GB balanced disk and USD 144 a month for one 30-day control plane, a USD 217.36 monthly total for one developer; access to products may be limited during the Free Trial to prevent abuse.
Bindingvolume-quota There is no free workstation allowance, so the new-customer credit is the only cap on spend; after 90 days or USD 300 the management fee and control plane fee meter continuously, with the control plane billed even while no workstation runs.
Identitycard The Free Trial sign-up requires an email or Google account and a credit card or other payment method valid for the trial, with a temporary USD 0 to USD 1 authorization hold; depending on the country a bank account must also be verified, and identity and payment are verified before the trial starts. Cloud Workstations access is then gated by IAM.
EgressInternet data transfer out billed separately at Compute Engine rates: under the Premium Tier default, 0 to 1 GiB per account per month is free, then USD 0.12 per GiB to North America, Europe and Asia excluding Korea and Indonesia, USD 0.19 per GiB to Australia, Indonesia, Korea, South America and Saudi Arabia, and USD 0.23 per GiB to China excluding Hong Kong; inbound transfer is free.

Interfacebrowser, ssh Browser-based IDE, local code editors such as VS Code and JetBrains IDEs, or SSH; access is controlled with Cloud Identity and Access Management rules.
endpoint: https://console.cloud.google.com
ToolingGoogle Cloud console, gcloud CLI and the workstations.googleapis.com API; the workstation cluster and configurations are managed through IAM.
Composes withA managed IDE with a persistent disk and per-second compute can be stopped by an idle timer to cap spend, and its outbound bytes bill at Compute Engine rates, so a data-heavy build should keep transfers inside Google Cloud or budget the egress tier.
ReliabilityThe control plane fee accrues for every hour of a 30-day month regardless of whether a workstation runs; an idle timer stops a workstation but does not stop the cluster fee; Free Trial access to products may be limited to prevent abuse.
NotesCloud Workstations has no Always Free tier; the 90-day USD 300 trial credit is the only no-cost path and needs a card. The lowest published machine, e2-medium at USD 0.08350571 an hour, still incurs the USD 0.20 cluster fee each hour and USD 0.05 per vCPU-hour management fee while running.
