NotesArithmetic: $300 over 90 days is a $100 monthly budget. The first 1 GiB of each month is free, then $0.12 per GiB: $100 buys 1 + 825 GiB = 826 GiB = 0.81 TiB a month. Spent in one month instead, $300 buys 1 free GiB, 1,023 GiB at $0.12 = $122.76, then $177.24 at $0.11 = 1,611 GiB, so 2,635 GiB = 2.57 TiB total. Either way the credit is short of three months at 1 TiB a month, so the credit binds and the 90-day clock does not. The free-trial page also bars GPUs, quota increases and Windows images on a trial account, none of which touch a WireGuard ingress.
