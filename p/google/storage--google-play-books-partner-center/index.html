Free tier2 GB per content file ('Each file must be less than 2 GB. This limit includes cover image files'). An ebook should be at least 4 pages. A supplemental PDF is one per audiobook and must be 'Under 100 MB in size'; an audiobook runs from 5 minutes to 100 hours, and multiple audio parts may be supplied separately or zipped into one file.

Identityany-phone a Google Account, or a new account: the checklist says to sign in using a Google Account or create a new account. Publishing then needs a payment profile whose form asks for a business name, contact name, address and phone number, and all publishers must submit tax information. No card is charged at signup.
