Free tierGoogle publishes two numbers that answer the survival question directly and neither is an interruption rate. Estimated uptime is 'the minimum amount of time that you can expect most of your Spot VMs to run before Compute Engine preempts them', and it is bucketed into exactly three values: '60 minutes (3,600 seconds)', '10 minutes (600 seconds)' and '1 minute (60 seconds)'. Obtainability is a separate score from 0.0 to 1.0 for whether the creation request succeeds at all. The preemption rate from advice.capacityHistory is defined as 'Total preempted Spot VMs in a day' divided by 'Total Spot VMs that stopped running in a day'.
unit hours; metered on wall-clock; none
Pricenone stated
Bindingnone Estimated uptime is bucketed to three values and is a floor for the majority of VMs, so it cannot be converted into a per-hour probability without assuming a distribution.
Identitycard card: 'the Free Trial is USD 300 over 90 days and requires a credit card or other payment method at signup'. Google submits an authorisation request that is 'a hold, not an actual charge', with 'a value between $0.00 and $1.00 USD', typically released within a few days to a month. 'Prepaid cards aren't accepted for payment'; a debit card with a Visa or MasterCard logo is accepted but a debit card that requires two-factor authentication is not, and virtual cards 'may not be accepted'. Spot VMs sit outside the Free Tier - 'The Google Cloud Free Tier credits for Compute Engine don't apply to Spot VMs' - and a non-billable Free trial account cannot add GPUs or request a quota increase.

NotesThe comparability trap is the denominator. A GCP preemption rate of 0.50 does not mean a VM has a 50 per cent chance of preemption in an hour; it means half of the VMs that stopped that day were stopped by preemption rather than by their owner. Published third-party tables that put 'GCP ~2.3 per cent per hour' next to an Azure per-hour eviction probability are mixing two statistics. Also of note: preemption history is unavailable for N1 machine types with GPUs attached, which is exactly the cheap GPU lane, and the whole feature is marked Preview. fails-identity via a05-spot.
