NotesThe agent-sandbox product is documented. The how-to at docs.cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/docs/how-to/agent-sandbox and the concept page at docs.cloud.google.com/kubernetes-engine/docs/concepts/machine-learning/agent-sandbox describe it; the older .../concepts/agent-sandbox path answers not-found and the concept page moved to the machine-learning path. Agent Sandbox adds no charge of its own and GKE pricing applies to the resources created, including the compute the controller uses. The free unit is Google's: the GKE free tier covers one Autopilot or zonal Standard cluster and nothing else, and the Free Trial credit expires and is gated on a credit card.
Evidence notedocumented documented from first-party Google Cloud pages: the Agent Sandbox how-to (no extra charge, kubectl setup), the concept page (the controller is installed in the user's cluster and the user upgrades it), the GKE pricing page (the monthly free-tier cluster credit) and the Free Trial terms (the welcome credit, its expiry and the credit card at signup). Egress is published on none of them, so it is not asserted.
