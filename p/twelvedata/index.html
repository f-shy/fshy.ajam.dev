Free tierBasic plan USD 0/month: 8 API credits per minute with an 800 per day cap, 8 trial WebSocket credits; real-time US equities, forex and crypto
unit credits; metered on request-count; A standard request costs 1 credit per symbol; heavier endpoints weigh more (income_statement is 100 credits per symbol). The daily quota resets to 800 at midnight UTC. Batch requests, technical indicators and a global trial symbol set are on Basic. time_series returns at most 5000 rows per call, and daily intervals hold the complete history since the first trading date for most symbols. The cheapest paid individual plan is Grow at USD 29/month with 55 credits per minute and no daily cap.

Bulk path: no bulk path; batch requests and outputsize=5000 are the widest pulls a free key has.
Bindingrequest-rate Over a full day the 800 per day cap binds before the 8 per minute rate does.
Identityemail The signup form asks for a full name, an email and a password of at least 8 characters, or a Google or Apple sign-in; it has no card field. Card and PayPal payment are named for the paid plans. apikey=demo answered a daily time_series call for AAPL with no signup.
Egressnot metered in bytes; the 800 credits a day bound what a free key can pull
Per-object cap5000 rows per time_series call (the outputsize maximum)
Interfacerest GET https://api.twelvedata.com/time_series with symbol, interval and apikey; a WebSocket on trial credits on Basic. Each response carries api-credits-used and api-credits-left headers, and apikey=demo answers for trial symbols.
endpoint: https://api.twelvedata.com
Toolingcurl or any HTTP client; official SDKs and spreadsheet integration are on every plan
Composes withFull daily history in 5000-row pages at 800 credits a day; it pairs with a real-time feed such as the Finnhub free quotes, or with Massive flat files on its paid tiers for a wide universe.
ReliabilityA call past the minute's credits answers HTTP 429; Basic covers US markets, with other markets only through trial symbols.
NotesCredits are a weight, not a request count: a 1-credit time_series call per symbol is the cheap path, and fundamentals at 100 credits per symbol exhaust a day's 800 in 8 symbols.
Evidence notedocumented The plan, the credit weights and the daily reset are documented on the pricing page and the support articles. A demo-key probe returned a daily series; the 800-per-day cut-off needs a free key to measure.
