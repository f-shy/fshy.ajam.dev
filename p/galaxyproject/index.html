Free tier"On the European Galaxy server every user has a 250 GB quota. This means you can use 250 GB of storage for free." The storage is funded by the de.NBI cloud and the University of Freiburg compute centre, which held 4 PB of user data as of January 2025, and the page states the quota form for extra storage has been discontinued because storage "has become increasingly scarce and expensive".

NotesRecorded separately from Galaxy Main because the operator, the funder and the storage policy differ, and because the withdrawn quota form is a fact that applies only here. Free tier"The main Galaxy site at https://usegalaxy.org [...] has been available since 2007 for anyone to analyze their data free of charge." "Anyone can use the public servers, with or without an account" and registration is "email, password, user name and go". A registered account gets 250 GB of storage and 6 concurrent jobs; an unregistered one gets 5 GB and 1 job. The hardware is not Galaxy's own: most jobs take 1 core and 8 GB on the dedicated Galaxy cluster at TACC, multi-core tools take 6 cores and 30 GB there or 10 cores and 30 GB on Jetstream m1.xlarge, and assembly jobs are routed to PSC Bridges (240 GB to 960 GB of memory, 5 to 20 cores, 24 to 96 hours) or TACC Stampede2 SKX (48 cores, 192 GB, 48 hours).
