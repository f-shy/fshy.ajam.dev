Composes withThe cleanest signalling example: because only SDP crosses the third party, there is no bandwidth quota to hit and no operator who could impose one. Note the caveat that WebRTC needs a TURN relay when both peers are behind symmetric NAT. That is where real relaying cost reappears, and Trystero does not supply it.
ReliabilityNo TURN service included; symmetric-NAT pairs will fail to connect rather than fall back.
