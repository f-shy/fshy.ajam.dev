Free: 2 TB, 50 GB of uploads a month, 1 upload at a time, 100 files per torrent. Community: unlimited storage, 200 GB of uploads a month, 1 upload at a time, 500 files per torrent. Credits: 50 to start, plus 200 once for linking Telegram and joining the channel; torrent add 2, cloud import 1, zip extract 2, copy 10. Leaving the channel returns the account to Free; nothing is deleted and new uploads stop while the account holds more than 2 TB.
Bindingvolume-quota new-upload volume is capped at 200 GB a month on Community (50 GB on Free), so filling a large archive takes years even though stored bytes are unlimited
Identityemail (placeholder) Sign up with Google, GitHub or an e-mail address; no credit card is needed. Unlimited storage also needs a linked Telegram account and membership of the Telegram channel.
EgressNo egress fee on any plan: 'Browsing, streaming, sharing and downloading never cost credits on any plan.' No bandwidth meter is documented.
Per-object capNo per-file size limit for uploads from the browser, saves from a link, torrents or cloud imports; imports from video sites stop at 2 GB a video and files sent through the Telegram bot are limited by Telegram.
Interfacerest rest
ToolingNo API on Free or Community ('API No' in the plan table). Pro is $9 a month ($90 for 12 months) with unlimited storage, 2 TB of uploads a month and the API; Max is $50 a month ($500 for 12 months) with unlimited storage and unlimited monthly uploads. Bulk paths on Free are torrent import and URL or cloud-drive import, both credit-metered.
Composes withCommunity tier provides unlimited cumulative storage with a limit of 200 GB of new uploads per month after joining the vendor Telegram channel.
ReliabilityThe unlimited tier lasts only while the account is in the Telegram channel; leaving returns it to Free (2 TB) and new uploads stop while the account holds more than 2 TB, with nothing deleted. At 200 GB of new uploads a month, 20 TiB needs about 110 months. The API is not on Free or Community. A POST to /createAcc answered HTTP 400 'Verification required. Please verify your identity first.' on 2026-10-21.
NotesFree: 2 TB. Community (join their Telegram channel): storage becomes 'Unlimited, while you are in the channel'. Both have no per-file size limit.
