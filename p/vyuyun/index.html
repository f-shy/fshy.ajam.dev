Bindingper-object-cap 50 GB free with a 500 MB per-file cap, and an email-only sign-up. The REST API and WebDAV are VIP only, so a free account has no programmatic route.
Identityemail the sign-up form asks for an email address ('电子邮箱:'), a nickname, a password and its confirmation, and ends with a slider puzzle to complete; it shows no phone field. Whether the mailbox is verified after submission is unverified: behind a sign-up
EgressUnmetered and unthrottled: '我们对下载速度和流量没有限制'. A per-IP download request limit applies, 10 requests per 10 seconds, and direct links are limited to 1000 requests per hour per account
Per-object cap500 MB per file for a registered member upload ('单文件上传500MB') (500000000 bytes)
RetentionA file or account may be deleted when the account has not logged in for six months ('如您的账号超过六个月未登录本平台时，我们可能会未经您的允许对账号、文件进行删除处理')
Interfacerest, webdav
ToolingThe vendor documents a REST API (token from an appid and appkey) that is open only to VIP accounts, and lists WebDAV under the VIP plan with a 50 MB per-file upload limit. A free account has the web page and the mobile apps. No third-party reverse-engineered client was found: the netdisk-fast-download parser for it is struck through in its list and answered a login-required error to a free account
Composes withThe distinguishing property is the identity cost: '无需手机号，不收验证码，纯免费50G，不限速', and the poster registered with a throwaway address. 50 GB times unbounded email accounts, with nothing automating the signup
ReliabilityA tiny domestic operator with no published SLA. The domain answered HTTP 200 with a 6.6 KB landing page in 2026-10
NotesRecorded despite low confidence because an email-only 50 GB pan with no human-verification step is the rarest thing in this topic. Two facts decide whether it is useful: whether a foreign throwaway address survives registration, and whether any API exists. Neither is established, and the record says so rather than guessing.
