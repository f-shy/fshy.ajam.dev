One number and nothing else. Transfers can be suspended, and no quota, rate or storage ceiling is published.
Bindingper-object-cap At 1 TiB a month the 4 GB ceiling is met on the first large file: a 1 TiB archive is 256 transfers. Nothing else in the published terms could bind earlier.
Identityany-phone Through the bot: a Telegram account, so any phone number. Through the site: 'partes del servicio' work with no account at all, and history or paid plans need registration.
EgressFiles are served by the service to whoever holds the link or password; the terms add 'historial o planes de pago' as account features. No bandwidth figure or price is published.
Per-object cap4 GB per file, from the bot's description. (4294967296 bytes)
RetentionNot given as a number. The terms say transfers are made 'con opciones de caducidad, contraseña' - expiry and password options chosen by the sender - so retention is set per transfer rather than by a site rule.
Interfaceunknown Two front ends: a Telegram bot (@DripFilesBot) and a website with upload, link share and email share. No API is documented on the pages read.
ToolingTelegram client or browser. No API and no WebDAV, so a script would have to drive one of the two front ends.
Composes withA handoff tool rather than capacity: 4 GB per transfer with a sender-set expiry is enough to move one file between two people or two machines, and it removes the need to expose a bucket for a one-off.
ReliabilityA young service with a Spanish-language terms page last updated on the day of this read and a bot that is its own distribution channel: both are signs of one operator with no published uptime. Files expire by the sender's setting, so the loss is predictable.
NotesIncluded because it is a real hosted store-and-forward service and not an account mirror: the file goes into the vendor's storage and comes back as a link.
Evidence notedocumented The bot description and the site's terms were both read and agree that the service stores and re-serves files. The 4 GB figure is reported by the bot only, not measured with an upload.
