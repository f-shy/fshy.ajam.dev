Free tierA free trial of a virtual server of up to 8 CPU, 16 GB RAM and 200 GB of disk, lasting up to three days for an individual and up to five for a legal entity, with the exact period depending on the configuration. The trial is obtained in three steps: register and create the server within those ceilings, fill in the legal entity details if there is one, then open a support ticket stating the purpose of the test, with the final decision on the period or on refusal taken by support staff. The help page was updated on 22 October 2026. The paid catalogue starts at RUB 430 a month for one core at 3.1 GHz, 1 GB RAM and 25 GB SSD, with locations in Russia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, the Netherlands, Turkey, Canada, the United States, Brazil and Uzbekistan, payment by Mir card, SBP, bank transfer or bitcoin, and the Belarus page advertising unlimited free traffic and testing without card binding.
