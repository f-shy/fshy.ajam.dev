The vendor page gives two different host counts in the same document ('65 hostingów' in the H1 and '64 serwisów w jednym koncie' two paragraphs later); the discrepancy is recorded rather than resolved.
Bindingvolume-quota At PLN 0.354/GB (38,99 / 110) 1 TiB of monthly transfer is about PLN 363, or EUR 83 at 4.3695; the balance is what stops the download, and the 1 GB registration credit is 0.1 percent of one day's stated need.
Identityemail Registration asks for a login and an email; the 1 GB credit is granted on signup. No card at registration.
EgressTwo products: a transfer balance ('Najtańszy pakiet transferu 2GB kosztuje tylko 2.99 zł') and premium host accounts bought through the same account. No speed cap is stated.
free allowance: 1 GB at registration

Per-object capnot stated: the unit is a transfer balance or a premium account, not a file size
Retentionnot applicable: a link generator, nothing is stored
Interfaceunknown Web interface plus a plugin family: Stremio add-on, Android app, Amazon Fire TV, Samsung and LG Smart TV, Synology, QNAP, Kodi, FanFilm, Chrome and Firefox extensions, JDownloader2 and pyLoad. No public REST API is documented, though the plugins imply one exists.
ToolingThe widest client list of any Polish service among debrid providers: JDownloader2 and pyLoad plugins, Kodi and Stremio, two NAS packages and three TV platforms.
Composes withThe JDownloader and pyLoad plugins mean a headless box can use it without a browser, which is the one thing the pay-per-GB Poles do better than the €3-10 subscription services that ship no client at all.
ReliabilityPrepaid balance with a 1 GB free start; the vendor advertises a GeoTrust certificate as its security claim, and the host count contradicts itself between the title and the body.
NotesCheapest free start in the Polish set after TwojLimit (1 GB vs 5 GB), and the only one with JDownloader2 and pyLoad plugins. Not a monthly subscription: at 15-30 TB/month the prepaid model costs EUR 1,200-2,500 a month, which is why the Polish services cannot be the base of the stack.
Evidence notedocumented The 1 GB credit, the 2,99 zł entry package and the plugin list are vendor-stated and read. The 110 GB / 38,99 zł package is third-party (debrid.pl's calculator), so the per-TB figure carries that caveat.
