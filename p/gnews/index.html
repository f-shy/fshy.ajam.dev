Free tierFree plan, EUR 0: 100 requests a day, up to 10 articles a request, articles 12 hours late, 30 days of history.
unit requests; metered on request-count; The counter resets at 00:00 UTC and unused requests do not carry over. Article content is truncated to a short excerpt on the Free plan, and pagination stops at 1,000 articles a query. CORS allows only localhost. The next plan up is Essential at EUR 49.99 a month for 1,000 requests a day. A free education plan built on Essential (1,000 requests a day, 25 articles a request, history back to 2020, full content) is granted after a manual review to students, researchers and teaching staff at a recognised institution.

Identityemail The sign-up form asks for an email and a password, or Google sign-in, and the email is verified; the Free plan never asks for payment details. A trial of a paid plan asks for a payment method.

endpoint: https://gnews.io/api/v4/search

ReliabilityOver the daily quota the API answers 403 until 00:00 UTC, and over 1 request a second it answers 429; the 99.95% uptime commitment covers only the Business and Enterprise plans.
