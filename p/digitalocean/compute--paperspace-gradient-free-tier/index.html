NotesThe best free GPU among documented hosts that needs no institution and no card: an M4000 with 8 vCPU and 30 GiB of RAM for six-hour stretches, on 5 GB of persistent storage, with unmetered egress. Three caveats. First, the marketing card says a 12-hour auto-shutdown while the documentation says 6; the documentation is recorded and the conflict is left visible rather than resolved. Second, free machines come from a limited pool and can queue, so availability is the real constraint on a busy day. Third, the free plan forces public projects, so a notebook's contents are visible. Whether the machine exposes a reachable SSH port on its public IP, as opposed to notebook-only access, is not stated on any page read. This record adds the shape and the free-GPU row that paperspace lacks; the two are different records because paperspace prices the C-series hourly and this one prices the free plan.
Evidence notedocumented Machine types, shapes, idle reaps, storage limits, and payment terms are quoted from DigitalOcean Paperspace pricing pages. Direct SSH access over public IP is unverified in published documentation; inbound listening is recorded as unknown.
