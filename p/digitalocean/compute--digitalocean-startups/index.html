Free tier12 months of DigitalOcean credits for accepted startups. Eligibility: must have raised USD 10m or less, must have a company website and an email address on that domain, and must create a registered DigitalOcean team account with a business email. Applicants with no VC, incubator or accelerator apply by selecting Other for the partner field. Participants are generally entitled to a USD 10,000 monthly limit, with charges applying above it.
unit none; metered on wall-clock; one-shot-credit
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • write-gated (binding) A program manager decides the award and the amount stays unnamed until the onboarding email, so an applicant cannot plan against a number; the one-year expiry and the USD 10,000 monthly ceiling bind after that. source
Identityemail The published criteria ask for a company website, a domain email and a registered team account, and provide an explicit route for applicants with no partner organization. No incorporation certificate is named on the program page. The credit amount is set by the partner, not by the applicant.
Egressnot published on the startup program page; the credits pay for ordinary DigitalOcean products, whose Droplet terms are: free outbound transfer included per plan from 500 GiB a month, then $0.01 per GiB; inbound is free
free allowance: 500 GiB a month on the smallest Droplet
price per tb: USD 0.01 per GiB
Interfacerest, cli REST API (HTTP requests) and the doctl CLI, both documented at docs.digitalocean.com/reference
Toolingdoctl, the official DigitalOcean CLI; the REST API reference has request samples in cURL, Python, Go and Ruby
Composes withThe core credits exclude every GPU product, so the value is CPU droplets, Spaces and managed databases: at a USD 10,000 monthly limit a small always-on stack runs for a year. GPU access is a separate application and is not covered, which is where the chain breaks for ML work.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesThe credit amount is genuinely not published: the page says DigitalOcean awards credits through partners and directs the applicant to a program manager. Recording a dollar ceiling here would be invention, so the ceiling is null and the monthly limit of USD 10,000 is what the page states. One directory reported a USD 100K figure from a 2024 press release, which the current page supersedes.
