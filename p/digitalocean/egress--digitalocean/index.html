NotesArithmetic: $200 over $0.01 a GiB is 20,000 GiB = 19.53 TiB in total. Spread across 60 days that is 9.77 TiB a month, which clears the 1 TiB a month test by a factor of 9.8. This is the best dollars-to-bytes ratio of the egress providers compared, and it is the only one where the bandwidth price is two orders of magnitude below the hyperscalers. The 500 GiB a month of included transfer on the cheapest droplet is 0.49 TiB alone and short of the test; DigitalOcean Startups is egress/digitalocean-startups. Note the egress price is documented and the credit is reported, which makes the whole record reported under the weakest-field rule.
