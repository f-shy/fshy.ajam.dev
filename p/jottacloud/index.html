Limits
  • per-ip-shaping (binding) speed throttled above 5 TB, described by HN as a 'harsh inflection' around source
Per-ip-shaping: upload speed throttled above 5 TB, described by HN as a 'harsh inflection' around 16 TB. Quotas are quoted in decimal TB, not TiB. Rclone about reports 9.095 TiB for a 10 TB plan (forum.rclone.org/t/45928), so the real capacity is ~9% smaller than the marketing number.
Bindingper-ip-shaping one stream is shaped, and aggregate throughput is the shaped rate times the number of streams
Identityemail email
EgressNo egress charge. 'No bandwidth limitation' is listed on Home 1 TB, and the Unlimited plan's only stated constraint is upload throttling, not download metering.
Interfacerest, rclone, cli rest
ToolingTier 2 rclone backend (md5, ModTime R/W, case-insensitive, metadata RWU). One of the few Tier-2 providers that is also a real commercial backup service. Duplicati lists Jottacloud as a natively supported provider. Official CLI plus rclone+restic documented by HN commenters.
Composes withThe only candidate here whose free-tier ceiling is 'unlimited' at a flat price, so it is the single account that can absorb a whole 20 TB archive without sharding. At the cost of upload speed past 5 TB. Pairs naturally as the durable tier under rclone crypt.
ReliabilityLong-running, well-documented provider; the throttle is policy, not breakage. The TB-vs-TiB reporting gap is measured, not speculative.
