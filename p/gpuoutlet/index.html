Limits
  • write-gated (binding) reserved capacity only, sold by introduction; there is no on-demand or hourly self-service rental, and every participant is a company source
buying is sales-assisted: a listing becomes a request and the team brokers the deal; some listings are indicative with no published rate
Bindingwrite-gated the gate is the company account and the brokered introduction, not a quota
Identityinstitution 'Every participant is a company, not a person.' Sign-up takes a first name, last name and work email with a 6-digit email code (or Google or GitHub); Buyers pay the marketplace nothing and sellers pay a 2% success fee.
Egressnot applicable: the seller delivers the capacity under its own contract with the buyer
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is reserved GPU capacity, not an object
Retentionnot applicable: the marketplace gives no access to machines
Interfacerest a web dashboard (Radar board and requests) and a partner REST API at api.gpuoutlet.ai for requests, opportunities and introductions; neither gives access to a machine.
Toolinga dashboard with a Radar board of listings by GPU model, region and availability window, plus a partner API; after a seller accepts a request, the two companies contract and pay each other directly.
Composes withnot a route to a machine: the marketplace introduces a buyer company to a seller that holds reserved nodes, and the capacity is reserved for a term, not rented by the hour.
