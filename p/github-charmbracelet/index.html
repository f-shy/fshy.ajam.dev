Pricenone stated
Bindingnone the agent has no quota of its own; the endpoint's quota decides capacity
Identityanonymous no account for the agent, which installs from package managers; Hyper sign-up is 'Sign up with Google, GitHub, or email', and a Shred plan or prepaid credit is billed through Stripe
Egressnot applicable: the chosen endpoint serves the inference
Per-object capnot applicable: a coding agent meters nothing itself
Interfacecli a terminal UI. Headless: crush run "prompt" runs non-interactively, --continue (-C) and --session (-s) resume a session, crush session list --json lists sessions, and --yolo skips every permission prompt
Toolingthe user installs and runs the crush binary (Homebrew, npm, winget, scoop, apt, yum or go install) on a rented or free machine; providers come from the Catwalk model list or a custom OpenAI- or Anthropic-compatible entry, and the HYPER_API_KEY variable selects Hyper
Composes withCharm Hyper is the first-party endpoint at hyper.charm.land/v1 with OpenAI and Anthropic protocols: Shred Diet USD 10 a month buys 180 Hypercredits a week (about USD 9.00 of inference at 5 cents a credit), 100 per five hours and 2 concurrent requests; Shred Classic is USD 20 for 250; prepaid credits start at USD 5. Any key in inference/ works instead
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded; Shred subscriptions on Hyper are not zero-data-retention, while Business plans and prepaid credits are
NotesNot a subscription by itself: the agent is free and source-available, and its first-party plan Charm Hyper is a separate hosted offer with no free tier and a USD 10 entry plan, under USD 20. Hyper models on the probe include prism, kimi-k3, glm-5.3, glm-5.3-flash, deepseek-v4.1-flash, qwen3.8-max, minimax-m3 and gpt-oss-120b.
