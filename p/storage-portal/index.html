Bindingvolume-quota There is no subscription and no rate to hit: the prepaid balance is the only constraint, and at 1 TiB/month it costs $75 (at the 600 GB pack rate). It never expires, so unused traffic is not lost.

RetentionGenerated links are time-limited: 'Сгенерированные ссылки для скачивания действительны до 24 часов' on the home page; the Turbobit page says '~36h direct links'. The purchased traffic balance itself never expires. (1 days)
Interfacewebdav, rest 'Монтируйте через протокол WebDAV для просмотра и управления файлами прямо в файловом менеджере.' Links can be handed to IDM or aria2 ('send it to IDM / aria2'). A REST endpoint is implied by the mounting and the purchase flow; no public API documentation page was read, so the rest token is the weaker of the two.
ToolingBrowser form plus WebDAV mount; generated links work with aria2/IDM because they are plain HTTP with resume.
Composes withThe per-GB balance replaces a monthly debrid subscription: a 600 GB pack at $45 covers 1 TiB of monthly transfer twice over and never expires, so it pairs with a cheap seedbox for the torrent half.
ReliabilityA generated link dies after 24 h and must be regenerated from the balance; the vendor states Russian cards do not work and routes Russian users through WebMoney WMZ/WMT with a RUB bank transfer, which is a payment path, not a download limit.
NotesThe best card-payable price found among debrid providers and the answer to the sector question: a foreigner pays $1-$60 by Visa/Mastercard/Apple Pay, a Russian pays the same packs through WebMoney with a RUB transfer. The RU interface plus the RUB article is evidence of targeting, not of incorporation, and no ownership claim is made here. The about page advertises sister brands, one of which is CoolDebrid.
