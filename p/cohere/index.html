Free tierA trial (evaluation) key is free but limited in usage: 'Trial keys (and prod keys on newer Chat model variants) are limited to 1,000 API calls a month.' Chat is 20 req / min per model on a trial key. Embed runs at 2,000 inputs / min on a trial key, the same as production, and Rerank at 10 req / min. The other trial limits are Audio Transcriptions 5 req / min, Embed (Images) 5 inputs / min, EmbedJob 5 req / min, Tokenize 100 req / min and Parse 500 req / min; anything not listed defaults to 500 req / min. The page does not say whether the 1,000 calls are counted per key or across the account.
