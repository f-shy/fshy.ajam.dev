Residential zones created after July 7, 2026 need a human-reviewed KYC approval, open only to registered companies that verify a corporate email domain, started only after real funds are added, and submitted with a company registration document; ISP and Datacenter proxies and the Web Unlocker API need no KYC. Approved residential traffic is scoped to the KYC use case: requests to domains or categories outside it return an Access denied error. Government websites are blocked on every network. There is no no-KYC residential path for a zone created after July 7, 2026: an Add-a-Zone request from an account that has not completed KYC is blocked with HTTP 403 and the compliance error kyc_required or business_account_required. The immediate (no-KYC) residential access mode, with its 402 bad_endpoint errors, applies only to zones created on or before July 7, 2026, and immediate access to the Residential network is no longer supported effective 2026-10-19. The trust-center KYC FAQ still offers immediate residential proxy access without KYC and links to an immediate-access section that the Residential network access policy no longer has; that FAQ page was last modified on 2025-09-08.
Bindingvolume-quota Residential access is gated by a KYC review open only to registered companies, a gate that no limits[] kind names. Of the kinds, volume-quota binds: residential traffic is billed per GB from the first byte, USD 4.00/GB with code RESIGB50 (USD 8 list) on pay as you go, and no recurring free allowance covers proxy traffic.
Identityinstitution Residential: a registered company that verifies a corporate email domain, funds on the account balance, and a KYC form with the business, the use case and a company registration document, reviewed by a person; the status is updated within 48 hours of completing the process. The compliance team may ask for a government-issued ID of the point of contact, such as a driver's license or passport, and for a brief video call. Accounts on a personal email (Gmail, Outlook, iCloud) cannot be approved. ISP and Datacenter proxies and the Web Unlocker API need no KYC; adding a payment method verifies an account.
EgressRotating residential traffic is metered per GB: $8/GB list, $4/GB with code RESIGB50, then plan volume of $499/month for 141 GB, $999/month for 332 GB and $1,999/month for 798 GB with no rollover. ToolingAny HTTP client that accepts a proxy: curl --proxy brd.superproxy.io:44445 --proxy-user brd-customer-<customer_id>-zone-<zone_name>:<zone_password>, or a SOCKS5 client. Targeting and sessions are options in the proxy username (-country-, -city-, -asn-, -os-, -session-). A session string keeps one peer while it is unchanged and for up to 5 minutes idle, a keep-alive request every 30 seconds holds it longer, and an unavailable peer answers 502 No peers available once before a new peer is assigned. Residential and Web Unlocker traffic needs the Bright Data SSL certificate for end-to-end TLS.
