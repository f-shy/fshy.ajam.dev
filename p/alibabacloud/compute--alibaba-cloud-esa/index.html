NotesTwo vendor statements conflict and both are recorded: the Entrance guide says traffic and requests within the plan are not charged, while the plan comparison table lists the free accelerated traffic allowance as None. The conservative reading is that only the 100,000 daily function requests is a published, unambiguous number, and the traffic term needs a live bill to settle. Possibilities: (a) the Entrance plan is a time-limited promotion; refuted by 'permanently free' on the guide and 'always free' in the developer-drive page. (b) Entrance includes Chinese mainland acceleration; refuted, the developer-drive page says the free plan does not include mainland acceleration by default. The page also warns that the feature set of Entrance may change over time.
