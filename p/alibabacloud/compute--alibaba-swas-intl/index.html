Bindingper-ip-shaping 4 Mbit/s is about 500 KB/s sustained, so the 1 TB quota is roughly what the port can move in a month anyway; the port is the ceiling, not the quota
Identitycard email plus a mobile number from outside the Chinese mainland for security verification; real-name verification is required only for resources located in the Chinese mainland or for a credit line, not for an overseas region. A card is bound at signup and 'The system may place a temporary authorization hold on your bank card (only for verification; no money is deducted)'. The payment FAQ gives the amount and the outcome: 'A USD 1.00 pre-authorization (or local currency equivalent) verifies your card', and that charge is returned, so the card is verified rather than charged. Prepaid and virtual cards fail that check on https://www.alibabacloud.com/help/en/user-center/support/payment-faq: 'Prepaid, gift, virtual, and UnionPay-only cards are not supported.'
