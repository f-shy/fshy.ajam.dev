Free tierOn 2026-10-23 the international free-trial documentation describes product trials as per-product resource quotas and solution trials as points: 'Points are deducted from the actual cloud-resource bills generated by the trial resources, at 1 point : USD 1.' The initial claim is 50 points, with 5 points per additional solution up to 10 solutions, valid for one year, and any single solution trial is capped at 168 hours. A separate promotion offers USD 200 on approval of a business use case.
unit mixed; metered on wall-clock; points at 1 point to USD 1

the product trials are per-product resource quotas and the solution trials are points; the help page states no blanket signup credit
Bindingvolume-quota the 168-hour per-solution cap stops any single long run first, and the 50-point initial claim plus the one-year validity bound the whole lane
Identitycard card at signup; a USD 1.00 pre-authorisation verifies the card; debit accepted if enabled for international payments; prepaid, gift, virtual and UnionPay-only cards refused because they cannot pass pre-authorisation; PayPal also supported; a verified phone number, and one identity may hold one account
Egressinbound traffic is free and only outbound traffic is charged, by bandwidth or by traffic, at a region-dependent unit price (reference example USD 0.123 per GB in China (Hangzhou)); with CDT enabled on pay-by-traffic the free quota is 220 GB of Internet transfer a month (20 GB mainland regions, 200 GB regions outside the Chinese mainland); the CDT tiered overage price is not stated on the pages read
free allowance: 220 GB per month with CDT enabled (200 GB for regions outside the Chinese mainland, 20 GB for mainland regions)

Composes withThe money-denominated lane is now points at 1 point to USD 1, capped at an initial 50 points with one year of validity, so it buys a small trial rather than a season of compute. The earlier 200 GB a month of non-mainland egress is the term that would survive a quiet account if it still exists.

NotesTwo offers sit behind one brand and they are not the same door. The international lane takes a Visa or Mastercard and needs no national document, which is why this record is trial-only rather than fails-identity; the mainland lane at CNY 300 needs Chinese identity verification and is the one that cannot be opened without a PRC identity. The USD 300 figure in credit roundups is stale; the vendor's own community post says USD 90. The free trial page at alibabacloud.com/free is login-gated and served no content to any reader route, so the per-product list could not be read directly. The USD 90 over 3 months came from a localised page that the international help documentation no longer states. The money-denominated lane is now points at 1 point to USD 1 plus a USD 200 application. The payment hold is USD 1.00, on the payment FAQ. The refusal of prepaid, gift and virtual cards is confirmed there too, which is why prepaid counts as refused for this vendor.
