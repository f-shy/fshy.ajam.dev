Free tierA new account gets an independent free quota per model, 'typically 1,000,000 tokens', counted as one combined pool of input plus output, for models in the Singapore region with the International deployment scope only: no other region carries a free quota. The quota is valid 90 days and is not renewed: 'Re-registering a new account does not grant an additional new user free quota', and unused quota is voided at expiry. The grant follows the release list: the 2026-10-29 removal list takes qwen-plus, qwen-flash, qwen-max, qwen3.6-plus and qwen3-8b, among others, out of the grant for new users, while qwen3.8-max, qwen3.8-flash, qwen3.7-plus and deepseek-v4-pro print '1 million tokens' on the price list. A dated snapshot and its undated alias are two separate models with two separate quotas. Separately an OAuth lane carries 'an independent free quota for model inference (2,000 calls per day)' that the console does not display.

Identitycard the account is created with an email address (mobile number optional), and the free trial then needs the completed sign-up: the account help page states 'Activate a free trial' needs a completed sign-up, which requires 'Mobile number, payment method, billing address'. The payment step may place a temporary authorization hold on a bank card, only for verification with no money deducted, and virtual credit cards are not supported; PayPal is a listed payment method. The Model Studio free-quota page requires the account information to be completed before activation and links the step to a login-gated page, so that the same payment method is asked there is unverified: behind a sign-up. Individual identity verification (passport or driver's licence) is a separate stage; whether the free trial requires it is unverified: behind a sign-up. The highest gate is therefore the payment method, not the phone
