Free tier3,000 run-minutes included every month for every organisation, with no monthly fee; beyond that USD 0.0040 a run-minute. A run-minute is one minute of a 2 vCPU Linux x64 machine, so a 4 vCPU job spends two run-minutes a minute and a 1 vCPU job spends half of one.

the meter is weighted by vCPU: 1 vCPU spends 0.5 run-minutes a minute, 4 vCPU spends 2, 8 vCPU spends 4, Linux ARM64 is 0.625x the same size on x64, and Windows is 2.5x
Bindingrequest-rate 3,000 run-minutes is the first thing to fail, and the vCPU weighting makes it smaller than it looks: on a 4 vCPU runner it is 1,500 wall-clock minutes a month, and the card requirement gates the account before the pool is spent.
Identitycard a card: the homepage and the pricing page both say "Card required", and the card is charged for the minutes jobs run
Egressno bandwidth charge is published: the pricing page lists queue wait, cold start, control-plane calls and state reads and writes as not billed, and the homepage says the customer pays only for the minutes jobs run
Per-object capnot applicable: a job is bounded by the runner size, and the meter is weighted by vCPU count rather than capped per job
Retentionnot applicable: a job runs and is destroyed; state that is kept is priced separately at USD 0.50 a GB-month
Interfacegit a GitHub Actions workflow changes its runs-on label to a runners.io label such as runnersio-2vcpu-ubuntu-2404; every job runs in its own fresh virtual machine
Toolinga one-line runs-on change on an existing GitHub Actions workflow; no agent or self-hosted runner is needed
Composes withGitHub Actions on a public repository stays free and comes first; runners.io buys private-repository minutes at USD 0.0040 for 2 vCPU against GitHub list price of USD 0.0060, and the 3,000 included run-minutes cover a small private pipeline, but a card is required before the first job
Reliabilitythe page publishes no job wall clock and no concurrency cap; the meter is weighted by vCPU, so a wider runner spends the pool faster, and a priority label doubles both the rate and the run-minutes
NotesA hosted GitHub Actions runner market that includes 3,000 run-minutes a month for every organisation and meters by vCPU-weighted time rather than by seat. The weighting is the fact a reader must carry: 3,000 run-minutes is 1,500 wall-clock minutes on a 4 vCPU runner and 6,000 on a 1 vCPU one. A card is required before the first job, so this is not a no-card lane.
Evidence notedocumented The included pool, the run-minute definition, the weighting and the card requirement are read from the vendor own pages. Raise by running one job on the included pool and reading the run-minute counter and the first invoice line.
