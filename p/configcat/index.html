unit mixed; metered on request-count; The plan is metered by config JSON downloads and network traffic, not by seats or monthly active users. Unlimited seats, service connections, MAUs, contexts and flag reads on every plan. ConfigCat calls downloads and traffic soft limits and continues serving config past them while contacting the account; the flag, environment and product counts are plan limits.
Bindingvolume-quota The 5 million monthly downloads and 20 GB traffic bind first for a client-side app that refreshes flags on each page view; flag count is capped at 10 but does not grow with traffic.

Retention7-day audit log retention on Forever Free; 35 days on Pro and Smart; 2 years on Enterprise and Dedicated (7 days)
Interfacerest A Public Management API at https://api.configcat.com based on HTTP REST with JSON, with an OpenAPI v3 description and a Swagger UI; it creates, reads, updates and deletes flags, configs, environments and products. The API is not used to evaluate flags: SDKs or the ConfigCat Proxy download config JSON from the CDN and evaluate flags locally.

Composes withServe remotely controlled flags and config to an app on free compute, downloading config JSON from the CDN and evaluating locally to stay under 5 million downloads and 20 GB traffic a month.
ReliabilityDownloads and network traffic are soft limits: ConfigCat continues serving config on a temporary spike and contacts the account on sustained overage. Uptime target is 99% on Forever Free; no SLA on the free plan. Support is best effort through Slack or email.
