NotesThis is the single most valuable structural finding in the DOE stack: ALCF DD is the only tier found anywhere in this survey where the institutional user agreement is explicitly waived, which removes the signature that blocks INCITE and OLCF DD. What remains is an employer field and a DOE foreign-national authorisation (ANL-593) under DOE Order 142.3, which is a process rather than a contract. If a collaborating organisation can be named, ALCF DD is the lowest-friction route to an exascale-class GPU allocation that this survey found. ALCF publishes no node-hour range ('Award size: Small', 'The size of the award varies'), so the 20,000 node-hour figure belongs to OLCF and must not be carried across. The AI Testbed accelerators (Groq, Graphcore, Cerebras, SambaNova) are reached through the same rolling form and are the only route found to non-NVIDIA accelerator classes.
