A compute-minute is one started minute of a Small (1 vCPU, 1 GB) sandbox; Medium and Large consume 2 and 4 compute-minutes a minute. Guests have offline networking, no arbitrary images, no snapshots, no public ports and no inherited secrets. Current capacity is serial and queued, not reserved concurrency.
Bindingwrite-gated A new account cannot run a job at all until prepaid credit is added; the USD 10 minimum top-up is the first gate a user meets, ahead of the per-minute charge.
Identityemail the docs say to create the Sandbox account with an email address, and no existing AGNT account or app subscription is required; an agent can sign up headlessly with no email or browser, receiving a secret shown once, and fund the account through a human claim link or x402 in USDC on Base. Prepaid execution needs no card; the docs say there are no automatic card charges.
Egressnetworking is offline only inside the VM, and monthly artifact downloads are 1 GB on PAYG or Starter, 5 GB on Pro and 15 GB on Business
free allowance: 1 GB of artifact downloads a month on PAYG and Starter
Per-object capnot an object-storage offer; job inputs are up to 8 root filenames combined 8 MB and each artifact export is at most 8 MB
Retentionexported artifacts remain after the VM is destroyed for 1 day on pay-as-you-go or Starter, 7 days on Pro and 30 days on Business; the VM itself is destroyed at job teardown (1 days)
Interfacerest an HTTP API at https://sandbox.agnt.gg/sandbox/v1 with /jobs, /jobs/quote, /artifacts and /agents endpoints, an OpenAPI document, and JavaScript and Python job helpers; agents can sign up and fund themselves over HTTP
endpoint: https://agnt.gg
Toolingan HTTP client and a Bearer sandbox_ key; headless agent signup returns a secret, and funding is by a human claim link or x402 with USDC on Base
Composes witha bounded Python, Node or shell job runs in an isolated Kata-backed VM and exports selected files through the API, with a spend cap on each run; the offline guest has no inherited credentials, so outside data must be passed in as inputs.
ReliabilityGuests run offline with no network access and no inherited credentials. Capacity is serial and queued rather than reserved concurrency; a busy or absent worker rejects new admission. Customer-code failures and timeouts consume runtime; confirmed lost-worker failures on managed jobs are refunded.
NotesThe operator's provider list prices AGNT Sandbox at USD 6.00/h billed per minute; the vendor's published sizes are Small at 6 US cents a minute (USD 3.60/h), Medium 2 vCPU/2 GB at 12 cents a minute (USD 7.20/h) and Large 4 vCPU/4 GB at 24 cents a minute (USD 14.40/h). No free tier is published: the monthly Starter plan costs USD 5 and bundles 100 compute-minutes. The 2 vCPU/4 GB size in the list is not a published size.
