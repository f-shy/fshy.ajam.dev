Offline transfer (a URL source rather than a cloud endpoint) is included on the free tier, which is the feature that overlaps the debrid topic.
Bindingvolume-quota 5 GB a month free fails at 1 TiB by a factor of 200. The unlimited annual plan at $119 removes the byte counter, and the remaining limit is the provider list rather than a number.
Identityemail A MultCloud account, then OAuth authorisation of each connected cloud. Payment by card, bank/wire transfer, check or invoice through CleverBridge.
EgressTransfer is metered by volume through the service: free 5 GB/month, then 100 GB/month at $19.99. 'Offline transfer/sync/backup' is included even on free.

Retentionnot applicable: MultCloud moves bytes between accounts the user already owns and keeps no copy
Interfacerest, webdav A web console over 30+ providers, and it accepts WebDAV and FTP as endpoints among them. The 30+ list on the page includes Google Drive, Google Photos, Google Workspace, Dropbox, OneDrive, MEGA, Amazon S3, Box, pCloud, MediaFire, Putio, Backblaze, Yandex, Baidu, NAS, MySQL and Egnyte.
ToolingBrowser console plus scheduled sync jobs; no rclone backend and no public API was read. rclone covers the same ground for free with a local binary, which is the comparison this record exists to make.
Composes withThe only service in tested services that moves bytes between two clouds not administered here. The 'Putio' endpoint entry is the debrid-adjacent one: it can pull from a put.io account into another drive without a local machine in the path.
ReliabilityTransfers run inside MultCloud's session, so each connected cloud's own rate limits and re-authorisation requirements break jobs silently; a revoked OAuth token stops a scheduled sync without an error the user sees.
NotesAt $19.99 per 100 GB the metered plan is one of the most expensive per byte in tested services; the $119 annual unlimited plan is the only sensible tier and it is still above a debrid subscription. rclone does the same job for nothing on a rented box, which is why this record is a comparison rather than a recommendation.
