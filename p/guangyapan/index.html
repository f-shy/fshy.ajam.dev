Bindingper-ip-shaping 2 TB of capacity is the largest free allocation in this survey after Ctfile, and 2 GB a day at high speed is. The traffic cap is what decides the plan, and as a cold archive it never binds.
Identityany-phone SMS only. POST /v1/auth/verification returns 400 'Email or phone_number must be set' when empty, and every email-shaped path returns 404, and the driver's own code only ever sends phone_number. So there is no email-only path
Egress2 GB a day of high-speed traffic on the free tier, then throttled to an undisclosed low speed. Members get 1 TB a month down and 1 TB a month up, and self-download, online playback and third-party mount all count against the same terabyte, with no overage package
free allowance: 2 GB per day at high speed
Per-object cap100 GB per file on the free tier, 1 TB for members, with a 1,000,000-file batch transfer (100000000000 bytes)
RetentionNo stated expiry for stored files; a 30-day recycle bin
Interfacerest, webdav
Toolinga merged OpenList driver, guangyapan, which gives WebDAV and 302 direct links once mounted; two-stage SMS login needing a client id, an X-Captcha-Token and an X-Device-Sign; CloudDrive2 v1.0.11 or later reads it
Composes withProvides 2 TB of free storage per account with OpenList driver integration, WebDAV support, and TV streaming clients. For active content distribution, the 2 GB daily bandwidth limit is prohibitive. For cold archival storage, the daily limit does not impede initial single-pass uploads.
ReliabilityAuth is SMS-only, so an account with only an email address cannot register. The OpenList docs carry an explicit danger block: use for multi-person sharing, image hosting, software hosting or video external links causes the provider to close accounts, which is the exact workload this survey targets. Xunlei's two previous netdisks both died, and the price rose from CNY 108 to 188 within months of launch
NotesThe two numbers the marketing merges are capacity and traffic, and they are ten thousand times apart in practical effect. The SMS-only registration is what closes it.
