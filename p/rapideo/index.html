The package ceiling is the product itself; there is no separate fair-use paragraph and no concurrency figure on the pricing page.
Bindingvolume-quota 600 GB in 30 days is 0.6 TB against 1 TiB of monthly transfer, so the package stops at 60 percent of the requirement; two consecutive packages would be needed and the site does not say whether they queue.
Identityemail Registration form on the home page asks for a login, an email and a password, and a terms checkbox. No card at registration; the 1 GB credit is granted on signup.
EgressTime and volume sold together: a package is a period with a ceiling ('30 days up to 600 GB', '360 days up to 7200 GB'). Traffic packages are sold separately at 31 groszy per GB.

ToolingBrowser, Smart TV apps and a host-list generator page; no JDownloader plugin or CLI is named on the reachable pages.
Composes withA 30-day 600 GB package covers a single heavy week and then stops, so it composes with a monthly host subscription only as an overflow: a Polish package buys one burst and the always-on account stays elsewhere.
ReliabilityOperating since 2009 and reporting 818,728 users, with a Trustpilot profile linked from the home page; the volume ceiling is what stops a heavy month, not speed.
NotesThe Polish market is priced per gigabyte, not per month, and Rapideo is the only one of the five that also sells a timed package. At 1 TiB/month the 30-day ceiling falls short by 40 percent; the 360-day package reaches EUR 10.69/TB, which is under HIGHWAY's EUR 16/TB and over AllDebrid's effective zero. Two currencies in one record: the vendor quotes EUR on the Polish page, so no conversion is needed.
