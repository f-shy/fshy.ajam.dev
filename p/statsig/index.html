Free tierDeveloper tier, free: 2 million metered events every calendar month at no charge, with free access to feature gates, dynamic configs, experimentation and core product analytics, and 1 year of analytics data retention; past 2 million events gate and config targeting keeps working and metric lift computation stops
unit requests; metered on request-count; The unit is the metered event: an SDK call that logs an event, an exposure check with metric lift enabled, an ingested metric or a custom metric. The vocabulary has no events term, so requests is the nearest. An account starts on the Developer tier at sign-up; the Pro tier is USD 150 a month with 5 million metered events.
