per-object-cap: 100 MB per attachment via Messenger, no limit on the number of attachments; an incoming email larger than 20 MB including attachments is rejected. Stored attachment files are retained indefinitely by default. Request-rate limits are not stated on this page.
Identityemail The 14-day free trial signup asks for a company email address and needs no credit card. Account fields beyond the email are unverified: behind a sign-up.
EgressAttachments are served through signed URLs: 30 minutes for Messenger and chat uploads, around 6 months for email attachments. Expiry does not delete the file; a fresh signed URL is generated when the conversation page loads. The page states no price or bandwidth limit for downloads.
Per-object capper-object-cap: 100 MB (100000000 bytes)
RetentionBy default, Intercom retains the stored attachment file indefinitely. Expiry of a signed URL does not delete the file; deleting an attachment or its message revokes access immediately but may not purge the stored file.
Interfacerest rest
ToolingREST API GET call on an attachment returns a fresh signed URL; the endpoints /articles and /files and official SDKs are not named on the attachments help page.
Composes withThe pricing page offers a 14-day cardless free trial and paid helpdesk plans. The attachments help page states a 100 MB per-attachment limit and no limit on the number of attachments.
ReliabilityImportant control: the CDN signature is NOT verified. Fetching the same object with the signature stripped and the filename changed still returned 200 with the identical 498,804 bytes. So Intercom CDN URLs are effectively unauthenticated and permanent, which is a reusable pattern (and means 'signed' URLs elsewhere may also be decorative).
