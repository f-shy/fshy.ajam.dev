Free tierFree plan, expires monthly: 200 EPDs (executions per day), 20-minute minimum interval, 5-second timeout, 10 logs & predictions
unit requests; metered on request-count; An EPD counts a job's executions on its busiest day (a job every 10 minutes occupies 144), so the free plan holds 2 jobs every 20 minutes, 8 hourly jobs or 200 daily jobs. The free plan's expiry is 1 month after the last renewal, and renewing takes a login to the plan page. Email notification, failure-log retention, custom HTTP and webhook notification are not on the free plan. A new account starts on a 7-day Individual Trial.

Each execution times out after 5 seconds on the free plan. The free plan expires 1 month after the last renewal and its cron jobs are disabled on expiry; renewal is a login to the plan page.
Bindingrequest-rate The 200-executions-a-day budget at a 20-minute minimum interval binds before the API quota.
Identityemail The Create Account for Free button posts only a session id and a timezone to /user/tryit and opens the account with no form; logging in again takes an email address and a password, which the account adds later, and keeping the free plan takes a login every month. The free plan needs no payment to register.
Egressnot applicable: an outbound HTTP caller; the free plan card lists Receive 100 MB

Retention10 logs & predictions on the free plan; execution logs kept 6 months at most
Interfacerest EasyCron API v1 at api.easycron.com, with the API key in an X-API-Key header; it creates, reads, updates and deletes cron jobs and groups and returns execution logs. An older token-in-query API at www.easycron.com/rest also answers.
endpoint: https://api.easycron.com/v1/cron-jobs

Composes withCalls a free HTTP endpoint at most every 20 minutes on the free plan with a 5-second timeout, so it suits short trigger calls into a serverless function, not long-running jobs.
ReliabilityFree accounts can be stopped by the vendor at any time; an unrenewed free plan disables its jobs after a month.
