5TB free for each PI on one project (UT System institutions), $60/TB/year beyond; a hard cap of 200,000 files per allocated terabyte. Allocations last one year. S3 access needs an allocation of at least 25TB.
Bindingvolume-quota the free allocation is 5TB for each PI and every allocation carries a cap of 200,000 files per allocated terabyte; beyond 5TB the price is $60/TB/year
Identityinstitution PI status needs a faculty, staff or postdoctoral position at a US based institution; users from non-US institutions are not eligible for PI status but may be invited to a project by a US based PI
EgressNot metered in the doc, but there is no public HTTP download path. Corral is a mounted/POSIX store, so serving is not its purpose.
Per-object capNo limitation on the size of files; by default 1 million files per 5TB allocated (200,000 files per allocated terabyte).
RetentionFiles are never purged by automated processes; each allocation lasts one year and must be renewed. After expiry data is typically retained for 6 months, but may be deleted at any time at the discretion of the system administrators unless an allocation request is pending.
Interfacesftp, scp, rsync, s3-compatible sftp
Toolingscp and SFTP (Cyberduck documented) to data.tacc.utexas.edu; an S3 version 4 compatible endpoint is available only by request for allocations of at least 25TB, not for protected data; TACC allocation portal. rsync is not named on the page.
Composes with5 TB free × UT System PIs, then linear $60/TB/yr. The file-count rule is the real limiter: 'There is also a limit of 200,000 files per allocated terabyte'. At 5 TB that is 1 million files.
ReliabilityThe free 5 TB applies to UT System institutions only, and PI status needs a US based institution; storage beyond 5 TB costs $60/TB/year. Each allocation lasts one year and must be renewed, so this is a 5 TB niche tier, not a free archive.
