Bindingvolume-quota 6 GB of storage fails first: 1 TiB of monthly transfer cannot be stored on a free account at all, and the 3-task/day count fails alongside it.
Identityemail PikPak registration is by email: 'An email address for registration and verification... No credit card is needed to start the basic free plan.' The bot then needs the account linked.
EgressDownloading out of PikPak is metered per account: 'Free PikPak accounts have a daily Downstream limit of 20 GB, which resets at 0:00 Singapore Time. All Connected Apps together can use up to 25% of this daily limit, or 5 GB per day.' Upgrading to Premium removes that limit.
free allowance: 20 GB/day downstream on a free account
Per-object capNo per-object cap is stated for the bot. The binding free limits are the 6 GB account store and 'three Cloud Download tasks per day'.
RetentionFiles stay in the PikPak account until deleted or until the account lapses; a free account has 6 GB, so retention of a large library is a paid feature.
Interfacerest A Telegram chat that acts as a remote control for a PikPak account. The file moves Telegram -> PikPak, so Telegram is the source and PikPak the store.
ToolingAny Telegram client. Forwarding a channel post or sending a magnet/HTTP/Twitter/TikTok link is the whole interface.
Composes withSweeps files and magnets out of Telegram into a 6 GB free cloud store with a 20 GB/day egress meter; the reverse of a Telegram-as-drive path, and the only hosted bot found that moves bytes out of Telegram at scale.
ReliabilityFree accounts are metered on both storage and downstream; reaching either daily or monthly connected-app limit slows traffic to about 100 KB/s rather than failing it.
NotesAbandon if the 6 GB/3-task free tier changes. The 10 TB in the bot description is marketing for the paid tier and must not be read as a free number.
