unit none; metered on none; Premium is EUR 1.50 per month and is bought for 1 to 5 years. The 4-month Discovery offer, chosen at signup or later in the account, is a single payment of EUR 6 that gives the Premium plan for 4 months and keeps the address for life; it is paid, not free. The features table lists customized domain names under Premium. Family is EUR 3 per month for up to 5 Premium accounts in a Family space, and Pro Start EUR 3.60 per month with tax for 2 accounts sharing 50 GB. Declaring an external domain is free; a domain bought at Mailo starts at EUR 15 per year with tax.
