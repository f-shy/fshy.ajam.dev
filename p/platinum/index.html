Free tierA new org starts at $0. Add credits before you create a sandbox. A promo code adds credit too.
unit credits; metered on wall-clock; No standing free tier is published: credit must be added before a sandbox can be created. Promo credit lasts as long as its campaign says and is spent first; a search snippet of the vendor homepage advertises USD 10 free for new accounts, but that phrase is absent from the homepage reads of 2026-11-25 and from the billing docs, so the amount is unverified. The operator list's $0.108/h is the 2 vCPU / 4 GiB rate: $0.0324/vCPU-hour plus $0.0108/GiB-hour. Self-hosted control plane is documented separately and is not the hosted offer priced here.

Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) A new org starts at USD 0 balance; credit packs are USD 10, 50 or 200, or USD 5 to 1,000 custom; running sandboxes get a 60-minute grace period at a zero balance and are then auto-stopped, never deleted source
  • request-rate Free tier: 2 vCPU, 2 GiB RAM, 10 GB disk per sandbox and 4 vCPU, 8 GiB, 40 GB org total running at once; tiers rise with a verified email and $5 paid, a saved card and $25 paid, and so on source
Rates per second while running: $0.0324 per vCPU-hour, $0.0108 per GiB RAM-hour, $0.000072 per GB disk-hour ($0.0526 per GB-month); a stopped sandbox bills only disk and snapshots. GPU attachments are free.
Bindingvolume-quota Prepaid balance is what stops new sandboxes first: at a zero balance every new create answers 402 insufficient_credits, before the free org's 4-vCPU concurrency cap is reached.
Identityemail An account is created at https://www.platinum.dev/dashboard#signup and an API key minted there. The billing page says credits are bought through a Stripe-hosted checkout; a card is stated only for auto top-up ('your saved card') and for tier t2 ('a verified email, a saved card and $25 paid'). Nothing on the pages read says a card is required to open an org or to buy the first credits, so no card requirement is asserted for entry. The sign-up page itself is behind the dashboard and was not read.
EgressEgress is the data a sandbox sends to public internet addresses and is priced at $0.04 per GiB; inbound data, preview URL traffic, sandbox-to-sandbox traffic and volume traffic do not count.
price per tb: USD 0.04 per GiB (USD 40.96 per TiB, derived: 1024 GiB x USD 0.04)
Per-object capnot applicable: the metered unit is a running microVM, not a stored object; the free tier caps a sandbox at 2 vCPU, 2 GiB RAM and 10 GB disk
RetentionData is not deleted when a balance runs out: a stopped sandbox keeps its disk and is archived after 7 days by default at a lower disk rate, and snapshots and volumes keep billing. (7 days)
Interfacerest, cli, ssh, browser REST API at api.platinum.dev/v1, TypeScript and Python SDKs, an MCP server, a pt CLI, a dashboard, and terminal/SSH into a sandbox. Each sandbox is a Cloud Hypervisor microVM on KVM.
endpoint: https://api.platinum.dev/v1
ToolingThe user installs the pt CLI or an SDK and passes a scoped API key; a self-hosted control plane is documented separately and is not the hosted offer priced here.
Composes withPrepaid metered microVMs can run agent code for a short task and stop to a disk-only bill between calls, which pairs with a separate object-storage record for longer-lived artifacts.
ReliabilityAt a zero balance new compute answers 402 and running sandboxes stop after a 60-minute grace period; data is never deleted for lack of credit, though a user-configured auto_delete_days timer still applies.
NotesPlatinum sells hardware-isolated Cloud Hypervisor microVM sandboxes for AI agents, billed per second while running. The unbundled rate is USD 0.0324 per vCPU-hour plus USD 0.0108 per GiB-hour of RAM, USD 0.108 an hour for 2 vCPU and 4 GiB; egress is USD 0.04 per GiB, paid from prepaid credit. A new org starts at a USD 0 balance and must add credit before it can create a sandbox, and its free shape is capped at 2 vCPU, 2 GiB and 10 GB per sandbox with 4 vCPU running at once, so credit and the free cap bind first. The home page advertises USD 10 free credit, but the sign-up form is behind a login wall and was not read; the exact credit and identity gate are unverified. An anonymous OAuth client registration grants no sandbox capacity. A self-hosted deployment of the control plane is documented separately and comes last behind this hosted offer.
