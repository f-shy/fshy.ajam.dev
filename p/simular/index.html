One cloud Linux desktop per account. The box sleeps when idle except on the M plan; a plan's own box is not charged by the hour.
Bindingvolume-quota The 5,000 credits expire after 90 days and are the first limit reached; at 200 credits an hour they buy 25 awake hours before the $19 or $39 plan or a top-up is needed.
Identityemail Sign-in is through a Simular account with Google or GitHub OAuth; the MCP connection opens a browser page where the person approves which boxes it may use and for how long. A personal bearer key from box.simular.ai/access works for clients that cannot sign in. No card is asked for on the free tier.
EgressA residential IP add-on costs 1,000 credits per 100 MB on paid plans; no general egress allowance or per-GB rate for ordinary traffic is published.
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is one cloud Ubuntu desktop with a persistent shell, not a stored object
RetentionA box asleep 14 consecutive days is deleted with its files and saves, 30 days if the account has paid for box or Sai, and never while it has an S or M plan; any wake resets the count. (14 days)
Interfacerest, browser One hosted MCP server at https://mcp.box.simular.ai/mcp (Streamable HTTP, OAuth sign-in), plus a live screen at box.simular.ai/box and a personal key for clients that cannot sign in.
endpoint: https://mcp.box.simular.ai/mcp
ToolingAdd the remote MCP URL to Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, OpenCode or VS Code, or run npx @simular-ai/box@1.2.2 connect on the user's own computer.
Composes withA 4 vCPU / 8 GB box at 200 credits an hour is $0.20 an hour, or $0.10 an hour at half duty; its persistent desktop keeps browser logins between agent sessions and hands files to other records.
ReliabilitySystem images may be reconfigured, so installed packages can need reinstalling; an idle box is deleted after 14 days of sleep and a box's small model calls are billed separately from its awake time.
NotesThe hosted computer is a persistent Ubuntu desktop that sleeps when idle and wakes on connect; 5,000 credits are $5 and buy 25 awake hours at the published 200 credits an hour. The $19 S and $39 M plans include a box and monthly credits; extra boxes are metered. A separate self-hosted CLI configures the user's own AI apps and does not supply the box, so it is listed after this hosted offer.
