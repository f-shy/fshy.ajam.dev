The upstream input cost is published by a Brazilian host: BRFiles sells generator traffic at 'R$ 180,00 por cada TB de trafego' (brfiles.com/geradores-de-links/). At that cost R$ 39.90 of monthly revenue buys 0.222 TB, so a plan sold as unlimited at this price has an undisclosed ceiling somewhere below 250 GB/month. This is arithmetic on a third party's price list, not a statement by Links Premium.
Bindingvolume-quota At 1 TiB/month the operator must buy roughly R$ 180 of host traffic against R$ 39.90 of revenue, so the plan cannot be honoured at that volume; the first thing to fail is a volume ceiling the page does not name.
Identityemail Registration at /sistema/index.php/register creates the trial account. The page does not say whether a card is required to start the trial.
EgressPaid plans list 'Gerar links: Ilimitado' (unlimited link generation) and no byte allowance, no per-GB price and no speed cap. The trial is the only plan with a published number (300 MB per link).
free allowance: 300 MB per generated link on the 7-day trial
Per-object capnot applicable: the file stays on the third-party host, so the host's own per-file cap applies. The trial caps a generated link at 300 MB.
Retentionnot applicable: no files are stored. A generated link is time-limited and must be regenerated after it expires; the lifetime is not published.
Interfaceunknown Browser panel at linkspremium.com.br/sistema; registration and generation are web-only. No API is named on the pricing or affiliate page.
ToolingBrowser plus the site's own panel. The output is a direct HTTP link, which JDownloader, aria2 or a browser can take.
Composes withOne plan covers +45 hosts including 1fichier and Mega, the two hosts the archive actually sits on, so it substitutes for two separate host subscriptions at the price of one.
ReliabilityA single-operator Brazilian reseller storefront with recurring billing; a 365-day prepaid term depends on the operator staying up. The comparable Brazilian generator Libera Premium closed in 2026 (see liberapremium.json).
Noteslinkspremium.postador.com.br serves the identical page and prices, so the two hostnames are one service. The affiliate page is the site's front page, which is a small signal about how the business acquires users: commission is paid on renewals, and the affiliate does not need a plan.
