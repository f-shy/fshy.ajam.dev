Free tierA 24-hour trial of the entry plan, Ayoo VPS I (2 vCPU, 4 GB RAM, 50 GB NVMe), on request: order the plan without paying the invoice, open a ticket or message support, and the machine is activated if an IPv4 block is free; it is suspended after 24 hours and deleted a day later unless paid.

  • volume-quota no traffic quota: 'Transf. Mensal: Ilimitada' on every plan, bounded only by the terms' rule against network abuse source
The 24-hour window is a time allowance rather than a byte allowance; no limit kind names time, so it is carried in the free-tier unit (hours) and in the session cap.
Bindingnone Nothing in the allowance binds, because transfer is unlimited and there is no traffic counter; the CPF or CNPJ demanded at registration is what stops a registrant without a Brazilian tax number.
Identitynational-id The registration form marks a CPF or CNPJ as required ('CNPJ/CPF*', 'Digite o CNPJ para pessoa Jurídica, CPF para pessoa física'), alongside email, a phone number and a billing address; card payments may prompt a document request. Payment is Pix, boleto or credit or debit card.
EgressTransfer is advertised as unlimited on every plan ('Transf. Mensal: Ilimitada', 'Transferência ilimitada'); the terms require the customer to respect technical limits and avoid abuse of processing, I/O and network, and state no number.
free allowance: unlimited
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is a virtual machine, not a stored object
RetentionA trial machine is suspended after 24 hours and deleted one day later unless the invoice is paid; a paid machine persists while the monthly invoice is paid. The terms place backups and data recovery on the customer.
Interfacessh root SSH on the Linux images and RDP on the Windows images; the AyooPanel web console handles power, reinstall, password, backup and VNC; no API is documented on the vendor pages
Toolingany OpenSSH client for a Linux image, an RDP client for Windows, and the AyooPanel console; no provider CLI, SDK or documented API
Composes withA 24-hour root machine in Canada with a dedicated IPv4 and unlimited transfer covers a job that needs a full OS and an inbound port for one day; anything longer starts at BRL 49.90 a month, so the offer is a route to Brazilian payment rails and a Canadian address rather than a cheap standing host.
ReliabilityThe 24-hour trial is granted by ticket and queued on IPv4 supply, so activation can wait; the terms disclaim data recovery and put backups on the customer; the service is run by a single Brazilian company, CNPJ 69.734.466/0001-17.
NotesThe standing price is BRL 49.90 a month at the panel's own store, with BRL 39.90 for the first three months under the coupon AYOO20; the English storefront prices the same entry plan at USD 14.00 a month and lists different shapes for plans II to VI, so the two storefronts disagree and the panel is the order point. The France batch (BRL 109.52 for 6 vCPU, 8 GB and 120 GB) is a limited-stock promotion, not a standing tier. The record is abandoned if the trial is withdrawn or the entry price leaves the panel store.
