Free tierNot published as a number. DICE is a funding instrument, not a product. It buys you 'personal drive storage' up to long-term preservation, priced by negotiation with whoever provides it. The EC put over EUR 6 million into Virtual Access to make it free for new users. What that buys in TB is determined per request.

ToolingWhatever the chosen provider offers; services are ordered through the EOSC Portal (marketplace.eosc-portal.eu) by selecting a service and submitting an order flagged as a DICE request. No uniform API.
Composes withBasic requests ('personal workspace') are granted same-day on registration alone; Standard (2–10 working days) and Customised (negotiated) tiers scale into archives. The multiplier is service requests, each of which is separately approved.
ReliabilityThe page still advertises the call but dates itself to a funding period that ended in 2023. Treat as a lead to re-check with the EUDAT helpdesk, not as an available quota.
