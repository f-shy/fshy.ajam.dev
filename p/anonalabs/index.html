NotesA hosted KVM microVM sandbox with an E2B-compatible API. The Free plan gives USD 25 of usage once with no card taken during the preview, 5 concurrent running sandboxes and a 1-hour timeout, and public internet egress is off; paid plans start at USD 20 a month with USD 20 of usage included. A 2 vCPU and 4 GiB sandbox is USD 0.284 per hour busy and about USD 0.048 per hour for a typical agent, and egress is USD 0.12 per GB. The public sign-in page shows an email field and email sign-in link, so the identity kind is email; a new account is by invitation and the waitlist.
