More than 57 hosting services are bundled ('57 kont w cenie jednego'). Time packs grant a daily transfer of 15 GB for the purchased period; transfer packs never expire. No per-host daily cap, concurrency limit or fair-use paragraph is published.
Bindingvolume-quota At the reported PLN 0.32/GB 1 TiB of monthly transfer is about PLN 327 (EUR 75); the balance runs out long before any rate or shaping limit could apply.
Identityemail The signup panel asks for login, email and password with a terms checkbox, and the login form is behind a Cloudflare Turnstile widget. Registration is free ('Rejestracja i posiadanie konta jest (i zawsze będzie) bezpłatne') and no card is asked at registration; the free transfer is credited after the confirmation link of the registration e-mail is clicked.
Egress'Nie ma żadnych ukrytych opłat' and 'Nie musisz kupować drogich kont premium'; transfer packs never expire ('Transfer nie wygasa') and the time packs are listed 'Bez limitu prędkości' (no speed limit).
