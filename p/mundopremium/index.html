The seller's own constraint is delivery time: 'Prazo Entrega: Alguns minutos, no maximo 12 horas, apos a confirmacao do pagamento'. A voucher bought in a hurry can arrive after the need.
Bindingvolume-quota The voucher changes nothing about the host's limits; at 1 TiB/month the BRFiles 2 TB allowance holds, at 2 TiB it does not.
Identityemail The buyer's own email, login and password are used at the host: 'O periodo premium e resgatado em seu e-mail, login e senha pessoal, voce pode alterar estes dados sempre que desejar.' That sentence is the difference between this product and a resold credential.
EgressNo egress terms of its own: the voucher activates the host's premium, which carries 'Baixe ate 2 TB por mes' at BRFiles.
free allowance: 2 TB per month, from BRFiles
Per-object capnot applicable: the voucher activates BRFiles premium, so BRFiles' own 8 GB per-file cap applies.
RetentionThe voucher buys a period of BRFiles premium; the seller prices 30 days up to a year inside 'R$ 29,90 atraves R$ 249,90'. Files then follow BRFiles' own no-expiry rule while premium is active.
Interfaceunknown The deliverable is a text voucher code, not an account: 'voce ira receber um Voucher (codigo) com este codigo acessara o site BRFiles.com e criara uma nova conta ou renovara uma conta ja existente'. The code is redeemed in the host's own panel.
ToolingNothing to install. The buyer redeems the code at brfiles.com/planos/ and keeps using the host's own site and download managers.
ReliabilityVoucher delivery is manual and can take up to 12 hours. A voucher that fails to redeem is a support ticket, not a lockout, because the account remains the buyer's.
NotesMundo Premium is the counter-example that makes the debrid main distinction usable. Same product category as the R$ 24.90 Real-Debrid listings - a cheap way to hold premium - but delivered as a voucher into an account the buyer controls. It also sells TakeFile at R$ 289.90 to R$ 1,069.90, so the catalogue covers hosts the multi-hosts do not.
