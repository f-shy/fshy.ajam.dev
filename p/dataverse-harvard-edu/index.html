Per-file 2.5 GB through the Harvard UI/API; larger files require the S3 direct-upload path, which Harvard has enabled (downloads already redirect to S3, so upload-redirect is configured). Dataset file-count cap observed live: effectiveDatasetFileCountLimit=1000 (12 used on the sampled dataset).
