Identityanonymous No card, no email, no signup form: 'your SSH key fingerprint is your account'. Payment is a prepaid Paddle balance with a USD 10 minimum top-up, refundable within 3 months; no card is stored by Shellbox. No hold.
EgressNot metered on the page read: no traffic allowance and no per-GB price is stated; the box gets a public IPv6 address and a managed HTTPS endpoint.
free allowance: not stated
RetentionParked (stopped) boxes keep their disk and a memory snapshot at USD 0.50/month per x1 slot; boxes auto-stop when the balance drops below USD 5 and are deleted at USD 0. Unused funds are refundable within 3 months.

ReliabilityStopped boxes resume from a memory snapshot in about 3 seconds; auto-stop and deletion are triggered by the prepaid balance, not by time.
NotesArithmetic at 24/7: USD 0.02/hour x 730 = USD 14.60 for 2 vCPU, 4 GB and 50 GB SSD. 10 h/day at 22 days = 220 h = USD 4.40, which is inside the budget only if the box is allowed to sleep. The default mode pauses 120 seconds after the last SSH session disconnects, so a 24/7 workload must be put in keepalive mode explicitly. Runs on bare metal in Hetzner Finland, one region. Not established: the x2 to x8 per-slot prices, whether an IPv4 address exists at all, and the egress terms.
