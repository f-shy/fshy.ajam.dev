Storage is sold in fixed tiers with no top-up and no published transfer allowance, so a client buys the tier above what it needs or none.
Bindingvolume-quota 4 GB free fails 1 TiB by a factor of 256 on storage alone, before any transfer question. On the paid side 1 TB at $14.99 still cannot hold a 1 TiB working set, so the tier below 10 TB always binds.
Identityemail 'No credit card required' for the free 4 GB account; an email registration at my.sonicbit.net/register. Payment is by card at upgrade, with a promo code in circulation ('10YEARSONICBIT', up to 60% off).
EgressPlans are sold by storage, not by transfer; the vendor's comparison table quotes storage tiers only and says nothing about a download allowance. Paid plans add 'Seeding 8 MB/s · ratio 10:1' on the top tier.

Interfacesftp, ftp, rest The plan table lists 'Remote access · SFTP / FTP / RPC' on the 1 TB tier, and the site advertises 'AI API' and an apps layer (Plex, Jellyfin, Sonarr, n8n) on top of the drive. The API endpoint itself was not read.
Toolingrclone-over-SFTP is the obvious mount for the paid tiers; the site states SFTP, FTP and RPC access. No official client was read.
Composes withThe Seedr shape: a torrent is added server-side, the local IP never joins the swarm, and the file arrives over SFTP. At 1 TB for $14.99 it is the cheapest storage-per-dollar seedbox in tested services and it composes with a debrid that cannot seed.
ReliabilityThe site's own help pages state that paid tiers cap concurrent torrents and that over-quota additions are 'Removed automatically', so a full drive loses the newest torrent rather than refusing it.
NotesThe vendor page is a Seedr comparison written to win the search for 'Seedr alternative', and it names Seedr's own tier prices alongside its own, which makes it usable as a cross-check on both. The 10 TB tier at $59.99 is $5.85 per TB, the cheapest per TB in tested services.
