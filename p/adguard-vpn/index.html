Interfacecli AdGuard VPN CLI (adguardvpn-cli) on Linux with a TUN mode and a SOCKS5 mode; apps for Windows, macOS, Android and iOS and browser extensions. The SOCKS5 mode is a local proxy on 127.0.0.1:1080 by default; the vendor documents no remote SOCKS5 endpoint with credentials.
ToolingThe vendor's CLI on a Linux machine gives a local SOCKS5 proxy (TCP and UDP) that any client on that machine can use; the apps and extensions cover the other platforms. The CLI's protocol option is HTTP2, QUIC or automatic.
Composes withA compute node runs the CLI in SOCKS5 mode and sends requests to 127.0.0.1:1080, so they leave at the chosen location's address; a Worker cannot reach that loopback proxy, so it needs a node in front of it.
ReliabilityThe 1 year card shows a struck-through $11.99 per month and a '-67%' badge, so the $3.99 may be a promotional rate that the page can change. unverified: no exit-IP registry class was measured.
NotesDerived: $3.99 per month x 12 months = $47.88 per year. The 1 year card shows a struck-through $11.99 per month and a '-67%' badge. The protocol is the vendor's own, TrustTunnel, over HTTP/2 or HTTP/3; there is no WireGuard or OpenVPN option named on the pages read. Port forwarding: unverified: no AdGuard VPN page read states port forwarding. BitTorrent rule: unverified: no AdGuard VPN page read states a BitTorrent or P2P rule.
