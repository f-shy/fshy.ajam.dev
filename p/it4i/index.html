Free tierThe 39th Open Access Grant Competition distributes up to 956,000 Karolina CPU node-hours, 103,000 Karolina GPU node-hours, 149,000 LUMI-C and 155,000 LUMI-G node-hours, plus 1,982,000 QPU seconds on the VLQ quantum computer, with each request capped at 10 percent of the resource, so a single request may ask for 95,600 Karolina CPU node-hours, 10,300 Karolina GPU node-hours, 15,500 LUMI-G node-hours or 198,200 QPU seconds. It runs three times a year, in February, June and October.

NotesThe per-request cap is what makes this record worth carrying: 10,300 Karolina GPU node-hours at eight A100s per node is 82,400 A100-hours, computed here from the two published figures, which is roughly thirteen times the 6,250 A100-hours an ACCESS Explore allocation buys. Note the eligibility sentence says employee of a Czech research organisation rather than Czech national, which is a weaker gate than several national centres impose and is worth rechecking for any applicant who holds an EU appointment.
