From n8n 3.0, n8n is only distributed through Docker.
- composes with: Last resort after the hosted workflow offers (the 14-day n8n Cloud trial in dev-services/n8n-cloud, and the hosted free tiers of Pipedream, Zapier and Inngest): triggers, schedules and integrations run on a machine that the user installs and keeps running.
