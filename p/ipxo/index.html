Free tierNo free IPv4 lease. The platform subscription has a Free plan at $0 ('Try the platform - free forever'), but 'IPXO platform subscriptions and IPv4 leasing are priced separately.' The 3x3 campaign rate for leased space is $0.25 per IP per month on a minimum 3-year term; the platform's yearly average lease rate is $0.38 per IP per month.
unit none; metered on none; The Free subscription gives access to the platform's resource view and pay-as-you-go tokens; it carries no address space. A published per-subnet price list is not on the pages read: the pricing page says 'IPv4 leasing prices depend on the IP block size, lease term, and available inventory', and the search that shows each subnet's monthly price sits behind the portal sign-up.

The vocabulary has no kind for a minimum purchase size or a contract term; volume-quota is the nearest and is used for the /24 minimum. A second gate is not a limit at all: the account must be a legal entity acting in the course of business, and a leased block is announced from an ASN the lessee names. The $0.25 per IP per month rate applies only to the campaign's 3-year terms.
Bindingvolume-quota The /24 minimum binds first on the price side: 256 addresses at the campaign rate, and a 3-year commitment, are the smallest purchase. Identity binds before any of it (legal entities only, with company confirmation by support), which is why the verdict is fails-identity; the limit vocabulary has no kind for that.
Identityinstitution A payment method (credit card, PayPal or wire transfer) is added after that. The terms describe a 'basic Know Your Customer (KYC) process'.
Egressnot applicable: IPXO sells the right to announce an address block, not a carrier port. The lessee announces the block from its own ASN or through a cloud's bring-your-own-IP feature; traffic is billed by that network, not by IPXO.
free allowance: not applicable: no traffic is carried by the vendor

Interfacerest A web portal handles search, lease, LOA, ROA, WHOIS and rDNS ('Assign IPs immediately with automated LOA, ROA, and WHOIS updates.'); the site's navigation lists 'API Access: Automate IP search and leasing workflows'. The API's endpoints and authentication were not read.
ToolingA BGP speaker, a cloud's bring-your-own-IP service or a hoster that announces the block for the lessee; IPXO issues the LOA and ROA and keeps WHOIS and rDNS in sync. The leasing page advertises 'Deploy IPXO-leased IPs across major platforms at lower cost.'
Composes withA leased /24 is 256 addresses in one prefix, one registry object and one announcing AS, so for a rotating egress it is one rung-3 unit with 256 source addresses; the lease supplies address space and no machine.
