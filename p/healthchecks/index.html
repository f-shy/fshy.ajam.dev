Free tierHobbyist plan at USD 0 a month: 20 monitored jobs (checks) per account and 100 log entries per job; the Supporter plan at USD 5 a month has the same limits
unit objects; metered on object-count; The unit counted is the check, one per monitored job. A check is up while pings arrive within its period plus grace time and goes down, with a notification, when they stop. The Business plan (USD 20 a month, 100 jobs) is offered free of charge to open-source projects and nonprofits on request.
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 20 checks per account on the Hobbyist plan source
  • retention-clamped 100 log entries kept per check, oldest removed first source
  • request-rate Management API: avoid more than 100 requests per minute; ping endpoints: a check pinged more than 5 times per minute may have requests ignored source
  • per-object-cap 100 kB of ping request body stored per ping source
Notification credits for SMS, WhatsApp and phone calls are listed on the Business and Business Plus plans only.
Bindingvolume-quota The 20-check cap is reached before any rate limit by an account that monitors many jobs; the 5-pings-a-minute rule binds only a job that pings in a tight loop.
Identityemail The login page offers Create Your Account and states that a magic login link is emailed; payment details are entered under Account Settings, Billing, when a paid plan is chosen.
Egressnot applicable: an inbound ping receiver; the ping log of a check is read back through the API, which returns 100 pings for free accounts and 1000 for paid accounts
Per-object capthe first 100 kB of the request body of each received ping is stored; the Ping-Body-Limit response header reports the limit in bytes (100000 bytes)
Retention100 log entries are kept per job on the Hobbyist plan and the oldest entry is removed when the limit is reached; a job that pings every 5 minutes is covered for about 8 hours
Interfacerest Ping endpoints at hc-ping.com take HEAD, GET and POST requests with no key in the URL path beyond the check UUID or slug; a Management API v3 at healthchecks.io/api/v3 takes an X-Api-Key header with a project-specific read-write or read-only key.
endpoint: https://healthchecks.io/api/v3/checks/
Toolingcurl or any HTTP client against the ping URLs and the Management API, and the web dashboard. As a last resort the server is open source under the BSD 3-clause license: the user installs and runs it on a rented or free machine.
Composes withDead man's switch for the scheduled jobs of free hosts: a cron line, a CI schedule or a serverless timer sends one HTTP request to a ping URL, and the check alerts by email or chat when the ping stops arriving.
ReliabilityPings above 5 a minute to one check are answered 200 but not recorded; an account inactive for over a year is closed 30 days after an email notice.
NotesThe free plan caps the number of jobs, not the number of pings, so a small set of frequently pinging jobs fits easily; the Business plan at USD 20 a month raises the cap to 100 jobs.
