Bindingnone At 1 TiB/month the requirement is 3.24 Mbit/s sustained; the 50 Mbit/s cap is about fifteen times that, so the speed limit does not bind at this volume and no byte quota exists. What actually constrains a workload here is the blocked-port list, which the seven kinds do not name: 'SMTP - 25 - 465 - 587 - 2525' and 'POP3/IMAP - 110 - 143 - 993 - 995' are closed, so a mail service on this prefix cannot work. Reachability is IPv6-only.
Identityemail https://tb.tahio.eu/sign-up asks for Email, Password and Full Name, with Nickname optional; no card and no phone field.
Egressno monthly byte allowance is published; the fair-use section caps speed instead: '50 Mbit/s up + down, kernel-shaped'; ingress: tcp+udp
free allowance: none published
Interfacecli the site hands out SIT, GRE and WireGuard configuration; a dyndns2 endpoint updater is offered per tunnel for dynamic IPv4
Toolingwg-quick or wireguard-tools; a plain SIT/GRE interface for the other two transports
Composes witha native WireGuard profile with no dashboard step makes it the cheapest way to put a free compute instance on a public IPv6 address with every port open.
Reliabilityno SLA: 'No SLA, no uptime guarantee.' Abuse, spam or scanning gives 'instant termination, no warning.'
NotesThe page contradicts itself on prefix size: the headline says 'BGP-routed /48s' while the network section says 'Each tunnel gets two /64s from the primary pools plus a /64 from a tertiary.' The /64 arithmetic is the concrete statement and the /48 is unexplained, so the record carries three /64s and flags the /48 as unestablished. AS198889, single PoP in Oslo, peered with Gigahost.
