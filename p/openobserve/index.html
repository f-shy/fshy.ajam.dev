Retention15 months of metrics and 30 days of logs, traces, RUM and other non-metrics data are included by default; further 30-day periods of non-metrics retention cost USD 0.02 per GB (30 days)
Interfacerest REST APIs for ingestion, search and management on the account's own host, authenticated by HTTP basic authentication with a base64 encoded user and password.
Toolingthe web app and the REST APIs. Last resort, after every hosted offer: the Open Source edition (AGPL-3.0) is free forever and the Self-Hosted Enterprise edition is free up to 50 GB of ingestion a day, and the user installs and runs the server on a rented or free machine.

NotesCloud Professional is the only Cloud plan listed; the Enterprise plan is custom priced.
