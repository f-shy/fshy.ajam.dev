ReliabilityTLS terminates at the edge, so it is not end-to-end encrypted; a phishing interstitial appears for first-time visitors; the server is MIT and self-hostable. A harness measured a 908 ms median latency and both 4 MiB transfers stopping at exactly 2,486,272 bytes, a per-request cap of about 2.37 MiB on the free anonymous tier.
NotesSSH-based reverse tunneling service. Provides stable public hostnames derived from client SSH keys without requiring account registration.
