ToolingPython/Java/R clients (synapseclient), REST API, and a built-in Docker registry. Also supports custom storage locations (your own S3/GCS). Which makes it a metadata layer over someone else's bucket rather than storage itself.
Composes withUnlimited projects per certified user. The interesting chain is the inverse: point Synapse at a custom S3/GCS location and you get a free DOI-style sharing and permissions layer over any bucket you already have.
ReliabilityCertification gate plus no published quota means capacity is discretionary; large uploads are known to be throttled/queued without a documented ceiling. Treat quota as unverified.
