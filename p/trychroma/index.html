unit credits; metered on none; metered on spend: the USD 5 credit is spent against usage billed on four meters, USD 2.50 per GiB written, USD 0.33 per GiB per month stored, USD 0.0075 per TiB queried and USD 0.09 per GiB returned

Bindingvolume-quota the one-time USD 5 credit runs out first: it is counted per account across all collections, and at USD 2.50 per GiB written it pays for 2 GiB of writes before any storage, query or returned-data charge
Identityemail sign-in with a GitHub or Google account; the sign-up page shows no card field; a vendor video posted 2025-06-30 says the USD 5 of free credits needs no credit card and says to add a credit card to keep using the service after the trial; the pricing FAQ lists credit card as the payment method for Starter and Team
Egressusage-based and metered out of the same USD 5 credit as storage and queries. The credit is the only ceiling, and no separate egress allowance applies
Per-object cap16,384 bytes per document, a Chroma Cloud quota; the total allowance is the credit (16384 bytes)
Retentionuntil the credits are spent
Interfacerest, cli a REST API; Python and JS clients; the open-source server is self-hostable

Composes withthe hosted Starter plan is a USD 5 credit for new accounts with usage-based billing after it; the open-source core, which the user installs and runs on a rented or free machine, is the last resort
Reliabilitya usage limit, whether set by the customer or by Chroma, pauses the service until the limit is changed
