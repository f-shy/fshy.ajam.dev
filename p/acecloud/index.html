L40S 48GB: INR 142.12 an hour 'starting at' on-demand, INR 83,000 a month for 1x (16 vCPU, 64 GB RAM). The Noida data center prices are shown; Mumbai, Atlanta and custom quotes are on the main pricing page. A third-party index (reported) lists the 1x L40S at USD 1.87 an hour.
