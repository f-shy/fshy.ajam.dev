NotesQoder International (qoder.com) and Qoder CN (qoder.cn, the former TONGYI Lingma) are separate accounts with separate prices and Credits; Qoder CN is its own record, coding-subs/lingma. The basic-model daily and monthly limit is unpublished on the pricing page, the documentation pricing page, the Credits page and the model selector page.
