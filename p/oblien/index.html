VMs can start at 0.1 vCPU, 128 MiB RAM and 64 MiB writable disk; a pool starts at 256 MiB RAM and 1 GiB storage.
Bindingvolume-quota No trial credit is granted, so the prepaid wallet and the 125 percent pay-as-you-go ceiling bind first; at full CPU a two-vCPU, 4 GiB VM is about $0.092 an hour.
Identityemail Account creation needs a valid email address, a secure password and accurate registration information, or a GitHub, GitLab or Google third-party sign-in; creating an account is free, and usage needs purchased credits, a paid plan or an administrator-granted allowance.
EgressManaged internet transfer is $0.10 per GiB billed separately; monthly pools include a shared transfer allowance, and public routes and a user's own proxy do not incur the managed-proxy charge.
free allowance: 100 GiB included with the 2 vCPU / 4 GiB / 40 GiB monthly pool
price per tb: USD 102.40 per TB at $0.10 per GiB
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is a microVM with a writable disk from 64 MiB to 1 TiB, not a stored object
RetentionRetained disks remain billable after coverage ends; retained disks persist 30 days after expiry and are not automatically deleted. (30 days)
Interfacerest, ssh, cli An SDK and REST API control full Linux microVMs, with SSH password or key auth, interactive shells over WebSocket, public port URLs and MCP access.
endpoint: https://api.oblien.com/v2
ToolingTypeScript SDK, REST API, command-line tool, SSH, MCP and a dashboard; images include Ubuntu, Node.js, Python, Go, Rust, Redis and Postgres.
Composes with$0.03 per active vCPU-hour plus $0.008 per reserved GiB-hour makes a two-vCPU, 4 GiB VM about $0.092 an hour at full CPU, and a monthly $18 pool includes 100 GiB of transfer with a 2 vCPU / 4 GiB / 40 GiB fleet.
ReliabilityCapacity admission can reject a pool when host inventory is unavailable; an empty wallet leaves a storage balance due rather than free compute, and accounts with overdue balances over 60 days may be terminated.
NotesAt full CPU a two-vCPU, 4 GiB VM is $0.092 an hour by the published rates, matching the operator list. There is no automatic trial credit: the account is free to create but every VM needs wallet funds or a pool. MCP access and the hosted service are the offered path; an open-source self-hosted deployment platform from the same vendor is separate and ranks after this hosted offer.
