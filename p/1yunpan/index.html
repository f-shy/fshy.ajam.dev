Toolingrclone s3 against s3.1yunpan.com, rclone webdav, s3cmd, any S3 SDK, and a raw GET on the per-user host. Registration is an email address and an emailed six-digit code with no phone field in the DOM; Google and GitHub OAuth are also offered
Composes withThe strongest chaining candidate in its group: a free bucket that answers both S3 and WebDAV drops into a chunk-and-replicate scheme as a first-class target with no AList shim. The 1 TB monthly bandwidth is the binding number, not the 500 GB of space
ReliabilityReads are real: a 1,326,142,372-byte MP4 came back byte-exact against Content-Length with Range honoured, and a ranged GET returned 206 with a correct Content-Range. The site notice states that files uploaded between 2026-10-05_22:00:00 and 2026-10-30_23:59:59 are temporarily not downloadable and that file upload is temporarily stopped, and config.json sets upload.temporarily_disabled to true. The 400 GB is streak-gated, so an account that stops logging in loses headroom
Notes500 GB reachable on an email address with no phone is unusual, and the S3 endpoint answering real S3 error semantics is what makes it useful: rclone, restic and Duplicati can each treat an account as a bucket. The check-in ladder is a daily quota rather than a wall-clock trial, so it is repeatable, but it also means the account is worth less the moment the streak stops.
