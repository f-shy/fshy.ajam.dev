The category page at /store/vps-hosting-lxc-lite-nat renders all four NAT plans and their prices, but every order button and the /store index resolve to an unrelated 'Radio Streaming Live' product: the order button href is '/store/vps-hosting-lxc-lite-nat/lxc-nat-basic' and that URL 200s with x-web-final-url https://client.arenastreaming.com/store/radio-streaming-live, and /store itself does the same. So a buyer reading the price page cannot reach the NAT cart through the links the page prints. Not established: the number of forwarded ports, whether SSH is one of them, whether they are assigned or chosen, whether UDP is forwarded, the billing cycles offered at checkout, and the payment methods.
