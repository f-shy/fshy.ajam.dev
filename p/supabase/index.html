Composes withThe premise '100 projects per free org' is WRONG on two counts: (a) quota is per ORGANIZATION not per project. 'The quota is applied to your entire organization, independent of how many projects you launch'; (b) the free project limit is TWO, and it is account-wide: 'You are granted two free projects. The project limit applies across all organizations where you are an Owner or Administrator.. two Free Plan organizations with one project each, or one Free Plan organization with two projects.' So the ceiling is 2 projects x but only 1 GB + 5 GB egress TOTAL. Paused projects do not count toward the limit, so pause/unpause rotation is the only lever, and it is manual.
