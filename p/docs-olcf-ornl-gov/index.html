NotesThis is the highest-value low-friction tier found: 15,000 to 20,000 Frontier node-hours at 8 GCDs per node is roughly 120,000 to 160,000 GPU-hours and 960,000 to 1,280,000 core-hours per award. The GPU-hour conversion is arithmetic from the quoted node composition and the quoted node-hour range, not a vendor figure, so the node-hour numbers are documented and the converted ones are inferred. OLCF states there is no DOE sponsorship requirement. The barrier is institutional signature plus export control, not scientific merit at INCITE scale. A foreign national participant triggers an ORNL PAS request that takes 15 to 35 days.
