A Brazilian or Argentine buyer pays in EUR or USD through a foreign processor; the localization is marketing, not a local payment rail. A 2026-era entrant with a Turkish-first interface (the /tr pages render plan names and reseller copy) and an affiliate-style reseller network shared with other small providers.
