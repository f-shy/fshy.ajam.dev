Free tierThe AWS Free Tier is now a plan with an instance-type allowlist. New accounts from 15 July 2025 get 'USD 100 in credits immediately' plus 'up to USD 100 more', valid 6 months, and on the Free plan the account 'closes on its own 6 months after you open it or when your credits run out'. Reporting of the console restriction is consistent: 'This instance type is not eligible under the Free Plan. Upgrade your account plan to access this instance type', and the eligible list is T3.micro, T3.small, T4g.micro, T4g.small, C7i-flex.large and M7i-flex.large. No GPU family is on it.
