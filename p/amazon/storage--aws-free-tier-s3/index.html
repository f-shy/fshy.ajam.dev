Free tierNo perpetual S3 storage allowance remains in the current published Free Tier. What exists now: up to $200 in credits ($100 at signup + up to $100 for activities), expiring 6 months after account creation, and the account itself 'closes on its own 6 months after you open it or when your credits run out'. The old 5 GB/12-month S3 allowance is not listed on the current free-tier page or S3 pricing page's Free Tier section, which now describes only the credit model.

EgressData transfer out from AWS Regions to the internet: first 100 GB/month free for all customers (announced 2021, still the standing allowance). CloudFront separately gives 1 TB/month free egress + 10M requests, no longer time-limited. But that is a CDN allowance bolted onto a paid origin.

ToolingS3 API, rclone 's3', aws-cli; CloudFront in front for the 1 TB egress allowance.
Composes withAWS explicitly aggregates usage across an AWS Organization, so claiming credits across several accounts fails (Free Tier Terms: 'You will not be eligible for any Offers if you or your entity create(s) more than one account'). A second account does not carry a second allowance.
ReliabilityThe most reliable infrastructure here, but the programme is now a time-boxed credit grant, not free storage. Anything still stored when the Free plan closes is at risk.
