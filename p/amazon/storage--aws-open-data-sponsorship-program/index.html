2-year term from acceptance, auto-terminates, renewable only if AWS offers ('AWS may offer to extend your participation prior to the end of the 2-year term'); renewal review looks at usage metrics. Rolling review, ~2-week decision. Licence must be permissive; Account must be dedicated to the dataset - no compute, no other services. 90-day wind-down with data retrieval after termination.
Identityinstitution (placeholder) Name of your institution
EgressFree to the public - programme covers 'distribution costs such as egress and cross-region data fees'. Direct S3 HTTPS GETs, no requester-pays.
RetentionUnless AWS terminates your participation for cause pursuant to Section 5.2(b) of the Agreement, there will be a wind-down period of no fewer than 90 days following termination ("Wind-Down Period"). (90 days)
Interfaces3-compatible, rclone, cli s3
ToolingPlain S3 API; rclone 's3' backend; AWS CLI; anonymous HTTPS GET for consumers.
Composes withOne dedicated AWS account per dataset, each uncapped. Multiple datasets = multiple applications. Dataset can then be re-served through Cloudflare in front of S3 for further egress cover.
ReliabilityProduction S3; no data loss. The failure mode is review: a dataset that stops looking 'high-value' or stops growing does not get renewed, and the bill reverts to you.
NotesNo published byte cap - 'covers the cost of Amazon S3 storage and data transfer for high-value cloud-optimized public datasets'. Registry datasets run to hundreds of TB; the programme as a whole is reported at 300+ PB.
