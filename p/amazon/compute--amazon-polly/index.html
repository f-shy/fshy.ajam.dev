Free tier'For Amazon Polly's Standard voices, the free tier includes 5 million characters per month for speech or Speech Marks requests.' The Neural allowance is '1 million characters per month ... for the first 12 months', Long-Form '500 thousand characters per month ... for the first 12 months' and Generative '100 thousand characters per month for speech requests, for the first 12 months'. The vendor's own conversion is '1 million characters ... ~23 hours, 8 min', so 5 million Standard characters a month is about 115 hours of speech.

Composes with115 hours of speech a month from Standard voices is enough to narrate a book a month, and it composes with any free object store for the audio. What breaks: the good voices are on the 12-month clock, so after a year the same pipeline drops to Standard quality or starts billing at USD 16 per million characters for Neural.

NotesThese are inference credits, not general compute. The distinction that matters is Standard versus the rest: Standard is permanent, everything better is a first-year allowance. AWS also replaced the old free tier with 'up to $200 in AWS Free Tier credits' for new customers from 2025-07-15, whose free plan 'will be available for 6 months', which is a separate and smaller lane. The 115-hour figure is the vendor's own conversion, not ours.
