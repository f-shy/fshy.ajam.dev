NotesArithmetic: $100 over $0.09 a GB is 1,111 GB = 1.01 TiB, and $200 is 2,222 GB = 2.02 TiB. Spread across the 6 months the account lives, $100 is $16.67 a month, or 185 GB, plus the 100 GB free each month, so 285 GB = 0.28 TiB a month; with the full $200 it is 0.43 TiB. The old 12-month Free Tier with 100 GB a month egress for a year is gone from this page; what replaced it is a credit that a reader has to earn half of. The always-free line, which the page describes as '30+ AWS services are always free within monthly usage limits', does not include a t3.micro with a public IPv4.
