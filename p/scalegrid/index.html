EgressDedicated Hosting and Shared Hosting plans include all data transfer costs; Bring Your Own Cloud plans use the standard transfer rights of the connected cloud provider.

RetentionIf a payment method has not been entered by the end of the free 7-day trial period, the cluster is automatically deleted. Paid Dedicated Hosting includes up to 5 free backups per deployment, with additional backups for a fee. (7 days)

NotesScaleGrid is a hosted managed database service, not software the reader runs. Its only no-cost path is a 7-day trial that deletes the cluster unless a payment method is added. The cheapest paid dedicated PostgreSQL plan is USD 13 per month. Shared Hosting, once a low-cost tier, is closed to new signups.
