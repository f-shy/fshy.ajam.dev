NotesA whole C-class range is one contiguous /24 prefix: 256 addresses behind one announcing AS and one registration class, allocated whole to one customer. In the United States the range is USD 500 a month (a 30-day order line) at 100 Mbps, about USD 1.95 per address. A range order is one line per region with a quantity of 1, and both range order pages total it as the sum of the listed range prices, with no quantity discount in the page: the desktop order summary has a Discount row at -$0.00 that its script never changes. The single static ISP product lists USD 2.80 per IP for 30 days in the United States, and the total its order window displays takes a quantity discount: 5 percent off for 1-5 IPs, 10 percent for 6-49, 15 percent for 50-199, 20 percent for 200-799 and 25 percent for 800 or more. For 256 single addresses that displayed total is USD 573.44 for 30 days (USD 716.80 at list price, less 20 percent), USD 2.24 per address, and the range costs about 13 percent less per address against it. The order requests of both products name each line by region code, days and quantity and carry no price, and the payment window shows the total held in the server's order data, so the amount that checkout charges is first shown after an order is created, and placing an order sends a visitor without a session to the sign-up page. No test address, no ASN and no prefix is published for the range product; the vendor offers free trials for some plans through customer service, and uses IPinfo as its reference for an address's region. A check of a delivered range takes a RIPE or ARIN whois lookup on one address for the registration class and a looking glass for the announcing AS.
