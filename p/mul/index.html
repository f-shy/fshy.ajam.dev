Composes withUseful for a one-off foreign file rather than a monthly plan: payment is per downloaded byte, and the fetched copy lives on an Iranian server for two months, which is the part that makes re-download cheap on domestic traffic.
ReliabilityHost coefficients drain the balance faster than the byte count, and the FAQ warns that a VPN or proxy breaks the debit calculation, so a client behind any tunnel can be charged wrongly.
