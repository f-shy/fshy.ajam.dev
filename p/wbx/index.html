Free tierA free welcome credit is granted to a new account with no card required: the sign-up page states USD 20 welcome credit, USD 8 after email verification and USD 12 after the first day. A no-signup try sandbox runs for 5 minutes, then is deleted, and allows network only to package registries.
unit credits; metered on wall-clock; The operator list's $0.108/h is the Pro/Team rate for a 2 vCPU / 4 GB sandbox: $0.032 per vCPU-hour plus $0.011 per GB RAM-hour. Pay as you go is $0.038/vCPU-hour plus $0.013/GB-hour, which the pricing table gives as $0.128 for the same box.

Fixed-price plans are Solo $15/month (3 concurrent, 6 vCPU / 8 GB total, 4 h per sandbox), Pro $49/month with $25 usage credit (25 concurrent, 24 h), and Team $99/month with $50 credit (100 concurrent, 48 h); all prices exclude VAT.
Bindingvolume-quota Welcome credit and then prepaid balance are what run out first: when credit is exhausted new sandboxes stop starting while running ones finish normally, well before the concurrency cap matters.
Identityemail The sign-up form asks for Your name, Email and a password of at least 10 characters, plus terms acceptance; Google and GitHub sign-in are offered. 'No card required.' Billing details (country, company) are asked only when paying, by card through Stripe or GoPay for EU customers. The no-signup try sandbox needs 'no account, no card'.
EgressA sandbox has full internet, an allowlist or no network, with IPv4, IPv6 or both, and a public HTTPS URL for any port; the pricing page states no separate egress volume allowance or per-GB price.
Per-object capnot applicable: the metered unit is a running Firecracker microVM, not a stored object; the free plan offers up to 6 hours per sandbox and 10 vCPU / 10 GB RAM in total
RetentionAn idle sandbox falls asleep and keeps its files at no CPU or memory cost; on pay as you go it is kept 7 days, extendable to 14, and its disk bills at $0.06 per GB-month above the plan allowance. (7 days)
Interfacerest, cli, ssh, browser, chat A REST API at https://sandbox.wbx.cz/v1, a Python SDK and a TypeScript SDK, an MCP server at https://sandbox.wbx.cz/mcp for AI assistants, a terminal command, and a portal; sandboxes are Firecracker microVMs hosted in the EU.
endpoint: https://sandbox.wbx.cz/v1
ToolingThe user installs the Python or TypeScript SDK or calls the REST API; MCP lets a coding agent drive sandboxes without a client of its own.
Composes withUSD 20 of welcome credit and a per-job price ceiling let an agent run bounded code tasks and batches, with sleeping named sandboxes that keep files between runs.
ReliabilityWhen credit runs out new sandboxes stop starting and the user is emailed; running sandboxes finish normally. Idle sandboxes sleep and are deleted after the plan's retention period (2 days' email warning); paid sandboxes falling asleep after 30 minutes or 10 minutes without a command stop running programs but keep files.
NotesThe no-cost route is a USD 0.00 pay-as-you-go plan with USD 20 of signup credit and a separate five-minute browser trial. Paid fixed-price tiers (USD 15, USD 49 and USD 99 a month) exist on the same page; they are not priced in this record.
