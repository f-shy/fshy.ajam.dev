NotesTwo clauses decide how a run has to be built. First, a powered-off instance still consumes credit, so the correct discipline is to destroy and not to stop, and a reader who stops a machine to save credit loses the credit anyway. Second, expiry destroys the data, so every artefact has to be copied out before the clock ends. The allocation is also discretionary, and the larger public grant is restricted to Indian researchers and startups, which is a country gate on the 100,000-hour figure. As of 2026-10-23 the page states 25 hours and 10 days; the earlier USD 100 over 30 days figure is wrong on both the amount and the window.
