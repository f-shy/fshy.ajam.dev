Free tierPay as you go includes $12 in credit each month per organization, renewing monthly, which covers 3,000 minutes on a 2-vCPU Linux x86 runner; there are no monthly subscription or per-seat fees. Sandboxes use the same compute rates as runners and are billed for reserved time.
unit credits; metered on wall-clock; The $12 monthly credit is the free allowance. The 2-vCPU/8 GB Linux x86 rate is $0.004 a minute, equal to $0.24 an hour; the operator's list gives Avrea Sandboxes at $0.24 an hour billed per second, while the pricing page says sandboxes are billed for reserved time and the blog says per-second billing.

macOS sandboxes require an account-level request and have a 24-hour minimum reservation. Forking is limited to supported paused Linux amd64 sandboxes and agent sandboxes cannot be forked.
Bindingretention-clamped Without a payment method, the 2-hour cap on a sandbox ends the session first, before the $12 monthly credit is spent; a paused sandbox is then deleted after 7 days.
Identityemail The console sign-in at console.avrea.com offers 'Continue with GitHub' and 'Sign in with single sign-on (SSO)', with no email/password sign-in field rendered. The app bundle renders an email input only after the SSO option is chosen, to look up a SAML provider for a company domain ('No single sign-on is configured for that email. Continue with GitHub above.'), so the gate is GitHub or company SSO, not email. No phone or document field is shown, and no card is asked for before the $12 monthly credit is used. The exact card gate inside the console is unverified: behind a sign-up.
EgressNetworking is included in the price without separate bandwidth metering: 'Networking is included in the price, without separate bandwidth metering.' Git Mirroring outbound clone/fetch is $0.15 per GB, a separate add-on, and a static IP is $100 per IP per month.
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is a machine, not an object. A persistent disk add-on is priced at $0.50 per GB-month and 25 GB of cache storage per repository is included.
RetentionA paused sandbox is deleted with its disk 7 days after its time limit ended. Sandboxes that cannot pause, such as Windows, macOS and Linux arm64, are deleted with their disk at the limit; agent sandboxes boot once and their disk is not kept. (7 days)
Interfacessh, cli, browser Sandboxes are created in the console or with the Avrea CLI (avr vm create, avr vm ssh); an SSH terminal, RDP or VNC, scp/rsync via SSH config, and published HTTPS URLs are supported.
endpoint: https://api.avrea.com
Toolingthe Avrea console and the avr CLI; SSH, scp and rsync through a generated SSH config; published HTTPS URLs for previews; no rclone backend is documented.
Composes withA sandbox can start from a connected repository with package caches, pause and fork an eligible Linux x86 VM for parallel experiments, and publish a port over HTTPS for an agent preview.
ReliabilityWithout a payment method a sandbox stops after at most 2 hours, extensions included. A paused sandbox is deleted with its disk 7 days after its time limit; Windows, macOS and Linux arm64 sandboxes are always deleted with their disk. Agent sandboxes are deleted at session end and uncommitted work is lost.
NotesHosted full VMs for agent and developer tasks at $0.004 a minute for 2 vCPU/8 GB Linux x86, the same rate as Avrea runners, with $12 of monthly credit per organization and $0.24 an hour on the operator's list. Without a payment method the session is capped at 2 hours and a paused sandbox is deleted after 7 days. Self-hosting is not offered; the macOS 24-hour minimum is a separate tier.
