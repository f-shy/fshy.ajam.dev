Bindingnone Nothing fails at 1 TiB/month. Sustained 100 Mbps carries about 32 TB in 30 days, and the 95th-percentile method ignores short bursts by construction. The 100 Mbps is recorded in limits as per-ip-shaping because it is a rate that can be exceeded, but exceeding it is billed at USD 0.20 per Mbps rather than cut off, so no constraint binds at this volume.
Identityemail (inferred, not read) Ordering is self-serve in the panel: "Pay in the panel, configure the generated tunnel details, then assign the IPv4 addresses." The payment and verification steps were not read, so a card requirement is inferred from a paid panel order rather than observed.

NotesCheapest per address of the providers compared: USD 1.50 per month is USD 18 per year for a /32, and the same price buys a /25. The price conflict is real and unresolved: the homepage tile says "From $1.25 / mo" and the product page says "$1.50 per IPv4 address per month" in three separate places. Every per-prefix figure on the product page is exactly 1.50 times the address count, so the homepage tile may be stale after a rise. $1.25 is not the 12-month term price, because 15 percent off 1.50 is 1.275, not 1.25. $1.25 may be a leftover campaign rate, like the page's other struck-through campaign prices, but that is unproven. The record keeps the product-page figure.
