Identitycard card plus phone plus an SSH key: 'Make sure to upload an SSH key, besides verifying your phone number and adding a valid credit card. Once this is done, your basic setup is done'. The card must be 3D Secure and a card already attached to another Genesis Cloud account is refused by default; Genesis places a temporary USD 1 hold that 'is not a charge and will be automatically released after 7 days', and refuses prepaid cards by default. Charges after the trial are 'billed to the card attached to your account on the first of each month'. SEPA Direct Debit in EUR is accepted 'alongside cards and wire transfers'.
