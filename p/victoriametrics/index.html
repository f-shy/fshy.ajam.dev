Bindingvolume-quota Each deployment has predefined capacity limits and alerts on spikes; the Initial Tier's active-series, ingestion-rate and daily-read-volume ceilings bind before any per-object cap, which is not published.
Identityemail Registration is via Google sign-in or email and password; adding a payment method is not required to register or use deployments during the 30-day trial, and the trial grants $200 in credits.
EgressEgress traffic is charged $0.09 per GB, matching AWS' rate; estimated traffic costs typically range from $1 to $30 per month. Storage is $0.511 per Gb per month; backups are included at no extra cost and usage is metered hourly.

Per-object capunverified: no single-object cap is published; the Initial Tier capacities are 500k active time series, 16.6k datapoints/sec and 170 GB daily read volume, which are deployment limits rather than a per-object cap.
RetentionA deployment chooses a retention period; the price calculator offers 1, 6, 12 or 18 months. At trial expiry existing trial deployments are automatically deleted; after the trial ends, deployments are stopped and deleted after 7 days if no payment method is found.

Composes withA managed VictoriaMetrics endpoint drops in as a Prometheus remote-write target and Grafana data source; the open-source single-node or cluster binaries that the user installs and runs are the last resort when a hosted deployment is not used.
ReliabilityUsage is metered hourly and billed monthly; each deployment has predefined limits and sends alerts on spikes rather than charging overages. Trial deployments are deleted when credits expire; egress is charged $0.09 per GB.
NotesVictoriaMetrics Cloud has a 30-day $200-credit trial but no permanent free tier; ongoing capacity tiers start from $200/month and egress is $0.09 per GB. Registration is email/password or Google with no card until the trial ends. Self-hosted open-source VictoriaMetrics is the last resort, not the hosted offer described here.
