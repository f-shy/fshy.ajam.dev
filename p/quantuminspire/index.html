Composes withQuantum Inspire is the one provider among documented hosts whose free tier is a physical device rather than a batch queue, so it composes with nothing: the 17-qubit machine cannot be used to prepare data for a GPU job, and the only chain available is the same account also using the emulator for circuit development before spending queue slots on hardware.

NotesA university lab, TU Delft through QuTech, that issues accounts to email addresses with no institutional check and publishes no expiry, which is why this is the cleanest lane fit in the file for the identity question. A third-party comparison table lists QuTech as "free access" that "does not expire", which agrees with the vendor but is not the vendor speaking, so neither sentence is treated as a quota here. Two technical reliability facts from the FAQ matter more than the qubit count: the chips go offline for calibration and upgrades, which the backends page also shows by marking Spin-2+ as taken offline, and the FAQ warns that Chinese providers such as 163.com and qq.com bounce the confirmation mail often enough that users must write to support, which is a practical obstacle for an address on those domains rather than a veto. The free tier's shot allowance per job is not stated on the pages read, so no circuit-throughput figure is given.
