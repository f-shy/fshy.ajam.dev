Free tierNo published byte quota. The platform documents hosting over 5 petabytes of data across 330+ products from 130+ organizations, serving approximately 700 TB per month for public data publishing. Documentation specifies no per-product, per-account, or per-file cap.
unit TB; metered on unstated

EgressFree and unmetered in practice for the reader: anonymous GET with no credentials, Cloudflare in front, R2/Turbo-adjacent CDN headers. No egress meter appears anywhere in the docs.
Interfaces3, rclone, cli s3
ToolingGenuine S3 proxy. Endpoint https://data.source.coop, --no-sign-request for reads. Upload credentials are Source-issued but in AWS format and valid only at the proxy; path is s3://<account-id>/<product-id>/ and you cannot write outside your own prefix. Accepts aws cli, boto3, rclone with a custom endpoint, plus a Source CLI and service accounts for CI.
Composes withMultiplier is data products per account/org, and orgs can hold team members. Because reads are anonymous and S3-native, this is the cleanest 'someone else's S3 on a real CDN' shape in the whole D area. It can be mounted by AList/rclone and fronted by Cloudflare exactly per §2.1.
ReliabilityBeta + announced-but-unpublished pricing means the free window has an unknown end date. Store only data that has a second home.

Free tierNo published byte cap: 'allowing trusted organizations and individuals to publish data of any kind at any scale'. Case study: Earth Genome publishes 60+ TB. Platform total reported at just over 5 PB.
unit TB; metered on unstated
Pricenone stated
Identityemail email
EgressFree public reads through the data proxy at data.source.coop - live probe returned HTTP 200 with Cloudflare in front (CF-RAY present), so reads are CDN-covered.
Interfaces3 s3
ToolingObjects served at data.source.coop/<account>/<product>/...; the proxy is open source (Rust, github.com/source-cooperative/data.source.coop). Bring Your Own Bucket (BYOB) points Source at an S3 bucket you own, read via OIDC federation (sts:AssumeRoleWithWebIdentity) with no long-lived secret either side.
Composes withPer data product, owned by 'an organization or an individual' explicitly. BYOB is the important primitive: it decouples the catalogue/CDN from the bytes, so a free bucket elsewhere becomes a Source product with Cloudflare-fronted reads.
ReliabilityNonprofit (Radiant Earth 501(c)(3) parent) with in-kind AWS/Azure; beta terms can change. Storage itself lives on commodity cloud, so durability is AWS-grade.
