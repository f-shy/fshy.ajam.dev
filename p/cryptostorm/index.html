Limits
  • volume-quota none published: "No. We don't do any kind of throttling/QoS."; servers are 1 Gbps (10 Gbps at Switzerland, Finland, Atlanta, Chicago and Oregon) source

Concurrent connections are capped by token length and the vocabulary has no kind for it: a one-week or one-month token allows 1 WireGuard key and 1 OpenVPN session, a one-year token allows 4 and 4, a two-year token 5 and 5, a lifetime token 6 and 6.
Bindingnone No byte quota, speed cap or request rate is published; the only stated ceiling is the concurrent-session count of the token, which the limit vocabulary cannot name.
Identityanonymous A token is the account. The order page's Monero form is labelled 'no 3rd parties, no email or JS required', and the vendor states it accepts Monero 'so people can order an access token anonymously without providing any personal information'. Card and PayPal orders go through PayPal or CCBill, and only recurring subscriptions get the token by email; other orders show it in the browser. The vendor states it has no KYC policy.
EgressNo byte cap and no throttling are stated: the FAQ answers 'Are there any speed/traffic limits?' with "No. We don't do any kind of throttling/QoS." Servers are 1 Gbps, and 10 Gbps at five named sites; bandwidth is shared with other servers in the same cabinet.
free allowance: none: no free tier
Retentionnot applicable: a tunnel, not stored bytes
Interfaceunknown A tunnel, not a data API: OpenVPN (ECC and RSA configurations) and WireGuard, authenticated with a token. Protocols: OpenVPN and WireGuard. UDP: the home page states 'UDP or TCP (1-65535)', so both transports run on any port. Port forwarding: yes, from 30000 to 65535, enabled at http://10.31.33.7/fwd inside the tunnel, 100 forwards per internal IP on each server. SOCKS5 endpoint: unverified: no page read states one. Obfuscation: SSH, HTTPS (Xray or stunnel) and obfs4. BitTorrent: allowed ('BitTorrent allowed').
ToolingAny OpenVPN or WireGuard client with the vendor's published server configs; the vendor states all server-side configs are public. Port forwards are made from a web page reachable only inside the tunnel. The vocabulary has no wireguard, openvpn or socks5 interface token, so the interface list says unknown.
Composes withA tunnel exit for traffic that must leave from a shared VPN address: a job connects with a token, and the upstream sees one of the provider's shared addresses (over 450 IPs listed).
ReliabilityThe FAQ states servers share bandwidth with other servers in the same cabinet, so peak hours can lower speed; port forwards are per server and have to be recreated when moving to another server.
NotesCheapest yearly price: USD 52 for 1 year, 4 devices (2 years: USD 94, 5 devices). The port-forwarding page says the vendor runs a yearly half-off sale every Black Friday to Cyber Monday; the price above is the list price read on the page.
Evidence notedocumented Documented: price, protocols, UDP, port forwarding, obfuscation and the BitTorrent line are quoted from the vendor's own home page, FAQ and port-forwarding page.
