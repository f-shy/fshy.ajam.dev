NotesPaid-only was wrong. Cloud Studio (product 1039) publishes a monthly minute allowance for general and high-performance workspaces. A second regime runs at cloudstudio.net, where machine-hours are granted and expire in 7 days. Caveats: an exhausted pack does not stop the application and bills on demand, a free high-performance workspace idle for 30 days is released without recovery, and reaching the paying account needs Tencent Cloud real-name verification, which the China site issues for mainland, Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan documents. The earlier free_tier sentence read 'no free tier', which contradicted the verdict; the minute allowance is the free tier and the machine-hour regime at cloudstudio.net is the second product.
Evidence notedocumented the billing document was read on 2026-10-22 (Chinese). It defines a machine-hour deduction factor per shape and states that a package transfers to pay-as-you-go when exhausted. There is no free tier, so the record's USD 2.98 credit is not established
