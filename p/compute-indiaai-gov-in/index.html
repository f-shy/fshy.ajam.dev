Requests of 5,000 GPU hours or fewer, with 50 GPUs or fewer, and with no subsidy sought, skip the PMEC committee and are approved by an institutional verifier or a MeitY Startup Hub CoE.

Limits
  • write-gated (binding) Registration requires a name, an 'institute / organization email address' and a mobile number, both verified with an OTP, and then an eligibility ID by category: a Google Scholar ID with h-index, ORCID ID or Scopus Author ID for researchers (h-index of 5 or more), an APAAR ID for UG/PG students, DPIIT recognition for startups, or an Udhyam ID for MSMEs; early-stage startups (3.6) need 4 years of founder experience and an endorsement by the CEO of IndiaAI or a JS rank officer and above. source
Identityinstitution institution: registration needs 'their name, institute / organization email address and mobile number', so a mobile number is collected alongside an institutional email. The student path adds 'Apaar ID' plus 'Endorsement by appointed academic faculty verifier and endorser or IndiaAI CEO'; the startup paths require 'Valid DPIIT startup recognition certificate along with DPIIT ID' and 'Certificate of Incorporation'. Composes withThe 40% is a discount on an empanelled provider's list price, so it composes with the provider's own trial rather than replacing it: the same GPU appears at E2E Networks, NxtGen and Yotta, and the undiscounted lane can be priced directly with those vendors while the subsidy is unavailable.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesIndian nationality is not required: the policy never states a nationality test. A company is not required on the 3.6 early-stage-startup path, which names only four years of founder experience and an endorsement, no incorporation document. An Indian institutional email is required, as stated at 4.1, and it is what actually stops a foreign individual, because APAAR IDs are issued by Indian institutions and the CoE endorsement chain runs through MeitY. Section 3.6 is therefore the only lane that a foreign individual might argue into, and it is decided by a discretionary endorsement rather than a document, so the verdict stays fails-identity and the open question is whether IndiaAI accepts a non-Indian organisation email at registration.
