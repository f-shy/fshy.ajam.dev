Identitycard The provider §1.1b lists "Entering payment information and billing address" as a registration step. §6.1c says the payment provider may "reserve or deduct and reimburse a small amount of money (less than $5.00 US) from the credit card or PayPal account or iDeal account" to verify the customer. A payment instrument is captured before the trial starts.
EgressNo separate traffic price is published; the trial is metered by downloaded volume and the plan list prices volume per month after it. The provider does not state an overage rate for the trial.

Interfaceunknown NNTP over SSL. The vendor publishes no API. The schema has no nntp token, so the array says unknown; the protocol is not in doubt.

Composes withA one-off 30 GB that must be spent inside 14 days on the largest remaining object, then cancelled before the monthly plan starts; it does not compose into a repeating month.
ReliabilityThe trial converts to a paid monthly plan at 7.95 € unless cancelled inside the 14 days; §6.4c stores the registration IP and §6.1d allows immediate blocking where payment data is judged false.
NotesA second address does not defeat this, because the gate is the payment instrument plus the stored registration IP rather than the network address. Abandonment: if the trial stops being priced at 0 € for a full 14 days, the record becomes paid-only.
