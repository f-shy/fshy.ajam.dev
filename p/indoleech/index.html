Free tierA free slot is published: 'Free - 1.0 GB Space, 1 Torrent Run, Unlimited DL, Support RAR, Zip'. Paid tiers are Rp 30,000 (10 GB, 3 runs), Rp 40,000 (20 GB, 5 runs), Rp 50,000 (50 GB, 10 runs), Rp 70,000 (100 GB) per month. The same page serves a euro ladder through ?cur=EUR: EUR 1.75 / 2.33 / 2.92 / 4.08 / 5.83 / 11.67 / 17.50 per month for 10 GB through 1 TB.
unit GB; metered on stored-bytes; The quota is disk space allocated to the leech, not downloaded bytes; downloads are advertised as unlimited. Two currency ladders exist on the same site.

The reseller's fuller ladder runs to a 1024 GB slot at Rp 440,000/30 days with 30 active torrents.
Bindingvolume-quota Disk space per tier is what a monthly transfer of 1 TiB hits first: 10 GB in, download out, delete, repeat. The 3-run concurrency caps the pace, not the total.
Identityemail Signup is an account on the site ('Buy Now' leads to /register). A euro price ladder is served at ?cur=EUR, so the checkout is built for buyers outside Indonesia as well; the method is still not published on the pricing page, and the documented Indonesian purchase channel (ViooMax) takes local bank transfer.

Composes withA cheap torrent-to-HTTP step in front of any download manager: 10 GB of working space is enough to cycle 1 TiB of monthly transfer through it in chunks, provided the 3 concurrent runs are enough.
ReliabilityFree tier is 1 Mbit/s with one run, which is a demonstration rather than a service; paid performance is vendor-stated and unmeasured, and the reseller warns prices move with the exchange rate.
NotesIndonesia's retrieval market is torrent leech, not multi-host debrid: the stores sell disk slots and concurrent runs. The euro ladder is the strongest signal among debrid providers that an Indonesian service is built to be bought from outside, even though the payment method is still unstated. Trigger condition: the site stops accepting registrations or the price ladder moves beyond IDR 500,000 for 1 TB.
