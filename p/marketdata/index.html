Free tierFree Forever plan USD 0: 100 daily API credits, standard (pricing) endpoints, 1 year of historical data, 24-hour delayed stock and options data.
unit credits; metered on request-count; Each successful response costs 1 credit; a response that lists several symbols costs one credit per symbol. The daily counter resets at 9:30 AM Eastern Time. AAPL stock, AAPL options and the VFINX mutual fund consume no credits and answer without a token, while a keyless call for another symbol answers HTTP 401. The plan is reached through a 30-day trial that switches to Free Forever when it ends. The next plan up is Starter at USD 30 a month when paid monthly, with 10,000 daily credits.

Plan gating: Free Forever reaches the standard pricing endpoints only; premium endpoints with reference data such as earnings are paid. Bulk path: multi-symbol responses cost one credit per symbol.
Bindingrequest-rate 100 credits a day is met first; a response that lists several symbols spends one credit per symbol.
Identityemail The sign-up starts with a choice of a 30-day Starter or Trader trial, both labelled no credit card required, and continues with an Account Info step and a Contact Info step. The account verification page lists name, address, email, phone number and employer details as the information collected for the identity check that paid subscribers complete, and free and trial account holders have not completed it. unverified: whether the Account Info and Contact Info steps of the free sign-up ask for a phone number or an address, behind a sign-up. The API token is emailed to the address used to sign in.
Egressnot metered in bytes; the 100 credits a day bound what a free token can pull, with at most 50 simultaneous requests
Per-object capunverified: no per-response row cap is stated on the pages read; each symbol in a multi-symbol response costs one credit
Retention1 year of historical data on Free Forever; a request for older data is denied
Interfacerest GET https://api.marketdata.app/v1/{endpoint} with a Bearer token in the Authorization header or in a URL parameter; the answer is HTTP 203 instead of 200 when it is served from the caching tier. A Google Sheets add-in reads the same data.
endpoint: https://api.marketdata.app/v1/stocks/quotes/AAPL/
Toolingany HTTP client; official SDKs for JavaScript, TypeScript, Python and Go; a Google Sheets add-in
Composes with100 credits a day of 24-hour-delayed prices with 1 year of history, and AAPL stock and options with no token for wiring tests; current quotes need a free real-time feed such as Finnhub or Twelve Data, or a paid plan here.
ReliabilityPast the daily credits the API answers HTTP 429 until the 9:30 AM Eastern Time reset; HTTP 203 is a cached success; free data is delayed 24 hours.
NotesFree Forever data is delayed 24 hours and covers the standard pricing endpoints; AAPL stock and options and the VFINX mutual fund answer with no token.
