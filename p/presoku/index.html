Composes withThis is how Japan buys retrieval capacity without a Japanese-issued subscription: one coupon per host, bought in yen, activated on the host. It chains with a global debrid badly (double-paying the same hosts) and with a single stubborn host very well.
ReliabilityBank-transfer orders are manually fulfilled with a stated 12-hour worst case; the code is non-refundable once delivered, and the shop carries no responsibility for the host's uptime.
NotesJapan's local layer is not a debrid: it is coupon resale at a markup per host. The interesting fact for the sector is that card payment works, so Japan is the one Asian market here where a foreigner can buy the local product directly. Trigger condition: the card rail is restricted to Japanese issuers.
