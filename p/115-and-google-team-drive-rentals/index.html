Retentionthe drive lasts as long as the underlying tenant does; the seller states no refund if the plan disappears, and one thread carries a buyer reporting that the drive vanished
Interfacerest, rclone
Toolinga Google Drive API client or rclone; the rental is a permission on a shared drive, not a new protocol
Composes withthe cheapest route to a pooled tenant that already exists, and the clearest evidence that an 'unlimited' Google quota is a quota being arbitraged by somebody else
ReliabilityThe underlying tenant is the failure point and the renter does not control it: one thread's own replies describe unlimited Workspace space as a bug-based expansion, and another carries a buyer reporting that the team drive vanished. No refund on a mid-term exit is stated in every listing read.
NotesSignal documentation rather than a purchase route. Four listings on one forum between 2024-05 and 2026-10 quote CNY 15 to 20 per month for unlimited Google team-drive space with a 400,000-file cap and a 750 GB per day upload cap, and two of the threads argue about whether the space is a genuine plan or an expansion bug. Where a rental market exists, the underlying unlimited quota is being arbitraged, and the intermediary account is what fails.
