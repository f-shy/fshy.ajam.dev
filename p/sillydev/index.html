Bindingvolume-quota 256 MB of RAM with no swap and no sleep binds first: a scraper with a headless browser or a pandas frame will be killed by the OOM reaper long before any traffic allowance matters.
Identityemail no card: quoted 'No card, no trial, no expiry' and the register form asks username, email, password, confirm password, and optionally first and last name. No phone, no document, no card field. Payment by Stripe exists only for the optional paid plan.
Egressnot published: no traffic allowance appears on the home page, the terms or the database page read. The terms state only that a web app is reachable at 'the address and port shown in your panel'.
Per-object cap'2 databases per account, 50 MB each'; the terms state 'each with a fixed disk quota shown in your panel' and 'A write that would go past it fails straight away'. The number of free servers per account is not published on the pages read.
Retention'No renewal button and no expiry timer. The only exception: a server never used at all in its first 7 days is removed, usually after an email warning.' Nothing else reaps it.
Interfacessh, rest a panel with a live console that accepts input, a file manager, SFTP, automatic restarts, and scheduled tasks; Node.js, Python and Bun runtimes. No root shell: the terms call it 'not a general virtual server'.
Toolingthe vendor panel, or SFTP; no SSH client of your own choosing
Composes withA process that must stay up and must not be visited to stay up: a scraper worker, a queue consumer, a Discord or Telegram bot, or a scheduled script with a stateful database beside it.
ReliabilityThird-party reports of panel outages exist: one Trustpilot reviewer (read) writes that the panel 'stop working' and was down 'for 3 hours'; another says 'sometimes the server stops'. The vendor status host did not respond when checked, so uptime is not established.
NotesThis is the best answer in this category to the complaint documented records: a free tier that states in its own terms that it does not sleep, does not expire, and does not renew, with a 23 October 2026 date on the terms, and a register form with no card and no phone. The caveats are real. It is one UK sole trader, so it is identifiable but small, and the entity is a person rather than a company. No egress allowance is published at all. The absence of a root shell means it is a process host, not a machine: a WireGuard node is not possible here, but a cron and a listening port are. Third-party reviews report panel outages that the vendor's own status page could not be checked against, because that host did not answer.
