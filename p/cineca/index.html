NotesThe try-project route is the closest thing to an email-only tier discovered anywhere in evaluated benchmarks: no form, no committee, about 10% of a Class C project (so roughly 1,000 Leonardo GPU hours), activated in 10 days. The Italian-affiliation gate appears in the general information page rather than in the try-project sentence, so a reconciler should read this as 'email-only request, but eligibility still Italian'. CINECA states it awards in excess of 500 million core processor hours nationally each year and about 1,700 million counting EuroHPC participation. The ISCRA pages return upstream_status 403 to automated retrieval while still serving content through the provider's cache, so the text is documented but the origin refused a direct read.
