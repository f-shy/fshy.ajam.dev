Free tierPricing page, quoted: 'Start free with $300 in credits for 30 days' and, in the FAQ, 'ClickHouse Cloud starts with $300 in credits over 30 days, no credit card required. ClickHouse open source is free forever.' The page adds: 'The trial ends after 30 days or once the credits are consumed, whichever comes first; the account then converts to pay-as-you-go.' Trial credits expire at the end of the 30-day trial period, even after a payment method has been added.
