Plans: Basic 50 EUR 2.95/mo (50 Mbit, 50 connections), Unlimited EUR 4.95/mo against a EUR 9.95 regular price (unlimited speed, 100 connections). Blocks: 100 GB EUR 9.95, 250 GB EUR 14.95, 1000 GB EUR 29.95, 5000 GB EUR 59.95 against a EUR 119.95 regular price. All blocks carry 200 connections and 'Data never expires'. Upload is not included on a block.
Bindingnone The Unlimited plan publishes no volume, speed or connection ceiling beyond 100 connections, so at 1 TiB/month nothing binds. The fixed allowance binds on the block tier, where a 1 TB block is exhausted in month one; that is recorded in limits[] rather than here because the tier this record prices is Unlimited.

Payment options read from the vendor page are cards (Visa, Mastercard, Maestro, Amex, Postepay, Carte Bancaires) and direct bank transfer or wallets (iDEAL, Sofort, Trustly, Giropay, EPS, Przelewy24, Multibanco, Bancontact). RetentionRetention is not stated on the plans page or the block page. The r/usenet map records 4,000+ days with a 2,600-day backfill via Newshosting. (4000 days)
Interfacenntp NNTP over TLS on port 563. No HTTP or object API exists for the download path, which is driven through SABnzbd, NZBGet or nzbdav.
Composes withAn Abavia block is the fill account beside an Omicron unlimited primary: two independent spools mean an article missing on one is likely present on the other, and the block never expires.
ReliabilityAbavia is the backbone (AS48345, Amsterdam) that also carries Bulknews, Cheapnews, UsenetAgency, Hitnews, UsenetDiscount, NewsGrabber, NewsXS, Stingy, TurboUsenet, UseNight and Usenet.on 2026-10-23. The 5,000 GB block at EUR 59.95 is the cheapest per-TB block found among debrid providers.
NotesTwo figures matter here and they pull in opposite directions: EUR 4.95/mo unlimited is the cheapest subscription on an independent backbone, and EUR 59.95 for 5 TB is the cheapest block. Both are vendor-stated on 2026-10-23. Cheapest per byte in the sector by a factor of three over the debrids, and the least conditional.
