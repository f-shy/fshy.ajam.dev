Pricenone stated
Identitycard Free tier is 2GB ('Free: 2GB'). Paid tiers start at Lite $3.95/mo for 10 GB; the FAQ lists 'Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)' as payment methods for paid plans. What a free sign-up asks for: unverified: behind a sign-up.
EgressNo daily or monthly limits for free users (the vendor says this can change at any time); paid plans have no monthly limits either. The storage limit is the binding constraint.
RetentionFiles remain in storage until deleted; free and paid accounts keep files indefinitely.
Interfacerest, webdav, sftp, ftps, ftp rest
ToolingBrowser + browser extension + a 'Seedr Connector' desktop agent. FTP/FTPS/SFTP access is a paid feature (listed on Basic and above: 'FTP, FTPS & SFTP access'), and WebDAV appears only at Pro ('FTP, SFTP & WebDAV storages'). So on the free tier there is no programmatic transport at all.
Composes withFree quota is 2GB ('Free: 2GB') and the useful transports (FTP/SFTP/WebDAV) are paid-plan features; referral bonus is 500MB per signup, up to 2GB total. Per-account 2GB makes multiplying accounts a low-yield route.
ReliabilityQuota is intentionally hidden, which is itself the finding. You cannot size a workload against an unpublished number.
NotesFree accounts get 2GB (FAQ: 'Free: 2GB'); paid tiers now start at Lite $3.95/mo for 10 GB (the home page: 'Start Now With 10GB Only $3.95').
