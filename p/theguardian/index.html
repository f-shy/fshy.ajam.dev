Free tierDeveloper key, free: up to 500 calls a day and 1 call a second, with article text, over 1,900,000 pieces of content.
unit requests; metered on request-count; The 500 a day is per API key and expires at the close of each day. One key is issued for each website that uses the content. The archive dates back to 1999. A commercial key has a custom throttle and quota, priced by usage. The access page listed 12 calls a second and 5,000 calls a day before a later change to 1 and 500.

A developer key with no use for 90 days is deleted.
Bindingrequest-rate 500 calls a day is met first; the 24-hour hold limit governs any local copy.
Identityemail The developer-key form asks for a name, an email entered twice, an educational institution or company name, a reason (student project, academic research, non-commercial app development, other), a short description of use, whether content will be displayed, a product name, optional product and company URLs, and acceptance of the terms; a Google reCAPTCHA is shown when Register is pressed, and the key is sent to the email. The institution or company name is a required free-text field of at most 200 characters with no list or pattern. No card or phone field. The Open Platform front page offers the developer key as free instant access, the key tool's repository describes self-serve key management, and the commercial key is a request that the team follows up. Posts on the vendor's support forum describe an email with a verification link sent after the form, a link that can be invalid or expired, a refusal to register again with an address already in use, and a key registered to a free-mail address; a course page describes a confirmation email followed by a second email with the key. Whether the institution or company name is checked after the form is sent is unverified: behind a sign-up. How soon the key email arrives is unverified: behind a sign-up.
