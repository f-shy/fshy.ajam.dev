Composes withIt is the debrid layer itself: it accepts a host link, torrent, magnet or NZB and returns a direct link, so it replaces the whole downloader chain rather than slotting into it.
ReliabilityCloudflare fronts the site and challenged a plain client probe (challenge detected on a 200 response), so unattended automation needs a browser session. Free jobs are capped so low that queue failures are cheap to retry.
NotesThe most precisely stated free tier in the premium-link class after LeechPremium, but it is a torrent/usenet trial rather than a host-link unlock. Documented as trial-only because the free path cannot fetch a host link at any size. Included because the pay-per-GB model is the only pricing shape in the sector that could beat a subscription for one 20 GiB pull, but the vendor publishes neither the rate nor the retention, so the comparison cannot be completed from its pages.
