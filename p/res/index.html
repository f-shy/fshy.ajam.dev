NotesThis is the most nationality-open allocation programme found in the whole assignment, and it is the one row a reconciler should prioritise: the PI may be from any EU or associated-state institution, not a Spanish one, and there is no minimum request size. It is still fails-identity because no European institutional affiliation is in place, and the distinction matters: RES fails on affiliation, not on nationality or on the science case. The hours are site-hours, not GPU-hours or core-hours, so no conversion is offered. Deadlines per the FAQ were HPC/AI/Quantum 15 September 2026, with fast access weekly.
