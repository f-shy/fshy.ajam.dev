Free tierA self-serve grant with no wallet extension and no card, but with no published amount. Quoted: sign in at the dashboard, choose 'Claim credits'; the form asks for 'Name - your full name, Email address - the address where your verification code will be sent, Discord or Telegram handle' plus four profile questions; then 'A 6-digit verification code has been sent to the email address you provided. The code expires in 10 minutes.'; then 'Verification successful - You are eligible for grant distribution' and 'The claimable amount in COMPY tokens is displayed'. The USD 100 figure is third-party: Ocean's own Substack states '$100 in complimentary compute credits to early adopters'.

Identityemail a verified email address, a full name and a Discord or Telegram handle: the claim form asks for 'Name - your full name, Email address - the address where your verification code will be sent, Discord or Telegram handle' plus four profile questions, then sends a 6-digit code with a 10-minute expiry. Signing in with email, Google or a passkey creates a smart wallet automatically, so no crypto wallet is needed. No card and no hold: the grant pays in COMPY. Paid jobs are funded in USDC or COMPY from an escrow balance, and whether a debit or prepaid card is accepted is not stated.
