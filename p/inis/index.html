Free tierNew accounts get EUR 1.00 in credit, no card required, and prepaid credit does not expire. Three fixed sizes metered by the millisecond while a session runs: small 1 vCPU / 1 GB at EUR 0.0144 per hour, medium 2 vCPU / 4 GB at EUR 0.036 per hour, large 4 vCPU / 8 GB at EUR 0.072 per hour. A paused session is billed storage only, about EUR 0.07 per GB per month.

EgressOutbound access is open to the public internet by default, with an optional per-session domain allowlist; no egress allowance or overage price is published on the pricing or networking pages.
RetentionThe continuous-live backstop force-pauses any session after 24 hours live, and after 6 hours on an unverified trial account; a session paused by its own max_lifetime_ms can never be resumed (HTTP 410).
Interfacerest, cli REST API at https://api.inis.run (versioned under /v1, bearer authentication) and the inis CLI, whose inis ssh command opens an interactive shell
Toolingthe inis command-line tool, installed with the inis.run install script (static binary); it wraps the HTTP API and needs no SDK
Composes withPrepaid credit that never expires plus millisecond billing makes this the cheapest place to park a long-lived 1 vCPU agent that idles; the paused tier (hot, warm, cold) is what lets it wake in tens of milliseconds without paying for the wait.

NotesConsidered as floor candidate. The unverified-trial limits (1 live session, 5 GB disk, 6 hour backstop) are the part a new account actually meets. Isolation technology was not read. GPU: none, every size is CPU only.
