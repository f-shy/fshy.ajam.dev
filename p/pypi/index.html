100 MB/file, 10 GB/project (both raisable; 40 GB grants are routine for large projects). 20 project creations/hour/user, 10/day/organization, 40/hour/IP (warehouse config.py defaults). Files cannot be uploaded to a release older than 14 days (MAXIMUM_AGE_FOR_NEW_UPLOADS). Deleting a file frees quota; yanking does NOT.

Composes withUNBOUNDED via projects: unlimited projects x 10 GB each (raisable to 40 GB. Uv and ruff were both granted 40 GB). 10 TiB = 1,024 projects at default 10 GB. Project creation is rate limited to 20/hour and 40/hour per IP, which is the binding constraint, not bytes.
ReliabilityDeletion is permanent and irreversible ('cannot be undone without administrative intervention'). Releases are append-only in practice but files can be deleted to free project quota, so unlike npm this is not immutable-by-design. Treat it as stable-but-deletable. Quota enforcement is a hard 400 at upload, so budget against the 10 GB project figure.
