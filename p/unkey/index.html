Composes withIssue keys and verify them from any service over HTTPS; combine with a separate API gateway or with Unkey Compute for deployment, which is a paid product outside the free allowance.
ReliabilityRequests are not blocked on passing the quota immediately; admins are emailed and, if the workspace stays on Free 5 days later, the API is limited to 5 requests a minute until upgrade. Logs on Free are retained 7 days, so older failed verifications cannot be inspected.
