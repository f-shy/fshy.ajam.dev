The balance also pays for model and API calls made through platform keys, so compute and inference compete for the same USD 25.
Bindingvolume-quota The prepaid balance is the only limit: USD 25 at USD 0.0274 an hour is 912 hours, and nothing else in the published terms stops a box running.
Identityemail Sign in with an email address or a crypto wallet; a reachable email is a hard server-side gate and a required username follows. The USD 25 match needs a one-time USD 5 top-up by card or crypto; a card on file is not a boot condition, and credits are prepaid and non-refundable.
EgressNo egress charge or allowance is stated on the pricing or billing pages; a box can be kept off the internet in private mode and on a tailnet, and its public HTTPS URL is included at zbox.sh
Per-object capFour sizes: Tiny 1 vCPU / 2 GB RAM / 20 GB disk at $0.0068 per hour, Small 2 vCPU / 4 GB / 80 GB at $0.0137, Medium 4 vCPU / 8 GB / 160 GB at $0.0274 and Large 6 vCPU / 16 GB / 320 GB at $0.0548; the monthly column is the 24/7 price at each size. Larger XL / XXL sizes are listed as coming soon.
RetentionPausing is free and saves the disk with files, email and wallets intact; deleting removes the machine but keeps its name, email and wallets on hold. At $0 a one-hour grace window keeps running boxes alive before they are paused, and credits never expire.
Interfacerest, cli, browser A desktop in the browser and on a phone, an HTTP control plane at api.tribes.xyz, TypeScript / Python / Go / Rust SDKs, a CLI and a hosted MCP server at mcp.zipbox.ai; model and API calls bill the same balance
Toolingweb dashboard; per-box itemised model and API spend
Composes witha cheap 4 vCPU box whose balance also pays for model calls, so an agent and its inference share one wallet
Reliability'A positive balance is what boots a machine', so a zero balance stops the box rather than billing overage.
NotesUSD 25 at Medium's USD 0.0274 is 912 hours, so the match buys 1.25 months of a 4 vCPU / 8 GB / 160 GB machine for USD 5 out of pocket, which is the lowest cash cost for a 4 vCPU month anywhere among documented hosts. It is card-gated at signup and the balance is shared with inference, so it is not a free tier.
