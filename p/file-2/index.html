Free tierPrepaid kiwi credits: 50 kiwi free on signup; 400 kiwi $1.20, 800 kiwi $1.60, 4000 kiwi $3.99, 8000 kiwi $6.99. 1 kiwi = 1 GB uploaded or 1 GB of retention extended by 1 day; files kept 7 days by default. Web Folder Basic is $0 with unlimited upload size and 90-hour auto-delete.
unit GB; metered on unstated
Price$6.99 per TB for a 7-day hold = $6.99 × (30.4/7) ≈ $30.36/TB/mo to hold 1 TB continuously. To hold 20 TB: ~$607/mo. Roughly 30× the 1fichier/Blomp floor. Dead end for storage; only rational as a paid download-enabler for a one-off link.
