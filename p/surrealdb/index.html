free allowance: unlimited
Per-object capno per-object cap published on the pricing or Earth limits pages; records and transactions are metered by request units
RetentionFree pauses at a limit and keeps your data; an idle Earth instance scales to zero about 60 seconds after its last query. No automatic deletion of data on the free plan is stated.
Interfacegraph, rest, cli SurrealQL over WebSocket (wss://<instance-id>.surrealdb.earth/rpc) and HTTP, plus PostgreSQL and MySQL wire protocols; the surreal cloud management CLI and SurrealDB Studio browser UI
ToolingSurrealDB Studio, surreal cloud CLI, official SDKs, HTTP queries, and psql or mysql clients over TLS
Composes withthe serverless Earth free tier scales to zero and can be reached from a Worker or a server-side app; when steady capacity is needed the paid Cloud single-node plan is the next step
ReliabilityEarth is in beta; features, limits and pricing may change before general availability. Earth takes no snapshots and has no backup schedule, and an instance that reaches its Free allowance pauses until the first of the next month.
NotesThe free allowance is shared across all Earth instances in one organisation, so many small databases do not each get 3M requests. Earth cannot coordinate a transaction across instances and has no change feeds, extensions, JavaScript functions or reliable file buckets, so workloads that need those features must use the paid Start or Scale node plans. The always-free Cloud single node (1 GB, 0.25 vCPU) is a separate free offer from Earth Free.
