Free tierA Free plan exists and is described in the vendor's own FAQ: 'Images generated on the Free plan are owned by Recraft. They are publicly visible in the community gallery.' The size of the daily allowance is reported by a third party that read the vendor page on 2026-10-16 as '30 credits a day (about 15 Recraft V4.1 images) and up to two images per prompt', against 2 credits per V4.1 image, 10 for V4.1 Pro and 32 for Nano Banana Pro.
