A commercial VPN is a server IP, so it fails the same class test as a VPS unless the debrid service has whitelisted the range. The whitelist is by range and by provider, not by customer.
Bindingper-ip-shaping Nothing in the VPN's own product limits 1 TiB of monthly transfer; what fails is the debrid side's range check, which returns a block for the whole exit regardless of how much was paid.
Identitycard Checkout with card; the order page says 'Accepting Credit Cards, PayPal, Bitcoin, and other Crypto payments'. No identity document was mentioned on the page read.
EgressStandard plan: $12.95 per month on the monthly term ('Billed $12.95 for 1 month'), 'Billed $40.80 for 1 year' on the 1-year term, $2.15 per month on the 2-year term ('Billed $58.20 for 2 year', marked '+3 months'), and $1.69 per month on a Black Friday 5-year term ('Billed $101.55 for 5 year', renewing at $1.69/mo after 60 months). The order page states '6,000+ VPN servers' and does not state a bandwidth cap; the plan table sells features, not bytes.
Per-object capnot applicable: a tunnel, not an object
Interfacecli Vendor apps and OpenVPN/WireGuard-style configs depending on platform; the order page sells the subscription, not the protocol list.
Toolingvendor client on desktop and mobile; a VM would run the Linux client or a WireGuard config. The exit address is shared with other customers, which is the point and the risk.
Composes withThe approved egress class for Real-Debrid, AllDebrid and Mega-Debrid, which refuse servers and proxies but authorize VPN ranges: at $2.15-$12.95 a month for unlimited bytes it is the only egress product among those read that is cheaper than the debrid subscription it carries.
ReliabilityShared exits attract abuse, and one debrid service's range check is answered per address: the same subscription can work from one server and be refused from the next.
NotesIt is here only as the price of the approved egress class, and the egress topic owns VPN records; if promoted, merge the pricing with that topic's VPN set rather than keeping two homes. The affiliate pages that rank for 'real-debrid vpn' (vpnalert, vpncompare, vpntrends) all assert cooperative status for NordVPN, Surfshark, ExpressVPN and PIA; they are affiliate content and none of them was treated as evidence.
