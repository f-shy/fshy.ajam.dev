NotesIncudal sold some of the cheapest NAT containers in the market - the France entry plan was CNY 1.25 a month, which at the CBR rate dated 24.10.2026 is USD 0.1871 a month for 266 MB, 2 GB, 300 GB and a NAT IPv4 - and a 2026 gist put the range at USD 0.28 to USD 0.49 a month. All of it is withdrawn. The operator published a shutdown notice, refunded Alipay and WeChat orders to source, asked crypto users for a USDT BEP20 address, and closed self-operated instances in sequence with a temporary power-on offered for backups. The technical corroboration is direct: every path on incudal.com including the apex returns HTTP 530, and the surviving portal at incudal.di0.uk is a JavaScript shell with no catalogue in its HTML. This is the second NAT seller in this corner of the market to close in a year, after KhanWebHost's NATKVM, which is worth noting as a pattern rather than as a comment on any single operator. Not established: the exact date of closure, and whether any third-party 'marketplace' nodes on the Incus panel still sell.
