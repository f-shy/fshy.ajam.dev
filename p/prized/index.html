The 30-minute full-CPU rule can pause the workspace and answer 409 workspace_held on the next create or wake; it is a use cap that no other provider here imposes.
Bindingvolume-quota The 30-minute full-CPU rule is what fails first for any real build; the credit itself is generous at the Nano size.
Identityemail 'the tour starts a box on it with no card' and the credit is once per person by email or by Google or GitHub account. No card, no phone, no ID at signup; paid plans use Stripe Checkout, cards only.
Egressnot published: the billing, machines, sandboxes and pricing pages state no egress allowance and no overage price; outbound traffic from a box is not filtered
Per-object capOn the free credit 'Nano, Micro, Extra Small, or Small only'; Medium and larger answer 409 tier_unavailable. A Nano is 2 vCPU / 2 GB / 40 GB at USD 0.014 an hour awake.
RetentionAsleep, a box costs only its disk at USD 0.0001 per GB-hour; a Small asleep for a month is about USD 22. Credit rolls over up to two months of the plan.
Interfacessh, cli, rest SSH to the box (plain ssh or prized ssh); the prized CLI (macOS, Linux, Windows); an HTTP API for boxes and sandboxes; a browser dashboard
Toolingnot stated
Composes withnot established
ReliabilityOn the credit a box auto-pauses itself after 2 hours idle unless the window is changed or switched off per box; it never pauses in its first 24 hours.
NotesUSD 30 / USD 0.014 = 2,143 hours, which is 89 days of a 2 vCPU / 2 GB box with no card. The page's own claim of 'about three months on a Nano' matches. This is the longest no-card calendar coverage in this category and it is a real VM, not a sandbox, but a 2 vCPU / 2 GB box is also the smallest shape any row here offers for a full month.
