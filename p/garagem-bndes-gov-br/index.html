Free tierA free acceleration programme selecting 100 impact businesses per year, 400 across the third edition to 2028, split into a Criacao track for early ventures and a Tracao track for those expanding. The call carries prizes totalling BRL 915,000: BRL 64,000 to BRL 160,000 for each of five Tracao winners, BRL 32,000 to BRL 85,000 for each of five Criacao winners, and up to BRL 30,000 under a separate Mais Brasil award for the North, Northeast and periphery-economy ventures.

Composes withThe prize is the only cash in the programme; the acceleration itself is mentoring, so the value to a compute budget is the BRL 32,000 to BRL 160,000 award rather than anything the programme spends on the venture.
