Bindingper-ip-shaping The 60-minute cap bounds each session and the measured 26.9 KiB/s bounds everything those sessions can carry: at that rate a whole month is about 69.8 GB, 0.063 TiB, so the rate is what fails and it fails by a factor of 16 rather than at a quota.
Identityanonymous no account for the 60-minute tier; the SSH session is unauthenticated
Egressno byte quota; measured 26.9 KiB/s on a 10 MiB object, which is 2.2 GiB/day at best
Per-object capnot applicable: a stream rather than an object store
Retention60 minutes per unauthenticated connection; after expiry the tunnel URL terminates.
Interfacessh ssh -p 443 -R0:127.0.0.1:PORT [address removed]
ToolingOpenSSH
Composes withthe free outbound-only ingress for a host that cannot open a port, which is the sealed-sandbox shape. It composes with anything that speaks TCP and composes with nothing that needs throughput.
Reliabilitythe free hostname is a random subdomain that changes every session; a harness measured an 836 ms median latency and about 50 kB/s, so a 4 MiB transfer does not complete inside the 60-minute session.
Notes2026-10-23: the free tier carries UDP, not only SSH TCP. Pinggy documents 'ssh -p 443 -R0:localhost:51820 [address removed]' as the way to expose a self-hosted WireGuard server, and the free host form returns a public UDP port such as udp://rndnj-103-170-183-11.a.free.pinggy.online:43210, so wg_path is inside-udp.
