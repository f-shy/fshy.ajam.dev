Interfacerest REST API at api.gladia.io (POST /v2/pre-recorded starts a job), plus WebSockets for live transcription and the official SDKs, documented.
ToolingOfficial SDKs: JavaScript/TypeScript (npm @gladiaio/sdk) and Python (pip gladiaio-sdk); the REST API is used directly otherwise; live transcription uses WebSockets.
Composes with312 hours of async transcription for free is the cheapest per-hour speech-to-text lane found among evaluated offerings, and it composes with a free object store for the audio and a free runner for the upload loop. What breaks: the one-year clock, and the 73-hour real-time figure, which is a quarter of the async allowance because real-time bills the connection rather than the audio.

NotesThese are inference credits, not general compute. This is a French vendor billing in EUR, so the figure is EUR 50 and not a converted USD number. Not established: whether signup demands a card; the page offers 'Start with EUR 50 free credits' with a direct app link and states no card requirement, but the form was not opened. Model training is 'Opt-in by default (free opt-out anytime)' on the standard tier and 'Opted out by default' on the enterprise column, which changes what the same 50 EUR buys in privacy terms rather than in hours.
