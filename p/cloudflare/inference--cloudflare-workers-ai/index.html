Free tier10,000 Neurons per day at no charge on both the Free and the Paid Workers plan. The page states the daily figure; the monthly figure of 300,000 and the token equivalents below are derived arithmetic on it, not vendor statements
unit tokens; metered on request-count; billing is in Neurons. The page prints a per-token price beside each model as an equivalent, so a single USD per million tokens figure for Workers AI does not exist. The free-plan input rate runs from 1,542 to 60,000 neurons per million tokens across 31 models, a factor of 38.9, so the token count behind one allowance is model-dependent: 194.6 million a month at the cheapest and 5.0 million at the dearest Derived figures and their bases: 300,000 = 10,000 x 30 days; 122.1 million input tokens = 300,000 / 2,457 x 1,000,000; 4.07 million a day = that divided by 30

Notes300,000 Neurons a month, not 375 million: 10,000 a day times 30. At 2,457 neurons per million input tokens that is 122.1 million input tokens a month and 4.07 million a day. The reference workload of 15 million input and 37.5 million output tokens a month on llama-3.2-1b-instruct needs 15 x 2,457 + 37.5 x 18,252 = 721,305 neurons, which is 2.4 times the whole free allowance; the output half alone is 2.3 times it. The allocation is real, it is model-dependent, and it cannot carry the reference workload, which is why the row is a fallback. Every figure beyond the page's own 10,000 a day is arithmetic on it and is labelled as such in free_tier.stated.
