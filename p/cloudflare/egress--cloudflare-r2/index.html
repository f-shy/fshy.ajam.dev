Free tier10 GB-month storage, 1M Class A and 10M Class B operations per month, egress free
unit GB; metered on stored-bytes; Standard storage only; the free tier does not apply to Infrequent Access. Above it, Standard storage costs USD 0.015 / GB-month, Class A operations USD 4.50 / million requests and Class B operations USD 0.36 / million requests; usage rounds up to the next billing unit (1.1 GB-month bills as 2 GB-month).
PriceUSD 15 per TB-month of Standard storage beyond the free 10 GB-month (USD 0.015 / GB-month); egress USD 0
Limits
  • request-rate 1M Class A and 10M Class B operations per month source
  • per-object-cap 4.995 TiB per object (5 TiB less 5 GiB); 4.995 GiB per upload request or part source
  • volume-quota 10 GB-month of Standard storage free each month; storage above it is billed at USD 0.015 / GB-month source
  • request-rate r2.dev public endpoint: variable rate limit, HTTP 429 above hundreds of requests per second, bandwidth may be throttled; a custom domain carries no such stated limit source
the 10 GB-month of free Standard storage is a monthly allowance and not a cap: storage above it is billed at USD 0.015 / GB-month, Class A operations above 1 million at USD 4.50 / million and Class B operations above 10 million at USD 0.36 / million
Bindingnone nothing binds egress, which is free when read directly from R2; the 10 GB-month of free Standard storage (volume-quota) is a billing threshold and not a cap, since storage above it is billed at USD 0.015 / GB-month
Identitycard A Cloudflare account (sign-up form: email and password; the address is verified by a mailed message) plus an R2 subscription added through a dashboard checkout, which needs a valid payment method on file even for the free monthly usage: a card (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, UnionPay), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Link, or a USDC stablecoin wallet whose permit covers R2 usage charges. A billing address is confirmed with the payment method, and gift or pre-payment cards may be refused for lacking one. No R2 API token can be created before the purchase. A phone number or a manual review at the checkout: unverified: behind a sign-up.
Egressfree for every storage class when read directly from R2 (Workers API, S3 API, r2.dev); metered services connected to a bucket may charge for their part, and Infrequent Access reads carry a USD 0.01 / GB data-retrieval fee
free allowance: none for egress: egress to the internet is free on every plan
Per-object cap5 TiB per object (5 GiB less than 5 TiB, so 4.995 TiB); one upload request or part carries at most 4.995 GiB (5 MiB less than 5 GiB), up to 10,000 parts (5492189429760 bytes)
Retentionnot applicable: bytes persist while the account is in good standing
Interfaces3-compatible, rest https://<account>.r2.cloudflarestorage.com
Toolingaws-cli, rclone (s3 backend)
Composes withthe origin that makes a free front free: R2 holds the bytes with zero egress and a Cloudflare front serves them, so the read path costs nothing at any volume and the 10 GB is the whole bill.
Reliabilitya failed payment-method preauthorization blocks access to the buckets (requests return errors), and the data may be deleted if the payment method is not updated within 30 days
