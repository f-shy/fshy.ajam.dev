Free tierA daily allowance of 10,000 Neurons on both the Workers Free and the Workers Paid plan: 'Our free allocation allows anyone to use a total of 10,000 Neurons per day at no charge.' Limits reset daily at 00:00 UTC. Neurons convert to tokens at a per-model rate, so the same 10,000 Neurons buys a different number of output tokens on each model: @cf/meta/llama-3.1-8b-instruct yields 133K output tokens a day at 75147 Neurons per 1M, @cf/qwen/qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8 yields 328K at 30475 per 1M and @cf/meta/llama-3.2-1b-instruct yields 548K at 18252 per 1M, a spread of about 4 across these three models. Eight models require a paid billing method rather than the Workers Free plan alone: kimi-k2.6, kimi-k2.7-code, glm-5.2, glm-5.3, glm-5.3-flash, deepseek-v4-flash-0731, deepseek-v4.1-flash-1017 and deepseek-v4-pro-0813.
unit minutes; metered on wall-clock; recurring-daily-allowance

Limits
  • request-rate (binding) Text generation is limited to 300 requests per minute, a default per task type that the limits page does not tie to one plan and that applies unless the model requires the Workers Paid plan. The models that require the Workers Paid plan are limited per account and per model to 20 requests per minute on standard Workers AI billing and 50 requests per minute with prepaid AI Gateway credits. Token volume is metered separately by the daily Neuron allowance on the pricing page. source

NotesAll arithmetic is an estimate and not a vendor figure, but the conversion factor is checkable against the vendor's own two columns: USD 0.011 per 1,000 Neurons is USD 11 per 1M Neurons, and 34,868 Neurons per 1M output tokens for llama-3.1-8b-fp8-fast times USD 11 per 1M Neurons is USD 0.384, exactly the price printed beside it. The same check holds for qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8 (30,475 x USD 11 / 1M = USD 0.335). So Neurons are a linear repricing of GPU seconds and the free allowance has a fixed dollar value of 10,000 x USD 0.011 / 1,000 = USD 0.11 a day, or USD 3.30 a month, whatever model is chosen. The model choice therefore buys tokens, not dollars: 328K output tokens a day on qwen3-30b-a3b-fp8 is about 9.8M a month, and 548K on llama-3.2-1b is about 16.4M a month at visibly lower quality. Verdict is qualifies-with-caveat because no probe was run: the REST endpoint needs a real account id and an API token, and the keyless probe against a placeholder account id returned 404 from the origin, which establishes only that the route requires a credential.
