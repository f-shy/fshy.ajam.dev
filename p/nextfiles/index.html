Bindingper-object-cap The price curve decides the tier and the tier is small, so the unstated per-file cap is the number a buyer cannot plan around. At EUR 10/TB/month on the 10 TB plan it is also four times Serverdiscounter, which is why it is recorded as a small-instance option.
Identitycard a nextfiles.one account and a card. The trial needs no card, per the plan text, and the paid order does. No identity document stated
EgressNo egress price and no traffic quota are published on the private-customer plans, so this field is false by the schema's own definition rather than on a stated guarantee. The features block says 'Unlimited storage space in the Cloud', which is about capacity and not about traffic
Per-object capNot published as a per-file cap on the private-customer plans. The plan table publishes storage, a recommended user count and the storage type ('Cluster')
RetentionContract term from one month with automatic renewal for the same period; a consumer may cancel any time before a new billing period. Disaster-recovery backups of the last 7 days are kept, with managed or own backups offered for up to 10 years
Interfacewebdav, rest, rclone
Toolingrclone webdav against any Nextcloud CE plan; the Nextcloud clients; administrator access to the AppStore. The Seafile Pro plans need the Seafile client or the Seafile WebDAV extension, which is a weaker tooling story
Composes withReseller providing managed Seafile hosting with library-level client-side encryption. The cost structure is lowest on smaller capacities and increases per terabyte on larger allocations, suiting small-scale deployments rather than bulk archives.
ReliabilityGermany, ISO 27001 data centres, renewable power. The per-terabyte price rises with size, which is the opposite of every other reseller here and is the signal that the large tiers are not built for price comparison
