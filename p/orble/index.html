Free tierNo published recurring free tier: usage is paid from prepaid credit. Free credit, such as credit sent by email, has no cash value and is used before credit paid for; an account made after 2026-11-13 must verify a card to use free credit, and one card unlocks free credit for one account. An account with no added credit can have 3 boxes at once.
unit none; metered on wall-clock; The card check for free credit is a Stripe verification that charges nothing. The amount of free credit sent by email is not published on the pages read. The S box is $0.015 per hour billed per second; a suspended box is $0 and storage is $0.05 per GiB-month.

Idle boxes suspend automatically and resume with state intact. Suspended boxes are billed only for storage. Boxes are suspended when credit runs out, not deleted, and may be deleted after 30 days at zero credit with an email warning.
Bindingvolume-quota Prepaid credit and the per-second meter are what run out first; the 3-box ceiling applies only to accounts that have never added credit.
Identityemail Sign-in uses a Google or GitHub account; signing in creates an Orble account and Google and GitHub sign-ins with the same verified email address reach the same account. The terms require the user to be at least 16. Using free credit on an account made after 2026-11-13 requires verifying a card with Stripe, charged nothing, one card per account; Pro is $20 per month or $200 per year charged to a card, and usage is always paid from prepaid credit.
EgressEgress rules control outbound network access on Pro; no egress price or allowance is stated on the pricing page read
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is a machine, not an object; the smallest S box has 64 GiB of disk
RetentionA box that stays suspended at zero credit for 30 days may be deleted after an email warning; suspending a box keeps memory and running processes, and only storage is billed while suspended.
Interfacessh, rest, cli Each box is a Linux VM with root access and SSH; SDKs for TypeScript and Python, an MCP server at https://orble.tech/mcp, and E2B and Daytona API compatibility.
endpoint: https://orble.tech/mcp
ToolingThe user installs the Orble CLI from https://orble.tech/install.sh to drive hosted boxes; TypeScript and Python SDKs, MCP clients and E2B/Daytona API endpoints are also documented. The capacity is hosted; the CLI is only a client.
Composes withA hosted Linux box with root, SSH and a per-port HTTPS URL; suspend keeps memory and processes for later resume, so state that must outlive a 30-day zero-credit suspension needs a separate storage record.
ReliabilityWhen credit runs out boxes are suspended, not deleted; a box suspended at zero credit for 30 days may be deleted after an email warning. An account found to abuse free credit or payment rules is suspended after a review.
NotesPaid-only: no published recurring free allowance; a Google or GitHub identity plus prepaid credit is required, with a Stripe card check for free credit. Hosted VM sandbox; the CLI the user installs only drives it.
