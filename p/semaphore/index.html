Per-object capexecution_time_limit is 1 hour by default and at most 24 hours (1,440 minutes); jobs are terminated at the limit, and a block or job limit longer than its pipeline's is rejected

NotesThe pricing page names one free allowance, USD 15 a month for every organization.
