Free tierDeveloper plan, free, no card: 1,000 API calls a month with a daily refresh at 00:00 UTC, USD as the only base currency; no daily or hourly rate limit.
unit requests; metered on request-count; The free key returns latest rates and the symbol endpoints only; historical rates need a paid plan, from Starter at USD 9.99 a month for 15,000 calls. The supported-currencies, currency-symbols and historical-data-limits endpoints need no key. The monthly quota may be spent in one day. At the free limit one email goes out and the API stops serving until the next month.
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 1,000 API calls a month on the Developer plan source
  • retention-clamped latest rates only on the free key; historical rates need a paid plan source
Rate: no daily or hourly limit on any plan. Bulk path: none on the free key; the time series and fluctuation endpoints need Professional at USD 99.99 a month, and the vendor blog shows a Python loop that writes the series to CSV.
Bindingvolume-quota The monthly count of 1,000 is the only limit, so it binds; the quota may be spent in one day.
Identityemail Free sign-up with Google or GitHub, or an email; no card. The terms say to sign up with a real company or personal email, that disposable addresses and shared mailboxes are deleted, and that accounts made by automated methods are removed. The sign-up runs on the vendor's APIFreaks platform.
Egressnot metered in bytes; the 1,000 calls a month bound what a free key can pull
