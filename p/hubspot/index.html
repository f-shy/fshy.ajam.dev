Per-object-cap: 20 MB per file on free tools (the binding constraint by a wide margin); the docs note 'If a file is 1 GB or more, you may experience issues'. request-rate: 100 req / 10 s / app and 250,000 / day per account on Free ('Privately distributed apps, Free and Starter, 100/app, 250,000/account'). Retention: none documented. Files persist until deleted. Download URLs can 429 if the rate limit is exceeded.
