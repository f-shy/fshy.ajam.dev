NotesThe strongest sibling found, and the one that makes this class worth a document. It is better shaped than the Open Intelligence Foundation offer on four counts the operator states itself: the top amount is an order of magnitude larger, the payment is cash rather than a donated machine, the recipient keeps the IP, and the winner list is public, so selection is observable rather than anecdotal. The failure mode is arithmetic and must travel with the number: 24 fellows selected from 11,813 applications is 0.20 percent, and adding the roughly 20 grants lifts the ceiling to about 0.37 percent. The window has closed for 2026: applications ran 1 January to 30 April 2026 and the FAQ states 'There will be no deadline extensions'. Reopening on 1 January 2027 is an expectation from the posting pattern, not a published commitment; no 2027 dates appeared on any page read. One internal tension worth noting: the page's 'No committee approvals' line sits against a published three-stage process that runs through a team call and a deeper conversation before a decision, so read that line as framing rather than as a statement about process.
