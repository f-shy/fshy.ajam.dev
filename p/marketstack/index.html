Free tierFree plan USD 0/month, free forever: 100 requests per month, end-of-day data, 1 year of history, no support
unit requests; metered on request-count; Each symbol in a request consumes one request, up to 100 symbols per call, and a page holds up to 1000 rows. The APILayer product page shows the free plan as 100 Total Requests while its FAQ on the same page says 100 per month; the marketstack.com pricing page and FAQ say per month. The cheapest paid plan is Basic at USD 8.99/month billed yearly with 10,000 requests per month. US end-of-day prices are licensed from Tiingo.

History window: 1 year on the free plan, 10 years on Basic, 15+ years above; no limits[] kind names a history window. Bulk path: no bulk path.
Bindingrequest-rate 100 a month, with each symbol counted, is spent long before the 5 per second rate or the row caps matter.
Identityemail The free signup form asks for a first name, last name, email and password, and under Billing Details an address, postal code, country and city (state optional); no card field was seen on the free form. Card payment is named for paid plans.
Egressnot metered in bytes; the 100 requests a month bound what a free key can pull
Per-object cap1000 rows per page and 100 symbols per call; 1 year of history on the free plan
Interfacerest GET https://api.marketstack.com/v2/eod?access_key=...&symbols=AAPL over HTTPS on every plan; v1 paths remain. A call with no access_key answered HTTP 401 missing_access_key.
endpoint: https://api.marketstack.com/v2
Toolingcurl or any HTTP client
Composes withIts US end-of-day prices are licensed from Tiingo, whose free plan allows 1000 requests a day against 100 a month here, so Tiingo is the larger free route to the same prices.
ReliabilityPast the monthly volume the API answers HTTP 429 too_many_requests, and past 5 requests per second it answers 429 rate_limit_reached; API errors are not counted toward the monthly quota; an error answer is a JSON object with an error member holding a code and a message.
NotesA working free key, but 100 requests a month with each symbol counted and 1 year of end-of-day history is too small for a data workload; the same US prices come from Tiingo, whose free plan is 1000 requests a day with 30+ years of history.
Evidence notedocumented The free quota, history and signup fields are documented on the pricing, FAQ and signup pages; the 5 per second rate is stated on the current Getting Started page of the APILayer documentation. A probe measured that a call with no key answers HTTP 401. The monthly cut-off answer, its body and its reset moment need a free key to measure (unverified: behind a sign-up).
