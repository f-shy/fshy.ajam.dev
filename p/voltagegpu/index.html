Free tierNo free tier. Prepaid per-second GPU rental in two tiers. Standard tier (no enclave): from USD 0.32 per GPU-hour; floors of USD 0.70 for the A100 80 GB and USD 0.61 for the RTX 4090 on the vendor page, and the index lists the H100 SXM at USD 3.60, the L40S at USD 0.76 and the RTX 4090 at USD 0.70. Confidential VM tier (Intel TDX): H100 USD 6.95, H200 USD 8.08, RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell USD 3.80 per GPU-hour. The index lists no L4. The only allowance is one RTX PRO 6000 hour (USD 3.80) added to a first top-up of USD 10 or more.
unit none; metered on wall-clock; none

Bindingnone No quota is stated: the wall is the prepaid balance and the per-GPU-hour rate. Capacity is first come, first served and the standard tier is a distributed host network with no enclave.

Composes withPer-second billing with a USD 5 minimum top-up makes short GPU runs cheap to start: an L40S at USD 0.76 or a 4090 at USD 0.61 to USD 0.70 an hour. Capacity is first come, first served, with no reservations.
ReliabilityThe index records 'Prepaid only, no invoicing on credit' and 'No reservations: capacity is first come, first served'; on 8x H100 confidential nodes the NVSwitch fabric is not attested. No outage was observed.
