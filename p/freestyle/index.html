unit hours; metered on wall-clock; An allowance that resets monthly, worth about USD 13.22 at the vendor's own rates, and a hard stop when it runs out. This is not a USD 100 credit.

ReliabilityA Free VM that hits the monthly allowance stops with no overage path, and Free has no persistent VM, so state has to be snapshotted or rebuilt.
NotesThe free allowance is real, but the useful comparison is against a month of uptime: 730 hours of a 1 vCPU box needs 730 vCPU-hours and 730 GiB-hours of memory, against 200 and 400 included. A 4 vCPU box lasts 50 hours. The allowance is worth USD 8.06 of vCPU plus USD 5.16 of memory at list, so about USD 13.22 a month, not USD 100.
