NotesLuxor Agent Cloud (formerly Tenki) sells disposable Linux VM sandboxes, GitHub Actions runners and a code reviewer from one workspace balance. The default Small sandbox is 2 vCPU / 4 GiB / 5 GiB at USD 0.17 per running hour from the per-second rate card, and the free Starter plan allows 5 concurrent sessions with a USD 100 one-time credit that requires adding a card. A paid Team plan raises the limits but does not change the per-second rates. The card requirement to unlock the free credit, and the USD 20 minimum top-up when the balance runs out, are the caveats.

Sandbox rates beyond the credit are $0.00084 per vCPU-minute, $0.00027 per GiB-minute, $0.0000018 per GiB-minute of disk after the first 5 GiB, and $0.20 per GiB-month of storage from the first byte. Billing is per second with no minimums.
Bindingvolume-quota The USD 100 credit and the Starter machine ceiling bind first: credit covers roughly 25,000 minutes on a 2 vCPU runner and a larger Starter size is blocked until a payment method is on file.

RetentionA paused sandbox keeps its files and memory; a non-sticky session is paused automatically at its maximum duration; disks are wiped on teardown; persistent volumes and snapshots are managed separately.
Interfacecli, ssh The luxor CLI and Go, TypeScript and Python SDKs against https://api.luxor.ai; the sandbox CLI reference documents port exposure and SSH into a session. No public IP is assigned.
