The product page makes no promise about concurrency, IP or password. The only consumer protection named is 'Garantia 7 dias Satisfacao ou seu dinheiro de volta', which is shorter than the 30 days sold.
Bindingper-ip-shaping The account is sold to multiple buyers from one registration, so the first failure at any real volume is the service noticing simultaneous sessions from different addresses, not a byte quota.
Identityemail A store account and an email address are enough to order: 'Envio Imediato - Entrega automatica em Segundos'. No CPF is requested on the product page; payment methods are not listed there, so the Pix path is unverified.
EgressThe reseller publishes no traffic terms. The account carries whatever Real-Debrid grants, and Real-Debrid's own account-sharing enforcement applies to a credential that several buyers may hold.
Per-object capnot applicable: the underlying service is Real-Debrid, whose own host and file caps apply. The reseller adds none.
Retention30 days: 'Conta Oficial do Real-debrid Por 30 dias'. After that the credential expires unless renewed through the same seller. (30 days)
Interfacerest The buyer receives Real-Debrid login credentials by email - 'voce recebera por email os dados, para utilizar direto no site do Real-debrid'. Everything after that is Real-Debrid's own web panel and API, not the reseller's.
ToolingReal-Debrid's API and the usual add-ons work with the credential, because it is a real Real-Debrid account. The buyer does not control the recovery email.
Composes withThis is a payment rail, not a capability: it converts Pix-era Brazilian payment into a Real-Debrid session. It chains with any Stremio or torrent add-on exactly as a direct subscription would, with one extra failure point between the buyer and the service.
ReliabilityThe buyer holds credentials registered by somebody else and cannot recover them: the recovery email is the seller's, the password can be rotated by the seller at any time, and Real-Debrid's own terms treat use from several addresses as grounds for a block. The 7-day guarantee is the entire recourse, against a 30-day product.
NotesThe word 'Oficial' in the listing means 'a genuine Real-Debrid account' as opposed to a cracked or trial one; it does not mean the buyer becomes the account holder. Three other Brazilian storefronts - contasvip.com.br at R$ 25,00, mercadocontas.com.br at R$ 24,90 and premiumturbo.com.br with 30/90/365-day variants - carry the same product copy and the same guarantee sentence, which is the signature of one wholesale source behind several storefronts.
