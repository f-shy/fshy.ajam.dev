Currency conversion is available to the seller ('Conversao automatica de moedas'), and international balances settle in USD, so the buyer is charged in a converted amount.
Bindingwrite-gated A foreign buyer is not blocked, only narrowed to a card. The gate that decides is whether the buyer's card is accepted; the local rails are simply absent.
Identityemail Email plus card for a foreign buyer. CPF and mobile are hidden outside Brazil: 'os estrangeiros nao precisam de CPF'.
Egressnot applicable.
Per-object capnot applicable: a checkout, not a resource.
Interfacerest A hosted checkout page at pay.kiwify.com.br with a URL parameter contract: '?region=br' forces the Brazilian fields (CPF, mobile, boleto, Pix, installments) regardless of the buyer's address.
ToolingBrowser only. The buyer selects a country in the checkout, fills card details, and pays. A Brazilian buyer abroad can switch the selector back to Brazil to restore Pix and boleto.
Composes withKiwify is where a Brazilian reseller's R$ 24.90 lands. For a foreign buyer the chain is: checkout card (no CPF) -> seller receives -> seller delivers credentials. The card step is the only one a foreigner can complete.
ReliabilityThe country gate is IP-based and the buyer can override it with a URL parameter, so a Brazilian abroad keeps Pix; a foreigner cannot obtain it because the CPF field, not the IP, is the real requirement.
NotesKiwify is the checkout under a large share of Brazilian digital resellers, including stores that sell streaming and premium accounts. The practical consequence for debrid: a foreigner can buy from a Kiwify storefront with a card and will be charged a converted amount, but cannot use the Pix price that the listing advertises.

The accepted card brands list (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Elo, Hipercard, Diners, Discover, Aura, JCB) is from a separate Kiwify help article dated 2023 in the index.
