Free tierTenstorrent's own documentation offers hardware-backed browser inference to people who own none of its hardware: 'No hardware yet? console.tenstorrent.com lets you run LLM inference and image and video generation in the browser, backed by Tenstorrent hardware.' The console catalogue entry adds 'Cloud hardware access and advanced workflows (deployments, agents) available in staged rollout.' The paid lane is Tenstorrent Cloud, sold per instance of 1 to 128 Wormhole or 1 to 32 Blackhole processors, up to 2 TB memory and 96 vCPU, price on request.

Composes withThe console is the cheapest way to touch Tensix silicon and it chains with the open-source TT-Metalium and TT-Forge stacks, which compile locally at no cost. It breaks at scale: anything beyond a demo needs a Cloud instance or the Koyeb rental, both billed.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesTwo open questions, stated rather than filled: whether the console costs nothing at all, and what it caps. The console terms say 'Certain Cloud Services and Models may be designated as fee-free or otherwise available without triggering a payment for a limited time or based on usage limits', which describes a fee-free tier without naming it. A signup attempt is the only route and verification holds no account.
