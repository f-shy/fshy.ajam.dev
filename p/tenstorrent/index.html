The console catalogue adds 'Cloud hardware access and advanced workflows (deployments, agents) available in staged rollout.' The Cloud Services Agreement says 'Certain Cloud Services and Models may be designated as fee-free or otherwise available without triggering a payment for a limited time or based on usage limits.' No catalogue price or free-tier size is published on the pages read.

Composes withThe console is the cheapest way to touch Tensix silicon and it chains with the open-source TT-Metalium and TT-Forge stacks, which compile locally at no cost. It breaks at scale: anything beyond a demo needs a Cloud instance or the Koyeb rental, both billed.

NotesTwo open questions, stated rather than filled: whether the console costs nothing at all, and what it caps.
