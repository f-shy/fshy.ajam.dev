NotesThe allocation is the largest of any no-card row among evaluated providers, and the imprint is unusually honest about the seller: it states in its own words that the company is a 'planned' S.R.L. that will be registered 'when the platform reaches operational viability'. That is precisely the pattern comparative records was burned by once, so it is classified as a caveat in the same sentence as the offer rather than as a disqualification. Two further defects: the two product pages give different free allocations (3 GB with 8 GB of storage on the game tier, 1024 MB with 5 GB NVMe on the bot tier), and no page states a traffic allowance or a sleep rule. It is also a game and bot host, not a general machine.
