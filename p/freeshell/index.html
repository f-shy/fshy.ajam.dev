Free tier'SSH to Ubuntu 24.04 LTS server with many tools and apps.' and 'Quota: 42 GB', 'Network: IPv4 + IPv6', 'Private TCP/UDP port: yes — on request'. The shell account is free and non-profit, online since 2002.
unit GiB; metered on stored-bytes
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • volume-quota 42 GB of shell quota, shared with mail and web space source
  • volume-quota nproc=150 and mem=4 GB per account source
  • write-gated (binding) activation requires a physical postcard mailed to a German address source
Bindingwrite-gated The postcard is the gate: nothing is provisioned until a piece of physical mail reaches Germany, which is a delay of days and a cost of one stamp, and no digital route exists.
Identityemail no card, no phone, no document, no national ID. The signup form asks for a desired login and an existing email address only; a token arrives by email and is then written on a postcard and mailed to a German address before the account is enabled. The identity cost is a postal address, not a payment instrument.
Egressnot published: no traffic allowance or port speed is stated. 'Liberal fair-use limits' is the only qualifier.
Per-object capulimit values per account: 'nofile=unlimited', 'nproc=150', 'fsize=unlimited', 'mem=4 GB'
RetentionNot stated on the pages read: no inactivity deletion rule is published. Backups run daily with 30 days retention, which is the only retention number on the page.
Interfacessh, sftp, webdav SSH to Ubuntu 24.04 LTS, a ShellInABox browser terminal, rsync/scp, WebDAV through ownCloud, MySQL over the CLI and phpMyAdmin, and a private TCP/UDP port on request. Podman is available for containers.
Toolingany SSH client; Podman for containers; rsync/scp for transfer
Composes withA shared shell with 4 GB of memory, 42 GB of disk, a real IPv4 and a private port on request, for no money and no card: the closest thing among evaluated providers to a free always-on host, at the cost of a physical postcard and a shared kernel.
Reliabilityno reliability event observed. It is a non-profit club, not a company, so continuity rests on volunteers; the page states it has been online since 2002.
NotesThe best free always-on shape found among evaluated providers that takes no card: 4 GB of memory, 42 GB of disk and an inbound port. Three caveats. First, activation is by physical postcard to Germany, which is days of latency and a stamp, and it is the only route. Second, it is a shared host, not a VM: no root, a shared kernel, and Podman rather than Docker daemon access. Third, no vCPU count and no egress allowance are published, so the compute and bandwidth sides of the shape are unverified. Not previously in earlier readings. A third-party aggregator states 20 GB and 1 Gbps for this service; both disagree with the vendor's own 42 GB, and the vendor page is the one recorded.
Evidence notedocumented Quota, memory, nproc, the IPv4+IPv6 pair, the private port and the postcard activation are all quoted from freeshell.de's own services and get-started pages read. Egress allowance, vCPU count and the inactivity rule are not published on any page read.
