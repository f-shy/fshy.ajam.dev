Bindingnone Lite's 50 GB/month is exhausted in under two hours at 1 TiB/month, so the plan choice is the binding decision. Unlimited removes the byte counter and leaves connection count as the only ceiling.

Interfaceunknown Access is NNTP over TLS (port 563). The schema's interface enum has no nntp token, so this is documented as unknown rather than mislabelled as rest or cli; the real interface is a standard protocol every usenet client speaks.
ToolingEvery usenet client: SABnzbd, NZBGet, nzbget-compatible downloaders, spotweb. No provider-side API is needed or offered.
Composes withThe Unlimited plan is the feed behind a debrid or *arr stack: 100 connections and 6642 days of retention are what make an old post still downloadable, and 'Unlimited Data' is the only uncapped byte path in this whole lane.
ReliabilityNo incident found in the pages read. The plan page publishes 'Regularly: $19.94/mo' struck through against the $12.95 charged, which is a promotional anchor rather than a reliability matter.
NotesThe charged price has not moved in 36 months; what changed is that the vendor began publishing a list price of $19.94 in 2025 that it then discounts to $12.95. The page does not say whether a renewal bills $12.95 or $19.94, and the honest reading is that $12.95 is the price as long as the promotion runs - so the exposure is 54% on renewal, not a discount that can be banked. This is the largest published promo-versus-list gap in the set after Eweka. A client that pulls older releases meets removal far more often than age. Cheapest Omicron entry seen on any of its storefronts was a 15-month promo at $1.99/mo quoted by a third-party deals page, not by the vendor, so it is not documented as a price here.
