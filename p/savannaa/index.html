NotesThis is the closest thing found to an unlisted regional equivalent of the AWS micro free tier, and it is absent from every English roundup checked. The identity gate is unusually light for a regional provider: an email plus a phone number from one of four African countries for recovery, with no card and no national ID stated on the signup page. Two caveats: the hour pool is 12 months only, and the free tier page says 'Always free: Not offered yet', so the operator is explicit that this is introductory. The NGN 10,000 credit converts to roughly USD 7.5 at the published rate of NGN 1,327.24 per USD, which the vendor itself states is an estimate and not a price. The rate card lets the allowance be priced exactly: t2.micro is listed at NGN 32,438.35 a month, so the 750 free hours are about NGN 389,000 of compute across the twelve months, before the pooled egress allowance. That is a small number in absolute terms and a complete one: an always-on micro instance in Lagos for a year at zero cost, from a provider that appears in no English roundup checked. The exclusion that decides the verdict is the public IP: the free hours are compute hours, and a reachable server also needs an address billed at NGN 4,360 a month. That is a small sum, and it is the difference between a free tier and a free tier that works. The one recorded is the better-evidenced.
