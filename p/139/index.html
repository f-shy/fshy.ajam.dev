Bindingwrite-gated The capacity and the egress both qualify, and the gate is the carrier identity: a China Mobile subscriber number, with the promotions redeemable only inside the carrier app. The write gate on the driver's parameters is the second lock.
Identitycountry-phone +86 only, and the login is tied to a China Mobile subscriber identity in practice. Promotions are redeemed inside the carrier app's 权益超市, not on the web
EgressFree and unthrottled by design: '不限速：上传下载不限速', and members get carrier zero-rating on China Mobile. Forum consensus agrees China Mobile does not shape, unlike Baidu at 0.1 MB/s and Quark at 1.7 MB/s
free allowance: unlimited on net
Per-object capVaries by tier; single-file and upload caps are not published as one number
RetentionPromotional capacity expires. No retention clock on stored files is published
Interfacerest, webdav
Toolingan official open platform at open.yun.139.com, application and approval based; the OpenList 139Yun driver works off a browser-captured Authorization header and needs v3.41.0 or later; netdisk-fast-download's p139 parser is in the paid tier
Composes with1 TB free with no shaping is genuinely attractive for serving, and multiplying by accounts is the only path. The gate is a China Mobile number, and the large grants ride the carrier plan and evaporate when it lapses
ReliabilityIntegration relies on reverse-engineered driver code. Share endpoints change structure between getOutLinkInfoV5 and V6, both returning resultCode 01000101 to unauthenticated callers. Promotional capacity allocations expire on fixed schedules, preventing long-term retention.
