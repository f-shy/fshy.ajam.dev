Bindingvolume-quota 256 MB of RAM and 1.5 GB of disk bind long before the price or the traffic does; this is a single small service per box, not a host.
Identityemail The register page asks for an email address, a verification code sent to that email, a password and a password confirmation, plus a privacy checkbox. No card field, no phone field, no address field and no identity-document field is on it. The payment rails are not on any page read because the checkout sits behind the account.
Egress500 Mbit, unlimited traffic on fair use ('500M不限流量(合理使用)'). The vendor states that if a server runs past its traffic allowance the term is extended to compensate rather than the box being cut off: '部分服务器流量有限，若流量超额，会补偿时长至流量恢复。'
free allowance: unlimited, fair use
Retentionrefunds rather than retention are documented: the vendor's pre-purchase note says products at CNY 5 and above support a 24-hour refund and others can 'self-refund' to account balance within 24 hours, and that if a product fails the remaining value is returned to balance. No deletion window for an unpaid service is stated
Interfacessh Root in an LXC container over SSH, reachable on one of the 10 assigned IPv4 ports or directly on the dedicated IPv6 address. OS images offered are Linux only: Debian 13 and Alpine 3.23 are the two named on the configuration page.
Toolingany SSH client. The panel is a Chinese IDC billing system; no API is documented. The vendor states LXC containers may not themselves run Docker, because LXC and Docker are the same class of virtualization.
Composes withUSD 0.150 a month, the lowest price found among evaluated providers by a wide margin, for 256 MB and 1.5 GB with a dedicated IPv6 and 10 IPv4 ports; at this price it is a throwaway probe, a DNS resolver or a second tunnel endpoint, and the vendor itself calls the products 'toys'.
ReliabilityThe vendor publishes a status probe at status.jjfly.cloud and states in its own pre-purchase note that the products are cheap and positioned as toys ('定位为玩具属性'), that no China-mainland direct connectivity is guaranteed, and that data should be backed up. no reliability event observed.
NotesThe blocks are stated as IPv4-only, and the dedicated IPv6 address is described as carrying no blocks at all, which makes the IPv6 side strictly more capable than the IPv4 side here. The vendor publishes test addresses for both families, which is a useful first-party signal that the IPv6 is real rather than nominal. Not established: the payment rails (the checkout needs an account), the exact overage behaviour (the vendor says it compensates with time rather than cutting off), and whether the 10 ports are assigned or chosen. The storefront is at idc.jjfly.cloud, a different host from the jjfly.cloud marketing site.
