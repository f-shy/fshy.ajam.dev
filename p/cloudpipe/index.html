NotesEvery number in the ladder's CloudPipe row is confirmed. Two caveats the arithmetic hides: '3GB max file' is a file cap that the ladder converts into a per-job cap, and the 450 GB needs the Drive emptied between jobs because free Drive holds 15 GB. If the cap is per file inside a multi-file torrent, 450 GB is an undercount; if it is per job, it stands. The page does not say which. The cheapest paid price in the sector, but the free 3 GB cap and the 6-hour server window mean it cannot host the 20 GiB case even on the paid plan unless the paid cap is larger, which the vendor does not state.
