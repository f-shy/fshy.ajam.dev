No volume cap is published in EUR terms. The trial's 'uso justo' wording is the only admission that a fair-use ceiling exists.
Bindingnone A single user on one connection downloading 1 TiB/month has no published byte cap to hit; the constraint that would fail first is the personal-use clause, and only if the account is shared. Nothing documents a volume failure at this scale.
Identityemail Account creation plus email confirmation before subscribing: 'You must confirm your account by email before subscribing to the service'. Payment is 'Credit - Card Tarjeta de credito - Visa / Mastercard' or 'Crypto Bitcoin'. No local Latin American rail appears.
EgressNo byte allowance and no per-GB price. The page sells bandwidth as a property rather than a quota: 'Disponemos de un gran numero de servidores con un bando de ancha superlativo'. The English storefront states the company caps itself at 50% of infrastructure bandwidth to keep speed for users.
Per-object capnot applicable: files stay on the third-party host, whose own cap applies. AllDebrid publishes no per-file size on the offer page.
Retentionnot applicable: no storage is sold. The offer page says nothing about how long a generated link stays valid.
Interfacerest The API is documented separately at docs.alldebrid.com (v4, REST, JSON). The offer page itself only sells; the credential it issues works against the API.
ToolingREST API v4, browser plugins, mobile app, WebDAV access to the media folder, and named download managers (IDM, FDM, Mipony, Pyload).
Composes withA single EUR 3.99 payment covers 46-70 hosts plus torrent conversion with no account-sharing requirement beyond a single account, which makes it the default benchmark every local generator and reseller among debrid providers has to beat on price or on payment access.
ReliabilityThe Spanish, Italian, German and Portuguese storefronts are the same operator as alldebrid.com, so a localized price is not a local entity. Payment is card or Bitcoin only; a buyer whose card is rejected has no boleto or Pix fallback on this page.
NotesThis is the reference price the local market is measured against, in the language a LatAm buyer would read. The Spanish storefront accepts the same Visa or Mastercard a Mexican or Argentine bank issues, so a foreigner with a card can pay here without any local rail - which is precisely why the R$ 24.90 reseller listings only make sense for buyers with no usable card.
