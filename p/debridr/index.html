Per-object capnot applicable: files stay on the third-party host, whose own cap applies. debridr publishes no per-file size.
Retentionnot applicable: no storage. The Portuguese page states the output is 'um link HTTP normal e resumivel' and says nothing about how long it lives.
Interfacerest Web form plus a Telegram bot, '@debridrbot', where a link is pasted into the chat and the unlocked link comes back. Batch unlocking exists ('use o desbloqueio em lote'). No public API is documented.
ToolingThe output is a resumable HTTP link that JDownloader or any manager can take, stated explicitly: 'abra a pagina de download e copie o Link direto'. The Telegram bot is the scriptable path.
Composes withA free unlocker with no account is the zero-cost front of a chain: paste a 1fichier or Mega link, get a direct HTTP link, hand it to aria2. It costs nothing to keep as a fallback beside a paid multi-host.
ReliabilityAd-funded free unlockers are the least stable class among debrid providers: the vendor pays for server bandwidth with no subscription revenue, so a daily cap or a sudden closure is the expected end. The vendor already sells ad removal as the product.
Notesdebridr serves a Portuguese page and an English one, with no Portuguese-language commercial content beyond the interface; it is not a Brazilian company as far as the pages read show. It is the only zero-account multi-host found among debrid providers that names 1fichier and Mega together. A 2026-era entrant with localised host pages (pt, ja and others), which is how it surfaced in non-English searches. It states a daily limit exists and never states its size, so it is recorded at the unverified rung rather than estimated. Its 56-host list is broad and current: it includes Fikper-era hosts such as 10drives, Cloudfam and Zincdrive alongside the older Rapidgator/K2S set. The bot is the interesting part: a chat interface removes the need for a browser session and is drivable by a wrapper through the Telegram API. Whether the bot honours the same unstated daily limit was not tested.
