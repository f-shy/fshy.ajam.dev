The price is VND 12,000 for the first month renewing at VND 15,000, which is USD 0.4647 and USD 0.5809 at 25,822 VND per USD read from open.er-api.com, and the portal shows the same 20 percent discount against the 15,000 renewal, so the renewal is published rather than hidden. The interesting part is the network: a dedicated IPv6 address on a residential prefix rather than a datacentre one, with the carrier allowed to rotate the prefix and the vendor following it by hostname, plus residential NAT IPv4 egress that rotates weekly or monthly. That is a materially different product from a datacentre NAT VPS and it is the reason the row is worth keeping even while it is out of stock. Two gaps hold it down. No traffic cap is published anywhere on the plan card, which is a documented absence rather than a limit and cannot be used in arithmetic. And no legal entity is named on the pages read. What is not established: the checkout fields, the payment rails, whether any inbound IPv4 port is offered at all, and when it restocks. The portal says the plan and configuration remain viewable, so a restock check is a single page load.
