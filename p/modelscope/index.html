Free tierModelScope and AMD run a developer incentive programme. Step 0 is registering for the AMD AI Developer Program through the ModelScope Notebook page, which pays 100 hours of AMD GPU notebook time. Content paths stack on top: 25 hours per approved technical article capped at 50, 50 hours per approved notebook capped at 150, and 700 hours or more for an approved deployed application, which the vendor states totals 1,000 hours or more. The vendor page names MI300X only as an example AMD option and states no core, memory or VRAM figure; the machine shape is in free_tier.notes as a user-reported figure.
unit hours; metered on wall-clock; standing quota for each new user on the vendor notebook page: PAI-DSW GPU 8 cores, 32GB RAM, 24GB VRAM with 36 hours free quota, and EAIS GPU 8 cores, 32GB RAM, 16GB VRAM with 64 hours free quota; the AMD incentive hours in the statement above are a separate one-time grant; AMD notebook shape, reported by a user from their own console and not stated on the vendor page: 8 cores, 200GB RAM and 192G VRAM, with amd-smi showing 196288 MB total, ISA gfx942 (CDNA 3, Instinct MI300 series) and 80 compute units, a slice of a full MI300X rather than the whole card
