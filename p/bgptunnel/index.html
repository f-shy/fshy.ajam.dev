Bindingvolume-quota The identity gate (an owned ASN) comes first and is recorded in identity_cost. Once passed, the vendor states fair use up to 1TB/m and a 100Mbit/s limit on free tunnels; 1TB/m is the lower bound.
Identityinstitution an account whose email matches the email listed in WHOIS for the ASN the user owns; third-party list: 'Only BGP, bring your own subnet/asn.'
Egressno traffic allowance is published on the landing page; ingress: tcp+udp
free allowance: none published
Interfacecli BGP over GRE tunnels, self-service for ASN holders
Toolinga GRE interface, BGP daemon and an announced prefix on the client side
Composes withonly after obtaining an ASN and PI space, which is a paid prerequisite; not a path to free ingress on its own.
Reliabilityno SLA or allowance published on the landing page.
NotesThis is the successor to tunnelbroker.li and to route48.org. It is a transit product, not a tunnel broker: an ASN and PI space would be needed first, which NetAssist sells for EUR 300/year. That cost is what makes it unusable rather than the service.
Evidence notereported the landing page read establishes only a GRE-based BGP transit offer; the ASN requirement and the /126 come from the tunnelbroker.services list. A vendor page naming the prerequisites would raise it.
