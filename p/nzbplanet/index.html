The register page says 'Join NzbPlanet - free to browse, premium to download', and a vendor page cached in search results says 'The site is free to look around, you only need to pay for premium if you want to download nzb's'. caps declares <registration available="yes" open="no"/> and <limits max="100" default="100"/>.

Bindingrequest-rate The first thing that fails is the download allowance, which is zero on free: not a counter that runs out but one that never starts. The invite gate sits in front of it and would bind first only if registration were open.
Identityemail Email plus an invitation. caps says open=no, and the register page's invitecode field rejects an invented code; premium signup is the documented bypass.
EgressNo byte metering; the API returns pointers.
Per-object capnot applicable: an NZB pointer file
RetentionFree tier reported as 200 days, VIP or Platinum 2,500 days (nzbhydra.com, 2026-10-23). Not stated on the pages read from the vendor.
Interfacerest Newznab API at /api, capped at 100 results per request. Registration is invitation-gated: the register page accepts an invitecode and rejects a bad one with 'Bad or invite code older than 7 days'.
endpoint: https://nzbplanet.net/api?t=caps ; https://nzbplanet.net/api?t=search&q=<query>&apikey=<key>
ToolingNative Newznab on paper; in practice the key belongs to an account that cannot download without a paid tier, so Prowlarr and NZBHydra2 can add it and still get nothing useful on free.
Composes withA paid-only index: nothing on the free side contributes to a 1 TiB/month habit.
ReliabilityFree accounts keep full search and zero grabs, so a misconfigured arr stack sees results and fails every download rather than failing at search time.
NotesThe registerpremium page that search engines still hold returned 404 to a direct request, so its wording is cited as a snippet and not as a page read.
Evidence notedocumented The browse-only free tier and the invite gate are quoted from the vendor's own register pages read. Prices are third-party.
