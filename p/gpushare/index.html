Free tierPer-second metered GPU rental with an unusual funding gate. The vendor's own price document states that an account with no deposit may run one instance with one card, a deposit under CNY 100 raises that to three instances with one card each, and a deposit of CNY 100 or more removes the per-instance card limit. Each instance carries 50 GB of free local disk at /hy-tmp, with extra space at CNY 0.0004 per GB-hour. Third-party price pages that poll the vendor list RTX 4090 at CNY 2.00 an hour in east China, a 4090D at CNY 1.10 in the north-west, a 48 GB 4090 at CNY 3.45 in the south-west and an RTX 3090 at CNY 0.98, and one of them marks the vendor 免实名 (no real-name check) with Alipay and WeChat accepted.
