Composes withThe EUR 175,000 minimum eligible budget is the clause that decides usability: at a 70 percent rate the smallest viable award is EUR 122,500 of subsidy against at least EUR 52,500 of the applicant's own money, so this is a grant for a company already spending, not seed capacity for a person testing an idea.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesThe 85 percent and EUR 325,000 tier is the number most summaries omit, and it is reachable only by hiring a doctorate holder, which is a real cost rather than a formality. The EUR 250,000 cap is per company across the call, not per project: the resolution says 'por entidad beneficiaria'. A foreign founder can plausibly hold a Spanish S.L. and apply, because the eligibility test is registration and age, not nationality, and the resolution names no nationality condition in the articles read. So the identity gate is the company rather than the person, and it is one of the cheapest company gates, alongside Malaysia's RM 1,000 paid-up capital.
