2 TB of storage is included alongside 2 TB/month of downloads; the page does not say whether unused download allowance carries over.
Bindingvolume-quota At 1 TiB/month this plan is inside its 2 TB allowance, so it does not fail; at 2 TiB it does. The per-file cap never binds first for an archive of typical video or dataset objects.
Identityemail 'Cadastre-se' requires a registration before use; the premium page does not state which fields. Payment methods are behind a 'Formas de Pagamento' footer link that was not read, so card-only versus Pix is unverified.
Egress'Baixe ate 2 TB por mes' on premium, with 'Downloads simultaneos' and 'Velocidade maxima'. Direct downloads, no waiting, no captcha.
free allowance: 2 TB per month
Per-object cap'Baixe arquivos de ate 8 GB' and 'Envie arquivos de ate 8 GB' (8000000000 bytes)
Retention'Seus arquivos nao expiram' (premium). The page gives no day count, so retention is activity-independent while the subscription is live.
Interfacerest The generator-programme page states 'Gere links facilmente usando nossa API' and that a generated link can be sent straight to the customer. The public API documentation is not linked from the premium page.
ToolingJDownloader and other managers, named on the page as a premium benefit ('Suporte a gerenciadores'). The API is offered to approved link generators, not advertised to retail buyers.
Composes withThis is the cheapest per-terabyte host in the sector at premium rates, and it is also the upstream that Brazilian generators buy from: BRFiles sells traffic at R$ 180/TB to generators while selling 2 TB/month to an end user for R$ 49.90, so buying direct undercuts every generator that resells its files.
ReliabilityA single-country host: files are served from Brazilian infrastructure, and the site sells itself on avoiding international servers ('diga adeus aos servidores internacionais'). Files do not expire while premium is active, so a lapse is the loss event.
NotesBRFiles is a host, not a multi-host: it serves only files uploaded to BRFiles. Its place among debrid providers is structural. It publishes both sides of the generator market - R$ 49.90 retail for 2 TB/month, R$ 180 wholesale per TB of generator traffic - which is the arithmetic that makes an 'unlimited' R$ 39.90 generator impossible above roughly 220 GB/month.
