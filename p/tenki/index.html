ReliabilityUsage is metered by the second with no minimums; when the balance runs out Tenki stops starting new jobs and sessions until a top-up or auto-reload.
NotesArithmetic: 0.000014 x 3600 = USD 0.0504/vCPU-hour and 0.0000045 x 3600 = USD 0.0162/GiB-hour; 1 vCPU + 1 GiB = USD 0.0666/hour, x 730 = USD 48.62. At a 2 vCPU / 4 GiB shape the same rate gives (2 x 0.0504 + 4 x 0.0162) x 730 = USD 120.89 a month. Modelled at 200 hours a month instead of 730, the same shape would be USD 13.32.
