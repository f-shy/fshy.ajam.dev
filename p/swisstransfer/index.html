ReliabilityThe write path requires human verification at the API: POST /api/containers without a recaptcha field returns 400 'The recaptcha field is required. (and 1 more error)'. swissfer routes around this with an emailed 6-char confirmation, not with a token. The 30-day option is a trap. It silently deletes at 15 days unless the sender acts on day 12.
