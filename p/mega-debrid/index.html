Composes withThe French-market multi-hoster with 100 hosters on every plan tier; its API is the simplest of the set, so it is easy to wrap, but the token model makes a long-running daemon fragile.
ReliabilityA second login silently invalidates the running session's token; four bad logins or more than 50 requests a second ban the IP for minutes; a free account is indistinguishable from a broken one until the token call returns vip_end.
NotesPotential failure points: token invalidation, the free-account vip_end, or an IP ban. Token invalidation is the only one that hits an otherwise healthy paid account, and it is documented as a property of the token, not an error. The conditions page is French-only in content even on the lang=en URL, so the quota clauses are quoted in the original. Two vendor findings worth carrying: the API is premium-only by design, and authentication is credential-based with a token that dies on re-login. That combination makes it the worst of the eight for an unattended script, independent of price. Trigger condition: if a key-based auth appears, re-read the page the same day. The hosters page (/index.php?page=hebergeurs) is marketing only - '100 Hosters supported' and four logos - while the cap table lives on the registration page, where nobody looking for limits would think to go. Three host counts from one vendor: 100 on the offers page, 'over 200 hosts' in the meta description, 85 rows in the hosters table. The 85 is what a reader can count, so it is the figure to use. No annual plan: the ladder stops at 180 days for 16 EUR. Published comparison EUR 4.00 for 30 days is confirmed. A French proxy with a contract written for a traffic-limited subscription. Nothing in it promises a file will be there tomorrow. Trigger condition: if a tariff or FAQ page states a cloud or a retention window, this record needs a second entry. Same pattern as Debrid-Link, less severe: price identical to the euro for 35 months, host count down 162 -> 161 -> 152 -> 100, a 38% loss. The service is the cheapest 30-day term in the set alongside Debrid-Link and the least observed. Classified card-or-PayPal in the matrix because the vendor's own rail list could not be read and every reseller listing leads with card or PayPal; the Bitcoin leg exists at resellers and is documented here rather than counted in the crypto group. Included as the Spanish-language breadth point, not as a recommendation: debrid found no Latin American operator of a full multi-host. Every Spanish or Portuguese storefront read here resolves to a European operator (AllDebrid, MultiDebrid, Mega-Debrid) or to a Brazilian reseller of somebody else's accounts.
