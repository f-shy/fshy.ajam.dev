Free tierA free plan with $5 of use free every month after saving a card charged $0, equal to 370 tiny hours, 185 small hours or 11,100 CI minutes on small, with 2 small sandboxes running at once until the first top-up; after that, 40 units.
unit credits; metered on wall-clock; The month starts on the day the account was made and does not carry over. A small sandbox is 2 vCPU, 4 GiB and 40 GiB at $0.027 an hour standard or $0.0135 spot, so $5 buys 185 standard small-hours, matching the published figure.
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • volume-quota $5 of list-price use free every month, or 185 hours of a small sandbox; unused credit does not carry over source
  • write-gated (binding) a card must be saved before any sandbox runs; sign-up is by email, GitHub or Google and Stripe checks the card without a charge source
  • request-rate 120 free starts per 15 minutes on the free plan, 3 times the running count; each start past it costs one hour of the size's price source
  • retention-clamped a stopped sandbox is free for 14 days, then $0.05 per GB a month; with credit gone it is deleted after an email source
The free plan makes only tiny and small sandboxes; a medium needs the $100 plan and a large the $500 plan. Until the account has paid for a top-up it runs at most 4 units at once (two small sandboxes), then 40. One allowance per card: a second account saving the same card pays from its first minute.
Bindingwrite-gated The card saved at sign-up is what unlocks the $5 monthly allowance; without it no sandbox can run, so it binds before the monthly credit or the 120-starts-per-15-minutes rate.
Identitycard Sign-up is by email, GitHub or Google (https://agental.io/signup), and a card must be saved before anything runs: 'Sign up, save a card (charged $0) for $5 of free use every month' and 'Every account starts on the free plan, and saves a card before it runs anything.' Stripe checks the card and charges nothing. No phone or identity document is asked for.
Egressunverified: no egress price or allowance is published for sandboxes; mail is billed at $0.50 per 1,000 messages
Per-object capnot stated: the unit is a machine, not a stored object
RetentionA stopped sandbox keeps files and, on request, running programs free for 14 days after each stop, then $0.05 per GB a month until it is started or deleted. The 10 oldest snapshots are free; extras are $1.70 a month each. Until the first top-up, a stop keeps the disk but not memory, with at most 20 GB stored in all. (14 days)
Interfacerest, cli A REST API at agental.io with an OpenAPI 3.1 spec, an agental CLI and npx agental@latest sh, plus integrations for Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, opencode, the OpenAI Agents SDK, MCP and GitHub.
endpoint: https://agental.io/v1
Toolingnpx agental@latest sh signs in the terminal; the agental CLI and the REST API create, run and stop sandboxes. The Python and TypeScript flows use the same API.
Composes withA small sandbox at $0.027 an hour gives a coding agent a whole Linux VM with Chrome and Docker; a stopped sandbox keeps its disk for later runs, and the $5 monthly credit can be spent across sandboxes, mail and stopped storage from one balance.
ReliabilityWhen the monthly allowance and top-ups run out, running sandboxes stop within a minute with disks kept; after a payment the stopped sandboxes and snapshots are kept 30 days, before that a stop keeps files only up to 20 GB and deletes with an email three days before. A second account on the same card loses the free allowance.
NotesThe $5 monthly allowance matches 185 hours of the 2 vCPU, 4 GiB small sandbox at the published $0.027 an hour. It requires a saved card, so the identity cost is a card even though the charge is $0. The spot rate half-price sandbox can be reclaimed when a standard sandbox needs the room.
