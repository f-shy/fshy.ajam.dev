Upload traffic is bounded by terms stating monthly upload limits up to three times disk capacity (9 TB per month upload limit on a 3 TB plan). Per-object upload cap is 100 GB on Profi tiers. Public shared-link downloads are unthrottled and served directly from Yandex edge infrastructure.
Identityemail email
EgressFREE and explicitly unlimited: the plan comparison table states «Неограниченное скачивание по публичным ссылкам» (unlimited downloading via public links) and «Неограниченный доступ по вашим публичным ссылкам». No per-GB egress line exists anywhere in Yandex 360 consumer pricing. You cannot buy extra traffic because none is metered.
Per-object capper file: 100 GB (100000000000 bytes)
Interfacerest, webdav, rclone, webdav-proxy webdav + rest
ToolingWebDAV endpoint live: curl https://webdav.yandex.ru/ -> HTTP 302 to auth (expected; the mount is up). rclone yandex backend, rclone webdav with an app password, alist/OpenList yandex driver, official REST Disk API at cloud-api.yandex.net, plus the desktop client for Linux/Windows/macOS.
Composes withCannot be pooled: one Yandex ID = one Disk. But the Family plan gives 1 TB to each of up to 8 members on one subscription (250 ₽/mo annual ≈ $3.03/TB), which is the cheapest legitimate multiplication path of the storage providers compared. Profi and Family can be stacked. Yandex's own FAQ says the Disk volume sums across two plans on one account.
ReliabilityTwo real risks. (1) PRICE DISCRIMINATION IS DOCUMENTED AND MEASURABLE: the same page carries a «Оплата картой другой страны» (payment by a foreign card) block that routes to a separate tariff sheet, and the USD sheet at 360.yandex.com/premium-plans publishes $12.50/mo for 3 TB Pro versus 495 ₽ (~$5.99) on the RUB sheet. A 2.09× markup for a foreign card. Budget the USD number, not the rouble one. (2) Non-payment locks access progressively and then fully at 44 days (yandex.ru/support/disk/enlarge/disk-space.html). So this is not a set-and-forget archive unless the card keeps working.
