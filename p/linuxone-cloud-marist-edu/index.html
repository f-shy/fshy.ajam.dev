Free tierA free virtual machine on real IBM LinuxONE hardware on the s390x architecture, with full root via sudo, described by IBM as "a free, enterprise-grade, 24/7 cloud platform" where you "can get up to 120 days of free access - just register for a virtual machine and you're in" and "no cost, no credit card". The registration form's own heading reads "Sign up to get a 120-day complimentary trial".
unit hours; metered on wall-clock; the unit is a whole machine for a fixed term, not an allowance in hours; IBM's overview deck states the shape as 2 vCPUs, 4 GB memory and 50 GB disk; IBM publishes no egress term and no hourly figure
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • retention-clamped (binding) 120 days, then all resources are cleared of data and returned to the pool source
  • write-gated registration is not automatic: 'Your registration is under review. Your request may take up to one (1) business day to process.' source
  • per-object-cap one virtual server per account: the create-instance page refuses a second with 'There is an instance existing, delete the old one and then deploy this new one.' source
One account per email address.
Bindingretention-clamped The 120-day term binds: the machine is wiped and returned to the pool at expiry, so it cannot be an always-on box beyond four months without a new registration.
Identityemail The live registration form asks for an email address, a password, a first and last name, a country, a project description of 75 to 500 characters, and a Terms checkbox; the Organization field carries no required attribute; no card, no phone and no identity document. The phone and SMS-code block is present in the page source but commented out, and the controller that submits the form posts no phone field at all. One account per email address, and outlook.com and hotmail.com addresses are refused by the registration system.
Egressnot published on any page read: no egress term, traffic allowance or bandwidth figure appears in the terms, the registration form or the deployment guide, so nothing is asserted
RetentionThe server is granted for up to 120 days in two steps, 60 days and then a further 60 days, and the user must extend it at the 60-day mark after a reminder email. (120 days)
Interfacessh SSH with a key pair only: the terms state 'Only SSH keys are allowed.', and the deployment guide states that an SSH public key is required to deploy an instance and that sudo gives root authority
Toolingany SSH client with a key pair; ports other than SSH are opened with iptables on the instance
Composes withThe only free compute on this list with a non-x86 architecture, so it is the cheap way to build and test s390x artifacts without QEMU emulation, and it pairs with an x86 free tier that does the packaging.
ReliabilityThe portal's status line has shown 'Cloud Status: Stopped' and users reported the virtual-machine service as unavailable in July 2026; there are no backups, so an expiry or an outage takes the disk.
NotesSIGNUP PATH, read. The account is created at https://linuxone.cloud.marist.edu/#/register?flag=VM, reached from IBM's front door at community.ibm.com/zsystems/l1cc/ by the 'Try Virtual Machines' link. The form is an Angular template, and its source lists: email, password and confirmation, first name, last name, organization, country (a 242-option list defaulting to United States, with Viet Nam present), a project description of 75 to 500 characters, an optional event code, a Terms checkbox and reCAPTCHA v3. THE PHONE STEP IS GONE. The form in circulation, including IBM's own archived deployment guide, says 'Provide a mobile phone number that is capable of receiving SMS messages', and the live page still carries that block in its source - but it is inside HTML comments, the controller's submit payload has no phone field, and the SMS-code function is commented out. So the phone claim is superseded, and the row is email-only, which is why identity_cost.kind is 'email' rather than 'any-phone'. NO CARD, NO ADDRESS. address1 through zip are posted as empty strings by the controller. Organization is on the form but has no required attribute. WHAT STOPS A SIGNUP: manual review, not a gate. The form's own success message says the request 'may take up to one (1) business day to process' and that credentials arrive by email once approved, and it also has a refusal path, 'We need additional information to proceed with your registration.' IBM's front door additionally states that outlook.com and hotmail.com addresses are currently refused by the registration system, which is a documented, first-party, signup-level failure and the cheapest one to avoid. WHAT IT GRANTS: one virtual server, s390x, full sudo, a public IP, SSH-key-only access, 120 days. THE SHAPE: IBM's overview deck for the self-service virtual server states '1 server with 2 vCPUs, 4 GB Mem and 50 GB Disk'; the deck is undated and lists RHEL 7 and Ubuntu 20.04, and IBM's current deployment guide says only that there is one flavor, without its size. The earlier 40 GB disk figure is third-party. The console fetches flavor values at runtime, so the live values are not readable without an account. NETWORK: the instance gets a public IP and a firewall that opens only SSH, so inbound listening works but every port has to be opened with iptables first; that is documented in IBM's deployment guide. EGRESS IS UNPUBLISHED; the vendor can instead throttle or terminate for 'excessive network, computing or other resources'. VERDICT CHANGED to trial-only from qualifies-with-caveat. The grant is a fixed 120-day term after which 'all resources will be cleared of your data', which is a trial with a hard stop and not a renewable free tier; the earlier verdict was written when the 120-day term was read as a re-registration cycle. THE TERM: IBM's overview deck lists '60 + 60 Day = up to 120 day trial' with the user extending at the 60-day mark after a reminder email, the terms still say up to 120 days, and the portal carries an /extendaccount route; the deck is undated. RE-REGISTRATION: an IBM community blog of 2020 says 'In principle you can re-sign up as many times as you want'; a 2025 forum post reports that applying again after 120 days works with a different email; the live registration form checks the email against existing accounts and, for one that has an account, shows 'This email address has already been registered for the LinuxONE Community Cloud' and disables the other fields, so the same email cannot register afresh by that path. Whether an expired virtual-server entitlement frees the email, or offers a re-entitlement link, is unverified: behind a sign-up.
Evidence notedocumented The form, the absence of a phone field, the manual review, the blocked email domains, the 120-day term and the no-card door each come from a page or a template read and quoted on 2026-10-24, and the VM service was confirmed enabled by a live request that returned 200 with a JSON body. The machine shape is first-party but from an undated deck, and the console's live values need a login; the egress term is absent from every page read, which is recorded as an absence rather than filled in.
