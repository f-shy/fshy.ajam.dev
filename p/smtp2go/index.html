Free tier1,000 emails/month, 200/day, and 25/hour until a sender domain is verified
unit requests; metered on request-count; Sends above the daily limit are queued and released as the daily quota allows; past 1,000 in a month further sends are rejected. Up to 5 sender domains or single sender emails can be verified, and while no sender domain is verified the account is held to 25 per hour. A send from an unverified sender is rejected and still counts toward the monthly quota. Reporting covers the past 5 days, and support is ticket-only after 14 days. The free plan's page names no restriction on recipients.

Identityany-phone The free-plan signup asks for a work email at a private domain (addresses at public domains such as Gmail and Yahoo are refused), a full name, a company, a password and a contact phone number verified by a 5 digit SMS code; no card. Sending needs a verified sender domain or a verified single sender email, and a Gmail address cannot be verified as a single sender, so in practice the account needs a domain the sender controls.
