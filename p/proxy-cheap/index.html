NotesThe single most important number among debrid providers: it is the counter-example to 'a residential proxy costs $2/GB and therefore debrid is pointless'. The metered market is $1.40-$6.00/GB; the static market is $0.30-$2.71 per IP per month with no meter. The two markets are different products and published records should never quote one as the price of the other.
