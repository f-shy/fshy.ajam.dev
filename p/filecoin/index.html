Storage at $2.50/TiB is genuinely competitive, but total cost of ownership is dominated by egress: $420/mo of CDN to serve 1 TiB/day on top of $100/mo of storage = $520/mo. Also gated on holding USDFC (a stablecoin) and a refundable ~$0.50 lifecycle reserve per data set; each add-pieces call costs $0.008 + $0.003/piece, which is punitive for a 20 TiB corpus of many small objects.
Bindingper-ip-shaping one stream is shaped, and aggregate throughput is the shaped rate times the number of streams
