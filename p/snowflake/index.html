Free tierNo byte quota. A $400 credit balance and a 30-day clock, whichever ends first. 'The trial continues for 30 days (from the sign-up date) or until you've depleted your free usage balance.' Storage accrues cost against that balance at standard rates (~$40/TB/month per the documented Standard rate). So the nominal free storage is whatever the credit buys after compute: at $40/TB/mo, $400 buys 10 TB-months, i.e. 10 TB for one month or 1 TB for ten.
unit TB; metered on stored-bytes
Price$40/TB/month storage after the trial (Standard).
Identityemail email
EgressNot free. Snowflake bills egress/transfer separately and the trial credit is consumed by it.
Interfaces3, rest rest + s3
ToolingExcellent: PUT to an internal stage, full SQL, Snowflake SDKs, and external stages pointing at your own S3/Azure/GCS. Programmatically the best of the warehouse entrants.
Composes withOne trial per email; trials cannot be chained legitimately because the account is suspended at day 30. 'At the end of the trial, the account is suspended. You can still log into a suspended account, but you cannot use any features, such as … loading data.'.
ReliabilitySuspension at day 30 makes this a 30-day buffer, not storage. Any chunked-object scheme built on it would need a full re-upload cycle monthly.
