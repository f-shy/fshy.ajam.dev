NotesThe only vendor in the sector that lets the account set the retention clock, and the range tops out at 30 days. Trigger condition: if the rebuilt site publishes a longer maximum retention setting, the 7 should follow it. The only provider in the set whose headline price fell by more than 20%: the yearly went from $69.99 to EUR 40, nominally -43%, and the monthly stayed at about the same number while changing currency. The fall coincides with a rebuild by a new team, so it is a relaunch price. Two other things move in the window: the yearly oscillated $69.99 -> $54.99 -> $69.99 before the rebuild, and the plan names changed from 'The Good Kit'/'The Useful Partner' to plain Monthly/Yearly. The annual price is the cheapest per month among the western multi-hosters read here, but nothing on the site says how much traffic or storage that buys, and the free tier that older reviews describe is gone from the rebuilt pricing block. The Bitcoin claim has been on this FAQ for years and the FAQ sat above a rebuilt site, so the rail list may predate the rebuild. That is the reason the confidence stays at documented rather than measured. The yearly rate (3.33 EUR/month) undercuts TorBox's monthly rate if a year can be paid up front in Bitcoin. Third-party reviews quote per-host caps (Rapidgator 20 GB/day, Uploaded 10 GB/day) but none is on a vendor page, so none is documented as fact here.
