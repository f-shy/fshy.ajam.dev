Per-object cap is 20 MiB for free uploads and 100 MiB on the paid plan. Observed 15 MiB accepted and 25 MiB rejected with HTTP 413 'File size exceeds the maximum allowed size of 20 MiB'. Content-Type headers are validated against file content, rejecting application/zip and application/octet-stream with HTTP 415. No retention limit is stated.

ToolingBlossom protocol: PUT /upload with an 'Authorization: Nostr <base64 kind-24242 event>' header signed by any self-generated secp256k1 key. No signup, no account, no email. Any Nostr client (Amethyst, Coracle, nak, blossom-server CLI) speaks it.
Composes withIdentity is a pubkey you generate offline in one line of code. Unlimited free identities, zero cost, zero email. Each pubkey gets its own npub1..blossom.band hostname at upload time. 20 MiB x unlimited uploads x unlimited pubkeys. At 19 MiB per object, 10 TiB = ~550,000 uploads across ~550k keys, or a few thousand keys each doing 100 uploads.
ReliabilityA ~19 MiB upload returned HTTP 500 'Error uploading the file: unknown error' while 5 and 15 MiB succeeded, so the usable ceiling is somewhat below the advertised 20 MiB; one 5 MiB upload took 14 s from a user.
