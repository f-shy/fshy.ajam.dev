NotesArithmetic, stated so it can be checked: 40 CPU hours at the XMICRO rate of 0.04 credits/hour is USD 1.60; at the XXSMALL 4-core rate of 0.16 it is USD 6.40; 5 GPU hours on the 1xL4 XSMALL at 1.5 credits/hour is USD 7.50. A monthly total therefore falls between USD 9.10 and USD 13.90 depending on node chosen. The complaint thread is the reason to treat core-hour metering as a real design constraint rather than a quibble: a user on a 64 GB instance saw 6 hours billed for 10 minutes. The node table bills per core-hour, and the complaint is consistent with that reading.
