Free tierThis was the clearest free-GPU-CI offer in the open-source ecosystem and it is gone. Quoted at the top of the repository: 'The server will be decommissioned as of 2026-03-13 and the service will not be available from then on.' What it offered while alive: an OpenStack host with '48 Cores', '~500 GB Memory' and '6x NVIDIA Tesla V100', spinning up runners from gpu_tiny at 4 vCPU / 2 GB / 20 GB / 1x V100 up to gpu_4xlarge at 8 vCPU / 64 GB / 100 GB / 1x V100, free to conda-forge feedstocks that needed GPU CI. Quoted limitation: 'Only 4 GPUs can be exposed to the VMs at a time; expect queues. This is not per repository, but a server-wide limitation.'

NotesThe free GPU CI lane is not a category with several providers in it; it was one sponsored OpenStack host for one package ecosystem, gated on being a conda-forge maintainer, and it is decommissioned. The nearest live substitute is GitLab's OSS programme with its T4 runner, recorded separately, and the nearest paid substitutes are GitHub's GPU larger runner at USD 0.052 a minute and external runner vendors.
