NotesA genuine no-card VPS trial: full KVM, root, 1 Gbps, one day, no payment instrument at all. It is ranked below the always-free options because 24 hours is a benchmark window, not a machine. Two things are unverified. First, the trial's shape depends on which plan the ticket names, and the ticket is a human gate that can be refused. Second, whether the phone check accepts a number outside the vendor's home region is not stated. The vendor's own FAQ answers 'Can I get a free VPS forever?' with 'No, the free option is a 24-hour trial', so there is no free tier to be had here beyond the day.
