Reliabilityfree-tier downloads are HTTP-only and un-antivirused, unlike the paid tiers; the site has been running since at least 2016 and its help centre is now partly JavaScript-only.
NotesThe free tier is the smallest of the western torrent-to-cloud services: 1 GB cannot hold a single episode. The paid entry, 75 GB for $5, is the second cheapest per byte after TorBox Essential, but the free plan's per-day limit means the service is bought for storage, not queue depth. The ladder's own row says 'storage cap' and then adds the 1 GB into a byte-committed monthly subtotal. It is the one row in that subtotal that does not multiply. The number is defensible as a conservative floor, and it is wrong as a monthly ceiling in both directions: a 2 GB torrent is refused outright, and a 1 GB file can be downloaded more than once. Recording it as 1 GB/month is a choice, and it should be labelled as a floor rather than a counter.
