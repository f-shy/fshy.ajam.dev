Free tierPay as you go is priced '$0+ usage' and includes '1M included Gateway requests', 1M / mo tracing spans, 14-day data retention, 1 seat and a 30 req/min rate limit. Every plan includes monthly credits that can be spent on fine-tuning, evals, gateway routing and observability; the page gives no credit amount for Pay as you go and no per-token model price. The Growth plan at USD 250 per month adds a USD 50 one-time opening credit, 50M / mo gateway requests, 50M / mo tracing spans, unlimited data retention, unlimited seats and a 250 req/min rate limit.
