Free tier"Galaxy Australia is one of the international UseGalaxy servers that provide a common core set of tools and reference genomes, and are open to anyone." A non-Australian registered account carries a 100 GB data hosting quota and a 365 day hosted data retention period; an account registered with an address from the Australian research institution list carries 600 GB. Anonymous users get 5 GB and lose it when the browser session expires, and cannot create workflows or share content.
