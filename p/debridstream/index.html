The free tier is a browsing shell: it can list and play, but the automated-source-selection and addon ecosystem that make a debrid front end usable are paywalled. The app also cannot be driven from a desktop browser.
Bindingwrite-gated No byte cap is published, so the first thing that stops a working pipeline is the Pro paywall, which removes auto-play, filtering and addon support from the free tier.
Identitycard Google Play account for the app and a paid unlock for Pro, on top of a debrid subscription. The app itself installs free.
EgressNot metered by the app. Playback pulls from the debrid provider, so the provider's caps apply; Debrid Stream publishes no traffic figure of its own.

Interfacerest The site's 'Configure your account' button points at account.debridstream.com, which is where a debrid key is entered (third-party write-ups describe that portal and the Google TV app as the two ways to paste a key into a TV). No browser playback is advertised: the screens are Android TV, Fire TV and mobile.
Toolingan installable app per platform plus the web portal. The portal exists precisely so a TV remote never has to type a 40-character key.
Composes withThe alternative front end when a television-shaped app is wanted rather than a Stremio addon list: it holds the debrid key centrally and adds Trakt scrobbling and OpenSubtitles on the paid tier.
ReliabilityStore-distributed app: the free tier's feature set can change with a Play Store update, and reports of the price come from forum posts rather than the vendor.
NotesIncluded because it is the one front end found that is neither an addon nor a browser tab: a hosted account service plus native TV apps. Its weakness here is the same as every app: it needs a device, and the free tier is the shell rather than the engine.
