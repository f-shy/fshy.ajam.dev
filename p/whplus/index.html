NotesThe one row among documented hosts that answers the UDP question in words for the forwarded ports: '20 tcp/udp port + 1 redirect SSH port'. That matters because a NAT box with 20 TCP-only ports cannot host a WireGuard listener, and every other seller read either says nothing or says 'TCP' alone. The 1 GB tier at IDR 20,000 = USD 1.1045 a month is the better buy and shows 22 in stock, which is the largest stock count documented; the 512 MB tier at IDR 12,500 = USD 0.6903 shows 3. Two caveats. The 256 MB tier at IDR 7,500 reads no stock span on the listing, which is the stock-control-off state, and its cart answers 'Stok Tidak Tersedia', so the cheapest tier is not buyable and the price ladder starts at the 512 MB. And the cart route could not be made to price any NAT product: cart.php with pids 112, 113 and 158 all returned the cPanel hosting group, and with an invalid pid it returned a different group again, so the redirect is a property of the route rather than evidence about the product. The purchase path that does exist is the vendor's own payment page, which names bank transfer to BCA or Mandiri or PayPal, so a foreign buyer without an Indonesian bank account would need the PayPal route. What is not established: the checkout fields, whether the 1 GB tier's cart actually prices, and whether the ports are symmetric. The 1 GB tier is the row worth re-testing.
