Free tier20 GB of DagsHub Storage on the free Individual plan (the commonly cited '10 GB' is stale — the live pricing page says 20 GB), with unlimited public repositories and unlimited collaborators on public repos, and unlimited private repos. Every repo automatically gets BOTH an S3-compatible bucket AND a separate DVC remote.
unit GB; metered on stored-bytes
Pricenone stated
Identityemail (placeholder) not published
EgressFree/unmetered reads from the S3-compatible endpoint (https://dagshub.com/api/v1/repo-buckets/s3/<user>); public repos are readable via the Content API. No egress pricing is published on the Individual plan.
Interfaces3-compatible part of the text of Free tier, above
ToolingGenuinely good: boto3 / s3fs clients generated by the dagshub client, dagshub upload --bucket owner/repo, FUSE mount (dagshub.storage.mount) on Linux, plus plain dvc push/dvc pull. Endpoints supported: ListObjectsV2, GetObject, HeadObject, PutObject, CopyObject, DeleteObject + the full multipart set. So this is a real S3 API, not a shim.
Composes with20 GB per account x unlimited free GitHub-linked accounts = linear, trivially repeatable. Also per-REPO buckets: if the 20 GB is enforced per repo rather than per account (not stated on the pricing page), it becomes 20 GB x unlimited repos on ONE account. Worth probing with a throwaway account before committing to it.
ReliabilitySmall company (not hyperscaler-backed); storage is per-repo and tied to repo lifecycle. Deleting a repo deletes its bucket. No reported data-loss pattern, but no SLA either.
