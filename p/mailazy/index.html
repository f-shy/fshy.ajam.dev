Composes withA bring-your-own-SMTP sender: the Free plan holds contacts, newsletters and one automation, while delivery goes through a separate SMTP provider the reader connects and pays directly, so the SMTP provider's own sending limits also apply.
ReliabilityMailazy does not run mail servers; email leaves through the SMTP provider the reader connects. Logs store one row per delivery attempt, retries appear as separate rows, and test sends are not logged.
NotesA hosted send manager with a real free tier of 5,000 emails and 1,000 contacts per month and a REST API. The caveat is structural: the reader must connect and pay a separate SMTP provider for delivery, and the free caps and Pro-gated features bind early.
