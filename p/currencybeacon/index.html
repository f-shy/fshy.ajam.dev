unit requests; metered on request-count; No credit card is needed to start. The Timeseries API and base currency switching are not on Free or Basic, and a request to an endpoint outside the plan answers HTTP 403. Historical rates go back to January 1, 1996. The next plan up is Basic at USD 10 a month with 50,000 requests a month and 15 minute updates. The former name CurrencyScoop: currencyscoop.com and api.currencyscoop.com answer a 301 redirect to the currencybeacon.com hosts.
