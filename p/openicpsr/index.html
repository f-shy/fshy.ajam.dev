Per-object-cap 30 GB/file; per-deposit 30 GB and 1,000 files; ZIP extraction capped at 1,000 entries. Content is scope-gated to social/behavioural/health sciences and replication data, and every deposit gets a DOI, so deposits are citable publications rather than scratch storage.
Identityanonymous (inferred, not read) not published
EgressFree, 'no cost dissemination to other data users'. Downloads are immediate and public in the format submitted; no egress pricing exists in the product. Restricted-use options exist but are optional.
Per-object cap30 GB per file (30000000000 bytes)
Interfaces3, rest, rclone rest
ToolingWeb upload plus 'Import From Zip' bulk extractor (1,000 files/folders per zip), versioning with a new DOI per version, and the underlying ICPSR deposit pipeline. No public rclone backend; automation is a scripted HTTP/form loop rather than a documented S3-style API. This is the weak point versus RepOD.
Composes withUnlimited deposits x 30 GB each. 17 deposits = 510 GB; no account-level aggregate is published, and requests above 30 GB go to ICPSR Acquisitions for approval rather than being refused outright.
ReliabilityLong-running (ICPSR since 1962) with DOI permanence and version history; the failure mode is editorial/scope rejection, not data loss or throttling.
