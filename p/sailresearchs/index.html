The smallest Sailbox holds 16 GiB of RAM, and RAM bills whether or not it is touched, so the floor is USD 0.128 per hour before a single vCPU-second is used; USD 5 of monthly credit buys about 39 hours of that.
Bindingvolume-quota Active-CPU metering is the wall in both directions: a busy box costs USD 16.79 a month, and the disk keeps billing even while the box sleeps.
Identitycard The USD 5 monthly credit is attached to a payment method per the aggregator's plan table; the vendor marketing page states no payment terms. Card type and hold unverified.
EgressNo egress price and no transfer allowance are published on the page read; metered is recorded as false because nothing is known to be charged per gigabyte, which is an absence of evidence, not a promise of free traffic.
free allowance: not stated
Per-object capsandbox sizes S, M and L with a per-creation fee on the free plan (USD 0.005 / 0.01 / 0.012); the per-size ceilings were not readable first party
RetentionIndependent disks and persistent state; volume storage is billed at USD 0.000411/GiB-hour and is charged even while the attached Sailbox sleeps.
Interfacerest, cli, ssh HTTPS API at sailbox-api.sailresearch.com, SDKs (Python, TypeScript), the sail CLI and SSH to devboxes
Toolingthe sail CLI, the Python SDK (import sail) and the TypeScript SDK @sailresearch/sdk, all built on the public HTTPS API
Composes withA Sailbox is a full VM that never has to be torn down: the sleeping state is free, so a long-horizon agent keeps its filesystem, packages and running processes across days and pays only for the seconds it works. Pairing the USD 5 monthly credit with a sleeping box converts the credit into about 39 hours of active compute a month, which is an hour and a quarter a day.
ReliabilityIn-flight work survives pause and resume, and open connections survive for up to 10 minutes.
NotesArithmetic at 1 vCPU / 1 GiB fully active for 730 h: 0.015 x 730 = USD 10.95 + 0.008 x 730 = USD 5.84 = USD 16.79. The metering is on ACTIVE CPU, so a mostly idle box is cheaper: at 5% average CPU the compute line is USD 0.55 and the memory line USD 5.84, which is USD 6.39 and still over the line because memory is charged while the box runs. This is the provider whose pricing model most rewards idling, and it still cannot reach USD 5 at 1 GiB. The 'no runtime limits' claim is the vendor's own and was not tested.
Evidence notedocumented The full-VM description, 'no runtime limits', root access and the two active rates are quoted from the vendor's own page read. Volume storage and the plan/credit table come from the vendor docs and an aggregator respectively, and are marked in notes.
