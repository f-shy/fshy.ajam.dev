New Pro sign-ups join a waitlist when the operator is at capacity; paying users run before free trials, and a running free computer may be stopped to make room.
Bindingvolume-quota The 60-minute anonymous trial runs out first: a single small computer at USD 0.010 an hour would cost USD 0.60 for that hour, before the 4-computer account limit or the waitlist.
Identityanonymous The anonymous free trial needs no sign-up and no card: the CLI starts it directly. Paid plans and keeping the computer need an account, created with Google or a 6-digit code sent to email; Pro is paid by subscription or a one-time top-up.
EgressFull internet access on paid computers; the anonymous trial limits internet to code and package sites and AI model APIs. No egress allowance or per-GB price is published.
Per-object capA small computer has 10 GB of disk and a large one 20 GB; no larger single-disk size is published.
RetentionStopping keeps files, installed software and processes; an idle computer is stopped after an hour, and a trial computer is deleted after 3 days without use. No backup exists and files can be lost if hardware fails. (3 days)
Interfacecli A CLI with run, exec, stop and auth commands drives the computers; the SDKs and the browser sign-in flow are documented. Incoming connections and hosting a website are not supported.
endpoint: https://workpaper.co/api
Toolingworkpaper CLI, installed with a shell script, with SDKs for scripts and agents.
Composes withA CLI-driven computer can run agent tasks and keep files across stops; without incoming connections or backups, results must be copied out or kept in git.
ReliabilityComputers run on privately operated hardware in India with limited capacity and occasional 'no free computers right now' errors; no backups, no uptime guarantee, no GPUs, and no incoming connections.
NotesAn anonymous 60-minute trial is available without an account. Pro is USD 15 a month for 1,500 small-VM hours, which is USD 0.010 an hour; pay-as-you-go is USD 5 once for 150 hours, about USD 0.033 an hour. A large VM is USD 0.020 an hour on Pro. The trial's internet is filtered and the operator runs on its own limited hardware in India, with no backups.
