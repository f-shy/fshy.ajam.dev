NotesTHE NUMBER THAT DECIDES THE SHAPE: CNY 27,000,000 divided by at least 30 projects gives an average ceiling near CNY 900,000 per project, consistent with and slightly below the published CNY 1,000,000 maximum. That is arithmetic proof that the million is a per-project cap inside a fixed municipal budget, not a standard award. TWO LAYERS, and the distinction carries the verdict: the laboratory guideline is a credential screen written for the operating entity and its intake; MiraclePlus runs its own intake in front of it and publishes no such list, naming instead research capability, technical fit and project potential. An unaffiliated individual therefore faces no formal document barrier but a de facto one, because the intake behind the panel selects for exactly those credentials. The fails-identity verdict is recorded against this record because the public funding terms are the ones that bind; the sibling MiraclePlus record carries the softer verdict so a reader sees both. UNCERTAIN: whether MiraclePlus applies the six criteria literally to every compute applicant or uses them only as a preference. Nothing published says, and the two documents are silent about each other. Re-verified 2026-10-22 by raw fetch rather than by the text extractor. The notice is still served.
