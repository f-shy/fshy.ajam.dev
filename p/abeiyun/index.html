Free tierThe vendor withdrew the free cloud server on 2026-09-20. It was 1 core, 1 GiB RAM, 5 Mbps bandwidth and a 10 GiB data disk, with a dedicated public IP on BGP multi-line routing and a free 免备案 (no ICP filing) service. First activation ran 24 hours, an extension could be requested after 6 hours, and each approved extension added 5 to 10 days depending on real-name verification status; the vendor called the product permanently free. The notice states free instances were to be taken offline by 2026-09-27 and their disk data deleted. The paid cloud server starts at CNY 15 for the first month at 1 core, 1 GiB RAM, 5 Mbps and a 100 GiB data disk.
