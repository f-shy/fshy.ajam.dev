The terms permit one free account per individual, household or IP address. The bundled indexer is ikbin, an open-source binary indexer whose own FAQ says it is 'a demonstration and testing site' and that 'content may be cleared out rather unexpectedly if a beta goes south'. The premium account price is not published on the pages read; whether a datacenter address is accepted is not published.
Bindingrequest-rate Three simultaneous connections is the ceiling and it is published; no volume or retention figure exists to bind instead. Three connections will not saturate a line, so throughput, not volume, is what a 50 GiB month runs into.
Identityemail An email address: 'no credit card required, a password will be emailed to you'. The terms add that only one free account is permitted per individual, household or IP address.
EgressNothing published about speed, volume or retention; the only number on the site is the connection count
Per-object capno per-object cap is published; no per-object cap is published; the unit of retrieval is a Usenet article, approximately 750 KB by convention, so 1 TiB of monthly transfer corresponds to roughly 1.4 million article…
RetentionNot stated anywhere read. The site carries only the groups its members request: 'We only carry the newsgroups that our members have requested. To add a newsgroup, please email support.'
Interfacenntp NNTP over TLS on port 563. No HTTP or object API exists for the download path, which is driven through SABnzbd, NZBGet or nzbdav.
Composes withWould stack as a zero-cost secondary for recent, popular binaries if it is alive; it cannot be a primary, because three connections and a member-requested group list cannot cover a workload.
ReliabilityA demo server attached to a demo indexer, with a group list that only covers what members ask for and a stated risk that content disappears when the beta misbehaves.
