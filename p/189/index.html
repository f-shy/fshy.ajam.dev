Bindingper-ip-shaping Promotional allocations provide substantial nominal capacity but are restricted by a 2 GB daily upload limit, requiring approximately 5.5 years to transfer 4 TB. Registration requires domestic mobile identity verification.
Identitycountry-phone +86 only. OpenList labels the interface 逆向接口 and the web login a slider human-verification step, so even a scripted login is closed
EgressDownload is unmetered on net and throttled cross-carrier: '天翼云盘基本不限速，部分地区的非电信网络用户，可能会发现部分时段有限速'. The hard cap is on upload, not download
Per-object cap2 GB per single-file upload on the free tier (2000000000 bytes)
RetentionPromotional terabytes are attached to a China Telecom line and evaporate if it lapses. No retention clock on stored files
Interfacerest, webdav
ToolingOpenList drivers 189, 189_tv and 189pc, plus WebDAV out and CloudDrive2. The TV-edition OAuth client takes only an access token and is the one the community keeps alive because TV playback is unthrottled; a token can be minted by QR login, which the OpenList docs ship as an image
Composes with4 to 12 TB per carrier-promo account is the largest single free pool in this group, and the TV client plus OpenList plus WebDAV path is fully documented. The binding constraint is not capacity but the 2 GB a day free upload allowance, and the promos ride a China Telecom line
ReliabilityThe TV-client OAuth path is the one that survives; the desktop-client path has been repeatedly broken by client-version checks. Promotional terabytes evaporate when the line lapses, TV clients are being pulled from app stores, and the 10 TB migration programme closed on 2026-09-27
NotesOne record for the three deferred spellings: Tianyi Cloud Drive, the TV-edition variant, and the 天翼云盘 (China Telecom / 189邮箱) form. The TV client is the part worth knowing: it is the only interface that has survived, it takes an access token alone, and TV playback is unthrottled, which is why the community routes through it.
