Free tierNo sandbox free tier: SB-Box is available on paid plans only, and a Free account gets 403 on every call that creates or runs a sandbox. The account-level Free plan is 100 credits a day with a lifetime cap, but that plan cannot create or run a sandbox.
unit none; metered on wall-clock; 100 daily credits on the Free plan expire 24 hours after grant and stop once the lifetime allowance is used; they do not buy SB-Box access.
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 25 credits per vCPU-hour plus 8 credits per GB-hour while running and 0.05 credits per GB-hour disk while it exists source
  • volume-quota (binding) 100 daily Free-plan credits with a lifetime cap, expiring 24 hours after grant source
  • retention-clamped memory snapshot kept 14 days; frozen sandboxes kept at most 30 days after credits run out; disk 10 to 100 GB source
  • request-rate 50 running sandboxes on Plus, 100 on Pro and Team, 1,100 on Enterprise; 5 concurrent template builds source
A frozen sandbox bills only its disk and does not count against the concurrency cap; credits running out freeze sandboxes rather than killing them immediately.
Bindingvolume-quota The Free plan cannot create or run a sandbox at all, so the paid credit balance is the first gate; a running sandbox then spends 82 credits an hour at the 2 vCPU/4 GiB base size.
Identityemail gsk login is a browser login to a Genspark account; the account plans are Free, Plus, Pro, Team and Enterprise. The exact registration form fields were not read.
EgressOutbound internet is on by default; per-sandbox rules can block internet or allow/deny IP ranges, and port URLs can be gated behind a token. No separate data-out price or allowance is published.
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is a sandbox, not an object
RetentionA frozen sandbox keeps its disk until it is killed and its memory snapshot for 14 days of inactivity; sandboxes are frozen when credits run out and kept at most 30 days after that.
Interfacerest, cli, browser REST API at https://www.genspark.ai/api/sb-box/v1, the gsk CLI, a console, and official Python, TypeScript, Go and Rust SDKs.
ToolingThe user installs the gsk CLI or an SDK and logs in with a Genspark account to obtain a gsk- key; sandboxes run on Genspark's infrastructure.
Composes withSandbox disk snapshots become versioned templates that can be cloned or rolled back, and credits are shared with other Genspark products.
ReliabilityA sandbox freezes after 10 quiet minutes; its memory snapshot is dropped after 14 days and its disk is frozen but retained while billed. Credits reaching zero freeze running sandboxes and suspend commands with 402 until a top-up.
NotesHosted full-VM sandboxes billed in Genspark credits: 25 per vCPU-hour, 8 per GB-hour, 0.05 per disk GB-hour. Free accounts cannot create or run one; Plus starts at USD 24.99 a month.
Evidence notedocumented Rates, plan gate, lifecycle and limits are quoted from Genspark's own billing page and user guide read on 2026-11-25; the signup form itself was not seen.
