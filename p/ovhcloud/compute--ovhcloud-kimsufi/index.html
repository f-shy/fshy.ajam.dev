Free tierThe KS range is advertised from EUR 9.99 ex VAT a month: KS-B, Intel Xeon E5-1620v2 at 4 cores / 8 threads, 32 GB DDR3 ECC, 1 x 120 GB SATA SSD, 500 Mbps public bandwidth, plus a one-month installation fee. On 2026-10-22 that machine cannot be ordered in any datacenter. Nineteen of the twenty-six KS plan codes in the OVH catalogue return unavailable everywhere; the seven with stock are all either North American or Asia-Pacific: KS-4 at EUR 16.99 in Singapore, KS-3 at EUR 18.99 in Singapore and Sydney, KS-5 at EUR 17.99 in Beauharnois, Gravelines and Warsaw, KS-GAME at EUR 28.99 in Beauharnois and Sydney, and KS-A at EUR 54.99 in Singapore. The cheapest orderable Kimsufi is therefore KS-4 at EUR 16.99 in Singapore, and the vendor's own page states that the Asia-Pacific locations carry a 25 TB monthly traffic limit instead of the unlimited term the European, US and Canadian ones get.
