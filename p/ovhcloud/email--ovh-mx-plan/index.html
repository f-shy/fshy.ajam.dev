Interfaceunknown, rest, browser IMAP, POP and SMTP are documented on the MX Plan: incoming IMAP at imap.mail.ovh.net or ssl0.ovh.net (port 993, SSL/TLS), incoming POP at pop.mail.ovh.net or ssl0.ovh.net (port 995, SSL/TLS) and outgoing SMTP at smtp.mail.ovh.net or ssl0.ovh.net (port 465, SSL/TLS). Webmail is available from a browser (Roundcube, Zimbra or OWA, depending on the service). OVHcloud also publishes a first-party REST management API for MX Plan at /v1/email/mxplan/{service}/account with Bearer-token authentication, covering listing, creating, updating and deleting mailboxes and changing passwords; it manages the service and is not a message-access protocol. No JMAP endpoint or JMAP support is listed on the first-party MX Plan guides, the email FAQ or the developer reference pages read. The interface vocabulary has no term for IMAP, POP, SMTP or JMAP, so unknown is kept as the nearest term for those protocols.
