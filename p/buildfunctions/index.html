Free tierThe Free plan is $0 and includes $5.00 a month of CPU Functions and CPU Sandboxes usage, with GPU Functions and GPU Sandboxes, custom domains and 3 cron jobs; detailed compute and storage rates are in the estimator.
unit credits; metered on wall-clock; The $5 monthly CPU credit is the recurring free allowance. CPU Functions and CPU Sandboxes are $0.00001667 per GB-second plus $0.20 per million requests and $0.09 per GB of bandwidth out. GPU Sandboxes are $0.0000126 per vCPU-second, $0.00000137 per GB-second and $0.00013067 per GPU-second. The Pro plan is $20 a month with $20 of usage and 20 GB of model storage; the operator's price list puts Buildfunctions at $0.24 an hour.

Sandboxes run for up to 24 hours. Pro adds dedicated resources and 20 GB of model storage; storage above that is $0.39 per GB-month. The signup page reads 'Buildfunctions · v0.1.8 · Beta'.
Bindingvolume-quota The $5 monthly CPU usage credit binds first on Free: GPU Sandboxes have no stated free allowance, so any GPU run is paid from the start.
Identityemail The signup form at www.buildfunctions.com/signup shows a 'Create account' link with Email, Password and 'Show password', plus 'Or continue with' for other providers. No phone or document field appears; the page does not show a card field, and the card gate inside the dashboard is unverified: behind a sign-up.
EgressCPU Functions and CPU Sandboxes bill bandwidth out at $0.09 per GB, and GPU Functions and GPU Sandboxes bill bandwidth out at $0.09 per GB; inbound traffic is not priced on the page. The estimator exposes bandwidth out as a billable input for both sandbox classes.
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is a machine, not an object. GPU Sandboxes can be raised to 75 GB memory and 50 vCPUs on the Free and Pro plans; model storage beyond the included amount is $0.39 per GB-month.
RetentionA sandbox can run for up to 24 hours; retention of files after a run ends is not stated on the pricing or introduction pages and is unverified: behind a sign-up.
Interfacerest, cli, browser Sandboxes are created through an SDK and deployed from the dashboard; the docs describe CPUSandbox and GPUSandbox primitives and an SDK for sandboxing agent actions. No SSH endpoint is published on the read pages.
endpoint: unverified: behind a sign-up
Toolingthe Buildfunctions SDK with CPUFunction, GPUFunction, CPUSandbox and GPUSandbox primitives, plus the dashboard and CLI; no rclone backend is documented.
Composes withA top-level CPU or GPU Function spawns nested CPU or GPU Sandboxes for untrusted agent actions, with runtime controls for retries, budgets and loop breakers around the call.
ReliabilityThe service labels itself beta (v0.1.8) on the signup page; bandwidth out is metered at $0.09 per GB and model storage beyond the included amount at $0.39 per GB-month. Post-run sandbox file retention is unpublished.
NotesHosted serverless CPU and GPU sandboxes at $0.00001667 per GB-second for CPU and $0.0000126 per vCPU-second plus $0.00000137 per GB-second plus $0.00013067 per GPU-second for GPU, with $5 of monthly CPU usage free on the Free plan. Bandwidth out is metered at $0.09 per GB. Self-hosting of the SDK runtime is not presented as an offer on these pages, and is not recommended while the hosted Free plan exists.
