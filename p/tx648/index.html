  • retention-clamped (binding) points are charged for each day the instance is started; when the balance cannot cover a day the instance is shut down, an instance not started for seven consecutive days is destroyed, and an image kept for 60 days allows a restore. source
the check-in allowance is 50 points a day and the vendor's FAQ states 50 points exactly covers the base specs C+.I.M+.L and B+.I.L.L, so a started day on the free shape costs 50 points; at a zero balance a single missed check-in leaves the next day uncovered, and a server over 50 points a day needs extra points.
Bindingretention-clamped the check-in allowance is the binding constraint: points are charged for each day the instance is started, the instance is shut down when the balance cannot cover a day, and seven idle days destroy it.
Identitycard a WeChat account able to pay CNY 1 in the vendor's WeChat mini-programme. The vendor's own documentation states the check is not a real-name system, that it works because WeChat Pay requires a bank card and real-name information bound to the wallet, that SimpFun stores no real-name information, and that a phone-number binding was rejected as a privacy collection. WeChat Pay's own documentation lets an unverified overseas phone number bind an international card and pay without uploading an identity document, up to CNY 3,000 a transaction, and accepts a passport for a verified wallet, so a wallet without mainland real-name can pay the CNY 1. SimpFun states no rule on international cards, and WeChat Pay notes that some merchants do not accept them, so that part is unverified. The nearest kind is card; the true one is a wallet-payment kind, which the vocabulary lacks.
