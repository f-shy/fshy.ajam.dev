The 10 MB/s figure on Pro is a speed cap, not a byte cap, and it is the number that decides whether a 50 GB day is reachable in a day: 50 GB at 10 MB/s takes 1.4 hours.
Bindingvolume-quota Pro's 50 GB/day is 1.46 TB a month, above the 1 TiB monthly volume, so Pro clears this scale. Free's 5 GB/day is 150 GB a month, which fails by a factor of seven.
Identitycard Checkout through Paddle as merchant of record, which also carries the refund terms. The pricing page's checkout step asks only 'Enter your email'.
EgressFree 5 GB/day at 256 KB/s with one file per 30 minutes; Pro 50 GB/day at 10 MB/s with no download restriction; Max 'Download volume: Unlimited' at 'Unlimited' speed. Traffic can also be bought separately at 10 GB / $4.99, 30 GB / $9.99, 100 GB / $19.99 with a balance that 'never expires' and 'works without a premium plan'.
