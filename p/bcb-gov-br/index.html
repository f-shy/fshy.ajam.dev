Free tierNot a metered service and not a subscription. The central bank's Pix page states the fee: 'Gratuito para pessoa física e custo baixo para empresa' (free for individuals, low cost for businesses). The amount charged to businesses is not published on the page read.

Limits
  • write-gated (binding) a current, savings or prepaid payment account at a Pix participant institution is required. At a foreign IP the Kiwify checkout hides the CPF field and disables Pix and boleto; choosing Brazil in the checkout's country selector brings back the CPF and cellphone fields and Pix source
Kiwify states the exclusion from the merchant side: 'Quando um comprador acessa o checkout de fora do Brasil, nós escondemos o campo de CPF e desabilitamos o boleto bancário e o Pix.' Hotmart lists Pix under 'Métodos de pagamento exclusivos do Brasil'.
Bindingwrite-gated Nothing about volume applies. A buyer without a Brazilian account cannot complete the payment at all, which is the gate that fails before any other consideration.
Identitynational-id CPF plus an account at a Brazilian financial institution. The checkout hides the CPF field for foreign IPs and disables Pix, which is the operational proof that the rail is closed to a buyer outside Brazil.
Egressnot applicable.
Per-object capnot applicable: a payment, not an object.
Retentionnot applicable. A Pix charge is presented as a code with an expiry; the checkout pages read here do not publish the lifetime.
Interfacerest The buyer's surface is a payment code, described by Kiwify as 'um codigo unico de pagamento, podendo ser pago via ... Code ou pela opcao de Pix Copia e Cola'. Settlement is pull-based: the payer's bank app reads the code, so no card details cross the merchant.
ToolingAny Brazilian bank app or a copy-and-paste code. There is no client the buyer must install and no credential to store, which is why Brazilian resellers prefer it.
Composes withPix is the rail that makes the R$ 24.90 reseller listings work: a buyer with no international card pays in seconds into a Brazilian storefront, which then buys the foreign subscription itself. The chain adds a person between the buyer and the service, and that person holds the account.
ReliabilitySettlement is instant. The central bank page lists a 'Mecanismo Especial de Devolução do Pix (MED)' and a page for a mistaken Pix or a scam; the rules of that mechanism were not read. A payer travelling abroad can re-select Brazil in the checkout's country selector to get Pix back, per Kiwify, and a seller can force the Brazilian checkout with a URL parameter, so the checkout's gate follows its region setting, not the payer's location.
NotesA payment rail is not a retrieval service, and this record exists because the debrid question cannot be answered without it: every R$ price among debrid providers is a Pix price, and Pix is the one part of the Brazilian stack a foreign buyer cannot join. The Chinese parallel is Alipay or WeChat Pay; the shape is the same, a domestic clearing rail that local storefronts build on.
Evidence notedocumented Two checkout vendors document the rail's buyer-side gate on their own help pages, and the central bank's Pix page states the fee for individuals. The rulebook (Regulamento do Pix) and its limits were not read.
