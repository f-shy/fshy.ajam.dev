NotesThe credit figures are the accelerator's own statement of what its technology partners offer, not figures read from AWS, Google or OVH pages, so they are the accelerator's report of a third party's terms and are also qualified by 'Subject to availability and third-party terms'. The 14-week programme length against a one-year credit validity is the useful mismatch: the cohort ends long before the credits do. Who receives the Scale Tier is unresolved: either (a) the Scale Tier is automatic for all participants, plausible but not stated, or (b) it is a tier of Google's own startup programme that the accelerator merely refers to, which the FAQ wording 'Start Tier / Scale Tier' suggests because those are Google's tier names.
