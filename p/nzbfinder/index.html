Registration is declared open, which is the one difference that matters against every other invite-only indexer among debrid providers.
Bindingrequest-rate At a 1 TiB/month habit the daily counter is what stops a caller: 25 API hits a day is roughly one search per hour, and the 5-grab cap allows about 50 GB a day at a 10 GB average NZB, which is 1.5 TB a month only if nothing retries.
Identityemail Registration declares itself open in caps and /register serves an email field with the warning 'Temporary e-mail domains are banned', plus a verification link. Spam-blocking is the gate, not an invite.
EgressNo byte metering; the API returns pointers.
Per-object capnot applicable: an NZB pointer file
Retention'18+ years of posts in our DB' (login page strapline); a third-party listing claims 'over 3,600 days of binary content, 500,000+ NZBs indexed'.
Interfacerest Newznab API on the same host at /api. caps advertises 100 results per request and open registration. The site lists Sonarr, Radarr, Prowlarr, NZBHydra 2, SickChill/SickGear, Chaptarr, Mylar, Lazylibrarian and Lidarr as compatible.
endpoint: https://nzbfinder.ws/api?t=caps ; https://nzbfinder.ws/api?t=search&q=<query>&apikey=<key>
ToolingNative Newznab, no wrapper: Prowlarr, NZBHydra2 and every arr app add it with URL plus key.
Composes withAn open-registration indexer with a real API key on the free tier, so it can carry an arr stack at 25 searches and 5 grabs a day before EUR 10/year lifts the ceiling.
ReliabilityFree accounts have been re-capped before without notice (the 2013 status post reverted a temporary lift), so a free allowance is a policy number that can change under a running stack.
NotesThe open-registration flag in caps is the strongest single fact on this record, and it is what separates NZBFinder from DrunkenSlug, NZBPlanet, Tabula Rasa and NinjaCentral today.
Evidence notereported caps and the open-registration flag were measured on 2026-11-03; the free allowance that decides the verdict is third-party. One free account confirms the threshold and would move the record to documented.
