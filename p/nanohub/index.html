Free tier"Each free nanoHUB account comes with 10 GB of free storage space, and three app sessions can be run simultaneously. Each session can have many runs, and persist over multiple days so long as you remain active with them." Accounts are created with "your existing Google account, an email account, or your own institution's account if it is a member of InCommon", and the catalogue holds over 600 simulation apps whose "computational power is provided by nanoHUB [...] these are provided by servers or supercomputer clusters".
