Free tierThree days of any VPS tariff or of S3 object storage at no charge, with the campaign valid until 31 January 2027. The trial requires confirming a phone number and activating the period from the control panel. Two limits apply inside the trial: SMTP is unavailable on trial VPS tariffs, and channel throughput is capped at 10 Mbit/s. Trial S3 storage is capped at 500 GB. Files and settings are kept for seven days after the trial ends, and the provider's own VPS landing page describes the same offer in its meta description as free for a period of up to 12 months, which the body of the page contradicts.
