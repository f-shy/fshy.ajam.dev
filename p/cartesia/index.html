Free tierFree plan at USD 0 a month with 20K credits per month plus USD 1 of prepaid voice-agent usage per month. The credits are spent on text-to-speech at 1 credit per character (about 750 to 800 credits per minute of audio, which the plan table puts at about 27 minutes of Sonic-3.6 a month) or on speech-to-text at 3 credits per second of audio (about 1h 51m of Ink-2 a month); the USD 1 of agent usage is about 33 minutes at USD 0.03 a minute, before telephony and language-model usage.
unit credits; metered on unstated; models: text-to-speech sonic-3.6 (44 languages; sonic-3.5 and sonic-3 also accepted by the API) and speech-to-text Ink-2; managed voice agents. unverified: whether unused Free credits roll over, because the pricing page describes rollover only in terms of paid plans
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 20K credits per month on the Free plan, shared by text-to-speech and speech-to-text, plus USD 1 per month of prepaid voice-agent usage; with overages off, new requests are blocked once the credits run out source
  • request-rate Free plan concurrency: 2 concurrent text-to-speech requests and 8 concurrent speech-to-text requests; on Free plans a request over the limit returns 429 immediately, where paid plans queue it source
Bindingvolume-quota about 27 minutes of Sonic-3.6 speech or about 1h 51m of Ink-2 transcription per month runs out before the concurrency caps matter
Identityemail the signup form asks for a first name, last name, email address and password, or a GitHub or Google account; the pricing page names no card for the Free plan and the signup form shows none; unverified: whether a phone number, a card or a verification step is asked after the form
Egressnot published
Interfacerest REST (POST /tts/bytes, /tts/sse, /stt and sibling TTS/STT routes; GET /models and GET /v1/agents/models) and WebSockets at api.cartesia.ai, versioned by a Cartesia-Version header; Python and JavaScript quickstarts. No chat/completions or text-completion route is documented in any API version; LLM text generation is available only inside a Managed Agent over wss://api.cartesia.ai/v1/agents/websocket/{agent_id}.
endpoint: https://api.cartesia.ai
Toolingraw HTTP and WebSocket with an API key (sk_car_...) from the playground, or the Python and JavaScript quickstarts; the OpenAI SDK is not used against Cartesia; there is no OpenAI-compatible chat endpoint. An OpenAI-compatible shim exists for STT only (POST /audio/transcriptions).
Composes withthe monthly pool covers the speech-to-text side of a demo better than the text-to-speech side, because 20K credits is about 1h 51m of Ink-2 transcription but only about 27 minutes of Sonic-3.6 speech; run it from a free CI job for small voice-over or transcription batches. Unlike a one-time credit it renews each month.
Reliabilitya Free-plan request over the concurrency limit fails with 429 instead of queuing
