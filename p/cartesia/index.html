Free tierFree plan at USD 0 a month with 20K credits per month plus USD 1 of prepaid voice-agent usage per month. The credits are spent on text-to-speech at 1 credit per character (about 750 to 800 credits per minute of audio, which the plan table puts at about 27 minutes of Sonic-3.6 a month) or on speech-to-text at 3 credits per second of audio (about 1h 51m of Ink-2 a month); the USD 1 of agent usage is about 33 minutes at USD 0.03 a minute, before telephony and language-model usage.
unit credits; metered on unstated; models: text-to-speech sonic-3.6 (44 languages; sonic-3.5 and sonic-3 also accepted by the API) and speech-to-text Ink-2; managed voice agents. unverified: whether unused Free credits roll over, because the pricing page describes rollover only in terms of paid plans
Pricenone stated
Limits

  • request-rate Free plan concurrency: 2 concurrent text-to-speech requests and 8 concurrent speech-to-text requests; on Free plans a request over the limit returns 429 immediately, where paid plans queue it source
