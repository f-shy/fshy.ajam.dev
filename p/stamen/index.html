Per-object capin the legacy archive each tile is one object at s3://{bucket}{prefix}/{z}/{x}/{y}.{extension}; the archive is an outdated snapshot that does not cover the whole globe at every zoom level

endpoint: https://tiles.stadiamaps.com/tiles/stamen_toner/{z}/{x}/{y}{r}.png
ToolingLeaflet, MapLibre, OpenLayers and any XYZ tile client; the AWS CLI or an SDK with requester-pays enabled for the legacy buckets
Composes withthe Stamen styles spend the same Stadia Maps account and free tile allowance as any other Stadia Maps tile; the original keyless maps.stamen.com service ended on October 31, 2023, so a new project signs up with Stadia Maps or reads the legacy archive from requester-pays S3 buckets
Reliabilitythe keyless maps.stamen.com tile service ended on October 31, 2023 and its redirects no longer work; the legacy archive is outdated and incomplete, provided to be accessed and used at the user's own risk
NotesFree requests: under 200,000 tiles a month, through a Stadia Maps account. Rate limit: not stated on the Stamen pages. Bulk path: the legacy tile archive in requester-pays S3 buckets, billed to the downloader by AWS.
