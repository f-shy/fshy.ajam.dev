Web UI and speed are shared: 'n × 10 Гбит/с' is the uplink, not a per-client guarantee. A dedicated IP is offered per plan.
Bindingnone Traffic is unlimited and there is no published byte counter, so a monthly transfer of 1 TiB meets disk size and the manual provisioning delay, neither of which is a rate limit in the vocabulary. The disk plan is the only ceiling and it is bought, not enforced.
Identityemail Order form plus an emailed invoice; the vendor states it keeps only payment records by login ('ваши личные данные не хранятся в базах, ведется ТОЛЬКО учет платежей по логинам'). No phone or national ID is asked for on the pages read.
Egress'Трафик не лимитирован' in the tariff comparison table. For subscribers of Kazakhtelecom and its partners (megal ine, idnet) traffic from the seedbox is counted as internal: 'трафик для абонентов АО «Казахтелеком» и его партнеров является «внутренним» и не учитывается.'
free allowance: unlimited
Per-object capnot applicable: the disk plan is the ceiling; no single-file cap is published
RetentionFor the paid period. The order page says access credentials are sent after payment and the plan is billed by week, half-month, month or quarter; no post-expiry retention promise was read.
Interfacesftp, ftp, cli ruTorrent v3.10 on rtorrent 0.9.8 in the browser; 'Мы предоставляем полный доступ к файлам сидбокса через FTP/FTPS'. Torrent creation, MediaInfo and screenshots are built in; uploading to file hosts is explicitly not supported yet.
ToolingruTorrent web UI, FTP/FTPS, torrent-file creation on the box. The vendor states 'Могу ли я заливать файлы на файлообменники через сидбокс? В данный момент эта функция пока не поддерживается.'
Composes withA KZT-billable seedbox with unlimited traffic; it supplies the always-on swarm side and FTP delivery, and pairs with Storage Portal for the file-host half that it refuses to do.
ReliabilityProvisioning is manual and can take up to a day; the free tier that once made it testable is switched off, so a buyer pays before seeing the UI.
NotesThe Kazakh answer to the sector question: a foreigner can pay by PayPal (USD) or USDT TRC20, a Kazakh by Kaspi/Halyk in KZT, a Russian by Sberbank or YooMoney in RUB. That multi-rail design is unusual and is the reason the record exists. No price is included because none is public; the site sells by invoice. The Kazakhtelecom peering claim is the vendor's own and is not independently verified.
