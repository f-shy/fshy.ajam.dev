Composes withThe two halves do not stack into one cheque: the INR 20 lakh is a grant and the INR 50 lakh is a repayable instrument, so the free portion is the smaller number. Because disbursement runs through an incubator, the practical gate after DPIIT is an incubator selection, which is a second discretionary review.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesThe number worth remembering is 20 lakh, because the 50 lakh beside it is debt-like and a reader who takes the pair as a grant overstates the free capacity by a factor of 3.5. DPIIT recognition is not open to foreign founders: recognition requires an entity incorporated in India. Whether the scheme accepts a sole proprietorship is unresolved: the FAQ says 'incorporated', which in Indian usage usually means a company or LLP rather than a proprietorship. The 2-year incorporation clock cannot be paused: it is measured at the time of application.
