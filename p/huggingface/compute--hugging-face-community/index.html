Composes withThe grant is a route to a dedicated, always-on GPU behind a public URL with no card: a free account gets 2 ZeroGPU Spaces with 5 minutes of GPU a day (the compute record huggingface-spaces-zerogpu), and this grant is what a project applies for when 96 GB and a 5 minute window are not enough. It composes with inference APIs because the Space can sit in front of an external API and hold the connection. Hardware table on the Spaces GPU page (column 'Hourly Price', billed by the minute while a Space is Starting or Running; the page has no on-demand, spot or community label and states no minimum bill): 1x Nvidia L4 24 GB $0.80, 1x Nvidia L40S 48 GB $1.80, Nvidia A100 - large 80 GB $2.50. The Nvidia H100 rows are struck through and marked 'removed December 2025'. No GeForce row.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesDistinct from the ZeroGPU record already in earlier readings: ZeroGPU is a quota every free account holds, while a community GPU grant is a discretionary hardware allocation to one Space. The amount is unverified and deliberately so: the docs publish no tier list, no hour count and no approval rate, and the public applications found on Spaces range from a demo to 2,500 GPU hours, so the honest claim is the mechanism. A 'community grant' could cover hardware only, hardware plus storage, or a cash-equivalent credit. The docs describe hardware ('covering the GPU hardware upgrade costs') and a public applicant asked for storage separately, which supports hardware-only.
