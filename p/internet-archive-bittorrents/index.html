Free tierFree, unlimited distribution; ~1.4 million items already torrented, 'comprising almost a petabyte of public domain materials'. Egress is unbounded because the swarm and webseeds share the load.
unit unlimited; metered on unstated
Pricenone stated
Identityemail (placeholder) The torrent page names no account requirement for downloading. Creating the item that receives an Archive torrent needs a free archive.org account.
EgressTorrents rely heavily on webseeding: download directly from Archive servers when no peers have the files. The page states no price and no bandwidth limit for torrent or webseed download.
Interfaceunknown BitTorrent protocol: .torrent files per item with Getright-style webseeding, tracked by bt1.archive.org and bt2.archive.org
ToolingA BitTorrent client with Getright-style webseeding support; the Archive generates the .torrent per item and runs the trackers bt1.archive.org and bt2.archive.org.
Composes withArchive BitTorrents are made automatically for community-contributed items in many collections and updated when the item changes. The torrent falls back on webseeding from Archive servers when no peers have the files.
ReliabilityOld torrents for a mutated item loop forever re-downloading mismatched pieces; seeder counts are cached ~5 minutes and include webseeds.
NotesCurrently over 1.4 million Archive Items are available via the BitTorrent protocol, comprising almost a petabyte of public domain materials.
