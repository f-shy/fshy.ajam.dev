Bindingper-ip-shaping At 1 TiB a month nothing is counted, but the free tier's unstated throttle is what a client meets first; $2/month is the cheapest published way past it, and $15 buys 1 TB of Vault for retention rather than for throughput.
Identityanonymous Free use needs no account at all ('без регистрации'); a paid tier is bought through Patreon, so the identity is a Patreon account or a crypto payment from the donate page.
EgressDownload is served over HTTPS from the vendor's servers; the free tier is throttled and the FAQ says 'Премиум-пользователи получают более высокую скорость'. No byte quota is published.

Per-object capno maximum file size published; each torrent is fetched by the vendor's servers and served as HTTP
RetentionDirect links and streaming sessions are temporary: 'прямые ссылки и стриминг-сессии временные. Вы всегда можете обновить или пересоздать их из интерфейса библиотеки.' Vault keeps a torrent available with zero seeders for as long as Vault Points are spent on it.
Interfacerest, cli Web player plus a documented Stremio addon, Torznab/Jackett/Prowlarr integration ('Добавьте Torznab-ссылку своего индексера в профиле'), and an embeddable player SDK. The whole stack is MIT-licensed ('Все компоненты Webtor.io распространяются под лицензией MIT'), so a client can run the player or talk to the same API surface.
ToolingBrowser; Stremio addon; Torznab feed for Jackett/Prowlarr; open-source components under MIT. The Torznab integration means a client's existing indexer stack can drive it.
Composes withIt removes local BitTorrent exposure: the vendor's servers join the swarm and hand back HTTPS. That composes with a seedbox for private trackers and with a debrid for file hosts, and its Vault covers the zero-seeder case a debrid cache often misses.
ReliabilityPayments run through Patreon, and the vendor's own announcement warns that backers who signed in with Apple ID must log in with the Patreon account or the perks do not attach; crypto payment was added later from the donate page.
NotesA service with a real free tier that needs no account and no card, which makes it the default first hop when no payment method is available. The verdict is 'qualifies-with-caveat' for one reason: the free path is real and account-free, but it publishes no speed, /pricing is now a 404, and the one number a reader would grab (50 Mbps) is a card-gated Patreon trial. The ladder lists Webtor among the five steps that carry the bytes; it carries none, because it has no counter. If the 50 Mbps trial were card-free it would be 3.78 TB in 7 days at line rate (6.25 MB/s x 604,800 s), 3.9x the whole ladder; the Patreon checkout is what makes it group-3 rather than group-1. It claims no per-file cap on a free, account-free path. That claim is what makes it the candidate for a 20 GiB fetch with no payment, and it is unmeasured: a 20 GiB pull was not attempted, because an interactive magnet session cannot be driven by a page fetch. /pricing and /faq are 404, so every number here comes from the landing page or the operator's blog. A ~USD 12 floor is not a monthly price, so this is not comparable with TorBox's USD 3/month. It is the cheapest crypto purchase that buys anything at all in the sample only if the plan behind it is small; otherwise it is the largest first payment.
