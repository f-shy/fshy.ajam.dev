Free tierA free container VPS: 'Every plan includes NAT IPv4, and the free plan includes a dedicated public IPv6 address.' No price is stated for the free plan; the home page says 'For developers & students, no card required just Discord.' The vendor publishes no shape, but a user's benchmark posted in the vendor's own LowEndTalk thread on 2026-08-24 reports 1 vCPU at 3092 MHz, 512 MiB RAM, 512 MiB swap and 4.8 GiB disk on Ubuntu 26.04 under LXC.
unit none; metered on wall-clock; Vendor publishes no CPU, RAM or disk figures; specifications derive from reported benchmarks in vendor discussion threads.
Pricenone stated
Limits
  • retention-clamped (binding) the free period is 7 days and must be renewed by hand at most once every 24 hours; an unrenewed VPS is auto-suspended source
  • volume-quota one free container per account is implied by the FAQ's singular 'your VPS' but is not stated as a number anywhere read source
Container lifetime is seven days with manual renewal available once every 24 hours; unattended containers suspend after seven days. Hardware is consumer-grade without an availability guarantee.
Bindingretention-clamped a 7-day clock that resets only when a human renews is the first thing that fails: any lapse in renewal past 7 days suspends the machine
Identityemail No card and no hold: the home page says 'no card required just Discord', and the FAQ says 'no trial, no card required'. Sign-in is through Clerk (clerk.openworld.eu.org) on the /login page, whose form is a single 'Sign in' button and carries no card, phone or document field; the page footer credits the builder. Whether the Clerk instance offers an email-only sign-up or requires a social account was not established: the button is rendered disabled until the Clerk JavaScript loads, so the flow could not be read from the markup.

Composes withA throwaway IPv6 container with a dedicated public address is the cheapest way to test an inbound service or hold a trickle of state for a week. It composes badly with anything long-running: the 7-day clock and the 35% CPU throttle rule out indexing and builds.
Reliabilityone transient outage reported in the vendor's own LowEndTalk thread on 2026-08-24, when a user wrote 'looks like the server is dead beyond repair' and the operator replied 'There was a little downtime on the i7-4510U, but should be back'; the vendor publishes a HetrixTools status page whose 7-day table rendered with no values on the day read
NotesOpenWorld provides ephemeral container hosting with a 7-day lifetime requiring manual renewal every 24 hours. Hardware is consumer-grade and resource allocations are unmetered fair-use.
