Free tierLittle Lemur: 1 M messages a month, 20 connections, max 100 queues and max 10 000 queued messages on a shared RabbitMQ broker, free for as long as the instance is used, no card
unit requests; metered on request-count; The quota counts messages: a message counts once when it is published and once when it is consumed, so 1 M a month is about 500,000 messages that are both published and consumed. The monthly count resets on the first day of each month, and warning emails go out at 70 and 100 percent. An instance that crosses the queue or queued-message threshold is blocked from publishing and from creating queues. At the connection limit new connections are refused and existing ones stay up. Shared plans allow 200 channels per connection. An account may hold up to 49 free instances (650 once it has a paid instance).
