NotesREFUTATION OF A COMMON MISREADING. Gensyn appears in free-GPU roundups because RL Swarm is free to join, but joining means contributing hardware, not receiving capacity. Three independent facts kill it as a source of capacity: it needs 32 GB RAM or a listed NVIDIA card, its dashboard says no official swarms are running, and its reward unit is a token allocation out of a 2 percent testnet pool with no published per-node formula. A third-party guide also records that renting a GPU to run a Gensyn node costs USD 65 to USD 300 per month.
