Two gates stack: the upload right is reviewed, and the free retention is three days. A client that is not a webmaster cannot use this as storage at any price below USD 20/TB-month.
Bindingwrite-gated Before any byte or day can bind, the account has to be approved as a webmaster. At 1 TiB a month an unapproved account never reaches a quota question, and an approved one hits the 3-day retention on the free path.
Identityemail Registration is free, but the upload right needs a review of the applicant's website: 'FileQ provides file upload privileges exclusively to verified webmasters with established websites and significant traffic'.
