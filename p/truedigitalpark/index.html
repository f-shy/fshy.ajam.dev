Composes withThis is the cleanest chain among documented hosts: THB 100,000 buys the Thai company registration and the two-year visa that the Google credit requires, so the package converts a visa problem into a procurement problem. It composes with Google's own startup tiers because the credit lands on a billing account the founder controls, and it breaks if the 600,000 baht balance cannot be shown, which is roughly USD 18,000 parked in a bank.

NotesTHB 100,000 does not buy the credits: they are listed as a package benefit 'for verified startups' and verification is a separate step. Whether the fee is annual is unresolved: the page says '1 Year co-working space' and '2-Year' visa without stating a renewal price. The USD 25,000 is plausibly Google's standard startup tier rather than a True Digital Park grant, because Google's own Start tier is USD 2,000 and its Scale tier USD 100,000, so USD 25,000 sits between them and may be a partner-negotiated figure; the Google page was not read. The 25 percent shareholding rule is what stops a founder holding the company entirely through a nominee, and the 600,000 baht savings rule is a capital test rather than an identity test.
