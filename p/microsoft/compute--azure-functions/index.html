Composes with3,200,000 instance-seconds is the largest perpetual serverless budget found among evaluated offerings and it needs no IPv4 front, so it composes with an IPv6-only VPS as the scheduler that renews and health-checks it, or as the worker for jobs the 10 minute limit can divide.

NotesThe 400,000 GB-s figure is a memory-time budget, not a wall-clock one: the same 400,000 buys 37 days at 128 MB, 18.5 days at 256 MB, or 9.3 days at 512 MB. The 'paid consumption subscriptions only' footnote is the unresolved part, because it may exclude the free trial subscription a new account would start with; the portal quota page on a fresh subscription is the test. Azure App Service F1 is the separate always-free web lane and is already recorded in earlier readings.
