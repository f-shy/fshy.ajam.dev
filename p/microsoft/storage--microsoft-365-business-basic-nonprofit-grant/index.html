Free tierMicrosoft 365 Business Basic (Nonprofit Staff Pricing) is a 'Grant — Free up to 300 users', and every seat carries '1 TB of cloud storage per user' (OneDrive). 300 x 1 TB = 300 TB of pooled tenant storage at zero cost. SharePoint on top: total org storage '1 TB + 10GB per license purchased', max 25 TB per site collection.

Toolingrclone backend 'onedrive' (Tier 1, chunked upload, server-side move); Microsoft Graph API (/me/drive, /sites/{id}/drive); SharePoint REST. The Graph endpoints are the documented programmatic path.
Composes withThe multiplier here is seats, not orgs: 300 granted seats per nonprofit tenant, 1 TB each, plus 10 GB of SharePoint pool per licence with a 25 TB ceiling per site collection. So a single tenant can present 300 TB of OneDrive and multi-TB SharePoint document libraries. 300 seats at 1 TB is the largest free number found anywhere in this niche. Multiple 501(c)(3) entities = separate tenants.
ReliabilityThe grant survived the July 2025 cull that killed donated Business Premium and O365 E1. Business Basic is still listed as 'Grant, Free up to 300 users'. Unused seats are reclaimed under the 85% rule; inactive tenants risk losing grants at renewal.
