Bindingvolume-quota the credit is a total byte allowance that dies at 30 days. At 1 TiB a month it carries 2.18 TiB in that one month, so the credit itself is not the binding number; the 30-day expiry ends the arrangement while credit is still nominally available, because the second month does not exist.
Identitycard All you need is a phone number, a credit card or a debit card (non-prepaid), and a Microsoft account or a GitHub account. We don't charge your credit card or debit card anything when you sign up for Azure, but you may see a one-dollar (or equivalent) verification hold.

Reliabilitythe free account must move to pay-as-you-go within 30 days or when the credit is used up, whichever is first, or free services stop
NotesArithmetic: $200 over $0.087 a GB is 2,298.9 GB, plus the 100 GB free each month = 2,398.9 GB = 2.40 TB = 2.18 TiB, all inside a 30-day window. That clears 1 TiB a month for exactly one month. The wider offer also carries 12 months of free monthly amounts of selected services and 65 or more always-free services, but the 100 GB a month egress line is the only one that touches this test, and it is about a tenth of 1 TiB. Routing preference transit ISP at $0.08 per GB buys 2,599 GB = 2.36 TiB instead of 2.18, an 8 percent change that does not move the verdict.
