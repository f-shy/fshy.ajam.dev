The award, its size and its expiry are all decided after that conversation, so no number exists to record.

NotesThis is the negative half of Azure's academic lane, and specifically because it publishes no number: a reader who hears 'Azure research credits' and finds this page learns only that a conversation exists. The adjacent Azure lanes are worse, not better: AFMR granted 'up to $20,000 in Azure credits' through a university call that closed in January 2024, and the Azure-for-nonprofits grant is USD 2,000 a year but needs nonprofit status. The one Azure program that names individual researchers - AI for Good - is separate in this record and fails on a Washington State condition instead. So all four Azure academic routes close, on three different conditions.
