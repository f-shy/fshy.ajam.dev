ReliabilityThe most honest reliability disclosure and simultaneously the worst track record. The provider's own post-Mesa writeup describes a migration that 'took about 5 days' with intermittent unreachability, a multi-week server-delivery failure, and states plainly: 'do NOT use Kyun for anything that earns you money without a verified, up to date backup that you can roll over to at a moment's notice'. Also notes customers 'reported us to the cops'. No identity documents required — a rare property here.
