5 MB TOTAL across up to 10 files per message; 'Twilio rejects messages with over 5MB of media'. Paid per message ($ per MMS segment), not a free tier. Carrier-grade resizing mangles images.
Bindingper-object-cap 5 MB of media per message, and every message is billed, so capacity is paid for per message
Identityany-phone (placeholder) trial sign-up verifies an email address and a personal phone number and needs no credit card; the identity needed for a paid account that sends MMS is unverified
EgressAuthenticated only, and Twilio says so outright: 'If you need to embed media in web applications without exposing your credentials, download the media files and serve them from your own server or cloud storage service.' No public URL, ever.
Per-object cap5 MB total across up to 10 media files per message; Twilio rejects messages with over 5MB of media
RetentionNo quota. Media is retained indefinitely.
Interfacerest rest
Toolingtwilio-python / twilio-node; .media.list() and .media(sid).fetch(). Clean API, but nothing removes the auth requirement.
Composes with5 MB per message is the wall. To reach 1 TB you need ~200,000 MMS, each billed per message. This is the opposite of a free tier. Retention is excellent and completely unusable at this scale.
ReliabilityThe best retention semantics in the slot (never expires unless you delete) ruined by a 5 MB message cap and per-message billing. Interesting only as proof that indefinite retention is commercially possible.
NotesNo quota. Media is retained indefinitely. The docs say Twilio 'retains the stored media until you delete the related Media subresource instance', i.e. retention is customer-controlled and unbounded in time.
