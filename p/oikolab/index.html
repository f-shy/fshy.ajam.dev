Free tierPay As You Go plan with 1,500 data units a month free, then USD 0.01 a unit; a data unit is 1 location x 1 parameter x 1 month; up to 2,500 units per call, historical data from 1940, global forecast models and an air quality forecast; sign-up without credit card.
unit mixed; metered on object-count; The true unit is a data unit (one location, one parameter, one month of time series, or 1 degree x 1 degree x 1 month for NetCDF), which the unit vocabulary lacks. Ten years of two parameters for ten locations cost 2,400 units. Bulk path: multi-location calls (the Business plan allows 10 locations a call) and JSON, CSV or NetCDF answers; gridded NetCDF is listed under Enterprise. ERA5 history lags by 5 days and recent days are filled from forecasts. Next plan: Business at USD 150 a month with 50,000 units.
