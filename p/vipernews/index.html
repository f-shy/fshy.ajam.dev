Free tierNo ongoing free tier. The sign-up page offers a 7-day free trial on the periodic plans: 'Your 7-day free trial is completely risk free - no payment information needed.' The home page also states a 7-day money-back guarantee. The trial's own limits are not stated.
unit none; metered on none; The FAQ states that free trial accounts cannot post to Usenet; posting is enabled on paid packages.
PriceUSD 1.79 (entry plan)
Limits
  • request-rate 5 connections on VIPER10 (10 Mbit/s), 20 on VIPER50 (50 Mbit/s), 40 on VIPERUNL (unlimited speed) and on the block accounts source
  • volume-quota soft 5 TB/month on unlimited plans, reset on request via support - reported, not on the vendor page source
  • retention-clamped 4,500+ days per the vendor FAQ; the third-party r/usenet map gives a lower figure source
Periodic plans start at $1.79/mo (VIPER10), $2.69/mo (VIPER50) and $3.59/mo (VIPERUNL) when billed yearly. Block accounts do not expire: 500GB $13.99, 1000GB $23.99 and 2000GB $42.99, each a one-time payment.
Bindingnone The vendor publishes no volume cap; the reported soft ceiling is 5 TB/month, which 1 TiB/month does not reach.
Identityemail Email address and password. The sign-up page states 'Your 7-day free trial is completely risk free - no payment information needed.' Paid plans take a credit card, PayPal or cryptocurrencies; automatic renewal is not supported with cryptocurrencies.
EgressNo download limit on the periodic plans; block accounts carry a download limit; with one friend or family member; posting enabled by default on paid packages
free allowance: unlimited
Per-object capno per-object cap is published; no per-object cap is published; the unit of retrieval is a Usenet article, approximately 750 KB by convention, so 1 TiB of monthly transfer corresponds to roughly 1.4 million article…
RetentionThe vendor FAQ states 4,500+ days: 'Our platform currently retains Usenet messages that are more than 4,500 days old.' The third-party r/usenet map gives a lower figure, which does not match the vendor. (4500 days)
Interfacenntp NNTP over TLS on port 563. No HTTP or object API exists for the download path, which is driven through SABnzbd, NZBGet or nzbdav.
Composes withStacks as a cheap fill or as a low-cost primary for recent content; the ASN it shares with UsenetExpress makes it a weaker second feed than its independence suggests.
ReliabilityIndependent Dutch platform (ViperNews B.V., formerly mapped as Uzo Reto) that has shared an ASN with UsenetExpress since 2023, which makes it a less independent second feed than it looks.
NotesThe vendor states 4,500+ days of retention, about 12.3 years; the r/usenet map lists 1,500-day primary and 1,500-day secondary backfill, so the two sources disagree.
Evidence notedocumented Prices, plan limits, retention and the free trial are vendor-stated and were read on 2026-11-03. Only the monthly soft cap is third-party (r/usenet); the vendor publishes no download limit on the periodic plans.
