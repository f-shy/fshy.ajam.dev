Bindingwrite-gated A person reviews each request before anything is provisioned, so that gate is reached before any byte is transferred and it is what fails first at 1 TiB/month. Renewal is the second gate: a prefix that is not renewed annually is removed after a short grace period.

NotesThe two third-party lists disagree with each other and with the policy page on what is free: tunnelbroker.services says a /64 costs EUR 11.50/year, handwiki says a::/128 costs EUR 1.50 once and a /80 EUR 5.50 once, and the policy page speaks of /64 and /80 accounts as things that already exist when a /48 is bought. A free routed prefix is therefore not established. AS208753, PoPs in Milan, Frankfurt, Vienna, Bradford, Los Angeles, Las Vegas and Wuhan. The homepage read returned a server-rendered SEO fallback, not the React application; the policies page carried the substance. Re-read 2026-10-23: BGP is available at most locations, and the policies page carries the same sentence as the homepage.
