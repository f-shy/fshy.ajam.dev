per-object-cap: none documented per file; per-request volume soft-capped at ~10 TB per 2-month window per upload area, and upload areas are not backed up. Retention: transit area purged after 2 months if not submitted; once submitted, ENA is permanent. Binding constraint on READ is per-ip-shaping: single stream 45 KB/s, 8 parallel ranged GETs 430 KB/s (9.6x).
Bindingretention-clamped a clock, not a quota: bytes leave whether or not transfer is completed
Identityanonymous (inferred, not read) not published
EgressUnmetered and anonymous. ftp.sra.ebi.ac.uk is CORS-open (Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *) and already served through Cloudflare (Server: cloudflare), so CF is in front by default.
RetentionRetention: transit area purged after 2 months if not submitted; once submitted, ENA is permanent (60 days)
Interfacesftp, ftp, rclone, cli sftp
ToolingWebin-CLI (github.com/enasequence/webin-cli), plus plain FTP/Aspera. Read side is plain HTTPS/FTP with HTTP range support. Works with rclone's http/sftp remotes and curl.
Composes withOne Webin account = unlimited studies. The upload area is a transit buffer (10 TB documented expectation), while published accessions are permanent and unmetered on the read side. Multiply across accounts/centres; read side needs no account at all, so the fetch workload is uncapped regardless of how many deposit accounts exist.
ReliabilityUpload path is the weak side. FTP sessions drop out, Aspera recommended; upload area is explicitly 'not backed up'.
