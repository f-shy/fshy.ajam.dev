Free tierA Free plan exists on the members dashboard ('Your Plan - Free - No active plan') with 'higher daily quotas' reserved for Premium. The free allowance itself is not published on any public page read.
unit mixed; metered on unstated; Per-host pages publish one number, e.g. 'Daily limit: 30 GB' for Rapidgator, without saying which tier it belongs to. A JDownloader board post from a user reports 1 GB/day as a guest; a 2018 review reports 13 hosts for free users. Both are third-party and old, so the free number stays unverified.
PriceUSD 14.99/month; USD 6.66/month on the annual term
Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) per-host daily limits, e.g. 'Daily limit: 30 GB' on Rapidgator and 'Daily limit: 5 GB' on Filefox source
  • request-rate free plan is queued behind premium; 'Higher Quotas' is the advertised premium delta source
The generator reports 'Maintenance' and 'Daily limit: 30 GB' side by side on the Rapidgator page, so a host can be both limited and degraded.
Bindingvolume-quota The per-host daily cap is the only published number and it differs per host, so 1 TiB of monthly transfer depends on spreading files across hosts; the 30 GB/day Rapidgator cap alone is 900 GB/month.
Identityemail Premium is a voucher key emailed after payment; checkout is Paddle (card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, iDEAL), PayPal, CoinPayments crypto, Difmark or Paysafecard. No phone and no card are needed for a free account.
EgressTransfers run over a '10 Gbps backbone'; per-host daily limits apply and are shown per host. No total monthly byte allowance is published.
free allowance: unpublished; per-host 'Daily limit' figures such as 30 GB on Rapidgator
Per-object capnot applicable: Cocoleech does not host files ('Cocoleech does not host files.')
Retentionnot applicable: temporary links only. 'Create a temporary accelerated link'.
Interfacerest Web generator, Chrome and Firefox extensions, and a JDownloader 2 plugin. No public API documented on the pages read.
ToolingJDownloader 2 plugin, Chrome and Firefox extensions. That is the widest first-party tooling of the small providers among debrid providers.
Composes withIts JDownloader plugin makes it the least-friction free generator for a headless client, but at USD 14.99/month it is three to five times the sub-USD-5 providers, so it is a fallback for tooling, not for price.
ReliabilityA 2021 test by multihosts.de reported that Cocoleech 'completely discontinued its free service' and later pages still show a Free plan, so the free tier's existence has been unstable across years; per-host limits and 'Maintenance' states change without notice.
