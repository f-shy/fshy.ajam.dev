NotesThe largest published promo-versus-list gap in the set: EUR 14.49/month struck through against EUR 6.99 charged, 52%. Two things make it less alarming than the number suggests. The discount has run unchanged for 37 months, so the list price is never observed to be charged; and the 12-month term at EUR 9.00 sets the realistic renewal floor, which is 28.8% above the 15-month rate. A client who renews at the 15-month rate pays nothing extra; one who falls to the 12-month rate pays 28.8% more. Potential failure points: retention, an indexer listing that outlives the article, or a group subscription problem. All three are named by the vendor. It is documented as an observation about the documentation, not about the spool. The 15-month offer at EUR 6.99/mo is the lowest per-month figure the vendor itself publishes, at EUR 104.85 for the term.
