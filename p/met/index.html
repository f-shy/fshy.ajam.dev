Free tierNo fee, no key and no registration: Locationforecast and the other products of api.met.no answer any coordinate at up to 20 requests a second per application (total, not per client); the caller sets a User-Agent that names the application and gives a contact.
unit requests; metered on request-count; Over 20 requests a second needs a special agreement; throttled clients get HTTP 429, a missing or generic User-Agent gets HTTP 403, and coordinates are cut to 4 decimals. Locationforecast is refreshed every hour inside the Nordic area and every 6 hours elsewhere. Bulk path: the THREDDS server serves model files (NetCDF over OPeNDAP) for the Nordic and Arctic regions only, with no global files; historical observations come from the separate Frost API (see data-apis/met-norway-frost).

Free tierFree access to MET Norway's archive of historical weather and climate observations (quality controlled daily, monthly and yearly measurements of temperature, precipitation and wind, and station metadata) with a client ID issued for an email address; no numeric request quota is published.
unit requests; metered on request-count; Heavy traffic is defined as more than 1 request a second on average, which should be cached locally; users with high load or bad traffic patterns can be blocked. Large requests time out and are split by station or by time range. Bulk path: none beyond split requests; gridded model data are on THREDDS (see data-apis/met-norway).
