Free tierAs a free user, you can debride one link at a time. The FAQ puts the free account at 'up to 1 link per day'. The guest downloader covers only hosts that are already free: 1fichier, facebook, redtube, twitter, xnxx, xvideos, youporn, youtube.
unit requests; metered on request-count; Two different numbers appear. The FAQ says 1 link/day for a free account. The API reference says 70 unique files per 24 h per account (error 121), which is the paid-account ceiling. The conservative figure is used here. Guest 1fichier allowance is its own counter: 'Links : 0/1 Per Day'.
PriceEUR 4.50/month subscription; EUR 3 per 15 days one-time (USD 5.05 and USD 3.37 at the 2026-10-23 ECB-derived rate of 0.8915 EUR per USD)
Limits
  • pull-count (binding) 1 link per day on a free account; 70 unique files per 24 h on a paid account (web and API combined) source
  • request-rate 1 concurrent link as a free user; error 122 'Too many unrestricts in progress - retry in ~1 min' source
  • per-ip-shaping error 632 'VPN/proxy detected' and error 56 'Invalid IP address, waiting time not elapsed' block datacentre and VPN egress source
Hosts go offline individually; the downloader page showed 51 of 52 hosts online and filejoker listed as offline on the read date.
Bindingpull-count At 1 TiB/month the free tier fails on link count long before bytes: 1 link/day is 30 unlocks a month, so 1 TiB of monthly transfer would need a 34 GiB average file. On the paid tier the same counter binds first at 70 files per 24 h.
Identityemail Free account needs a username, an email address and a password. Google and Facebook sign-in also offered. No card and no phone on the free path. Paid plans are bought as a key or by subscription.
EgressDownload runs from BestDebrid's servers to the user. Premium advertises 'Unlimited bandwidth' and 'Unlimited remote traffic(1)', with the footnote 'Remote traffic (excluding Internet service providers)'. Free users are capped by links per day, not by bytes.
free allowance: 1 link/day (free account); per-host counters shown live, e.g. 1fichier 0/1 per day
Per-object capnot applicable: the service unlocks a file-host URL and does not store the object
Retentionnot applicable: no storage product. Generated links are temporary and the downloader keeps a history of them for the account.
Interfacerest, webdav REST API documented at /fr/restapi.php with API keys, per-host limits endpoint and error codes; the support form lists 'JDownloader, Kodi, Stremio, WebDAV, API' as client categories.
endpoint: https://bestdebrid.com/api/v1/hostslimits
ToolingJDownloader/IDM via the API, a documented REST API, and a browser downloader. No rclone backend.
Composes withA sub-USD-5 one-time 15-day pass (EUR 3) at unlimited remote traffic is the cheapest per-TB route among debrid providers for a user who needs many file hosts rather than torrent cache; it pairs with JDownloader for a resumable queue.
ReliabilityFree tier covers only hosts that are already free; filejoker was offline on the read date; VPN and proxy egress is refused with error 632; the paid plan allows VPN.
NotesThe free tier is not a free multi-hoster: its host list is the set of hosts that need no debrid. The sub-USD-5 band is the paid side. USD conversions use the exchangerate-api USD base read on 2026-10-23 (EUR 0.891529 per USD). Cheapest per-month shape is the 365-day one-time pass at EUR 45, or EUR 3 per 15 days for a short burst. The '2147483647 GB' bandwith figure is 2^31-1, an integer sentinel, not a measured allowance.
