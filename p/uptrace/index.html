Free tierUptrace Cloud is free up to 50 GB and 5,000 timeseries per month, then from $0.075/GB with automatic volume discounts; unlimited users, services and hosts. A separate free trial offers 1 TB of storage and 100,000 timeseries with no card required.
unit GB; metered on uploaded-bytes; The ongoing free allowance is 50 GB per month and 5,000 timeseries; the 1 TB / 100,000-timeseries figure is a signup trial. Metrics are billed per million ingested datapoints from $0.025 per 1M.

Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 50 GB free per month and 5,000 timeseries per month on Uptrace Cloud Free; beyond that from $0.075/GB source
  • request-rate Metrics billing starts at $0.025 per 1M ingested datapoints and decreases with volume; minimum supported collection interval is 10 seconds source
  • retention-clamped default retention 4 weeks (28 days); longer storage only via paid cold storage up to 100 weeks source
Bindingvolume-quota The 50 GB/month and 5,000-timeseries free allowance is the first ceiling a free user hits; retention is a fixed 28-day default and per-object caps are not published.
Identityemail Cloud signup is a free account with no card required for the trial; billing is handled by Paddle by credit card or bank transfer after signup. A public live demo can be explored without any account.
EgressSending data from AWS or GCP incurs outbound fees; the vendor says OTel Arrow cuts bandwidth 30-70% versus standard OTLP and compresses telemetry 15-30x. Ingestion above the free allowance is charged per GB, not egress from Uptrace.
free allowance: 50 GB per month included
Per-object capunverified: no single-object cap is published; the free Cloud allowance is 50 GB per month of ingested spans/logs plus 5,000 timeseries, which are volume quotas rather than a per-object cap.
RetentionUptrace Cloud keeps all data for 4 weeks (28 days) by default; longer cold storage on S3 or HDD is available for up to 100 weeks at $0.01 per GB-month for spans and $0.20 per 1,000 timeseries-month for metrics. (28 days)
Interfacerest, cli, browser, chat OpenTelemetry OTLP ingest for traces, metrics and logs; Prometheus, Vector, FluentBit and CloudWatch ingest; a built-in MCP server queryable from AI assistants using the existing Uptrace session token; UQL and PromQL-compatible query engines and a web UI.
ToolingOpenTelemetry SDKs and Collector, Prometheus, Vector, FluentBit, Grafana data-source support; the self-hosted Community Edition that the user installs and runs is the last resort
Composes withA hosted Uptrace Cloud endpoint takes OTLP telemetry from existing OpenTelemetry instrumentation; self-hosting the AGPL Community Edition on a rented or free machine is the last-resort path when the hosted free allowance is insufficient.
ReliabilityCloud data is stored in Hetzner's Germany datacenters with backups in Finland; 99.9% uptime is listed. Sending from AWS or GCP incurs outbound fees; dropped sampled data costs $0.01 per GB.
NotesUptrace Cloud has a genuine ongoing free tier of 50 GB and 5,000 timeseries per month with no card, plus a separate 1 TB / 100k-timeseries signup trial. Paid usage starts at $0.075/GB and data is kept 28 days by default. Self-hosted Community Edition is the last resort, not the offer this record describes.
