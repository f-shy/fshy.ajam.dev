Composes withSecond mirror in a 3-way npm serving tier (unpkg + hop.js + jsDelivr). Because all three read the same npm tarballs, publishing once to npm gives you three independent free CDNs with different per-file ceilings. JsDelivr 50 MB, unpkg and hop.js effectively npm-bounded. Shard at 45 MB and every mirror serves every shard.
ReliabilityLong-running but small-team; Cloudflare absorbs the bandwidth. Was 403 on the .exe? No. Unpkg served it; it is jsDelivr that blocks the extension.
