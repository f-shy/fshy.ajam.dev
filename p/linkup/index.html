NotesThe free balance renews monthly, which sets it apart from one-time signup grants in this class. The pricing page names professional email addresses as the eligible ones. The app tells any other sign-up that free credits are not granted by default and that adding a card, with no payment required, unlocks them; the amount unlocked, and whether the monthly top-up then applies, is not stated. An archived copy of the pricing page from June 2026 states the credit with no email condition, and one from August 2026 states it for professional email addresses. The x402 route bills a flat USD 0.01 a request on search and fetch.
