  • retention-clamped (binding) One charge, then a hard stop: the 500,000 toman is a single signup gift with no renewal, and Liara charges 200,000 toman a month for an IPv4 address alone, so the credit buys about one month of a small server plus one address and then the account needs an Iranian payment route. source
Identityemail an email address or an Iranian mobile number, with email sufficient: the login screen reads 'enter your email or mobile number to sign in or register; sign in with email'. Registering alone grants the 500,000 toman credit. No card is needed to register and no hold is stated. The record's notes state that paying beyond the credit needs an Iranian card or gateway, which the pricing pages do not enumerate, so debit and prepaid acceptance are not stated.
EgressVPS plan: 2,500 toman per GB of download traffic, upload traffic free; no included download allowance is stated on the page. The egress terms of the free cloud host plan are not published on the page read.
RetentionOne charge, then a hard stop: the 500,000 toman is a single signup gift with no renewal, and Liara charges 200,000 toman a month for an IPv4 address alone, so the credit buys about one month of a small server plus one address and then the account needs an Iranian payment route.
Interfacecli, rest Liara API and Liara CLI, per the docs.liara.ir documentation index, which lists "Liara API"
Toolingthe provider documents its own CLI, "Liara CLI", on docs.liara.ir
Composes withThe signup credit plus a free-hosting plan is the composition that works: the free plan holds a small app with no clock, and the 500,000 toman buys a real server for the month when the app needs CPU, with the caveat that the IPv4 line item eats 40 percent of the credit before any compute is bought.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesThe one Iranian provider found where the door is an email address rather than an Iranian mobile number, which makes it the exception in a market where national identity is usually the first gate. Two limits notable: the credit is denominated in a currency that loses value, so the USD equivalent moves with the rial and is not stated here as a number; and paying beyond the credit needs an Iranian card or gateway, which the pricing pages do not enumerate. The PaaS lane is more useful than the VPS lane because it does not need an IPv4 surcharge. The one recorded is the better-evidenced.
