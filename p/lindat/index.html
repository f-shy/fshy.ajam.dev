Account is the gate, not bytes: you need an eduGAIN institutional login, a CLARIN account, or a manually granted local account. Files >2 GB need help-desk handling. Every deposit signs a Distribution License Agreement and must carry adequate metadata, so bulk dumping of undocumented blobs will be rejected.

ToolingDSpace REST/OAI-PMH plus a documented submission workflow (repository/mydspace); handles as PIDs; SWORD-style deposit contract per collection ('Distribution License Agreement'). CLARIN SPF / eduGAIN SSO, and a local account on request.
Composes withUnlimited items per depositor x no per-item byte cap. Multiple CLARIN centres (LINDAT, CLARIN.SI, CLARIN-D, plus 20+ national nodes) each run their own DSpace with the same open door, so the chain is 'one account per centre'.
ReliabilityRun by UFAL with an explicit preservation policy since 2010; stable, but the metadata/contract review and the >2 GB manual step make it a scripted-assisted path rather than a fire-and-forget API.
