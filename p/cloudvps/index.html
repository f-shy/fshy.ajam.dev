Free tierA 7-day free VPS test: fill in a form with the chosen configuration, the application is checked, credentials arrive by email, and the server runs free for seven days. The paid catalogue starts at BYN 19.99 a month for the cloud Micro shape of one core at 3.6 GHz, 1 GB RAM and 15 GB NVMe on a 200 Mbit/s port, shown at BYN 14.99 a month with a 25 percent discount applied on the page, with 5 TB of traffic included per calendar month and expansion to 37 TB. Payment is accepted by international and Russian cards in any currency, and the provider states it. A separate VPS line in Belarus starts at BYN 29.99 a month for 1 core, 2 GB RAM and 30 GB disk.
