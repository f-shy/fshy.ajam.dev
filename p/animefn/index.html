ReliabilityShared gigabit and shared disk mean a neighbour's swarm sets the observed speed; the page publishes no uptime figure and no refund condition.
NotesThe only Arabic-language seedbox storefront found among debrid providers that publishes prices. Its relevance to retrieval is indirect: it seeds and stages, it does not unlock file hosts, so it composes with a debrid service rather than replacing one.
