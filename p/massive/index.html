Free tierStocks Basic USD 0/month: 5 API calls per minute, 2 years of historical data, end-of-day data; each asset class (stocks, options, indices, currencies, futures) has its own free Basic tier at 5 requests a minute
unit requests; metered on request-count; Basic reaches end-of-day aggregate bars, reference data, corporate actions and technical indicators over REST only; trades, quotes, snapshots, WebSockets and flat files start at the paid tiers. Paid tiers have no request limit: Stocks Starter is USD 29/month with 5 years of history, 15-minute delayed data and flat files. Extra API keys share the account's limits. Polygon.io was renamed Massive on 2025-10-30, and polygon.io redirects to massive.com.

History depth on Stocks Basic is 2 years, end-of-day; no limits[] kind names a history window, and the term it needs is history-window. Bulk path: flat files over S3 from Stocks Starter (USD 29/month) up; no bulk path on Basic.
Bindingrequest-rate 5 requests a minute is the only numeric limit on Basic; the 2-year window bounds what those requests reach.
Identityemail Signup offers GitHub, Google or email; registration asks for information that may include a name and an email. The stocks page states the free tier needs no credit card. A call without a key answers HTTP 401 with 'API Key was not provided'.
Egressnot metered in bytes; 5 requests a minute per asset class bound what a free key can pull
Per-object capnot applicable: one JSON response per call
Interfacerest, s3-compatible REST at https://api.massive.com, with api.polygon.io answering in parallel. Flat files are compressed CSVs over an S3-compatible endpoint, https://files.massive.com, bucket flatfiles, from the first paid tier up; the free Basic tier is REST only.
endpoint: https://api.massive.com
Toolingcurl or any HTTP client; flat files with an S3 client (the AWS CLI, rclone, the MinIO client and boto3 are named)
Composes withFree end-of-day bars for two years at 5 calls a minute; for history past two years the Alpha Vantage weekly or monthly series and the Twelve Data daily series reach further back for free, and the paid tiers sell flat files.
ReliabilityThe api.polygon.io base still answers but is announced for phase-out; api.massive.com is the current base.
NotesThe free tier is a sampler: 5 calls a minute per asset class, end-of-day, two years back. The value of the provider is the paid flat files, which are the only bulk path among the four market data records.
Evidence notedocumented The Basic tier, the per-asset-class limit and the flat-file gate are documented on the pricing page and knowledge-base articles. Keyless probes of both API bases answered 401 with the same error; the 5-per-minute cut-off needs a free key to measure.
