Free tierPlus is USD 5 per month for 5 sandboxes, 2 GB of storage, a 4-hour session timeout and up to 4 vCPU. Pro runs from USD 15 per month (10 sandboxes, 2 GB) through USD 30 (25 sandboxes, 10 GB, 8-hour timeout, up to 8 vCPU), USD 60 (60 sandboxes) to USD 100 (150 sandboxes). Code executions are not limited by plan: plans differ on sandbox count, storage, session timeout, compute capacity and support. There is no free plan.

NotesThe cheapest paid shape recorded among documented hosts by subscription, and the honest floor for a Docker sandbox that must not be rebuilt from scratch. It is recorded as paid-only because the vendor states plainly that there is no free plan, which refutes the 'start free' line in the page description that the page itself then contradicts in its own FAQ. The RAM per sandbox is not stated on the pricing page, so shape carries null for RAM; 'up to 4 vCPU' is the only compute number published.
