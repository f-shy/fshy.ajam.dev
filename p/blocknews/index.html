NotesThe block is the answer to the debrid question in its purest form: nothing to renew, nothing to lapse. What it does not give is storage of a chosen file - it gives access to articles the provider happens to hold. Trigger condition: a published article-retention figure, or a change to the non-expiry claim. At $32.55/TB it is cheaper than every UsenetExpress block except CubeNet's 2 TB tier, and it sits on a different backbone, which is what a fill account needs.
