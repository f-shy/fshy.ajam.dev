NotesThe cheapest Hong Kong-native row among evaluated providers and the widest carrier menu. Miaomiao sells four separate Hong Kong and Taiwan NAT groups on real ISP lines - HGC, HKT, HKBN and HiNet - plus a Germany group and an IPv6-first group that pairs a /64 with a DNS64 egress. HGC-Nano at CNY 6.60 = USD 0.9873 a month for 256 MB, 10 GB, 1 TB bidirectional and 10 forwarding rules is the cheapest of them; HKT-Nano is CNY 8.80 and HKBN-Nano CNY 9.90 for the same shape on different carriers. That carrier choice is the reason this store is worth a row: an HGC or HKBN address behaves differently from a datacentre address for anything that cares about the network it comes from, and an English-language search does not surface it. The IPv6-first group is the other reason: HKIDCv6-Nano at CNY 9.90 = USD 1.4810 gives 512 MB, a /64 and a DNS64 egress, which is the same pattern Privex uses and only the second seller among evaluated providers to publish it. Three caveats. The cart route is broken for every product id tried - pids 39 and 45 both rendered the German DEIDC group rather than the product - so neither stock nor the payment step was established, and the price comes from the listing rather than a priced line. The store states '无技术支持 无退款', no support and no refunds, in its own header. Registration needs a name, city and postcode but no ID and no required phone, which is a low identity cost by the standards of this group of sellers. What is not established: stock on any group except HiNet-Nano, which reads '0 可用'; the payment rails; and whether the forwarding rules carry UDP. A buyer who wants a Hong Kong-native address should treat the price as listed rather than confirmed.
