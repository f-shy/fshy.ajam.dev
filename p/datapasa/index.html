Free tierNo free tier. Cheapest paid offer: VPS-1, 1 vCPU, 1 GB RAM, 16 GB SSD, 900 Mbit/s shared port, 1 TB traffic, KVM, in Germany (Falkenstein), USD 1.81 per month, which is USD 0.0025 per hour; the monthly figure is the hourly rate over 730 hours. Servers are billed by the hour from a prepaid USD balance, with no setup fee and no minimum term, and prices exclude VAT.
unit none; metered on wall-clock; VPS-2 is 1 vCPU, 2 GB RAM, 32 GB SSD and 32 TB traffic at USD 3.54 per month; the unmetered n1.micro is 2 vCPU, 2 GB RAM, 32 GB SSD and a 50 Mbit/s guaranteed port at USD 29.20 per month; dedicated servers show out of stock

Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 1 TB of traffic included on VPS-1; USD 2.00 per TB beyond the plan limit source
  • per-ip-shaping 900 Mbit/s shared port on VPS-1; the unmetered plans guarantee 50, 100 or 250 Mbit/s source
  • per-object-cap 16 GB of SSD on VPS-1; 32 GB on VPS-2 source
Registration is limited to 10 attempts per hour per IP address and login to 10 attempts per 15 minutes per email and IP address.
Bindingvolume-quota 1 TB of included traffic on the cheapest plan is smaller than the 32 TB on the next plan up, so traffic is the limit that binds before disk or port speed.
Identityemail Registration takes first name, last name, email, password and an accepted-terms flag; the request has no phone or identity-document field. A verified email address is required before ordering. The prepaid USD balance is topped up from USD 5 by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe, PayPal, PIX, or about 100 cryptocurrencies, so no card is needed. Authenticated API calls other than /api/me return 403 FINGERPRINT_REQUIRED until a device check is completed in the control panel.
EgressVPS-1 includes 1 TB of traffic on a 900 Mbit/s shared port; traffic beyond a plan's included limit costs USD 2.00 per TB; unmetered plans exist from USD 29.20 per month with a guaranteed port instead of a shared one
free allowance: 1 TB on VPS-1 (the period is not stated on the pricing page)
price per tb: USD 2.00
Retentiona server is billed by the hour until it is deleted; the vendor's billing wiki covers suspension after the balance runs out, and the suspension period was not read
Interfacerest, ssh REST API at https://cloud.datapasa.com/api described by an OpenAPI document at https://datapasa.com/openapi.json; SSH access and a VMmanager panel are listed in the vendor's server-management wiki; the plans table states a VNC console on all plans
Toolingbearer-token REST API: POST /api/auth/login returns a 15-minute access token and a 30-day refresh token; no API keys or OAuth are issued; /api/public/catalog lists locations, plans and USD prices without credentials; authenticated endpoints other than /api/me answer 403 FINGERPRINT_REQUIRED until a device check is completed in the control panel
Composes withA German KVM box that is created and deleted through a documented REST API and billed by the hour from a USD 5 minimum balance, with cryptocurrency top-ups accepted, fits short-lived workers and proxies; the 32 TB traffic plans start at USD 3.54 per month.
Reliabilityunverified: no outage history was read; the site states a 99.9% uptime SLA.
NotesHourly-billed German KVM VPS reseller operated by DataPasa Limited. The same site also sells game servers, domains and RIPE NCC ASN registration or sponsorship at USD 99 per year. A second location, Sao Paulo, is listed as coming soon. The offers-feed title quotes USD 1.79 per month with a coupon; the list price on the pricing page is USD 1.81.
