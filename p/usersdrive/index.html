NotesThe verdict is about the scripted path, not about the host: a browser or a debrid reaches the file. 73 GB/day x 30 = 2,190 GB = 2.19 TB = 1.99 TiB if the vendor's GB is decimal, which is what the rest of the table implies (5250 Mb file cap). Treated as GiB the month is 2.14 TiB. The claim that this 'beats USD 3.00 for one TiB' is true of the quota and false of the price comparison: TorBox Essential at USD 3.00 sells a 10 TB/month non-flagging ceiling, 9.095 TiB, across many hosts. Free wins on cash for 1 TiB from this host; it does not win on capacity, coverage or time. Two defects: a plan that publishes no allowance cannot carry a per-TiB figure, and 70/12 is USD 5.83 only on a 12-month year with no days in it. The plan belongs in the flat-month group, and its price is USD 70.00/year = USD 5.75/month. The reason it matters is ranking: a per-TiB label put a single-host key in the same column as plans that publish a byte cap. The finding that matters is the free tier, not the price: 73 GB/day free with unlimited storage is the only free allowance found that covers the whole month without a key.
