The pricing page lists the signup credit in its Pay As You Go plan card, priced Free, with every data source while the free credits last and then web, arXiv and PubMed on pay-as-you-go, and the academia page says new accounts get the credits across every source and then pay as you go; the documented plan IDs are tier_0 Pay-as-you-go, tier_1 Ridiculously Cheap, tier_2 Comfortably Pro and tier_3 Serious Business, with no plan named for signup credits
