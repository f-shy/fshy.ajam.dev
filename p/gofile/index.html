content is deleted after 10 days of inactivity unless it keeps being downloaded; past the storage period content may be moved to cold storage and importing it back requires Premium; most API endpoints (listing, direct links) return error-notPremium; 'Repeatedly exceeding rate limits may result in automatic IP bans'
Bindingretention-clamped a clock, not a quota: bytes leave whether or not transfer is completed
Identityanonymous No account required.
EgressFree traffic may be counted by IP address, so on a shared IP other people's downloads can count toward the usage shown. Bandwidth is not throttled; over the allowance some download functionality is restricted.
free allowance: 100 GB per 30 days
RetentionContent is kept for 10 days of inactivity, and stays longer as long as it keeps being downloaded.
Interfacerest, rclone rest; rclone (the FAQ names an official rclone implementation)
ToolingREST API at api.gofile.io (documented, CORS, per-endpoint rate limits); rclone backend 'gofile' (Tier 1) but its docs warn 'if you wish to connect rclone to Gofile you will need a premium account'; guest account creation is a single unauthenticated POST
Composes withAn upload without a token creates a guest account and returns a guestToken to reuse; the API reference says to create one account and reuse its token, not one per upload. Each free account is capped at 10,000 stored contents. A separate traffic allowance per guest account is not stated.
Reliability10-day inactivity expiry plus cold-storage migration makes it storage-with-a-TTL, not a drive; content-count cap (10k) bites hard on many-small-file datasets
NotesNo storage figure at all on the free tier. Free accounts get temporary storage with a 10,000-content cap per account ('Free accounts are capped at 10,000 stored contents'); free traffic allowance is '100 GB traffic / 30 days'.
