Bindingretention-clamped Unmetered bandwidth means no byte quota exists, so nothing in the volume sense fails at 1 TiB/month. What stops a month-long run is the 7-day instance life: miss one renewal and the machine and its address are gone. The vocabulary has no kind for instance lifetime, so retention-clamped carries it, with the caveat that the vendor discloses the 7 days rather than clamping silently.

Composes withFull root means WG terminates here and a conf is generated locally, so this is an ingress endpoint plus relay in one free box.
ReliabilityThird-party write-ups record the provider's NAT VPS being unusable during outages; a missed 7-day renewal deletes the instance and its address.
NotesThe best volume story in the sweep: unlimited bandwidth, full root, no card. Against it: 7-day manual renewal through a Telegram code, a 8-10 port shared IPv4, and a history of the provider's servers going down. The no-hardware premise is satisfied because this is a rented-free VM, not owned iron.
