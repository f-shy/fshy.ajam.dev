NotesThe best price-per-megabyte in the band after TierHive: USD 0.2333 a month on annual prepay for 256 MB, 2 GB of SSD, 200 GB of traffic, 20 NAT ports and a /64. The UDP statement is the cleanest found on any vendor page - the port calculator says in one sentence which port is SSH and that the other 19 carry TCP or UDP. What it does not have is a counterparty. The about page is a table with 'Registered name | VirNAT' and a dash in every other field: no address, no telephone, no tax number, no registration. The storefront is in Chinese and the brand has no history in documented records. That is the opposite of the dissolved-company problem: not a company that stopped trading, but no company at all that a reader could pursue. The 200 GB allowance is also the tightest in the band. Not established: who operates it, whether the annual SKU renews at USD 2.80, and whether DC1 is in stock.
