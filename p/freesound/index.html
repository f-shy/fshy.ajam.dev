1 GB per upload (summed across a batch); every sound must be given one of exactly three licences and pass human moderation; sounds must be sounds, not songs. 'don't be offended if your sound gets rejected by a moderator'. That moderation queue is the throughput ceiling.

EgressFree downloads, no metering, CDN-served. The site is explicitly built for bulk download (a Freesound Data Packs Portal exists for model-training dumps).

Interfacerest rest
ToolingPublic Freesound APIv2 (freesound.org/docs/api/) with OAuth2 for upload; documented endpoints for search, download, and upload. Bulk metadata via CSV/XLS upload ('Bulk description of files', unlocked after 40 sounds).
Composes withUnlimited sounds per account x 1 GB batches. The API makes it fully scriptable, unlike every podcast host here. 40 uploads unlocks bulk describe, which removes the per-sound metadata tax that otherwise makes this unusable at volume.
ReliabilityServes the original, byte-for-byte, and says so explicitly: 'When you download the sound you get the exact same original file that was uploaded, no transcoding nor any kind of editing at all, without the artefacts'. The in-site player is a low-bitrate mp3/ogg preview, the download is the master. Data does not vanish, but a moderator can reject an upload before it is ever stored.
