Composes withThe AED 500,000 cash half is equity, not a grant, so the effective free capacity is the AED 250,000 in-kind tranche, which is spent with Hub71's service providers rather than on a cloud bill; that is what limits it as a compute resource even though the headline number is the largest in the MENA slice of evaluated providers.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesThe prose on two Hub71 pages has drifted apart: a 2023 press release describes 'up to AED 750,000 in cash and in-kind incentives' while the current Access page splits it into two AED 250,000 tranches plus a performance top-up, which happens to sum to the same figure. Whether the in-kind tranche includes cloud credits is unresolved: no page read names a cloud provider, and the ADGM page describes the in-kind as housing, office space and health insurance. The cash tranche is not free money: it is explicitly 'in exchange for equity through a SAFE note'. A foreign founder can plausibly hold 100 percent of the ADGM subsidiary: the FAQ says the entity is a subsidiary of the holding entity 'regardless of the holding entity's location', which is the cheapest incorporation gate among the programmes read, after Malaysia's. The relocation clause is the real cost and it is not priced on any page read.
