Free tierNo free tier: a paid plan or prepaid credits are required to launch a computer. Without a subscription, credits are bought in USD 10, 25 or 50 packs, expiring 12 months after purchase, and a standard computer runs at USD 0.12/hour billed by the second; starting anything needs at least USD 0.25 of credit.
unit none; metered on wall-clock; The operator list's $0.12/h is the pay-as-you-go Standard computer rate for 2 vCPU / 4 GB. The Solo plan is $29/month for a pool of 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM and 80 GB across up to 4 computers.

Monthly plans: Solo $29 (4 computers, 4 vCPU / 8 GB / 80 GB pool), Studio $99 (16 computers, 16 vCPU / 32 GB / 320 GB), Swarm $399 (32 computers, 64 vCPU / 128 GB / 640 GB). A built-in computer-agent run stops after 60 minutes.
Bindingvolume-quota Prepaid credits are required before any computer starts and are what run out first; at zero credit running computers are suspended or stopped with disks kept, and nothing new starts.
Identityemail The sign-up page offers an email field, a password of at least 8 characters, and Continue with GitHub or Continue with Google. Credits and plans require payment; the terms and payment page are not gated by a phone number or ID document on the pages read.
EgressEach computer can serve a port on its own HTTPS address under mandala.dev, open to anyone with the link or private to signed-in account users; the pricing page states no separate egress volume allowance or per-GB traffic price.
Per-object capnot applicable: the metered unit is a running virtual machine, not a stored object; files up to 2 GiB can be uploaded into a computer, and a pay-as-you-go computer allows up to 4 vCPU, 8 GB RAM and 80 GB each
RetentionScheduled snapshots are opt-in and bounded by the plan's window: 7 days on Solo, 7 daily plus 4 weekly on Studio, adding 12 monthly on Swarm. A computer out of credit for 30 days may be deleted, with an email warning at least seven days before. (30 days)
Interfacerest, cli, ssh, browser, chat A REST API with Python and TypeScript SDKs, a mandala CLI, an MCP server for coding agents, a graphical Linux desktop with screenshot/click/type controls and an embeddable live view.
endpoint: https://app.mandala.computer/docs
ToolingThe user installs the Python or TypeScript SDK or the mandala CLI, or connects an agent over MCP; each computer is a hardware-isolated Linux VM.
Composes withPersistent desktop VMs let an agent use a browser and desktop applications across sessions, with snapshots for recovery, alongside inference records that supply the model calls.
ReliabilityOut-of-credit computers are suspended with sessions saved or stopped with disks kept; a computer out of credit for 30 days may be deleted with an email warning at least 7 days before. Snapshots count against snapshot storage, so a long schedule can leave less room for manual snapshots.
NotesCloud Linux desktop VMs for agents, priced per plan or by the second; no free tier or sign-up credit is published. The built-in agent is a separate metered add-on billed through Mandala AI credits.
