Free tierDownloading is free and needs no account: 'Direct Links - Share a single link - no account required for whoever downloads it' and 'Unlimited Downloads - No caps on how many times a file can be downloaded'. The free path's speed and waiting time are not stated; only the premium page prints 'Waiting time 0 seconds'.
unit none; metered on none; Free downloads exist and are uncounted by download count. What the free path costs is time, not bytes, and the site does not quantify it.
PriceUSD 23.30 per TB-month on a 30-day pass; USD 7.22 per TB-month at the annual rate
Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 50 GB per day (Premium), 200 GB per day (Premium Pro), reset daily source
  • write-gated uploading requires Partner status, 'granted after a review' source
The traffic counter starts 'From 50 GB' and does not accumulate: a day unused is a day lost. The write gate is separate and only affects publishing, not retrieval.
Bindingvolume-quota At 1 TiB a month (about 34 GB a day) the 50 GB daily bucket on Premium is enough, so the binding constraint becomes the monthly bill rather than a technical wall; at 1 TiB a day only Premium Pro's 200 GB would still fall short by a factor of five.
Identitycard Buying premium needs a card: 'Card, crypto and more' on the checkout. Downloading needs nothing at all, and a prepaid premium key can be activated without a card ('If you already have a premium key, you can activate it on the My Profile page').
EgressPaid traffic is counted per day: Premium carries '50 GB Traffic per day', Premium Pro '200 GB Traffic per day'. Within the allowance: 'High download speed', 'Unlimited parallel downloads', 'Download-Accelerators support'.
free allowance: 50 GB/day on Premium; 200 GB/day on Premium Pro
price per tb: USD 7.22 per TB at the 365-day rate (computed: USD 129.95 / 12 months over 1.5 TB per month)
Per-object capPremium prints 'Max file size: Unlimited'; Premium Pro repeats 'Unlimited file size'. No free-tier per-file cap is stated.
RetentionNot stated on the pages read.
Interfacerest, unknown 'Download-Accelerators support' and 'Unlimited parallel downloads' on the paid tier; multi-thread clients are the intended path. No API is advertised.
ToolingDownload managers (IDM, JDownloader class) as named by 'Download-Accelerators support'. A premium key can be activated on the account page, which is the resale surface these records checks elsewhere.
Composes withA host that a debrid broker resolves, priced here as the alternative to one: 50 GB a day for USD 34.95 a month is 45 times the traffic of a FilesMonster 30-day pass for twice the price, and both are far above a multi-host debrid subscription.
ReliabilityPublishing is closed to new accounts: 'Not every account can upload files - Partner status is granted after a review'. That keeps the catalogue curated and also means a client cannot use this host as free storage, only as a retrieval target.
NotesThe record exists for the daily-bucket comparison: it is the only host in this set that sells traffic by the day with no rollover, which is the shape a downloader with bursty demand wants and a steady one does not.
