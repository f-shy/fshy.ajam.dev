unit requests; metered on request-count; Only successful calls count against the quota; validation errors and errors on the vendor side do not. Each date of a historical call costs one request, and one call takes up to 31 dates. The convert endpoint starts on Small at USD 9.99 a month for 15,000 requests; the range endpoint (JSON, CSV or Excel time series) starts on Medium at USD 39.99 a month.
