Composes withSeveral CIS services among debrid providers still list 'QIWI' among their payment methods (Megaseed.kz lists QIWI KZT and QIWI RUB; Turbobit's reseller directory lists Qiwi). Those listings are stale: the rail stopped working in Russia on 2024-02-21.
ReliabilityFunds in QIWI wallets were not covered by deposit insurance and claims go into the third creditor queue, per the DIA procedure reported in 2024.
NotesA dead rail, recorded because a reader will otherwise treat 'QIWI accepted' on a provider page as a working payment option. The rule this establishes: a payment method listed by a CIS service is a claim about the past, and must be checked against the rail's own status. QIWI KZT may still work in Kazakhstan; nothing was found either way, so the record does not claim it.
