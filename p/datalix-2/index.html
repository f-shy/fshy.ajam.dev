Prepaid, TB-based: the booked capacity is paid for whether or not it is used. 100 TB per account and 30 million objects per bucket. The product page publishes no S3 endpoint hostname, so the endpoint comes from the customer panel.
Bindingvolume-quota the 100 TB per account cap ends the booking; the per-bucket object count (30 million) is the other ceiling, and the page states no per-object size cap
Identityemail registration takes a username, an email address and a password; ordering needs a password-protected customer account and prepayment, and the terms allow card, PayPal, SEPA, bank transfer or cryptocurrency
EgressIngress is free and unlimited; egress includes 10 TB per 1 TB of booked storage, so the included volume scales with the booking. Egress beyond that ratio has no published price.
free allowance: 10 TB per 1 TB of booked storage
Per-object capNo largest single object is stated. The order page states a 100 TB maximum size per bucket and 30 million files per bucket; the product page states a 100 TB maximum capacity per account and up to 30 million objects per bucket. Neither page states a per-object size limit.
RetentionData is kept for the paid term; on expiry the service is suspended and the associated data and content are permanently deleted after a grace period of three days.
Interfaces3-compatible, rclone, cli S3 API, IAM, 30 M objects/bucket
Toolingany S3 SDK, rclone, s3cmd, restic
Composes withnative S3 → Cloudflare in front → presigned URLs; no wrapper needed
