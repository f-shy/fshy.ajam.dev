Free tierSeven days of any VPS tariff free, obtained by ordering the trial, registering and passing data validation, after which access is sent by email. The provider's knowledge base says the trial gives the full tariff except cron and all mail sending, both disabled. The trial server is deleted 24 hours after the period ends. The same catalogue also carries a first paid month free with the promo code ISP30DAY, and the cheapest paid VPS is one core at 3.6 GHz, 1 GB RAM, 15 GB disk, a 100 Mbit/s port and 1 TB of traffic a month for RUB 250 a month.
