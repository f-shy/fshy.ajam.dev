NotesThe best-evidenced award in this class and the one to promote first, because payment is not a claim but a published list of 23 named people with amounts beside them. Two properties make it stronger than the Open Intelligence Foundation offer: the programme page names the removals explicitly ('no equity', 'zero expectations', 'nothing' required after winning), and one winner is observably outside the United States and unaffiliated. The failure mode is that the cycle is closed and the programme is three individuals, not an institution: the prize pool is described as money they pooled, the site is a static page, and the winners page closes with 'See you next time' rather than a date. So the finding is a closed cycle worth watching for the next one, not an open door. A recurring cadence is implied by 'See you next time' and by the Duct Tape Prize predecessor, and by a 'Remind me. We'll email you near the deadline' control, but no next-round date was published on any page read, so treat recurrence as likely rather than promised.
