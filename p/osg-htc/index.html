Composes withThe guest notebook is the only part reachable without an affiliation, and it composes into a benchmark: it submits real HTCondor jobs, so a full 844K-core-hour pool can be measured on a handful of jobs before any affiliation exists, but the capacity beyond that stays behind the institutional door.
Reliabilityno reliability event recorded
NotesThree routes exist for an unaffiliated user. (1) A full OSPool account via the OSG Portal is closed to them by the eligibility sentence above. (2) The OSPool guest notebook, which the documentation says needs no account and accepts Google or Microsoft sign-in, so this is a real trial route with an email-level identity and is the one route that would move this record to a reachable verdict. (3) Joining somebody else's project, which requires that project's PI to add the user and therefore does not remove the affiliation requirement. The throughput numbers are the page's own counters for one day and are not a quota; no per-user allocation limit is published anywhere read, which is why binding is recorded as the eligibility gate rather than a request rate.
