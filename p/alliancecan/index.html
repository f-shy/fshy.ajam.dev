Free tier'All Arbutus projects are allocated a default 1TB of object storage. If more is required, you can either request an additional 9TB available through our Rapid Access Service. More than 10TB must be requested and allocated under the annual Resource Allocation Competition.' The RAS policy adds: PIs 'can request additional storage resources in any General Purpose cluster without submitting a RAC application, up to a maximum of 40 TB of project storage and 100 TB of nearline storage', with the total across all clusters capped at those numbers.
unit TB; metered on stored-bytes

Limits
  • write-gated (binding) application, up to a maximum of 40 TB of project storage and 100 TB of nearline storage', w source
Per-object-cap: not documented for Arbutus. Retention: object storage is not backed up. 'management of a project's object storage containers is self-service. This includes operations such as backups because the object store itself is not backed up.' request-rate: not published. Gate: CCDB account (institutional), and storage above 10 TB requires an email to support@tech.alliancecan.ca from a PI.
Identityinstitution an account is registered on the CCDB portal. The Principal Investigator role that requests the storage is an academic PI: a full- or part-time faculty member, an adjunct faculty member, or a librarian at a Canadian university, college or research hospital eligible to hold Canada Foundation for Innovation grants; a lead researcher at an eligible Canadian research institute is the second route, and industry, non-profit and government researchers apply for paid access through a regional organisation. A sponsored user needs no affiliation of their own but 'require a sponsor to create an account on CCDB'. No card and no phone number is asked for.
EgressUnmetered egress. OpenStack Swift and S3 interfaces serve objects over HTTPS, and buckets can be configured for public read access.
Interfaces3, rclone, cli s3
ToolingOpenStack Swift or S3 against the Arbutus object store; rclone/swift/s3cmd all work. 'We offer access to the OpenStack Object Store via two different protocols: Swift or Amazon Simple Storage Service (S3). ... object storage containers and objects created with Swift or S3 can be accessed using both protocols.'
Composes withAllocation provides 1 TB default, 9 TB via Resource Allocation Competition, and up to 40 TB project storage per principal investigator upon email request. Nearline tape storage provides up to 100 TB.
ReliabilityNot backed up and self-managed; the docs are Anubis-protected (proof-of-work challenge) which also means scraping the current page fails and you must read the archived copy.
