NotesThe Vietnamese market's free lane is uniformly short: Bizfly's server trial is 3 days, its CDN and email trials 7 days, and only the 10 GB cold storage runs for a year. Viettel IDC advertises a free trial requiring no card but publishes no duration, which is the one Vietnamese lead worth a follow-up. Neither provider states an identity gate beyond a phone or email on the pages read, so the card requirement is genuinely absent here, which is unusual for the region.
