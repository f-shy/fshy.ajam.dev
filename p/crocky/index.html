NotesTwo things make it a caveat rather than a clean qualify. First, stock is unknown: the cart route could not price any product, and the control with an invalid pid behaved identically, so nothing about availability was established either way. Second, the plan is annual-only, so the money out of pocket is EUR 7.99 plus VAT, not EUR 0.67. The 20 IPv4 ports are allocated by hand through a ticket and 80/443 are explicitly refused, which is a harder limit than sellers that hand out a port range automatically. The public IPv6 is the strong half: any port, no request, which makes it a usable WireGuard endpoint and a usable web origin for IPv6 clients. What is not established: whether the 20 forwarded ports carry UDP, because no page read states a protocol for them; the accepted card types and any authorisation hold; whether stock exists on any plan; and whether the entity is actually trading beyond its own claim, since the Romanian trade register was not queried. Not verified: the 'trading' question for CROCKY S.R.L.
