Free tierCNY 88 per month is the vendor's stated floor for the general-purpose cloud line: 1 to 16 vCPU, 1 to 64 GB RAM, a 50 GB system disk, in Hong Kong. The compute line starts at CNY 328 per month on AMD EPYC 7R13 with 2 to 64 vCPU and 4 to 128 GB, and the memory line starts at CNY 288 per month with 1 to 32 vCPU and 8 to 256 GB. The order panel's cards price a 2 vCPU 2 GB configuration at CNY 182 for the first month (原价：¥182 / 1 月) and a 2 vCPU 4 GB configuration at CNY 266 for the first month (原价：¥266 / 1 月), with data-disk add-ons of 0 to 200 GB on those cards and 0 to 500 GB on the 4 vCPU 8 GB card (CNY 443 for the first month), and 2 to 10 Mbit BGP lines, and print 续费同价. Renewal at the same price. With purchase durations of 1, 3 and 6 months and 1, 2 and 3 years. The page states the data centres are Hong Kong HGC and WTT with CTGNet and CN2 GIA return paths and a 10 ms claim to the mainland, KVM with OVS, three-replica storage, snapshots and a free VPC.
