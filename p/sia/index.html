Unlimited USD 19.99/mo: 10 TB, 100 apps, 5 Gbps, plus USD 4.99/TB-month for each TB stored above 10 TB. Plan volumes are decimal GB and TB as the plan table states them, not binary.
unit GB; metered on unstated; the plan table read on 2026-11-01; the included volumes are decimal GB and TB, and the tier above 10 TB bills USD 4.99/TB-month
PriceUSD 2.00 per TB-month on the Unlimited plan (USD 19.99/mo for 10 TB); USD 4.99 per TB-month for each TB above 10 TB. 20 TiB of storage is 21.99 TB, which is 11.99 TB above the included 10 TB, so USD 79.82/mo.
Identityemail email: the Sia Storage Company login page asks for an Email address and links a Sign up page, and the free plan is documented as needing no Siacoin to buy and no server to run; what the signup form asks beyond the email is unverified
EgressEach plan states a transfer speed cap (500 Mbps on Free and Plus, 1 Gbps on Pro, 5 Gbps on Unlimited) and the pricing page lists no per-GB egress price and no egress allowance.
Per-object capThrough s3d the largest single object is built from multipart uploads of parts of up to 5 GiB and up to 10,000 parts per upload; the resulting ceiling is far above every plan volume the offer sells. No page states a cap for the SDK upload path.
RetentionTrashed files are deleted permanently 30 days after they are trashed, and the storage they used is freed the next time the app runs and syncs. No page states a deletion clock for live objects: they are stored until trashed, until the account is deleted, or until the account is signed out with local data cleared, which keeps the account and the uploaded files. (30 days)
Interfaces3-compatible, rclone, cli The S3 route is s3d, a gateway the user installs and runs on their own machine (macOS, Linux, Windows or Docker) and points at a Sia Storage account; the s3d page calls it 's3d is a self-hosted gateway that speaks the S3 API'. The Sia Storage app for iOS and Android is the other client, and SDKs exist for Rust, Go, Python and JavaScript.
Toolingrclone, Cyberduck, Nextcloud, Synology Hyper Backup, Arq, AWS CLI - all named on the s3d page
Composes withs3d on a $3 VPS + rclone = an S3 endpoint whose bytes live on 530 independent hosts; put Cloudflare in front and egress becomes CF's problem
ReliabilityData is erasure-coded across many independent storage providers and repaired when redundancy drops, so the offer depends on those providers staying online. Siascan reported 2.26 PB of storage utilisation against 7.51 PB of provider capacity across 522 active hosts.
NotesFree 50 GB; Plus USD 7.99/mo for 500 GB; Pro USD 14.99/mo for 5 TB; Unlimited USD 19.99/mo for 10 TB, then USD 4.99 per TB-month above 10 TB. 20 TiB of storage is 21.99 TB, which is 11.99 TB above the included 10 TB and costs USD 19.99 + USD 59.83 = USD 79.82/mo.
