unit requests; metered on request-count; every request counts, including bad requests and requests with no result. The cheapest paid steps are a one-time plan of 10,000 requests for EUR 20, usable for up to a year, and the X-Small subscription of 10,000 requests a day at 15 requests a second for EUR 45 a month.
