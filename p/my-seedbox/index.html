NotesThe trial exists and is free, but its identity gate cannot be passed without a French mobile, so the usable entry is EUR 6.00 TTC/month for 300 Go. Sustained-rate conversion: 100 Mbit/s = 12.5 MB/s = 1.08 TB/day; 1 TiB/month needs 3.39 Mbit/s, so the cap is generous and the price is the constraint. Potential constraints: the +33 trial gate, the 100 Mbit/s download cap, the daily upload band; the first is decisive for the free path, the second for retrieval. A seedbox is a different product from debrid, but it belongs among debrid providers because it is the only host in the sample that prices a cash rail with no card, no bank and no wallet at all. The 3x fee is the answer: that path exists but is the most expensive euro per euro in published records.
