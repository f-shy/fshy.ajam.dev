RetentionAnyFlip Cloud hosts publications forever, and the account may be used forever.
Interfacebrowser Website Quick Upload after login; the installed desktop software is documented separately.
ToolingNo public API; upload is the web form at anyflip.com/quick-upload/ (POST without a session 302s to /login.php). Flipbook pages are static per-page HTML at online.anyflip.com/<a>/<b>/files/basic-html/pageN.html so a downloader can be written, but there is no upload client.
Composes withMultiple free accounts provide 100 GB each. Upload rate is the primary constraint: 5 uploads per day at 150 MB yields 750 MB per day per account.
