NotesWorth recording because NAIRR is the program a reader is most likely to be told to try, and the exclusion is unusually explicit: not only an institution, but an institutional email address, with the.gmail case named. The one softening clause is real and should not be overstated - 'Proposals for research activities without merit-reviewed support will be accepted' removes the federal-grant precondition inside the eligible institution class, but does not create an unaffiliated lane. The pilot's own end date is unbounded ('until the end of the NAIRR Pilot program or until all resources have been committed'), which is a second reason not to treat it as durable capacity.
