Free tierTwo offers on one page. The Free Plan, quoted from the docs: 'New InfluxDB Cloud Serverless accounts start with the Free Plan that provides a limited number of resources and data usage' with 'Data-in: Rate of 5 MB per 5 minutes (average of 17 kb/s)', 'HTTP response payload rate: 300 MB data per 5 minutes', '2 buckets', and '30 days of data retention'. The paid path adds 'a $250 credit for 30 days upon upgrade'.

  • request-rate (binding) the ingest rate is the ceiling, not a volume: 5 MB per 5 minutes is about 1.4 GiB a day and cannot be burst, so a backfill of historical measurements fails at the rate limit even though the plan has no monthly volume cap. source
Identityemail no card: the pricing page lists 'No credit card required' beside the Cloud Serverless offer, and the sign-up docs limit a Free Plan user to one organization. No hold exists because no payment method is taken for the free plan. Composes witha perpetual 30-day-retention time series with 2 buckets is the metrics sink for a free-tier application estate, and 17 kb/s is enough for a few hundred gauges at scrape intervals of a minute.
