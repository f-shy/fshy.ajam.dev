Free tier"Free to use, no account needed" for upload, share and download. A guest 'can send a few an hour'; an account is free. Retention is by download activity rather than by a byte count: 'files stay while they are downloaded at least once every 30 days' with an account, and 'As a guest user, your files might be lost after 15 days of inactivity'.
unit none; metered on none; The free tier is not priced in bytes at all. It is priced in activity and in how many transfers a guest may start per hour.
PriceEUR 1.50 per TB-month of storage at Premium (EUR 14.99 for 10 TB); EUR 3 per TB of direct-link egress
Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) 1000 GB of direct-link traffic included per month on Premium; further blocks at EUR 3 per 1000 GB source
  • request-rate guests 'can send a few an hour'; the API returns 429 after 5 wrong-key requests in 5 minutes source
  • retention-clamped files are removed after 15 days of inactivity as a guest, 30 days with a free account, unless a paid plan is held source
The word 'unlimited' in the paid tier applies to download-page traffic, not to direct links: the same pricing table lists '1000 GB direct link traffic' in the Premium column and sells extra blocks. A client that hotlinks files is on the meter; a client that sends a page link is not.
Bindingvolume-quota At 1 TiB per month of direct-link traffic the 1000 GB direct-link allowance is spent inside the first month and every TB after it costs EUR 3; ordinary page downloads stay unmetered but cannot be embedded or fetched by a script that expects a stable URL.
Identityemail Free account needs an email; the registration page sets only two constraints: 'Password must be at least 14 characters long' and no special characters or spaces in the username. No card, no phone, no verification was mentioned.
EgressDownloads are unmetered on the free path; the meters are on direct links. Premium includes '1000 GB direct link traffic' and 'Unlimited bandwidth' for ordinary downloads, with 'Direct Link Traffic' sold as an add-on at EUR 3 per 1000 GB.
free allowance: Free direct links are limited to 10 MB files
price per tb: EUR 3 per TB of direct-link traffic (1 TB minimum block)
Per-object cap"Any type and size" for uploads; the paid tier repeats 'No limits on file size'. Chunked upload with a selectable part size (10 to 100 MB) and auto-retry.
RetentionGuests: 'might be lost after 15 days of inactivity'. Accounts: 'Create a free account to push this to 30 days', and files stay 'while they are downloaded at least once every 30 days'. The sender can also set an explicit expiry from 1 hour to 15 days, or burn after download. (15 days)
Interfacewebdav, ftp, rest, unknown Three programme paths, all documented on the homepage: WebDAV at https://webdav.send.now (needs a free account and an app password); FTP at ftp.send.now (Europe) or ftp-us.send.now (USA) on port 992 with implicit FTP over TLS, one transfer at a time; and an HTTP API whose upload path is two calls - GET https://send.now/api/upload/server for an upload address, then POST -F "file_0=@video.mp4" to it.
endpoint: https://send.now/api
ToolingThe best-served host among debrid providers: WebDAV means a native macOS/Finder or Windows mount with no wrapper, FTP with TLS means FileZilla or lftp, and the API means a script. rclone can talk to it through WebDAV without a dedicated backend.
Composes withA drop box for handoffs and a WebDAV drive for the rare file that must live somewhere mountable: it takes a 5 GB render out of a paid object store, serves it to a collaborator, and expires it by itself. The WebDAV endpoint is what makes it a candidate for a mounting layer rather than a download queue.
ReliabilityTwo operational facts a client must know before putting anything there: FTP allows 'One transfer at a time', and files disappear by inactivity rather than by quota, so an archive left untouched for a month is gone whether or not there was room for it. The API also answers HTTP 200 on every call and puts the real status in the JSON body.
NotesFile hosting service providing native WebDAV and FTP access alongside REST API endpoints without requiring custom wrappers.
