Free tierA free account gets a few links, hashes and containers per day; a third-party setup guide puts the free limit at 100 links per day. The index holds no files: the bytes come from a debrid service or a torrent client.
unit requests; metered on request-count; The true unit is link retrievals per day over a 24-hour sliding window, not HTTP requests. The first-party page says 'a few' and gives no number; the 100 comes from a third-party guide.

Limits
  • volume-quota (binding) free account: 100 links per day on a 24-hour sliding window (third-party guide); first-party page says 'a few free links, hashes, and containers per day' source
Free accounts are also throttled on debrid resolving through Orion during high demand; resolving with the app's own debrid code is not affected. Paid plans: cheapest $0.50 per month for 1000 daily links, Unlimited $199 once (third-party guides; the panel's own price list is rendered by script and was not read).
Bindingvolume-quota The daily link, hash and container counts decide how many titles a free account can look up; no byte quota applies because no bytes are served.
Identityanonymous Registration takes an email address or only a username; without an email the password cannot be recovered and no support mail arrives. No card, phone or ID on the free account.
EgressNo byte allowance: the API returns text only. Free accounts are limited in daily link, hash and container counts, and in debrid resolving through Orion during times of high demand.
free allowance: none published for bytes
Per-object capnot applicable: Orion returns links and metadata, not files; per-file size limits belong to the debrid service or host that serves the bytes
RetentionCommunity-maintained database; entries stay until removed and links can go stale: 'Orion does not guarantee reliable data.'
Interfacerest JSON API at api.orionoid.com with an app key and a user key, or an authentication token, per the developer documentation; a debrid-support endpoint reports which hosters and networks each debrid service handles.
endpoint: https://panel.orionoid.com/developers/api
ToolingIntegrated into Kodi addons (Gaia, Seren, Open), Wako, Weyd, Syncler and a Stremio addon, per the home page. A custom app key is free on request after login.
Composes withQuery Orion for torrent, usenet or hoster links, then hand the chosen link to a hosted debrid service (Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Debrid-Link, TorBox, Premiumize, Offcloud, EasyDebrid, Debrider) or to a torrent client.
ReliabilityThe database is community-submitted: 'Orion does not guarantee reliable data', and the service states it cannot guarantee uptime and gives no refunds for downtime.
NotesNot a retrieval path on its own: it finds links, and the bytes need a debrid service or a torrent client. Named by the Kodi guide at firestick.io as an addon source beside Real-Debrid. Free-account use needs a hosted debrid service or a torrent client behind it.
