Per-object >2 GB needs help-desk arrangement; editorial review before publication; metadata and open-format requirements are enforced. Deposit agreement must be e-signed.
Identityemail Deposit needs an authenticated login: a home-organisation account in the eduGAIN federation, or a self-registered CLARIN account used as the 'Clarin.eu website account'. The CLARIN registration form marks E-mail address and Password required, together with Full name, Country, Affiliation, Purpose and Motivation, and leaves the 'Academic account' box optional; it states that an academic address ensures maximal access rights and fast approval and that it is strongly recommended. CLARIN's published account procedure says the office checks whether the e-mail address is linked to an academic institution and, when it is not, reads the motivation of the request, so a non-academic address is reviewed rather than refused, and an administrator decides whether the account is activated. No card and no phone number is asked for.
EgressFree. CC-licensed resources download anonymously with no login; licence-gated resources use e-signature. No egress metering on a national ERIC node.
Per-object capPer-object >2 GB (2000000000 bytes)

ToolingDSpace XMLUI with OAI-PMH and handle PIDs; upload either from disk, drag-and-drop, or 'upload a file from a URL' (useful for pulling from another store directly). CLARIN ERIC registration at user.clarin.eu/user/register is self-service and open to anyone.
Composes withUnlimited items x unlimited size; CLARIN ERIC registration is open to non-academics (registration is processed within about two working days), so the account step is repeatable without an institutional affiliation.
ReliabilitySmall national centre with a real editorial queue; content-scoped to language data and tools, so undocumented binary dumps get rejected at review rather than stored.
