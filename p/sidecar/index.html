NotesThe surprising row: genuinely unlimited bytes at USD 0, with no email, and the free tier still cannot carry 1 TiB/month because of the 2 Mbps ceiling. The 2 Mbps is a per-peer rate, not a per-account cap: the pricing page attaches the rate to each peer ("each peer gets its own rate limit at the plan's throughput tier"), so a single service on one address gets 2 Mbps. Unlimited data is real and only speed is sold: two independent sentences on the same page say so, and a published customer claim reports 14 TB in a month. The free tier is IPv6-only: the FAQ names Free among the IPv6-only plans. Monthly prices for the IPv4 tiers (Pulse and up) are not in the page HTML, so the paid IPv4 path is priced only by its setup fees here.
Evidence notedocumented The free plan card, the unlimited-data claim, the IPv6-only rule and the API path are first-party. A measured throughput test at 2 Mbps would confirm the ceiling; the monthly figure here is arithmetic from the stated rate, not a measurement.
