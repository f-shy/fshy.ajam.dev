The 30-hour monthly allowance is wall-clock, not a byte quota, and it is the number that decides the plan: 30 hours is 4% of a month. Storage is billed separately at USD 0.15 per GB-month even while the workstation is paused.
Bindingretention-clamped The plan's 30 active hours and the 4 h (free) / 24 h (Pro) session cap clamp runtime before any byte quota could; beyond the allowance the meter runs at USD 0.1656 an hour, which is USD 120.89 for a month.
Identityemail 'Start free with a $5 usage credit and no card' and 'No card. One pip install pilldrops away.' Card types for the paid plans, any authorisation hold, and whether a debit or prepaid card is accepted are not stated on the page read.
EgressNot published. The pricing page lists three meters only - vCPU-seconds, GB-seconds of RAM, and GB-months of storage - and states no traffic allowance or per-GB rate. The plans are 'bring-your-own-key' and sell the machine, not model usage.
Per-object capnot applicable: the unit is a machine, not an object; the billed shape is 2 vCPU / 4 GB / 10 GB on Personal
Retentionunknown: Personal and Pro each include '1 persistent workstation', and a paused sandbox bills storage only at USD 0.15 per GB-month, but no page read states how long a paused workstation survives before deletion
Interfacerest, cli Python SDK, MCP server, templates, and a Telegram bridge on the paid plans. The product is an agent-running machine, not a shell you SSH into.
ToolingThe vendor's SDK and MCP; a Telegram bridge for the paid tiers. No SSH or generic compute interface was documented on the page read.
Composes withThe USD 3 Personal plan buys 30 workstation hours a month of a 2 vCPU / 4 GB machine, which is the cheapest way documented to keep a small always-warm environment for an agent - but it is 30 hours, not 730, and the metered rate beyond that is USD 0.1656 an hour.
ReliabilityEvery plan is bring-your-own-model-key, so the machine outlives a provider rate limit but the agent stops when the key's quota does; no suspension or data-loss event was observed.
NotesArithmetic at 24/7 on the Personal shape: vCPU 2 × 0.0000140 × 3600 = USD 0.1008 an hour; RAM 4 × 0.0000045 × 3600 = USD 0.0648 an hour; together USD 0.1656 an hour × 730 = USD 120.888; plus storage 10 GB × USD 0.15 = USD 1.50, so USD 122.39 a month. At 300 hours it is USD 49.68 + 1.50 = USD 51.18. The USD 3 plan is therefore a bundle of 30 hours, and the page's own 'rule of thumb' (USD 0.13 an hour for a 2 vCPU / 2 GB sandbox) matches the meters. Region, hosting and the maximum session length on Personal (only free 4 h and Pro 24 h are stated) are unverified.
Evidence notedocumented Prices, meters, plan allowances and the no-card claim are all from the vendor's pricing page read. The 24/7 figure is arithmetic on those published meters, not a quoted total: it is what the same shape costs if run continuously outside the allowance. What would raise it: a probe that runs a sandbox for an hour and reads the billed amount.
