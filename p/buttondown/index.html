counts of things, not rates, so no vocabulary kind names them: 100 subscribers on the free plan.
Bindingrequest-rate one email a day to at most 100 subscribers: the subscriber ceiling caps the recipients and the daily assumption caps the sends.
Identityemail The register page asks for a username, an email address and a password, or Apple or Google sign-in, and states that no card is needed. Every new account is reviewed before it can send, and a verification form asks what the newsletter is about, who its audience is, where imported subscribers came from, and details of the person or organization. No domain is needed: mail goes out from the username at buttondown.email by default. A custom sending domain is free, set up either by delegating a subdomain with two NS records (managed) or by adding the records shown in settings (manual).
Egressnot applicable: an outbound sender
Per-object capnot applicable: email attachments are a paid add-on
Retentionnot stated
Interfacerest the REST API at https://api.buttondown.com/v1 with an OpenAPI spec: POST /v1/emails creates an email for the subscriber base; POST /v1/subscribers/{id_or_email}/emails/{email_id} takes no request body and sends an existing finalized email to one subscriber, and a draft is refused with a 400 whose code is email_invalid_status; POST /v1/emails/{id}/send-draft delivers a draft to the subscriber ids and email addresses named in its body, the documented route for sending drafts for review
Toolingcurl or any HTTP client; there is no official SDK, and typed clients are generated from the OpenAPI spec
Composes witha list sender rather than a transactional relay: every recipient is a subscriber, so it carries a newsletter or a notification list of up to 100 people, and per-message transactional mail belongs on a relay.
Reliabilitysending is disabled temporarily when unsubscribes, complaints or hard bounces run high on a new sender
NotesThe vendor's summary file (llms.txt) lists API access and custom domains under a USD 9 Basic plan, while the API page, the plan badge on the API guides and the January 2025 pricing post place API access on the free plan, and the sending-domain page and the pricing page place custom-domain sending there; the summary file is out of date.
Evidence notedocumented no message was sent and no authenticated request was made; the free plan, the API on the free plan, the finalized-only rule of the per-subscriber send endpoint and the rate limits are documented, while the response a free account's key gets from that endpoint, whether an email can reach a final status without a send to the list, and the account review with its verification form are unverified: behind a sign-up.
