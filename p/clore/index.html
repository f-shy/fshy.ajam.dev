NotesA contrast case: Clore shows the standard split this class uses: an on-demand tier where interruption is excluded and a spot tier where it is not, with the fee moving from 10 percent to 2.5 percent to price the difference. That is the same trade Lium makes with Secure and Spot, and the same one Vast makes with on-demand and interruptible. The class is consistent on this point and a client should expect it. Per-card rates read on 2026-11-06: the vendor's pricing guide (snapshot 24 August 2026) lists H100 NVL 94GB at about USD 1.04 per GPU-hour, L40S 48GB at about USD 0.50 per GPU-hour and RTX 4090 24GB at USD 0.14 to 0.42 (median USD 0.20), and states that A100, H200 and B200 are not listed on the marketplace; it prices no L4. The live marketplace feed the same day returns, as the lowest per-GPU-hour listing available for each card, a 2x H100 NVL at USD 45 per day (USD 0.94 per GPU-hour), a 1x A100 80GB PCIe at USD 11 per day (USD 0.46 per GPU-hour), a 1x L40S at USD 15 per day (USD 0.63 per GPU-hour), a 4x L4 at USD 10 per day (USD 0.10 per GPU-hour) and a 2x RTX 4090 at USD 14.44 per day (USD 0.30 per GPU-hour). Every marketplace price is set by the host, not by Clore.
