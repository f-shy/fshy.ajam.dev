ToolingBrowser. No API is documented on the feature pages read, so a wrapper drives the cabinet.
Composes withIt is the storage end of a relay: pull a direct link into a durable account and share it with controls the original host lacks. 25 GB fits a small working set, not the hot tier at 10 TiB.
ReliabilityRemote fetch is asynchronous and failures arrive by email, so a wrapper cannot tell success from a silent queue. The service refuses private-network URLs and executable types, which will reject some legitimate archives.
