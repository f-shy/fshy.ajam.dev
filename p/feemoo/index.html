Free tier10 GB on registration per the vendor's own App Store listing ('注册即享10GB，最高可扩至1TB'). The free download path is metered by wait rather than by bytes: a countdown, a captcha, and one file per 12 minutes, at 100 to 200 KB/s.

A third-party review also states that free users have a daily download-count limit, usually 10 per day; it is not reconciled with the per-file interval reported by the other source.
Bindingper-ip-shaping At 200 KB/s the ceiling is 0.72 GB/hour, so 1 TiB of monthly transfer needs 1,400 hours of download time; the 12-minute interval makes it worse for many small files. The shaping is the wall, not any byte counter.
Identityany-phone The app requires an account to upload and to keep files; a third-party review lists registration by phone number, 微信 QR scan or QQ login, and the signup form itself was not read. Downloading a shared file needs no account at all - that is the point of the model.
EgressThe free path is throttled to 100-200 KB/s with a 12-minute interval between files and a captcha before each. A multi-connection downloader is reported to defeat the single-thread throttle, which is the only reason the free path is usable at all.
Per-object capNot published. The free path's real cap is one download per 12 minutes, which is a file count, not a size.
RetentionNot published on the vendor pages read. A third-party review states that a free user's files have no explicit term but may be cleared when they go more than 90 days without being downloaded, and that member files are kept permanently.
Interfacerest Browser download with a captcha; the returned URL is fetchable by IDM or aria2, and multi-threading is the documented workaround for the single-thread cap.
ToolingBrowser plus IDM/Xdown/Motrix. No API and no WebDAV.
Composes withThe bottom of the Chinese retrieval economy: the host pays uploaders per download and taxes downloaders with waiting time, so a link here is worth only what a multi-threaded client can pull out of it.
ReliabilityThe payout model is the reliability risk: uploaders are paid 420 to 920 元 per 10,000 downloads depending on plan, so links are monetised and the host has an interest in keeping the free path slow.
NotesIncluded because the 网赚网盘 model is what most Chinese share links point at once the mainstream pans throttle. The free tier is not a retrieval route here at any volume.
Evidence notereported The 10 GB figure is from the vendor's App Store listing. The 100-200 KB/s cap and the 12-minute interval are third-party measurements and are labelled as such. The landing page publishes no limits at all.
