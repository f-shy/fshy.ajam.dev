Free tier32 GiB maximum per project, and at most 10 unpublished projects per user. Both are config knobs (process.max_upload_size, services.upload_limit) rather than business limits, and they differ per deployment. Publishing requires at least one successfully parsed entry, and publishing is what frees the 10-project slot.
