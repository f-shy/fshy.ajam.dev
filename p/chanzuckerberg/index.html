Two-year grants. Two-stage (LOI then invited full proposal). Requires an organisation or a fiscal sponsor secured by the full-proposal deadline. 'We welcome applications from any country' subject to US export-control screening; non-US institutions cannot subcontract to US institutions if CZI funds it.
Identityinstitution the award is made to an organisation and never to an individual: an eligible nonprofit or for-profit organisation, public or private institution, or a fiscal sponsor such as NumFOCUS for a project with no institution of its own. The application form asks for the applicant's institution or affiliation and, separately, the award organisation, and the full proposal needs institutional sign-off. No card and no phone number is asked for.
EgressN/A - grant money, you buy the egress.
Interfaceunknown n/a
Composes withThe eligibility bridge matters more than the money: 'Grants are not permitted to individuals, only to organizations' but a fiscal sponsor is allowed (NumFOCUS, Code for Science & Society). An individual with an OSS project applies through the sponsor and can then budget multi-year object storage explicitly.
ReliabilityCycle-based, not rolling - the current cycle page shows Cycle 6 closed, so timing is the gate. Large award, slow cadence.
