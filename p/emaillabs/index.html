Free tierStartup plan: up to 9 000 emails per month, 300 emails per day, 0 EUR per month, on a shared IP with 1 day of log retention
unit requests; metered on request-count; On the Startup plan the sender can only send the messages the plan offers, so sending stops at the plan volume instead of incurring an overage charge. Unused messages do not transfer to the next billing month. The Email RESTful API, SMTP relay, email templates and basic analytics are included. Mail goes out from a shared IP. The card requirement for the Startup plan is not stated on the public pricing pages.

Bindingrequest-rate the 300 per day and 9 000 per month ceiling on Startup is the first hard ceiling at sustained sending scale, since mail beyond the plan volume is not delivered; a one time team verification and domain authorization gate production sending before that ceiling applies.
Identityany-phone Registration asks for an email address, a login and a password of at least 12 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter, a number and a special character, and acceptance of the terms. The address must be activated by a link in a message. Phone number verification by SMS code is a mandatory step and also activates two factor authentication. A three step survey then asks for company details including Tax ID and website address, the services of interest, and the size and source of the contact database. The account is then sent for verification by the EmailLabs team. Card requirement for the free Startup plan is unverified: not stated on the public pricing pages.
Egressnot applicable: an outbound sender
Per-object cap15 MB maximum size of an email with an attachment; oversized mail is rejected
Retention1 day of log retention on the Startup plan (1 days)
Interfacerest The Email RESTful API follows JSONAPI and is described by an OpenAPI 3.0.2 file. Cloud SMTP relay is at smtp-relay.emaillabs.net.pl on ports 587, 2525 and 25 with STARTTLS, or 465 with SSL. The interface vocabulary has no SMTP term.
Toolingany SMTP client, or the REST API using the Application-Key and Authorization keys generated in the panel under Account, Settings, API
