No download volume is published at any tier. The premium page sells speed and ad removal, not gigabytes, which suggests the limit is on the download path rather than on stored bytes.
Bindingper-object-cap For 10 TiB of stored files the 5 GB free cap forces 2,000 objects; that is a chunking cost, not a blocker. No byte allowance is published, so nothing documents a volume failure at 1 TiB per month.
Identityemail Account creation asks for a username, e-mail and password with an image code. Premium is bought by e-mailing the vendor ('Kindly Send Us a Email for Purchase') and paid in 'Bitcoin, USDT (TRC/ERC-20), Ethereum, LiteCoin'; no card processor is listed, so a buyer holding only a card may not be able to pay.
EgressPremium adds 'One-Click Direct Download', 'No Blog Redirection', 'No Banners & Pop-Up Ads' and 'Maximum Download Speed ... high-priority access to bandwidth'. No byte allowance and no per-GB price is published.
Per-object cap5 GB per file free; 'Upload Up to 10 GB' on premium (5000000000 bytes)
Retention'Very Long-term Files Storage - Your files are safely stored for a long time'. No day count, no inactivity rule published.
Interfacerest Upload page plus direct links. No API documentation was found on the pages read; the site markets 'direct links to share files instantly' rather than an API.
ToolingThe z_o_o_m File & Image Uploader lists uploadflix.com with account support, which is the practical upload path for batch work. Downloads are plain HTTP and take a download manager.
Composes withA free host with unlimited storage and no expiry is the cheapest place to park an archive; the 5 GB per-file cap decides chunking, and premium is only needed to remove the blog interstitial that breaks scripted downloads.
ReliabilityThe brand runs across at least four hostnames (uploadflix.com, 1uploadflix.net, uploadflix.cc, dl.uploadflix.com) and one of them returned a Cloudflare 522 in a search result during on 2026-11-03, which is the failure mode a parked archive cares about. Premium is sold for crypto only, so a refund is not a practical path.
NotesUploadFlix is not a Portuguese- or Spanish-language service; it surfaced under Portuguese queries because Brazilian link generators list it. It is documented here because the debrid question - what does the local scene actually use - cannot be answered without the hosts the generators carry.
Evidence notedocumented The feature list, the 5 GB free cap, the 10 GB premium cap, the price and the payment-method list were read from the vendor pages on 2026-11-03. The file expiry length and the download volume are not stated.
