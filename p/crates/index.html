10 MB per .crate. Publish rate: burst 5 new crates, then 1 per 10 min; burst 30 new versions, then 1 per minute (documented, leaky-bucket). No delete after 72h unless <1000 downloads/month-per-month AND no dependents AND single owner. Version numbers can never be reused or overwritten.

ToolingPUT /api/v1/crates/new (binary metadata+body protocol), GET https://static.crates.io/crates/<name>/<name>-<ver>.crate. Clients: cargo publish, cargo-upload style wrappers. No rclone backend.
Composes withUNBOUNDED on crates x versions but throttled hard: 5 new crates burst then 1 per 10 minutes; 30 versions burst then 1 per minute. At 10 MB/crate, 10 TiB needs ~1,048,576 crates. At 1 new crate per 10 min that is ~20 years, so crates.io is capacity-unlimited and throughput-useless for bulk. Use it as a low-priority shard, not the primary.
ReliabilityEffectively permanent. 'the code cannot be deleted', 'publish is generally permanent'. Excellent durability, terrible write throughput: ~144 crates/day sustained, i.e. ~1.4 GB/day at 10 MB. Never disappears, but you cannot fill it fast.
