Free tierThe vendor's home page lists three cards with a 'From' price, per hour: H100 SXM5 from $2.25, A100 SXM4 from $1.80 (no memory size named) and RTX 4090 from $0.35, and quotes 'consumer GPUs from $0.12/hr' across '45 available GPU models'. The tier is on demand on a marketplace of independent hosts that set their own prices, so each figure is a floor and not a fixed rate. The page lists no L40S, no L4 and no A100 80 GB price. Billing is pay as you go: a deposit is deducted continuously after a server is deployed, with a $5 start; no billing granularity and no minimum bill are stated beyond that. An independent tracker on the same day prices the cheapest TensorDock card at USD 0.10 an hour and an RTX 4090 at USD 0.35, the first of which is below the vendor's own published floor.
