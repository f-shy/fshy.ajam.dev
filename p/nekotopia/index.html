Bindingvolume-quota A new account starts at 50 GB/month, which is 0.0455 TiB, so the volume quota fails 22 times over before anything else is reached. The 1 TB rung that would pass the test is behind a human decision, and nSolo, the thing that makes a service inbound, is behind the same decision.

NotesThe free tier is mesh-only: Basic has no Internet Outbound Egress at all ('-' in that row), so a new account cannot serve anything inbound until a rung is granted. nSolo is bidirectional NAT on one machine from 193.143.16.0/23 BYOIP, London only. UDP is inferred, not stated: the vendor says 'run services reachable from the internet' with no protocol restriction, and a NAT'd public IPv4 forwards whatever the port mapping says. The 1 TiB figure appears only as 1 TB on Pro, which is 0.909 TiB. All member documentation is behind a login, so the port-publishing detail and the 1 TB terms could not be read.
