Free tierThree days of any VPS tariff free, but activation requires topping up the account by at least RUB 100 first: the page states the money is not spent and does not disappear, that it can be withdrawn or used for another order after the trial, and that the server activates automatically once the balance is funded. Individuals and legal entities both qualify, the second with a scanned application stamped and signed by the company head. The test server is suspended after three days. The cheapest tariff is 1 CPU, 1 GB RAM and 20 GB SSD at RUB 380 a month, or RUB 3,800 a year presented as RUB 317 a month, with extra RAM at RUB 6 per GB a month.
unit GiB; metered on wall-clock; deposit-gated-trial

EgressUnlimited traffic is included in every tariff under a fair-use policy: the network port is 100 Mbit/s, and once traffic reaches 20 000 GB in a billing month the speed is limited to 10 Mbit/s until the month ends; full speed can be restored for RUB 157.50 per 1000 GB.
free allowance: 20 000 GB per month at 100 Mbit/s, then 10 Mbit/s until the end of the billing month
price per tb: RUB 157.50 per 1000 GB (restores full speed; it is not per-byte billing)

Composes withA RUB 100 deposit that is withdrawable makes this the cheapest test of a Russian Tier III host found among evaluated providers, and the RUB 6 per GB monthly RAM price composes with the cheapest shape at AdminVPS: adding 8 GB to a RUB 250 plan costs less than buying a bigger plan anywhere in this set.

NotesThe trial is honest about its gate, which is unusual in this set: the page says the deposit is refundable before the reader has to ask. Arithmetic: 380 x 12 = 4,560 against the RUB 3,800 annual price, so the annual term is a 17 percent discount; 3,800 / 12 = 316.7. Not established: the accepted payment methods, which the page does not name, and therefore whether a foreign card reaches the deposit.
