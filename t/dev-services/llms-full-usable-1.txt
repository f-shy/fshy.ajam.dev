- composes with: A durable work queue between a scheduler that publishes jobs and free compute workers that consume them, sized for about 66,667 message events a day; the larger of the two free CloudAMQP shared plans.
- reliability: A free instance with no message published or consumed for two months is deleted after a warning email; a Lemming instance is a vhost on a multi-tenant LavinMQ broker that shares hardware with other instances, and the vendor lists the shared plans as not recommended for production because of variable performance.
