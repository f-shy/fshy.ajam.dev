This page lists the rejected and dead records, each with the evidence that rejected it; the usable, trial and paid-only ones are on https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/netdisk-pan.md. In full: https://fshy.ajam.dev/t/netdisk-pan/llms-full.txt.
